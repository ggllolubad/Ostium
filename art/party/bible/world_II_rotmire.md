## II. The Rotmire (Toxin + Water, Toxic Pool)

Fen villages on stilts where the water turned to poison and nothing in it rots. **Toxin** ripens bodies: swelling, blisters, moss and fungus. **Water** preserves them: bog bodies tanned to leather, skin stained brown. The cast are fenfolk, hags, eel-catchers and peat-cutters, and the beasts of the fen. **Palette:** peat brown, bile green, lantern light (a dull amber, the only warm note).

### Rulers

#### The Mire Mother

- **Lore line:** "The fen's oldest drowned head, swollen to the size of a house. The hags call her Mother."
- **Was:** a woman drowned in the fen as an offering, centuries before the Breaking.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a head only, sunk to the chin in black water, filling the frame.
- **Body:** a colossal head of bog-tanned skin, brown and leathery, the features flattened by peat pressure. A braided rope noose still around the neck. Reeds, moss and toadstools grow from the scalp; fen lanterns hang from the reeds where the hags tied them. **The Tooth** is lodged in her lower jaw as one of her teeth.
- **Head:** heavy lids half open on bile-green eyes, a mouth that opens to show a wet cave with the hags' offerings inside.
- **Signature shape:** the huge head rising from water, crowned with reeds.
- **Colour:** tanned brown skin, bile green eyes, amber lantern light.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow blink and bubbles at the lips. Attack is the mouth opening and a gush of poisoned water.

#### The Stilt Hall

- **Lore line:** "When the water turned, the village climbed into the moot-hall to wait. In the morning the hall had walked off."
- **Was:** the stilt villages' moot-hall, a long thatched house on oak piles where the reeve held court and the eel tithe was stored, and the families who sheltered in it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a long house standing knee-deep in black water on eight of its oak piles as legs, leaning toward the company. The thatch ridge is cut off by the frame top and the rear gable by the right edge. Its weight sits on the front piles, which are bowed and splayed under it.
- **Body:** toxin has ripened the piles into legs: the oak has swollen soft at two points on each into knee-like burls ringed with shelf fungus, mould fur creeps up from the waterline, and white mycelium threads the timber like veins. The wattle-and-daub walls bulge between the frame posts like a belly between ribs, blistered with bile-green pustules where the daub has split. Water has preserved everything below the thatch: the piles are tanned black and slick, and **the household is in the walls**, tanned bog-brown and unrotted, pressed half out of the daub at the two windows and the open door, still holding what they carried in (a lantern, a swaddled child, a ladle).
- **Dress and gear:** a reed thatch slumped and furred with moss, toadstools fruiting along the ridge; a rope ladder hanging from the door, its rungs swollen; a row of willow eel traps hung under the eaves, the tithe eels inside dried to black strips; the reeve's notched tally sticks nailed beside the door; a hags' horn lantern on an iron hook at the door, lit; a drowned dog hanging by its rope from one pile; a broken punt jammed between two legs; a carved ward-sign over the door split by a bracket fungus; daub holes patched with peat sods; a smoke-blackened kettle on its chain just inside the doorway; leeches on the lower piles; a hag's twine charm of frog bones knotted round the front-left leg.
- **Head:** none. The low, wide doorway is its face, and the household stands in it.
- **Signature shape:** the long thatched house walking on bowed piles.
- **Colour:** peat brown timber and thatch, bile green blisters and mould, one point of lantern amber at the door.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow, creaking shift of weight from pile to pile, water running off the thatch. Attack is a front pile lifted and stamped down on a rank, while the household reaches out of the doorway to pull a hero inside.

#### The Old Heron

- **Lore line:** "It fished the fen before there were stilts. It has come to prefer the fishermen."
- **Was:** the great grey heron of the fen, older than the villages, which fed in the eel shallows under the stilt houses.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, standing in the shallows on one leg with the other drawn up, body hunched, neck folded back into an S. The head and the top of the neck are above the frame; in the attack the beak comes down into frame like a thrown spear. Centre of mass over the standing foot.
- **Body:** a heron's body the size of a cart, grey plumage clumped into wet, directional locks, with bile-green mould grown into the mats. Toxin has pushed one part hard: **the crop and gullet have swollen into a hanging sack** below the folded neck, wider than the body, the skin stretched thin and blistered, the outlines of swallowed things pressing through it (an eel trap, a boot, a hand). Water has preserved the legs: long and scaled, tanned dark brown like bog leather, with thick knuckled joints and long toes splayed on the mud. The long breast plumes hang lank and have rotted into holes at the tips.
- **Dress and gear:** what the fen has hung on it: a fowler's net tangled over one wing with its cork floats; a line of eel hooks and gut line caught in the breast feathers; a hag's twine charm of frog bones knotted round the drawn-up leg; a broken arrow shaft in the shoulder, its fletching rotted; a tuft of reed thatch caught in the back feathers from roosting on the hall; leeches fat on the bare shins; a shelf fungus on the standing knee; peat clods on the toes; a snapped stilt-strap of leather round one ankle; a lantern's bail ring hooked on a claw; a fish bone through the web of one foot; old moult feathers stuck in the mud around the foot.
- **Head:** seen when it strikes: a long dagger beak stained dark at the tip and blistered at the gape, a black crest plume hanging lank, a single round amber eye, dull and old.
- **Signature shape:** the folded S-neck over the hanging, swollen crop, on one stilt leg.
- **Colour:** grey and bone plumage, peat brown legs, bile green mould and blisters.
- **Motion:** idle is utter stillness, the crop swaying a little and the standing leg sinking an inch into the mud. Attack is the neck unfolding: the beak spears down from above the frame and lifts a hero toward the crop.

### Elites

#### Bog Troll

- **Lore line:** "Moss, mud and muscle. It heals faster than you can cut."
- **Was:** a peat-cutter giant, or a man the fen built bigger.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3, hunched, long arms resting on its knuckles.
- **Body:** a huge frame of muscle under a hide of living moss and mud; peat slabs plate its shoulders like armour. Wounds visibly close with green growth. A peat spade is stuck in its back, forgotten.
- **Head:** small for the body, sunk between the shoulders, a heavy brow and a lipless mouth.
- **Signature shape:** the hunched mass with the spade handle sticking up.
- **Colour:** moss green, peat brown.
- **Motion:** idle is moss stirring as wounds knit. Attack is a two-fisted overhead smash.

#### Coven Mother

- **Lore line:** "She leads the hags of the fen. Her antlers are not hers."
- **Was:** the eldest fen hag, midwife and curse-seller to the stilt villages.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, tall and bent, leaning on a staff.
- **Body and dress:** gaunt, long-fingered, in layered sodden shawls hung with charms, dried frogs, bones and twine. **A stag's antlers are bound to her head with cord and wire**, too big for her, the skin around the bindings raw.
- **Head:** a long face, a hooked nose, one bile-green eye and one milky.
- **Signature shape:** the borrowed antlers over a bent figure.
- **Colour:** peat brown shawls, bone, bile green.
- **Motion:** idle is muttering, fingers knotting a cord. Attack is pointing the staff and spitting a curse.

#### The Tithe Reeve

- **Lore line:** "He took one eel in ten for the hall and the fattest for himself. He is still taking."
- **Was:** the reeve of the stilt villages, headman of the moot-hall and collector of the eel tithe.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, portly and upright, leaning back against the weight of his front. A carrying pole hung with tithe eels rests across his right shoulder; his left hand holds out a tally stick as if presenting a bill. Feet planted wide in the mud, weight on the heels.
- **Body:** a big, well-fed man. Toxin has ripened his neck into **a huge goitre** that hangs over the collar onto his chest, mottled bile green and brown, studded with pustules, a ring of small toadstools fruiting at its base. His hands are puffy, the rings sunk into the fingers. Water has tanned his exposed skin brown and leathery and stained the nails dark; his legs are thin under the bulk.
- **Dress and gear:** three layers: a linen shirt gone brown, a quilted wool doublet split at the buttons over the swelling, and a reeve's gown trimmed with otter fur, matted and moulting, its hem rotted into holes and mould fur. A wide leather belt carries a ring of pitted iron moot-hall keys, a leather purse of tithe coins, a horn ink pot and a knife. The carrying pole is black bog oak, a dozen dried eels tied to it by the tails. A bundle of notched tally sticks is bound with cord under his arm; a pewter chain of office has sunk into the goitre's folds. Hose patched on one knee; eel-skin boots split at the toes.
- **Head:** a heavy jowled face over the goitre, small shrewd eyes, a wet lower lip, thin hair plastered under a felt hat with a drooping brim and a heron feather in the band.
- **Signature shape:** the hanging goitre on the stout body, with the pole of eels across the shoulder.
- **Colour:** peat brown gown and skin, bile green goitre, grey pewter.
- **Motion:** idle is counting notches on the tally stick with a thumb, lips moving. Attack is a heavy swing of the eel pole; his other action taps the tally and puts a hero in his debt.

#### The Midden Sow

- **Lore line:** "Every house kept a pig under the floor to eat what fell through. In the Rotmire, a great deal fell through."
- **Was:** the sow kept in the pen beneath the stilt houses, fed on what was dropped through the floorboards.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.8 at the shoulder and 2.4 long, a heavy quadruped standing in mud to the hocks, head low and forward, shoulders higher than the rump. Weight on the forelegs.
- **Body:** a sow ripened to the size of an ox. Toxin has pushed her back hard: **from shoulders to rump the spine is a ridge of bracket fungus and puffballs**, layered like shingles and sweating spores. The flanks below swell with pustules and ripe blisters, and the teats hang swollen in two rows. Water has tanned the hide to brown leather, the sparse bristles clumped into wet tufts; the belly drags in the mud. Thick legs, split hooves, mud caked to the knee.
- **Dress and gear:** an iron nose ring with a length of broken tether chain dragging from it; the owner's notches cut in both ears; a panel of the wattle pen tangled on one flank where she broke out, the withies snapped; a hag's twine charm against sickness tied round a foreleg; three broken pitchfork tines in the shoulder where the village tried; a chewed boot in the corner of her jaw; leeches on the belly; a peat sod plastered on the rump; a strip of sacking caught on the fungus ridge; an old rope burn round the neck from a halter.
- **Head:** a huge long head carried low, small eyes deep in folds of tanned skin, ears flopped forward and notched, short yellowed tusks, the snout wet and mud-caked.
- **Signature shape:** the long, low body under its ridge of bracket fungus.
- **Colour:** peat brown hide, bile green fungus and blisters, bone tusks.
- **Motion:** idle is rooting in the mud, the ridge shedding a haze of spores. Attack is a heavy charge that scatters the front rank, spores bursting from the ridge on impact.

### Cast

#### Bog Body

- **Lore line:** "Tanned by the bog. Leather now, and hungry."
- **Was:** an offering or a murder victim sunk in the peat.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, stiff-jointed, walking as if the limbs are too dry to bend.
- **Body:** skin tanned dark brown and shrunk tight on the bones, flattened in places by peat pressure; red hair dyed by the bog; a rope still around the neck; fingernails long.
- **Signature shape:** the twisted, flattened body and the noose end.
- **Colour:** leather brown, rust-red hair.
- **Motion:** idle is a creaking twitch. Attack is a lunge with clawing hands.

#### Fen Hag

- **Lore line:** "She knows the safe path through the fen. She does not share it."
- **Was:** a village wise-woman.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.85, crouched low, one hand on a pole.
- **Body and dress:** stooped, in sodden shawls, a pole for testing the bog, a lantern on her hip, a bag of herbs and something dripping.
- **Head:** a sharp face under a hood, wet grey hair, a knowing smile.
- **Signature shape:** the bent figure with pole and hanging lantern.
- **Colour:** peat brown, lantern amber.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping the pole. Attack is throwing a fistful of foul mud.

#### Mire Toad

- **Lore line:** "Fat on the fen's poison."
- **Was:** a fen toad.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6 tall, wide and squat.
- **Body:** a toad the size of a dog, warty, blistered with bile-green poison sacs that swell and shrink as it breathes.
- **Signature shape:** the broad squat body with bulging throat.
- **Colour:** mud brown, bile green blisters.
- **Motion:** idle is a pulsing throat. Attack is a tongue strike that leaves poison.

#### Leech Swarm

- **Lore line:** "The fen's own. They wait in the warm shallows."
- **Was:** medicinal leeches from the village barber.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a heap about 0.5 high.
- **Body:** a writhing mound of fat black leeches spilling out of a cracked apothecary jar with a faded label.
- **Signature shape:** the jar and the spill.
- **Colour:** glossy black, brown glass.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow ripple. Attack is the heap surging and clinging.

#### Fen Lurker

- **Lore line:** "A log in the water. Then not."
- **Was:** a fen pike or crocodile-like beast that grew old.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5 long, nearly flat to the water.
- **Body:** a long armoured back that looks exactly like a mossy log, until the jaws open: a long snout, rows of peg teeth, small eyes above the waterline.
- **Signature shape:** the log silhouette, and the open jaws in the attack.
- **Colour:** moss and bark brown, pale gums.
- **Motion:** idle is total stillness, one eye moving. Attack is a sudden lunge and snap.

#### Eel-Catcher

- **Lore line:** "Every eel in the Rotmire has bitten him once. He has kept the marks."
- **Was:** an eel-catcher of the stilt villages, who speared and trapped eels in the shallows under the houses.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, a wading stance, knees bent, the eel glaive held low in both hands with its tines toward the water, ready to stab. Weight on the front foot.
- **Body:** a lean, ropy fenman. Toxin has swollen his forearms and hands to twice their size: each old eel bite has ripened into a blister or a fungal knot, and the fingers are fat and stiff. Water has tanned his skin brown and wrinkled the shins above the waders.
- **Dress and gear:** **an eel glaive**: a long ash pole ending in a flat iron head of five sprung tines like a comb, one tine bent. A long conical willow eel trap slung across the back on a strap, its mouth by the hip and an eel's tail hanging out. A waxed-canvas smock over a wool shirt, the hem rotted into holes and mould fur; thigh waders of leather tied to the belt with cord; a gutting knife and a spool of gut line at the belt; a twist of salt in a rag; a rag bound round one thumb.
- **Head:** a long face, hollow cheeks, stubble, a knitted cap pulled low, one ear swollen with a blister.
- **Signature shape:** the comb-headed glaive held low, with the long trap on the back.
- **Colour:** peat brown canvas and skin, bile green blisters, grey iron.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow stab at the water and a glance at the trap. Attack is a downward stab that pins a hero's foot.

#### Stilt-Fowler

- **Lore line:** "He walked the deep fen on stilts to net the wildfowl. He has not come down since."
- **Was:** a wildfowler who crossed the deep fen on wooden stilts to set his nets and decoys.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6 on his stilts, a thin man perched high on two long poles and leaning forward on a long net pole like a third leg. Knees locked; he sways to keep his balance.
- **Body:** wiry and starved. **His shins have grown into the stilts**: toxin has knitted wood and leg together at the strapping with swollen fungal knots and grey mould, so the stilts read as long jointed legs with bracket fungus at the joins. Water has soaked the poles black and tanned his bare thighs brown.
- **Dress and gear:** leather straps and cords binding the stilts to the legs, sunk into swollen flesh; a short wool tunic, belted, its hem rotted; a long net pole with a folded clap-net at the tip; a wooden decoy duck hanging from the belt on a cord; a brace of dead ducks slung by the necks over one shoulder, plump and unrotted; a sling pouch of stones; a reed whistle on a thong at the neck; a moss-stuffed hood pushed back.
- **Head:** a narrow bearded face, a sharp nose, hair matted with reeds, the whistle at his lips.
- **Signature shape:** the tall thin figure on two stilts with the net pole.
- **Colour:** peat brown, bile green mould, grey.
- **Motion:** idle is a teetering balance, one stilt lifting and setting down again. Attack is the clap-net thrown down over a hero from above.

#### Turf-Wife

- **Lore line:** "She cut turf for the village fires and carried it home on her back. The load has taken root."
- **Was:** a peat-cutter's wife who cut, stacked and carried turf from the cuttings to the stilt houses.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, bent forward under a carrying frame stacked with peat sods taller than she is, leaning on a stick. Knees bent, weight forward.
- **Body:** a stocky, bow-legged woman. Toxin has ripened the load into her: the sods have fused with her shoulders, moss and toadstools fruiting up through the wool, and her spine has swollen into a ridge of fungal knots under the frame. Water has tanned her bare forearms dark brown.
- **Dress and gear:** a willow carrying frame lashed with rope over the shoulders, with a head strap across the forehead; **the stacked sods**; a narrow winged turf spade hung on the frame; a sackcloth apron over a wool skirt kilted up, the hem rotted; wooden pattens; a tuft of bog cotton stuck in the frame; a rag tied round one knee; a horn cup on a string.
- **Head:** a broad weathered face under a headscarf, the strap biting a groove across her forehead.
- **Signature shape:** the tall stack of peat on the bent back.
- **Colour:** peat brown, bile green moss, bone.
- **Motion:** idle is shifting the load with a grunt. Attack is a swing of the turf spade; on a heavy turn she lets the stack topple onto a rank.

#### Frog-Catcher

- **Lore line:** "He caught frogs for his mother's pot. When the pot ran out he ate them as they came."
- **Was:** a stilt-village boy who caught frogs in the shallows for the pot.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, standing knock-kneed under a wet sack slung over one shoulder, a reed spear in the other hand. Weight on the heels against the sack's pull.
- **Body:** a thin child with a round belly on stick legs. Toxin has swollen **his throat into a pale pouch** that hangs like a toad's and fills when he croaks, and raised warts and blisters on his cheeks and hands. Water has tanned his skin brown and wrinkled his fingers.
- **Dress and gear:** a hessian sack over the shoulder, wet through and bulging with something that moves; a man's wool shirt belted with cord, sleeves rolled; a reed spear with a barbed bone tip; a small wicker cage of frogs at the hip; bare feet, mud to the knee; a rag bandage on one foot; a twine charm on the wrist.
- **Head:** a pinched, hungry face, cropped wet hair, a wide mouth stretched by the swollen throat.
- **Signature shape:** the small figure with the hanging throat pouch and the sack over the shoulder.
- **Colour:** peat brown, bile green throat, bone.
- **Motion:** idle is the pouch slowly filling and emptying. Attack is a croak that causes stress.

#### Lantern Gnats

- **Lore line:** "They come to the hags' lamps after dark. So, in the end, do you."
- **Was:** fen gnats that bred in the poisoned shallows and swarmed to the hags' lanterns.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a swarm about 0.6 high around a lantern hanging from a leaning reed pole driven into the mud.
- **Body:** thousands of gnats swollen by toxin to the size of wasps, their abdomens dull bile green and fat, some split open. The swarm is densest in a ball round the lantern and trails down to the water in a twisting tail. Water has kept the dead ones from rotting: they crust the lantern's panes in a thick, unbroken layer.
- **Dress and gear:** a horn-paned iron lantern with one cracked pane and a guttering tallow stub inside; a twine loop hanging it from a bent reed pole; a frog-bone charm knotted to the loop; moss up the pole; a mould-furred rag tied round the pole as a path marker; a second pole fallen in the mud beside it.
- **Head:** none.
- **Signature shape:** the leaning pole with the lantern inside its ball of gnats.
- **Colour:** lantern amber, bile green, ink black.
- **Motion:** idle is the swarm breathing in and out around the light. Attack is the swarm pouring off the lantern over a hero and leaving blisters.

#### Sin-Eater

- **Lore line:** "Paid a coin to eat the bread off a dead man's chest, and his sins with it. The fen has kept him busy."
- **Was:** the village sin-eater, paid to eat a funeral loaf off each corpse before burial.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, hunched over a round loaf held to his mouth in both hands, elbows out, belly forward, weight on the heels.
- **Body:** a gaunt man with **a hard, swollen gut**: toxin has ripened what he ate into a distended belly that strains his shirt, the skin over it blistered and veined bile green. His lips and chin are crusted with mould from the bread. Water has tanned the rest of him, bones showing at the wrists and collar.
- **Dress and gear:** the funeral loaf, grey and furred with mould; a coarse black wool cloak over a stained linen shirt that hangs open over the belly, the cloak's hem rotted into holes; a rope belt hung with the coins of the dead on cords, bored through; a wooden bowl of funeral ale at the hip; a strip of burial shroud tied as a scarf; a horn spoon tucked behind one ear; bare feet with blackened nails.
- **Head:** a long hollow face, sunken eyes, hair hacked short, the jaw always working.
- **Signature shape:** the hunch over the round loaf, with the swollen belly below it.
- **Colour:** peat brown, bile green mould, bone.
- **Motion:** idle is chewing steadily, never swallowing. Attack is a lurching bite; his other action eats a curse off an ally.

#### Leech-Barber

- **Lore line:** "He bled the whole village for its health. The leeches have grown fond of him."
- **Was:** the stilt villages' barber, who shaved, pulled teeth and bled the sick with leeches.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, stooped, both bare arms held out in front of him as if offering them for inspection. Weight forward on bandy legs.
- **Body:** a soft-bodied man whose **forearms are sleeved in fat black leeches**, each swollen by toxin to the size of a sausage and hanging in a fringe; the bites beneath have ripened into weeping pustules. His face and neck are pale and puffy, blistered where leeches have dropped off. Water has tanned and wrinkled his hands.
- **Dress and gear:** a barber's linen apron stained brown, over a leather jerkin and a wool tunic; a cracked apothecary jar slung on a strap at the hip, the same as the Leech Swarm's; a pewter bleeding bowl on a cord at the belt; a razor in a horn case; tooth-pulling pliers through the apron string; a string of pulled teeth round the neck as the sign of his trade; clogs; a rag bandage round one ankle.
- **Head:** a round, shaved face with a nick from his own razor on the chin, mild eyes, a fixed professional smile.
- **Signature shape:** the outstretched arms hung with a fringe of leeches.
- **Colour:** peat brown, ink black leeches, bile green pustules.
- **Motion:** idle is turning one arm to admire the leeches. Attack is a grab: the leeches cross onto the hero and feed.
