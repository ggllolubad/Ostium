# Act I: The Waters Below

## I. The Drowned Kingdom (Water + Void, Drown)

A coastal cathedral kingdom sunk into a dark where sound still carries but nothing else does. **Water** marks bodies with bloat, barnacles, kelp and verdigris; **void** leaves clean-rimmed holes where flesh, metal or stone should be, and eyes may appear. Everyone here was a citizen of a sea-kingdom: sailors, fishers, clergy, knights and its children. **Palette:** deep blue-black, verdigris, drowned gold (tarnished, never bright). Lore lines come from the game data.

### Rulers

#### The Drowned King

- **Lore line:** "He kept his crown. The sea grew it into him."
- **Was:** the king, crowned on the day the sea came in.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, seated on a throne of coral-crusted cathedral stone, leaning forward, one hand on the armrest. Cropped by the frame top.
- **Body:** a huge waterlogged body, skin grey-white and soft as wet paper, swollen at the neck and hands. **The crown has grown into the skull**: its tines push out through the scalp like antlers of tarnished gold crusted with coral and barnacles. **The Tooth** sits in the crown's front socket where a jewel was, a pale shard of stone.
- **Dress and gear:** rotting royal robes of deep blue velvet, the ermine trim gone grey and stringy, kelp tangled through the fur. A sceptre fused into a barnacled iron rod.
- **Head:** a long, sagging face, the jaw slack, the eyes white and fish-like. A void hole, perfectly round and clean-rimmed, runs through one cheek and shows the dark behind.
- **Signature shape:** the crown-antlers spreading from the skull.
- **Colour:** grey-white skin, tarnished gold, deep blue velvet.
- **Motion:** idle is slow breathing that lifts the whole robe; bubbles rise from the mouth. Attack is a slow, enormous backhand with the sceptre.

#### The Bell Below

- **Lore line:** "Rung once for every soul that drowned. It has not stopped ringing."
- **Was:** the kingdom's great cathedral bell.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, hanging from a broken length of chain that rises out of frame; the bell's lip touches the sea floor.
- **Body:** a bronze bell three storeys tall, heavy with verdigris, its inscription band legible in places. **Its surface is crusted with the drowned**: bodies fused into the bronze, arms and faces half sunk in the metal like reliefs on a cathedral door. A void hole has opened in its waist where bronze should be.
- **Dress and gear:** the clapper is a drowned bell-ringer's body bound to the iron tongue, visible through the void hole.
- **Head:** none. The faces in the bronze serve.
- **Signature shape:** the bell profile, unmistakable.
- **Colour:** verdigris bronze, drowned gold in the inscription, blue-black inside.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway on its chain. Attack is one swing and a toll, shown as rings in the water.

#### Mother of Brine

- **Lore line:** "Every siren is her daughter. Every drowned child is hers."
- **Was:** a fishwife and midwife of the harbour, the one who delivered the kingdom's children.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, rising waist-deep from black water, arms open wide as if to receive a child.
- **Body:** a vast woman, pale blue skin with a sheen of salt, heavy breasts and belly, her lower body dissolving into kelp and long trailing fins. Barnacle clusters on the shoulders. **Sirens and drowned children cling to her**, two or three small figures at her hips.
- **Dress and gear:** a midwife's linen apron, soaked and translucent, its strings floating. A string of drowned gold coins at the neck.
- **Head:** long weed-dark hair floating upward, a broad motherly face, eyes a little too large and wet.
- **Signature shape:** the open arms and the floating hair.
- **Colour:** pale blue-grey skin, kelp green-black, drowned gold coins.
- **Motion:** idle is a rocking motion as if soothing an infant. Attack is an embrace that closes around a hero.

### Elites

#### Hollow Tidewarden

- **Lore line:** "A temple guard grown into the sea. Something lives in the hole in its chest."
- **Was:** a guard of the drowned cathedral's doors.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, upright, a halberd planted beside him.
- **Body and dress:** full plate armour fused with the man inside, barnacled and green with verdigris, coral growing from the joints. **A clean void hole passes through the breastplate and chest**, and something pale with long feelers lives in it, half seen.
- **Head:** a closed sallet, its visor rusted shut, water running from the sights.
- **Signature shape:** the tall halberd and the round hole through the chest.
- **Colour:** verdigris steel, blue-black void, the pale creature.
- **Motion:** idle is the feelers in the chest testing the water. Attack is a sweeping halberd cut that throws water.

#### Abbess of Salt

- **Lore line:** "Her mouth is sewn shut. She preaches anyway."
- **Was:** abbess of the drowned cathedral, and once an Ostiarius. The order sent her through the first door to seal it from the far side. She failed and came back once; the order sewed her mouth shut and sent her back through.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, standing very straight, hands clasped on a crosier.
- **Body and dress:** a nun's habit encrusted white with salt crystals, the folds stiff and sculptural. Her skin is crusted with salt too, cracked at the joints. **Under the salt crust, remnants of her Ostiarii habit show** where the crust has cracked away: undyed grey-brown wool at the cuffs and hem, a strip of black scapular, the knotted rope belt. **An Ostiarii iron key ring still hangs at that belt.**
- **Head:** a stiff salt-crusted wimple, **the mouth sewn shut with the abbey's own waxed cord**, the eyes open and fervent.
- **Signature shape:** the crosier and the rigid, salt-stiff habit.
- **Colour:** salt white, blue-black shadow, drowned gold on the crosier head.
- **Motion:** idle is a silent sermon, the head nodding to a rhythm. Attack is raising the crosier; salt flakes from her sleeves.

#### The Grasping Deep

- **Lore line:** "Only one arm reaches up through the floor. Pray it is only one."
- **Was:** something vast in the trench below the kingdom. No one has seen the rest of it.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.4, a single arm rising through a breach in the cathedral floor.
- **Body:** **one enormous drowned arm**, human in shape but the size of a mast, grey-white and swollen, the skin hung with kelp, rope and a length of anchor chain. The hand is open, fingers longer than a man. Broken flagstones ring the breach, and black water wells around it.
- **Signature shape:** the single upraised arm and open hand.
- **Colour:** pale grey-white flesh, kelp green-black, blue-black water.
- **Motion:** idle is the fingers slowly curling and uncurling. Attack is a downward slam or a grab that drags a hero toward the breach.

#### Pearl Diver

- **Lore line:** "It goes for the soft ones at the back. It always has."
- **Was:** a pearl diver of the royal beds, the best in the kingdom.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.8, crouched and coiled, ready to spring.
- **Body:** long, lean and muscular, grey-blue skin. **Pearls have grown into the skin** along the spine and shoulders like a nacre crust. Webbing between the fingers.
- **Dress and gear:** a diver's loincloth and a rope belt hung with net bags of pearls, a diving knife, a stone weight on a cord.
- **Head:** shaved head, eyes milky with nacre, nose and ears worn smooth.
- **Signature shape:** the crouch and the stone weight swinging on its cord.
- **Colour:** grey-blue skin, pearl white, drowned gold.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway, the stone weight swinging. Attack is a leap over the front rank toward the back, knife first.

### Cast

#### Drowned Thrall

- **Lore line:** "Sailors who went down with the fleet. They still haul at anything that floats."
- **Was:** a common sailor of the royal fleet.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0. Slumped, shoulders rounded, head hanging forward, weight on the back foot. Moves as if walking through water.
- **Body:** **bloated** from long immersion: puffy face, swollen hands with wrinkled, peeling skin, a distended belly pushing the shirt out. Skin grey-white with blue lips and nail beds. **Barnacle clusters on both shoulders** and the back of the neck; small mussels in the folds of the clothes. One forearm has a clean void hole through it.
- **Dress and gear:** a **torn striped sailor's shirt** (faded blue and grey stripes), rope-belted canvas trousers rolled to the shin, barefoot. **Weapon: a rusted iron boat hook** on a long wooden pole, held two-handed.
- **Head:** **kelp hair** hanging in wet strands over the face, a gaping slack mouth, white eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the long boat hook held across the body.
- **Colour:** grey-white skin, faded blue stripes, verdigris and rust.
- **Motion:** idle is a sway with water dripping from the hair. Attack is a swing of the hook that catches and hauls.

#### Brine Siren

- **Lore line:** "Its song is the last thing the drowned remember. It wants you to remember it too."
- **Was:** one of the harbour girls; now a daughter of the Mother of Brine.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.9, half-raised from a pool of black water, leaning forward.
- **Body:** pale scaled skin from the waist down fading into a long eel-like tail; a human torso, too thin, ribs showing; webbed hands with long nails.
- **Head:** long floating hair, a sweet face with a mouth that opens far too wide when she sings, rows of fine needle teeth inside.
- **Signature shape:** the arched torso and the wide-open singing mouth.
- **Colour:** pale blue-white, kelp green, blue-black water.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway and humming. Attack is a song, the jaw dropping open.

#### Eel-kin Biter

- **Lore line:** "They feed by latching on and not letting go."
- **Was:** a harbour urchin who swam for coins under the piers.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, low on all fours, darting.
- **Body:** a thin child-sized body with eel skin, slick and dark; the jaw lengthened into an eel's mouth with backward-curving teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the low, long head and open jaw.
- **Colour:** slick blue-black, pale belly.
- **Motion:** idle is a twitching crouch. Attack is a lunge that latches onto a limb and hangs there.

#### Drowned Bell-Ringer

- **Lore line:** "The bell is rung for the dead. Down here, that is everyone."
- **Was:** a cathedral sexton who rang the funeral bell.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, bent under the weight of what he carries.
- **Body and dress:** a drowned sexton in a rotting cassock, hands raw and rope-burned. **He carries a hand bell the size of a bucket** in one hand and a coil of bell rope over the shoulder. His ears are gone: clean void holes on both sides of the head.
- **Signature shape:** the big hand bell raised high.
- **Colour:** black cassock, verdigris bronze.
- **Motion:** idle is a small, silent swing of the bell. Attack is a toll that causes stress.

#### Barnacle Knight

- **Lore line:** "Oathbound to a drowned order. The sea has kept his armour on for him."
- **Was:** a knight of the cathedral's military order.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, heavy and slow, shield forward.
- **Body and dress:** plate armour **so encrusted with barnacles, mussels and coral that it looks like rock**, the original shape surviving only at the helm crest, the sword and the order's cross on the shield, which shows faintly through the growth.
- **Signature shape:** the lumpy encrusted outline, broader than any human.
- **Colour:** grey barnacle white, verdigris, a hint of drowned gold on the crest.
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy settle. Attack is a slow, crushing sword blow.

#### Lamprey Swarm

- **Lore line:** "A knot of mouths. Where one is, the rest follow."
- **Was:** lampreys of the harbour.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a writhing knot about 0.6 high.
- **Body:** a knot of grey lampreys, each with a round sucking mouth of ringed teeth, coiled around a drowned body's leg that still wears a boot.
- **Signature shape:** the writhing knot and the boot.
- **Colour:** grey-brown, pale mouths.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant writhe. Attack is the knot surging forward.

#### Bloated Corpse

- **Lore line:** "Gas swells the dead until the skin will not hold. Do not be close when it fails."
- **Was:** any drowned citizen.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, but nearly round, waddling.
- **Body:** gas-bloated until the skin is stretched shiny and the clothes have split at every seam; the belly marbled with blue veins; crabs in the pockets.
- **Signature shape:** the round, swollen body on thin legs.
- **Colour:** marbled grey-blue skin, split brown cloth.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow wobble. Attack is a burst of foul water.

#### Hookline Fisher

- **Lore line:** "Still fishing. The catch has changed."
- **Was:** a line fisherman.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, standing in a fishing stance, the rod held high.
- **Body and dress:** an oilskin coat and wide-brimmed sou'wester hat, waders, a creel at the hip containing something that moves. **A long rod with an iron hook on the line.** The skin of the face has slipped and sags.
- **Signature shape:** the hat brim and the bent rod.
- **Colour:** dark yellow oilskin gone brown, grey skin.
- **Motion:** idle is a patient twitch of the rod tip. Attack is a cast that hooks a hero.

#### Weeping Nun

- **Lore line:** "She weeps for the drowned. Her tears close their wounds."
- **Was:** a sister of the drowned cathedral's infirmary.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, hands folded, head bowed.
- **Body and dress:** a wet black habit clinging to a thin body, a wimple stained with tide lines. **Water runs endlessly from her eyes**, two streams that never stop, darkening the habit.
- **Signature shape:** the bowed hooded head.
- **Colour:** black habit, pale skin, deep blue water.
- **Motion:** idle is quiet weeping, shoulders moving. Her action is to lay a hand on a wounded ally; her tears fall on the wound and close it.

#### Anchor Brute

- **Lore line:** "The anchor chain runs through his arm. He has stopped noticing."
- **Was:** a dock porter.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6, massive shoulders, knuckles low.
- **Body:** a huge drowned labourer, skin swollen and grey. **An anchor chain is threaded through the meat of his forearm**, in at the wrist and out at the elbow, and the iron anchor at its end is dragged like a flail.
- **Signature shape:** the anchor at the end of its chain.
- **Colour:** grey skin, rusted iron.
- **Motion:** idle is the anchor scraping. Attack is a whirling anchor swing.

#### Wreck Crab

- **Lore line:** "It wears what it ate."
- **Was:** a shore crab that grew fat in a wreck.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3 wide, low and armoured.
- **Body:** a crab whose shell is **a fused section of the ship it ate**: hull planks, a porthole and a broken mast stump, with a figurehead's face on its back. Claws of barnacled shell.
- **Signature shape:** the hull-plank shell and mast stump.
- **Colour:** dark wet wood, barnacle white, rust.
- **Motion:** idle is claw clicking. Attack is a claw crush.

#### Drowned Choirboy

- **Lore line:** "He has been singing since the water came in. He does not need to breathe to sing."
- **Was:** a boy of the cathedral choir.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, standing very still, hymnal open.
- **Body and dress:** a small drowned boy in a white surplice gone grey, a ruff collar, a hymnal swollen to twice its size. Pale skin, blue lips.
- **Head:** cropped hair, an angelic face, the mouth open in song. **No sound comes out; the air around his mouth is a void.**
- **Signature shape:** the small figure with the open hymnal.
- **Colour:** grey-white surplice, blue lips.
- **Motion:** idle is singing silently. Attack is a note that causes stress.
