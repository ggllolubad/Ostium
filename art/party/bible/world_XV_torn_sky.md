## XV. The Torn Sky (Lightning + Void, Discharge)

A heaven. Void's betrayal of the Mother of Courses tore it open; there was no war. The ruins float in the storm and something looks in through the tears. The only world whose cast was never human. **Lightning** scars everything with violet branching burns; **void** cracks bodies open onto darkness. **Gold leaf** is allowed here, cracked over black. Eyes are expected (void world). **Palette:** gold leaf cracked over black, violet lightning.

### Rulers

#### The Shattered God

- **Lore line:** "She held all six courses in one body, and the world was whole. The void betrayed her, and now it is all that runs out of her."
- **Was:** the Mother of Courses, the being in whom all six courses met and stayed in balance; this is what the void's betrayal left of her. She is not a separate god and not a male one.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 5, a vast seated feminine figure broken into floating pieces, cropped on all sides. She sits slumped and still, the head tipped forward, the hands open and empty in the lap. Nothing in the pose wills anything.
- **Body:** a colossal woman's figure of gold-leafed stone, like a cathedral Madonna, **shattered and hollow**: pieces of head, shoulders, breast, lap and hands hang apart in the air where they broke, keeping her shape, and every piece is an empty shell, black inside. The gold leaf is cracked over black everywhere. **Void leaks from every crack**, black seeping out and thinning the broken edges into nothing. Violet lightning has burned the gold in branching Lichtenberg scars that run from crack to crack; it burns and never arcs, so no bolt crosses the gaps between the pieces. **Her eyes are missing**: they are scattered through the void worlds (the Starless Eye, the Choir of Eyes, the Garden). **Her mouth is missing.** **The Tooth** is a shard floating where the heart was.
- **Head:** a serene, broken woman's face under a cracked stone veil, slack and without will; clean-rimmed empty eye sockets, black void where the mouth should be.
- **Signature shape:** the floating, hollow fragments of a vast seated woman.
- **Colour:** gold leaf cracked over black, black void, violet lightning burns.
- **Motion:** idle is the fragments drifting slowly apart and back while void seeps from the cracks. Attack is a discharge: the violet burns flare along every crack and the same branching burns open across the heroes.

#### The Wheel of Thrones

- **Lore line:** "It carried the god's seat. It is still turning, looking for the weight."
- **Was:** one of the Thrones, the living wheels that bore the god's seat through heaven.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, two great wheels set one inside the other at right angles, turning slowly in the air above a broken floor. The upper rims run out of the frame top and the near rim is cut by the frame edge. It hovers, but the lowest point of the outer rim grinds along the floor and carries its weight there.
- **Body:** rims as broad as a road, gold leaf cracked over black, **studded all round with eyes** of different sizes that blink out of time. The gold is crazed into a net of cracks with black void in every one. Violet Lichtenberg burns branch across rims and spokes; the hub is a lump of metal fused by the strike that welded the axle solid. Several spokes have snapped, and one whole sector of the outer rim is missing with clean edges, darkness behind it.
- **Dress and gear:** the empty socket on top where the seat was fixed, its bolts sheared; the traces of the god's chariot trailing from the axle; harness rings for the creatures that drew it, pulling nothing; tatters of a canopy caught in the spokes; an inscription band running round the inner rim; scorched iron tyre bands, one sprung loose and flapping; a feather caught in a spoke; drips of molten gold frozen on the underside; chunks of floating ruin caught in its turning and carried round; a cracked halo hung on one spoke; the god's seal stamped on the hub, half melted.
- **Head:** none. The eyes on the rims serve; the largest, at the hub, stays closed until it attacks.
- **Signature shape:** the two crossed wheels with eyes along the rims.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow, grinding turn, the eyes rolling past one after another. Attack is the wheels spinning up and the hub eye opening; a discharge leaps from every eye at once across the party.

#### The Fourfold Beast

- **Lore line:** "It had a face for each wind, to watch the throne from every side. The void took three of them."
- **Was:** one of the living creatures that guarded the god's throne, with the faces of a man, a lion, an ox and an eagle.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, couched like a sphinx on a fallen slab of heaven, forequarters raised, great wings spread up and out of the frame top, the head turned to the party. Its weight goes down through the lion's forepaws.
- **Body:** a lion's body, eagle's wings and one broad neck carrying four faces, all in gold leaf cracked over black. Violet branching burns run along the flanks; the wing feathers are fused by the strike into stiff, scorched vanes, and **the wings are full of eyes**. Only the lion's face remains whole: the man's face is cracked away to black void, the ox's is a clean-rimmed hole, and of the eagle's head only the beak hangs on by a shard.
- **Dress and gear:** a jewelled collar of cracked gold with every setting empty; a snapped chain from the collar to the throne, trailing; a snapped gilt pole from the throne's canopy, driven into the flank when the throne fell; claws of metal fused by the strike; gold scales on the chest, several missing; a shattered halo-ring floating behind the heads in pieces; a torn standard trapped under one forepaw; the fallen slab under it, cracked and carved; shards of its lost faces drifting round it; the eagle beak swinging on its shard; burn-blackened pinions at the wingtips.
- **Head:** four faces on one neck, facing four ways; the lion's face turned toward the party, heavy-maned, mouth open.
- **Signature shape:** the four-faced head on the couched, winged lion.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is the faces turning in slow sequence, the lion's always coming back to the party. Attack is a roar from the lion's face and a discharge from the void where the others were.

### Elites

#### Thunder Seraph

- **Lore line:** "It holds the sky together with its spear. It is losing."
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.4, braced, a spear thrust upward into a tear in the sky.
- **Body:** a gold-armoured seraph with six wings, some broken; **its spear is wedged into a tear** above it and crackles with lightning; its arms shake with the effort.
- **Head:** a helm with a face of gold, cracked across.
- **Signature shape:** the upthrust spear and spread wings.
- **Colour:** gold, violet, black.
- **Motion:** idle is straining against the spear. Attack is a lightning strike down the spear.

#### Rift Leviathan

- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.5, coiling through a tear in the sky, only part of it in frame.
- **Body:** a long eel-like body of black void with stars visible inside it, edged in violet light; a jaw lined with gold teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the coil through the tear.
- **Colour:** black, stars, violet, gold.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow coil. Attack is a lunge through the tear.

#### The Recording Angel

- **Lore line:** "It wrote down every soul. It has started crossing them out."
- **Was:** the angel who kept heaven's book of names.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.2, tall and stooped forward over a huge open book held against its chest on a strap, a quill raised in the other hand, wings folded. Weight on the back foot.
- **Body:** a tall, thin angel. Violet Lichtenberg burns branch over the face and down both hands, and the strike has **fused the writing hand to the quill**. The face is cracked across like glazed clay, black void showing in the cracks. Among the names on the open pages, eyes have opened.
- **Dress and gear:** long black robes edged with cracked gold leaf; **the book**, as tall as its torso, with cracked gold clasps and scorched pages lifting in the storm, the names in black ink and many struck through in red rubric; a chain from the book's spine to the belt; an inkhorn of black horn with a burn-split rim; the quill, a seraph's flight feather scorched at the vane; a pen-knife and a seal at the belt; a belt of wax tablets; one wing burned to bare quills; a halo cracked into three pieces that drift apart and back; a strap across the chest worn through to the lining.
- **Head:** a long, ascetic face, eyes half closed in concentration, hair standing up from the strike.
- **Signature shape:** the huge open book against the chest and the raised quill.
- **Colour:** black robes edged in cracked gold leaf, violet burns, red rubric in the book.
- **Motion:** idle is writing, then a slow, deliberate stroke through a name. Attack is striking a hero's name out; a discharge hits that hero, who cannot be healed until the turn after next.

#### The Weigher of Souls

- **Lore line:** "It weighed every soul against a feather. It has lost the feather."
- **Was:** the angel that held the scales of judgement at heaven's gate.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3, standing very straight with both arms held out level to the sides, rigid as a beam; from each wrist a great pan hangs on chains to below the knee. Weight dead centre: it reads as a T.
- **Body:** the strike came at the moment of weighing and **fused its arms rigid into the beam of the scale**: shoulders, arms and wrists are one bar of gold-leafed metal and flesh, with violet Lichtenberg burns flaring out from the shoulders. The body hangs below the bar like a plumb line. Void cracks open down the torso. A metal pointer, the scale's needle, rises straight out of the crown of its head.
- **Dress and gear:** a long black tabard with a border of cracked gold leaf; chains from both wrists, scorched violet at the links; **two pans of hammered gold**, cracked, one hanging lower than the other; in the low pan, a heap of grey ash that was souls; the high pan empty, where the feather should be; a girdle of plaited cord; wings folded tight behind, one of them gone to a clean void edge; bare feet, one toe missing; a cracked halo around the pointer; a weight-stone hung at the belt; a string of small gilt weights on a cord.
- **Head:** a narrow, impassive face, eyes closed, the pointer rising from the crown.
- **Signature shape:** the rigid T of the arms with the two pans hanging.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns, grey ash.
- **Motion:** idle is the pans swaying and the pointer wobbling. Attack is weighing the party: the pans tip, the needle settles on the hero with the most health left, and a discharge strikes them.

#### Porter of Heaven

- **Lore line:** "It kept the gate. The gate is gone. It still has the key."
- **Was:** an angel doorkeeper of heaven's gate.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.15, thickset, standing guard with a huge key carried over one shoulder like a club and a section of gate grille strapped to the other forearm as a shield. Weight square on both feet.
- **Body:** a heavy, short-necked angel. Violet Lichtenberg burns branch down the key arm from the shoulder; the gold-leafed skin is cracked along the jaw and collarbone with black void beneath, and a third eye has opened in a crack on the brow. One wing is torn off at the root, the stump cracked open onto darkness.
- **Dress and gear:** **the key**, as long as an arm, gold leaf cracked over black iron, its bit cut with intricate wards; a section of the gilded gate grille strapped to the forearm; a ring of smaller keys at the belt; a chain from the key's bow to the belt; a short gilded mail coat over a warden's tabard worked with a key device; a padlock hanging open at the hip; sandals with scorched straps; a cracked halo.
- **Head:** a square, heavy face, cracked along the jaw, the third eye in the brow crack, the mouth set like a closed door.
- **Signature shape:** the giant key over the shoulder.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping the key on its shoulder. Attack is a swing of the key; on a hit, the hero is locked and loses their next turn.

#### Herald of Heaven

- **Lore line:** "It was sent to sound the end of the world. It is still sounding it. This is how long that takes."
- **Was:** a herald angel, one of the trumpeters of heaven.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, slim, leaning back, a long straight trumpet raised to the lips and levelled forward, a banner hanging from it. Weight on the back foot, chest lifted.
- **Body:** slender. The strike **fused the mouthpiece to its lips** mid-note, and violet burns branch down the throat and chest from there. A void crack runs across the ribs, black showing through.
- **Dress and gear:** **the trumpet**, long and straight, gold leaf cracked over black, its bell split; a square banner hanging from it, scorched at the edges and worked in gold thread with the god's sign; a herald's tabard; a baldric with a satchel of sealed proclamations; half-raised wings with scorched tips; bare feet; a cracked halo; a tasselled cord wound round the trumpet's shaft.
- **Head:** a young, ageless face, hair standing from the strike, eyes closed, cheeks set hard around the mouthpiece.
- **Signature shape:** the long, level trumpet with the banner hanging from it.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a long, held note, the banner stirring. Attack is a blast; a discharge rolls along the front rank and causes stress.

#### Putto

- **Body:** a plump winged infant with skin of gold leaf cracked over black, like gilt carving, creased at every fold; violet burns across the belly; half the face cracked away onto darkness, with an eye looking out of the dark.
- **Dress and gear:** a scrap of drapery round the hips; a garland of gilt leaves over one shoulder; a small bow and a quiver of three arrows; a ribbon trailing from the quiver; one wing tip broken off; a scorched knee.

- **Signature shape:** the small, fat winged figure with the drawn bow.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a lazy bobbing hover. Attack is a loosed arrow that carries a discharge.

#### Scorched Doves

- **Body:** eight doves, their white feathers scorched with violet branching burns, the wing tips crisped black, the breast feathers standing out from the strike. Void cracks have opened across some bodies, black inside; one dove has too many eyes; one has no head and flies anyway.
- **Dress and gear:** tiny message scrolls tied to their legs; cracked gold leg rings; a sprig of gilt leaves in one beak; loose feathers falling behind the flock; a scrap of burnt banner caught on a foot.
- **Head:** small round dove heads, beaks open.
- **Signature shape:** the wheeling flock of scorched white doves.

#### Throne Lion

- **Lore line:** "Twelve lions stood on the steps of the throne. The steps fell. The lions did not."
- **Was:** one of the gilded lions on the steps of the god's throne.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3, a lion standing four-square on a broken step, head low, one forepaw raised. Weight on the three planted feet.
- **Body:** a lion of gilded stone, gold leaf cracked over black, the carved mane in stiff curls. Violet burns branch over the shoulders. **Its hindquarters have broken off** and float a hand's width behind the body, held in place by a web of violet burns across the gap, black void in the break.
- **Dress and gear:** the fragment of step it stands on, cracked gilded marble; the iron pin and lead fixing where it was set into the step; a carved collar, cracked; a chipped ear; the tail's end missing; a carved inscription on the step's edge.
- **Head:** a carved heraldic lion face, mouth open, teeth chipped, blank stone eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the four-square lion with its hindquarters floating apart.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a grinding sound as the floating haunches drift and resettle. Attack is a pounce, the two halves slamming together on impact.

#### Rift Crawler

- **Lore line:** "It came in through a tear and has been eating the floor of heaven ever since. Heaven is mostly floor."
- **Was:** something from outside that came in through the tears.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6 long, low, a segmented body on eight thick jointed legs, splayed and planted, the front segment reared. Weight spread low over the legs.
- **Body:** each segment a plate of black void with violet-burnt edges; **rows of eyes along its back**; side-opening mandibles crusted with flakes of the gold leaf it has eaten; chunks of gilded floor tile visible inside it through the cracks between segments.
- **Dress and gear:** scored and chipped segment plates; a broken halo caught round one leg; a strip of banner dragged under it; gold dust on the leg tips; a tooth-marked shard of floor in the mandibles; one leg shorter than the rest, healed crooked.
- **Head:** a blunt head with no face, only clustered eyes and the mandibles.
- **Signature shape:** the low, segmented crawler with its reared front.
- **Colour:** black void, violet edges, flecks of gold.
- **Motion:** idle is the legs testing the edges of things. Attack is a rearing lunge and a bite.

#### Star-Lighter

- **Lore line:** "It lit the stars every evening. Most of them have gone out. It is doing its best."
- **Was:** an angel lamplighter, who lit and trimmed the stars.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, slightly stooped, a lighting pole held upright and taller than itself, a small cage at its top. Weight on the leg nearest the pole.
- **Body:** a slight angel, its wings bound flat to its back with cord to keep them from the flame. Violet burns branch up the pole arm; a void crack runs from collarbone to hip.
- **Dress and gear:** **the pole**, hooked at the tip, with an iron cage hanging from it, and in the cage **a dead star**, a black cinder crazed with violet; a bag of wicks; trimming shears; a tinderbox at the belt; a black leather apron edged in cracked gold leaf; scorched sleeves rolled to the elbow; the cord binding the wings; a cracked halo.
- **Head:** a tired, gentle face, cracked across one cheek, looking up at the cage.
- **Signature shape:** the tall pole with the caged dead star at its tip.
- **Colour:** cracked gold leaf over black, violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is reaching up to try to relight the cage. Attack is swinging the cage down onto a hero; the dead star discharges.
