# Act IV: The Stilling

## X. The Venom Spires (Lightning + Toxin, Neurotoxin)

A plain of travellers' roads. Lightning struck it, and the insects built towers to catch the lightning. Their venom stops the heart, then the will. **Lightning** marks chitin and flesh with branching white-violet burns; **toxin** swells, paralyses and hollows. The cast are the hive's insects and the travellers it has taken. **Palette:** chitin black, venom green, white-violet strikes.

### Rulers

#### The Hive Queen

- **Lore line:** "The heart of the spires. Every sting on the plain is hers."
- **Was:** the first wasp to nest on a lightning-struck tower.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, clinging head-down to the inside of a spire, abdomen curling forward.
- **Body:** a colossal wasp-queen of glossy black chitin with venom-green banding, **a swollen egg-laying abdomen** pulsing with light, translucent wings torn and buzzing with static; the spire's lightning rod runs into her back. **The Tooth** is embedded in her stinger.
- **Head:** compound eyes, mandibles, antennae crackling.
- **Signature shape:** the curling abdomen and stinger.
- **Colour:** chitin black, venom green, white-violet.
- **Motion:** idle is a pulsing abdomen and twitching antennae. Attack is a stinger strike that paralyses.

#### The Procession

- **Lore line:** "The pilgrims were carrying their saint to the shrine when the storm broke. The hive carries all of them now."
- **Was:** a pilgrim procession on the plain road, and the painted wooden saint they carried on a float.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 to the saint's head, moving left at a slow sway: twelve pilgrims shoulder to shoulder under the carrying poles of a wooden float, the saint's statue standing on it above them, cropped by the frame top. The weight is in the bearers' bent knees.
- **Body:** lightning struck the saint's iron halo, the tallest thing on the plain, and ran down through the statue and the poles into every bearer: one branching fern-burn crosses all their backs and arms, carrying on from body to body as a single pattern, and the poles are scorched and fused into their shoulders, so the bearers have grown together into one mass of bowed backs and legs (lightning). The hive then filled the hollow statue with grey paper comb, which has spilled down over the float's edge onto the bearers; their skin is swollen and shifts with larvae, their muscles are locked stiff with venom and their heads loll (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** pilgrim cloaks, broad-brimmed hats, scrips and gourds. Secondary details: the saint, carved wood painted and flaking, with a staff and a pilgrim's gourd; the iron halo melted at its rim, its gilt flaking; votive offerings hung from the float's edge, wax limbs and tin hearts, the wax sagging; a scorched canopy fringe; lead pilgrim badges pinned on the hats; a processional banner on a pole, burnt to a rag; rosaries wound round the bearers' wrists; wasps coming and going through a split in the saint's face; bare, swollen feet; a dropped staff; candle stubs on the float; hair standing stiff under the hat brims.
- **Head:** the saint's painted face, split from brow to chin by the strike, wasps at the split; below it, the bearers' faces bowed under the poles.
- **Signature shape:** the tall saint on its float above a mass of bowed bearers.
- **Colour:** dust grey cloaks gone venom green with comb and swelling, chitin black wasps, white-violet burns; dull gilt on the halo (holy).
- **Motion:** idle is a slow processional sway, the saint nodding. Attack is the procession lurching a step forward as the float tips and brings the saint down on the front rank (Paralyse).

#### The Cicada Under the Road

- **Lore line:** "It went underground the year the lightning came. It has come up to sing."
- **Was:** a cicada nymph that burrowed under the plain road the year of the first storm and fed on roots in the dark for seventeen years.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, heaving up through the broken paving of the road. The front half of the nymph is reared out of the ground, its digging forelegs hooked over the cobbles; its back has split open along its length, and the pale adult is pushing up out of the old shell with its wings still crumpled, cropped by the frame top. The weight hangs on the hooked forelegs.
- **Body:** the nymph's shell is dull black and earth-caked, with broad, spade-toothed forelegs. The split in its back follows the lightning scar where a storm struck the road above it: the edges of the split are scorched, the scar branches across the shell in Lichtenberg ferns, and the shell's seams are fused shut everywhere except along the strike (lightning). The adult rising out of it is wrong: puffy and pale, banded venom green, pustules along the abdomen, the crumpled wings thick-veined and wet, the whole body swollen with venom (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** none; the road serves. Secondary details: paving slabs tilted up round it; a toppled milestone, its numbers half legible; cart ruts running into the hole; roots hanging from the shell; the empty husk of a smaller cicada clinging to a foreleg; clods of earth; a bent iron waypost; a cart wheel turned up with the stones; beads of venom on the pustules; the beak folded under the head; wasps circling the soft new body; bristles standing along the legs.
- **Head:** the blunt, broad head of the nymph, with the new adult's paler head pushing out behind it; both have dull black eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the reared shell splitting open along its back, with the pale body rising out of it.
- **Colour:** chitin black, venom green, white-violet scorch.
- **Motion:** idle is the split widening a little while the shell creaks and a hum rises. Attack is the song: a shrill that rattles the road stones and stuns the whole company (Stress, Paralyse).

#### The Courier

- **Lore line:** "The storm caught him in the open with a message. The hive has rewritten it."
- **Was:** a mounted courier of the plain roads, and his horse.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.2 to the rider's head, horse and rider side-on; the horse's near foreleg is raised mid-step and locked there by venom, and the rider sits upright and stiff, a message tube held out at arm's length. Weight on the horse's three planted legs.
- **Body:** the strike hit the rider and ran through him into the horse: one fern-burn branches from his crown down his back, through the saddle, across the horse's flank and down all four legs; the stirrups and the bit are fused into the flesh, and the horse's mane and the rider's hair stand stiff (lightning). Venom has locked them both: the horse's head is pulled up stiff, its neck muscles knotted in spasm and swollen where wasps nest in it, paper comb built under the saddle flap; the rider's face is slack and swollen (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a short riding cloak blown stiff, a leather jerkin with the courier's badge, high boots, gauntlets. Secondary details: the leather message tube, its wax seal broken and a wasp crawling out; a courier's horn on a cord, fused to his chest; saddlebags with comb bulging out of them; a riding crop; spurs fused to the horse's sides; a sword at the hip; a broken stirrup leather; the horse's iron shoes, one cast; the bridle's iron bit fused into the mouth; a road-pass token on a thong; the horse's tail standing out stiff.
- **Head:** the rider's felt hood blown back, hair on end, a slack face with the eyes rolled up; the horse's long head with its lips drawn back from the fused bit.
- **Signature shape:** horse and rider, with the raised foreleg and the message tube held out.
- **Colour:** chitin black, venom green swelling, white-violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is the horse twitching in spasm and the rider jerking with it. Attack is a trampling lunge.

#### Road Centipede

- **Lore line:** "It lies across the road at night. Carters take it for a fallen beam."
- **Was:** a giant centipede of the plain, which hunted under the road stones.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9 reared and 4 long: the front third reared high and curved over the company, the rest lying in a long S across the ground, its legs splayed low with their tips planted. Weight in the long grounded body.
- **Body:** thirty segments of black chitin, each plate edged venom green; thick, jointed legs, low and splayed; the venom claws under the head huge and hooked. A strike ran the length of its back and fused six middle segments into one rigid, scorched section, Lichtenberg ferns spreading across the plates and the fine bristles standing (lightning). The venom glands at the base of the claws are swollen into bulges that strain the head plates apart (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: three legs missing on the near side, the stumps healed black; a cart's iron tyre jammed round one segment; a traveller's shoe stuck on a leg tip; a strip of shed skin caught on the tail; long, swept-back antennae; a cracked plate; scorched leg joints along the fused section; a scrap of harness strap snagged on a leg; a drop of venom held on one claw tip; the two long rear legs trailing like a second pair of antennae; a dead wasp impaled on a leg spine.
- **Head:** a flat, plated head with the antennae swept back and the venom claws spread beneath it.
- **Signature shape:** the high reared curve with hooked claws, over the long low body.
- **Colour:** chitin black, venom green, white-violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is the antennae sweeping while the legs ripple. Attack is a downward strike with the venom claws (Paralyse).

#### The Dowser

- **Lore line:** "He walked the plain with a hazel fork and told each village where to dig its well. The fork still pulls. It pulls toward the spires."
- **Was:** a travelling water-diviner who went from village to village along the plain road, finding where to sink wells with a forked hazel rod.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, walking bent forward with both elbows tucked to the ribs and the fists turned palm-up, gripping the two arms of a forked rod whose point juts out level ahead of him and dips; the head is pushed forward after it. Weight on the front foot, as if the rod were dragging him.
- **Body:** thin and sun-dried from the road. The strike came out of the open sky onto the highest point on the bare plain, the rod's raised tip: the copper ferrule is fused into a scorched bead, the hazel is split along the grain, and its two arms are burned into his palms so the fists can never open. Lichtenberg ferns run from both wrists up the insides of the arms, meet across the chest and branch down to the belt, and his hair and beard stand out stiff (lightning). The hive found him resting at a dry well. Venom has hollowed him and locked him: cheeks and belly sunk in, the skin tight and shiny over the bones, swollen only at the stings on the neck and wrists into hard venom-green welts; the neck is set in a spasm that pulls the head down after the rod, and the jaw is clenched (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a long homespun coat belted with cord, its skirts scorched at the hem and frayed stiff; a broad felt hat; leggings and road shoes. Secondary details: the forked hazel rod, its bark worn smooth where his hands held it for thirty years; a lead plumb bob on a cord looped round one wrist, always swinging toward the nearest spire; two spare hazel forks tied at the hip; a wooden water bottle, dry, wasps going in and out of its mouth; a well-sinker's knotted measuring cord coiled at the belt; a small iron trowel; a tally board notched for every well he found, the notches burned through by the strike; a purse of village coins; one shoe burst at the sole where the strike left him; a scorched hole through the hat crown; a dead wasp caught in the beard.
- **Head:** a long, sun-cracked face pushed forward under the hat brim, the eyes fixed on the rod's tip and rolled half up, the jaw clenched, the beard standing out in a stiff fan.
- **Signature shape:** the bent figure with the forked rod gripped in both fists and jutting level ahead.
- **Colour:** dust grey coat, white-violet burns, venom green welts.
- **Motion:** idle is the rod's tip dipping and jerking him half a step forward. Attack is the rod snapping down to point at a hero; the ground under them splits and wasps pour up out of it (Toxin).

#### Struck Shepherd

- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, a dead lamb across his shoulders, a crook in the near hand held out with its hook forward. Weight on the back leg.
- **Body:** the strike came down the crook: its iron hook is fused into his palm, and a fern-burn spreads from the hand up the arm and across the chest, the hair standing up in a stiff fan (lightning). The lamb across his shoulders is hollowed and full of larvae that move under the fleece, and where it touches his neck the two have grown together, swollen and stiff (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a sheepskin jerkin, a wool cloak pinned at the shoulder, leg wraps. Secondary details: a sling and a pouch of stones; a horn of salve; a wooden water bottle; a bellwether's iron bell at the belt, cracked by the strike; a knife; a scorched hole in the cloak where the strike left him; boots burst at the seams.
- **Head:** a weathered face, the hair standing, the eyes rolled up.
- **Signature shape:** the lamb across the shoulders and the hooked crook held out.
- **Colour:** dust grey fleece, white-violet burns, venom green larvae.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow nodding while the lamb twitches. Attack is the crook hooking a hero and pulling them forward (Pull).

#### Highwayman

- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, crouched low behind a levelled crossbow, the stock braced at the shoulder. Weight on bent knees, the rear knee nearly on the ground.
- **Body:** lean and hard. - **Dress and gear:** a short hooded cloak; a buff leather jerkin; a grey kerchief over the lower face. Secondary details: a belt hung with several stolen purses; a goat's-foot lever for spanning the bow; a quiver of bolts at the hip; a long knife; scuffed boots; stolen rings on the fused fingers; a stolen lead pilgrim badge pinned to the hood.
- **Head:** the hood up, the kerchief over the mouth, one side of the face sagging.
- **Signature shape:** the crouched figure behind a levelled crossbow.
- **Colour:** chitin black leather, venom green swelling, white-violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a crouched aim with the finger twitching. Attack is a crossbow bolt at the back rank.

#### The Tinker

- **Lore line:** "He carried every pot on the road on his back. The lightning welded them to him."
- **Was:** a travelling tinker who mended pots and kettles.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0 (1.35 to the top of his load), bent forward under a towering wooden frame of pots, kettles and pans, a small hammer in the near hand. Weight forward over the toes to balance the load.
- **Body:** the strike hit the load: the copper and tin have fused into one lumpy shell that has grown into his back, the wooden frame charred inside it; Lichtenberg burns spread down his arms and legs from wherever the metal touches skin, and his hair stands (lightning). Wasps nest in the kettles, and the skin around the fused metal is swollen and blistered (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a leather apron, a patched coat, a leather cap. Secondary details: the fused pots, scorched black and green with verdigris; a string of spoons melted together; a lantern hanging from the frame, its glass burst; a pouch of rivets; a solder pot; tongs; a small iron stake anvil at the belt; a ladle handle sticking out of the load like a horn.
- **Head:** a soot-streaked face under the cap, the hair standing out, teeth gritted.
- **Signature shape:** the towering, lumpy rack of fused pots on his back.
- **Colour:** scorched black and verdigris green, chitin black, white-violet burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a clank and rattle as he shifts the load. Attack is a turn that swings the whole load into a hero (Stun).

#### Road-Warden

Venom locks him at attention with the jaw clenched, and the hive has built grey paper comb inside his open brigandine (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a brigandine of riveted plates on canvas; a kettle hat. Secondary details: the warden's badge on a chain; a horn; a short sword; a set of shackles for robbers at the belt; a lantern on a hook; the burst boots; a scorched glove; wasps crawling at the brigandine's opening.
- **Head:** a set face under the scorched kettle hat, the eyes wide, the hair standing.
- **Signature shape:** the very tall pike with its melted head.
- **Colour:** chitin black brigandine, white-violet burns, venom green comb.
- **Motion:** idle is a rigid stand with small twitches. Attack is a stiff pike thrust.

#### Spire Termites

Lightning struck the hilt: the crossguard is fused, and the clay round it has baked glassy and branched with burn ferns (lightning). The soldiers' heads are swollen with venom glands, ready to squirt (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: the sword hilt and fused crossguard; ventilation chimneys in the clay; a winged termite on the cone; a dead traveller's glove at its foot, half built in; scorched clay; the column of workers; a soldier rearing at the cone's mouth.
- **Head:** none; the soldiers' black heads ring the openings.
- **Signature shape:** the small clay cone with a sword hilt at its tip.
- **Colour:** grey clay, chitin black soldiers, white-violet scorch.
- **Motion:** idle is the swarm building, the cone growing a little. Attack is the soldiers spraying venom at a hero's legs (Toxin).

#### Antlion

- **Lore line:** "The lightning turned its pit to glass. Nothing climbs out of glass."
- **Was:** an antlion larva that dug its trap pits in the sand verges of the plain road.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.2 high and 1.8 wide: a funnel pit with a raised crater lip of fused, glassy sand; from its bottom the antlion heaves its head and front up, sickle jaws spread wide. The rest stays buried, its weight in the sand.
- **Body:** a fat, bristled larva with a flat head and two long sickle jaws lined with teeth. The strike fused the pit's sand into fulgurite: glass tubes and branching glassy roots stick out of the rim and out of the larva's own back, scorched where they enter (lightning). Venom swells the base of its jaws, and its body is puffy and pale, banded venom green (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: fulgurite tubes; the glass rim; standing bristles; a traveller's pack half sunk in the slope; the drained husk of a wasp; sand trickling down; scorched jaw tips; the iron rim of a cart wheel half buried.
- **Head:** a flat head pushed up out of the sand, the jaws spread.
- **Signature shape:** the open sickle jaws rising from a glass-rimmed crater.
- **Colour:** chitin black, venom green, white-violet glass.
- **Motion:** idle is sand trickling while the jaws open and close slowly. Attack is a flick of sand that drags a hero into the pit (Pull, Paralyse).
