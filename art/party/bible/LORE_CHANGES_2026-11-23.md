Lore changes from Cole (2026-11-23) that affect art. Canon source: Ostium: The Lore. Every change follows GUIDE.md, both review gates and the design-language rules.

1. CYCLOPS SMITH (World V, elite) -> SPARKBLIND SMITH. Eye motifs are void-world only, so the single giant eye goes. New design: a huge foundry smith whose eyes were burned out by sparks, behind a riveted, welded visor with a narrow glowing slit. Scarred cheeks under the visor's edge. Keep the scale, the elite tier, the Foundry palette and the hammer role. Update the bible entry, game name and art key; redraw, re-rig and re-review.

2. THE SHATTERED GOD (World XV, ruler) is the remains of the Mother of Courses, the being who held all six courses. It is not a separate or male god. Its body must read as a vast feminine figure, broken and hollow, with void leaking from the cracks and no will left. Her eyes are missing; they are scattered through the void worlds (the Starless Eye, the Choir of Eyes, the Garden). Gold leaf cracked over black, violet lightning. Revise the bible entry and the figure.

3. THE VESSEL: a new secret 7th calling, hero tier, Living Ink, 6.4 heads, faces right, rigged from the start. This is the Prismatic Elementalist after holding all six courses in one body. The six inks run in bands up the skin from the fingertips (vermilion, pale blue, gold, deep blue, sick green, violet-black). Eyes with no colour. Plain undyed robes worn thin. Calm and upright: the only hero who is not hunched. Course stacks sit visibly on the skin and do not hurt it. Needs: the figure, Guesthouse resting and garth poses, a portrait, and card art for 13 cards [Cole confirmed 2026-11-23: the list has 14 and 14 is correct] (Open Hand, Bear It, Take Into Me, Give Back, Six Breaths, Exhale, Spit, Swallow, Sponge, Mantle, The Weighing, Keep Her Quiet, Unclench, The Whole Conjunction). Full design is in the lore doc, section The Vessel.

4. AFFLICTION CARDS: 15 new curse cards, one per world. They read as wounds and fears, never as abstract penalties: Drowning, Fen Rot, Fever, Black Lung, Ringing, False Light, Frostbite, The Split, Barrow Sickness, Venom Tremor, Stillness, The Hush, Ash-Mark, The Bloom, Seen. Table in the lore doc, section Afflictions and rising doom.

5. THE TEETH are canon: each world's ruler carries a Tooth (a shard of her), and the Sacristy panel shows the ones brought home. Check whether the panel art already has 15 distinct Teeth. Each should be marked by its world's two courses, and they are warm (Sister Ide says so).

6. THE HOSTELLER is secretly the First Porter, the hermit who built the first chapel centuries ago. Never state it in the art. Add hints only: a mason's broken, callused hands; a guest book far older than the abbey's books; a heavy old iron key.

7. THE ABBESS OF SALT (World I, elite) was an Ostiarius sent through the first door to seal it from the far side. She failed and came back once; the order sewed her mouth shut and sent her back. Stitch her mouth with the abbey's own waxed cord, and keep remnants of an Ostiarii habit under the salt crust.

8. THE ABBEY CHURCH HAS NO BELL. The bell was dropped through the first door and became the Bell Below. Make sure the church belfry reads as visibly empty in the garth and on the title screen.

9. KEEPER DETAILS (optional props on the existing keeper sprites): Prior Aldric came out of the Black Pyre (old burn scars on the hands and neck). Brother Fenn was a ledger clerk in the Sulphur Pits (sulphur-yellow staining on his oldest ledger). Brother Lucan was a scribe at the Stilled Spire (a stopped pocket clock or brass astrolabe at his desk).

10. AUDIT: confirm that no creature outside a void world carries an eye motif. Void worlds: I Drowned Kingdom, XII White Silence, XIII Black Pyre, XIV Blighted Garden, XV Torn Sky.

The design bible doc only holds the original rosters. These rulers are now canon as rival claimants to each world's Tooth, and their lore cannot be written without the entries.
