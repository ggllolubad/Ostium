## XII. The White Silence (Frost + Void, Absolute Zero)

A tundra of herders and hunters. A black star rose and took all the warmth and all the sound. **Frost** whitens everything; **void** takes pieces away: faces, shadows, sound. This is the hardest world to read, by design: figures are near-white on near-white, defined almost entirely by ink line. Eyes may appear (void world). **Palette:** almost pure white, a black sun, and nothing else.

### Rulers

#### The Cold Star

- **Lore line:** "The black star came down to see what was making all the noise."
- **Was:** a star.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, hanging in the sky at the top of the frame, very close.
- **Body:** **a perfect black disc** with a thin white corona, around which the frost forms rings; nothing reflects from it. A single enormous eye opens in its centre when it attacks. **The Tooth** orbits it like a small pale moon.
- **Signature shape:** the black disc.
- **Colour:** black, white.
- **Motion:** idle is the corona flickering. Attack is the eye opening; warmth drains from a hero.

#### The Unspoken Bear

- **Lore line:** "The hunters never said her name aloud. Now nothing can be said aloud."
- **Was:** the old white she-bear the hunters honoured with offerings and never hunted.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, reared on her hind legs out of a snowdrift, forepaws raised wide, mouth open in a roar. The head is cut off at the brow and the raised paws by the frame top. Her weight sinks into the haunches and the drift.
- **Body:** a vast white she-bear, her fur in long clumped strands frozen stiff and hanging plumb, every clump tipped with icicles; rime so thick on the windward side that she reads as a drift that stood up. The void has taken pieces of her: **clean round holes** through the belly, through one forepaw and through the near shoulder, the white tundra showing through them, the rims sharp as a punch. She casts no shadow. Inside the open mouth there is no tongue and no throat, only black.
- **Dress and gear:** the hunters' offerings still hang on her: strings of bone beads knotted into her ruff; carved ivory amulets on sinew cords; three broken spear shafts and a harpoon head embedded in her flank from hunters who broke the rule, the harpoon's line trailing into the snow; a hide strap of tied offering bundles slung round her neck; a necklace of seal teeth; a notch torn from one ear; bald ridges of old scars across the chest; a shattered sledge under one hind paw; an offering bowl of bone overturned in the drift; a ring of offering stones half buried around her; a frozen fish still hanging from a cord.
- **Head:** a long white head with a black nose and black lips drawn back from the teeth, frost on the muzzle, the mouth a black hole; small black eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the reared bear with forepaws spread and the black open mouth.
- **Colour:** white, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway on the hind legs, snow sifting from the fur, with no sound at all. Attack is a two-pawed downward blow, then a roar that makes no sound and fills the party with dread.

#### The Hearth Grandmother

- **Lore line:** "She sews new faces for the ones who lost theirs. None of them fit."
- **Was:** the clan's eldest woman, keeper of the winter fire and sewer of every coat in the camp.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, seated cross-legged before a dead hearth, bent over her sewing, huge and round-backed under heaped hides. Her humped back and hood rise out of the frame top; her bent head is in frame. Her weight settles low and wide.
- **Body:** an enormous old woman with knotted, frost-split hands, her skin white with rime; the windward side of her back heaped with drifted snow; icicles hanging from her chin, her nose and the edges of the hides. The void has taken her ears, leaving clean round holes; she casts no shadow. **Her hearth fire is gone**: where it burned there is a round black hole in the ground, ringed by the hearth stones.
- **Dress and gear:** coats of hide layered fur-out over fur-in, every edge sewn with bone beads; **a hide mask half sewn in her lap**, a face cut from white skin with a slit for a mouth; a bone needle as long as a spear, threaded with sinew; a bone thimble; a hide needle case at her belt; a cord over one shoulder strung with a dozen finished masks, no two alike; an ulu knife at her belt; strips of cut hide draped over one knee; a cooking pot frozen to its tripod over the dead hearth; a sinew spool; a bone comb in her hair; the snow around her trampled flat by the faceless who come to be fitted.
- **Head:** a broad, flat old face with deep wrinkles, eyes nearly shut, mouth working without sound; two grey braids frozen stiff; a hide hood pushed back onto the hump.
- **Signature shape:** the vast bent figure over the half-sewn mask, the long needle raised.
- **Colour:** white hides, grey braids, black void hearth.
- **Motion:** idle is a long pull of sinew through the hide, the needle arm rising high. Attack is a stitch: the needle darts out at a hero and draws thread, and for a turn that hero's face goes blank and they cannot use their skills.

### Elites

#### The Pale Hunter

- **Lore line:** "It hunts the warm. There are not many left."
- **Was:** a tundra hunter.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, crouched over tracks, a bow drawn.
- **Body and dress:** long white furs, a bone bow, a quiver of ice-tipped arrows; **its face is a smooth blank where the features were taken**.
- **Signature shape:** the drawn bow and blank face.
- **Colour:** white furs, black void face.
- **Motion:** idle is sniffing for warmth. Attack is an arrow at the warmest hero.

#### Void Mammoth

- **Lore line:** "Its herd died out an age ago. It did not notice."
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.5, filling its rank.
- **Body:** a mammoth with long white shaggy hair, curling tusks, and **clean void holes through its body where flesh has been unmade**, the white landscape showing through.
- **Signature shape:** the tusked mammoth outline with holes in it.
- **Colour:** white, black void edges.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway. Attack is a tusk charge.

#### The Drum-Seer

- **Lore line:** "He beat the drum to call the herds home. The herds still come. You will not hear them."
- **Was:** the clan's drum-seer, who beat the great frame drum to call the herds and to speak with the dead.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, stooped, knees bent in a dancing step, the great drum held up beside his head on the near side, the beater raised in the other hand. Weight on the bent front leg, the rear heel lifted.
- **Body:** a lean old man frozen mid-dance: the rear heel off the ground, the coat's fringes caught in a swing that never fell, icicles hanging from every fringe tip, rime crusted on the windward side. **The drum skin has a clean round hole through its centre** where the last beat landed, white sky showing through it. He casts no shadow, and the snow around his feet is unmarked though he dances.
- **Dress and gear:** a long coat of white reindeer hide hung with **iron pendants, bone discs and long hide fringes**; **a frame drum as wide as his shoulders**, a bentwood rim with a cross-bar grip behind, its skin painted in black with herds, hunters and a black sun; among the painted figures one is an eye, and it blinks; a beater of antler wrapped in fur; a headdress of antler tines with a fringe of hide strips over the brow; a belt of bone plaques; a bear-claw necklace; soft boots with beaded tops; a pouch of knucklebones; mittens hanging on a cord; small iron bells on the coat with their clappers gone.
- **Head:** a gaunt face with black tattooed lines on the chin, eyes rolled up white, lips parted in a chant with no sound.
- **Signature shape:** the great round drum with a hole through it, held up beside the head.
- **Colour:** white hide, black ink figures, grey iron, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow dance step, the drum tilting, the beater tapping without a sound. Attack is one hard beat; the cold goes out through the hole in the drum and the front rank is weakened.

#### The Voiceless Chief

- **Lore line:** "He still gives orders. They are still obeyed."
- **Was:** the chief of the winter camp, who decided where the clan went and when.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.1, broad and upright, feet planted wide, an ivory baton of office raised and pointing, the other fist on his belt. Weight square on both feet.
- **Body:** a heavy man in his fifties frozen mid-shout: jaw open, the cords of the neck standing out. **A clean round hole passes through his throat** where his voice was taken; the snow behind shows through it. Rime crusts his windward side; icicles hang from the beard and the baton hand. No breath clouds from his mouth.
- **Dress and gear:** **armour of bone slats**, a cuirass of ivory and bone plates laced with sinew over a hide coat, the slats chipped and scored; a collar of white fox pelts with a fox head on the near shoulder and, on the far one, only a clean-cut neck where the head was; ivory labrets at the corners of the mouth; a baton of walrus ivory carved with tally marks; a long knife in a bone sheath; mittens with ivory toggles; a belt hung with the tally sticks of the herds; knee boots with bone spikes on the soles; a beaded hood thrown back; an old scar across the brow.
- **Head:** a broad, flat face, heavy brow, grey hair cropped short, frost-burnt cheeks, narrowed eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the bone-slatted figure with the baton raised and the hole through the throat.
- **Colour:** white bone and fur, grey ivory, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is the baton turning from one ally to another, the mouth shaping orders. Attack is the baton pointing at one hero; every foe on the field acts against that hero first.

### Cast

#### Hollow Wanderer

- **Lore line:** "It walks the tundra looking for its face."
- **Was:** a herder.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, walking, head bowed.
- **Body and dress:** furs and a herder's crook; **the face is a hollow void**, the hood framing nothing.
- **Signature shape:** the hooded figure with an empty hood.
- **Colour:** white furs, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is turning the empty face. Attack is a crook blow.

#### Silence Stag

- **Lore line:** "It makes no sound when it runs. It makes no sound when it kills."
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5, head lowered.
- **Body:** a white stag with enormous antlers rimed in frost; its shadow is missing.
- **Signature shape:** the antlered stag.
- **Colour:** white, ice.
- **Motion:** idle is utter stillness. Attack is a silent antler charge.

#### Starless Eye

- **Lore line:** "An eye that fell from the black star. It is still looking for the rest of the face."
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, floating.
- **Body:** **a single huge eye** with a black iris, trailing a ragged edge of void where it was torn from a face.
- **Signature shape:** the floating eye.
- **Colour:** white, black.
- **Motion:** idle is looking around. Attack is a stare that causes stress.

#### Frozen Saint

- **Lore line:** "It prayed for warmth. The black star answered."
- **Was:** a saint of the tundra folk.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, kneeling, hands raised in prayer.
- **Body and dress:** robes frozen white, a halo of black frost behind the head where gold should be; the hands are frozen together.
- **Signature shape:** the kneeling praying figure with a dark halo.
- **Colour:** white, black halo.
- **Motion:** idle is a frozen prayer. Attack is a cold blessing that weakens.

#### Rime Shade

- **Lore line:** "A shadow cast by nothing."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, flat against the snow.
- **Body:** a human shadow lying on the snow and slowly rising off it, with no body to cast it.
- **Signature shape:** the flat shadow peeling up.
- **Colour:** black on white.
- **Motion:** idle is shifting on the ground. Attack is rising up and enveloping.

#### Ice-Fisher

- **Lore line:** "It has been jigging that hole since the star rose. Something is nibbling."
- **Was:** an ice-fisher who sat over holes in the lake ice all winter.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.9, seated and hunched forward on a whalebone stool, a short jigging stick in one hand, the line dropping into a hole in the ice at its feet. Weight forward over the knees.
- **Body:** a stocky figure frozen to its own seat, rime thick on the windward shoulder and along the hood's rim, icicles hanging from the jigging stick and the line. The hole in front of it is **a perfectly round black hole** in the ice, clean-rimmed and deeper than any lake. Its far hand is gone at the wrist, the stump a clean rim, and a spare line is tied round the forearm above it.
- **Dress and gear:** a hide parka with the hood up, the fur ruff frozen into spikes; a sealskin mitten on the remaining hand; a jigging stick of antler with a bone lure; a three-pronged fish spear laid across the knees; a bone ice pick at the belt; a string of frozen fish beside it, each with a clean hole through it; an antler scoop for clearing the hole; a bait pouch.
- **Head:** the hood up round a frozen white face, eyes squeezed shut against the glare, an ivory lip plug.
- **Signature shape:** the hunched seated figure over the round black hole.
- **Colour:** white hide, grey bone, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a small jerk of the jigging stick, again and again. Attack is a sharp jerk of the line; something unseen pulls a hero forward a rank, toward the hole.

#### Hush-Child

- **Lore line:** "It cries without stopping. Be grateful you cannot hear it."
- **Was:** an infant of the winter camp, laced into its cradleboard when the star rose.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5 including the board, a hide cradleboard standing upright on its own in the snow with the child laced into it. Weight in the board's splayed foot.
- **Body:** a swaddled infant with only the face and one fist free, the skin bluish-white, rime on the windward cheek. The mouth is wide open in a crying face, and **inside the mouth there is nothing**: a black void that takes the cry. Tears have frozen into two icicles down the cheeks. It casts no shadow.
- **Dress and gear:** a cradleboard of bent wood and stretched hide, laced with sinew thongs that cross over the body; a hoop of bent wood arching over the head, hung with charms: bone beads, a carved ivory seal, a dried hare's foot, a tiny drum; a fur lining frozen into spikes; a carrying strap trailing in the snow; the board's foot splayed and frozen in.
- **Head:** a small, creased face, the brow puckered, the fist clenched beside it.
- **Signature shape:** the upright cradleboard with its hoop over the head.
- **Colour:** white hide and fur, grey wood, black void mouth.
- **Motion:** idle is the free fist shaking in silence. Attack is a long silent cry that causes stress across the whole party.

#### Lemming Drift

- **Lore line:** "There were always too many of them. There are fewer now. They have not noticed."
- **Was:** lemmings of the tundra, which swarmed in the hard years.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a low heaped drift about 0.5 high and 1.0 wide, pouring forward.
- **Body:** dozens of small lemmings in white winter fur, packed into a moving heap, their fur frosted, tiny icicles hanging from tails and paws. Some are frozen solid mid-scramble and carried along stiff-legged by the rest. The void has taken bites out of the drift: lemmings missing their back halves cleanly, and round holes through the heap with the snow showing beyond. The drift casts no shadow.
- **Dress and gear:** what they have gnawed and dragged along: a herder's mitten, a chewed harness strap, a string of bone beads, a ptarmigan feather, a scrap of hide; tooth marks on everything.
- **Head:** many small blunt faces with black eyes and black-tipped teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the low, wide, pouring heap.
- **Colour:** white fur, black void holes.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant churning seethe. Attack is the drift pouring over a hero and gnawing.

#### Lead Dog

- **Lore line:** "It still leads the team. The team is behind it somewhere."
- **Was:** the lead dog of a herder's sledge team.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.2 long, low and leaning into its harness, shoulders down, one forepaw reaching, the line taut behind it. Weight driving forward through the front legs.
- **Body:** a thick-furred sledge dog, tail curled over its back, its white fur clumped and frozen into points that hang down; rime crusted on the windward side, a frost beard on the muzzle, ice balled between the toes. **The line runs back taut to nothing**: a pace behind the dog it ends in a clean cut, still pulled tight as if the team were still on it. One ear is gone, the rim clean. It casts no shadow.
- **Dress and gear:** a hide harness with bone toggles, the breast strap worn bright; the braided hide line; a bone bead on the collar; hide booties on two feet only; an old bite scar across the shoulder; a frozen chewing rag caught in the harness.
- **Head:** a broad wedge head, small ears, the mouth open in a silent pant, frost on the whiskers.
- **Signature shape:** the low, pulling dog with the taut line ending in nothing.
- **Colour:** white fur, grey harness, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a lean into the harness, paws digging. Attack is a lunge and a bite, then a drag, as if the team were pulling.

#### Sledge Hauler

- **Lore line:** "The dogs died first. She took the strap. She has not put it down."
- **Was:** a herder woman who hauled the clan's goods on a sledge after the dogs gave out.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3, a huge, broad woman leaning almost forty-five degrees into a hide chest strap, a loaded sledge behind her, legs driving. Weight over the front foot.
- **Body:** heavily built, thick thighs and back, frozen in mid-stride: rime thick on the windward side, icicles hanging from her chin and from the strap, her braid frozen stiff and swinging forward. She leaves no footprints and casts no shadow, and a clean round hole passes through her forearm where the strap rubbed.
- **Dress and gear:** a long hide coat kilted up over fur trousers; snowshoes of bent wood and sinew; **the chest strap**, crossing her breastbone to the sledge's traces; the sledge, with bone-shod runners, heaped with hides, a rolled tent and a cooking pot, tent poles sticking out behind; lashing ropes; an empty cradleboard hoop tied on top of the load; one mitten lost, the bare hand white with frostbite; a knife at her hip.
- **Head:** a wide, wind-burnt face, the mouth clenched, the eyes fixed ahead.
- **Signature shape:** the steep forward lean against the strap, the sledge behind her.
- **Colour:** white hides, grey wood, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy pull and a slip back. Attack is letting the strap go slack and swinging the sledge round into the front rank.

#### The Silent Singers

- **Lore line:** "They sang into each other's mouths until one laughed and lost. Neither has lost yet."
- **Was:** two throat-singers of the camp, who sang face to face as a contest.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, two women standing face to face, each gripping the other's forearms, leaning in until their faces almost touch. Together they make one silhouette, an arch; their weight is on their outer feet, braced against each other.
- **Body:** both frozen mid-contest, rime on their windward sides, icicles hanging from their sleeves and hair. **Between their open mouths hangs a small black void**, a clean hole in the air where the sound was. Neither casts a shadow.
- **Dress and gear:** matching hide parkas with deep hoods thrown back; beaded yokes in white and black beads; long braids with bone ornaments; ivory earrings; a bone needle case at one belt; a mitten tucked into the other's belt; frost on their lashes.
- **Head:** two faces in profile, close and wide-mouthed, cheeks tattooed with black lines.
- **Signature shape:** the arch of two figures with their faces nearly touching.
- **Colour:** white hide, black beads and tattoo lines, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is a rhythmic rocking against each other, without a sound. Attack is a song no one can hear; it causes stress, and the heroes' ears ring with silence.

#### Snow-House Builder

- **Lore line:** "It builds a house for each of you. They are very small houses."
- **Was:** a builder who cut snow blocks for the clan's winter houses.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, standing with a big cut snow block raised overhead in both hands, back arched under the weight. Weight on the planted rear foot.
- **Body:** broad-shouldered, arms locked straight, frozen at the top of a lift; rime caked on the windward side, icicles hanging from the elbows and from the block's underside. A clean round hole passes through the block, and another through the near forearm. It casts no shadow.
- **Dress and gear:** a short hide parka belted at the waist; **a long bone snow knife** in the belt; hide knee pads worn through from kneeling to build; an antler snow probe slung on the back; fur-backed mittens; sealskin boots; a bone charm on a thong; the block itself, scored with saw marks.
- **Head:** hood back, cropped hair frozen stiff, a squint, chapped lips.
- **Signature shape:** the block held high overhead.
- **Colour:** white, grey shadow, black void.
- **Motion:** idle is the block wavering overhead. Attack is bringing the block down on a hero, burying them for a turn.
