## VIII. The Sundered Rime (Fire + Frost, Thermal Shock)

A northern hold of miners and shieldmaidens built on a glacier. The miners dug down and met fire coming up. Everything is half frozen and half burning, and **the strain shows as cracks**: every body in this world is split, one side rimed white, the other charred with glowing orange seams, the line between them cracking. **Palette:** white ice split by orange seams, black obsidian.

### Rulers

#### The Riven Jarl

- **Lore line:** "Half his body froze in the fall, half burned. He rules the split between."
- **Was:** the jarl of the hold.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, standing on a cracked glacier throne, a great axe resting on one shoulder.
- **Body:** **split exactly down the middle**: the left half frozen solid, white and rimed, beard full of icicles; the right half charred black with molten orange cracks, the beard burned away. Where the halves meet, the body is cracking open and glowing.
- **Dress and gear:** a fur cloak, half frozen and half smouldering; a mail shirt; a huge bearded axe. **The Tooth** is set in the pommel of the axe.
- **Head:** a horned iron helm (no horns on the burned side, melted away), two different eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the split figure with the shouldered axe.
- **Colour:** ice white, charcoal, molten orange.
- **Motion:** idle is steam rising from the crack. Attack is an axe blow that splits the ranks.

#### The Sleeper Under the Hold

- **Lore line:** "The hold was built on the glacier, and the glacier on her. The miners found her ribs first."
- **Was:** a giantess of the old ice, the eldest of the jötnar, asleep under the glacier long before the hold was built. The miners' deep galleries were cut into her.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3 high and 6 long, lying on her back in the glacier floor, seen from the side. The hill of her chest and shoulder fills the frame and runs off both sides; her head is thrown back at the left edge, cropped by the frame; one arm lifts from the ice and the hand hangs open over the company. Her weight is the ground itself.
- **Body:** blue-white skin like old glacier ice, faceted in broad planes, rimed thick and hung with downward icicles along the lifted arm, the jaw and the underside of the breast (frost). Her split runs from outside to inside: the miners cut timbered adits into her chest and shoulder, and the fire coming up from below has filled those galleries, so every tunnel mouth glows molten orange from within, its edges charred and ash curling up out of it (fire). Cracks run outward from each adit across the ice skin like crazed glaze, glowing at their roots and white at their tips; steam rises wherever the fire reaches the surface.
- **Dress and gear:** no clothing. A torque of black obsidian beads, each the size of a boulder, lies across her collarbone; the miners' works do the rest. Secondary details: three adits framed with timber props and lintels, charred inside and frosted outside; a miners' ladder of lashed poles up her flank; ore-cart rails running into a gallery between her ribs, a tipped cart beside them; a winch post driven into her shoulder with a rope coil on it; iron spikes and an abandoned pick left in the ice skin; tally marks cut by the miners beside an adit mouth; an ore bucket hanging from her lifted finger by its bail; a fall of grey frozen braids spilling off the frame edge from her head like a second glacier; long, cracked nails; a miner's lamp hook hammered into her jaw; a scatter of obsidian rubble along her side.
- **Head:** a broad, heavy face thrown back, eyes frozen shut in sleep, the lips parted and breathing steam.
- **Signature shape:** the hill of her chest pierced by glowing timbered tunnel mouths, with one hand hanging over it.
- **Colour:** blue-white ice skin, molten orange in the galleries, black obsidian.
- **Motion:** idle is one slow breath that lifts the hill while steam vents from the adits. Attack is the hanging hand coming down across two ranks, or a gust of burning air from every adit at once.

#### The Firstbreaker

- **Lore line:** "He swung the pick that let the fire in. He has been climbing out ever since."
- **Was:** the hold's foreman of miners, who was at the face of the deepest shaft when it broke into fire.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, climbing out of the deep shaft: only his head, shoulders, arms and chest show above the timbered rim. Both forearms are hooked over the rim and the near hand grips a pick driven into the ice for purchase. He leans out over the company, his head near the frame top, cropped at both sides by the headframe timbers. His weight hangs on his forearms.
- **Body:** a broad, heavy-shouldered miner grown vast. His split is horizontal and lies exactly at the shaft rim: below it his chest, belly and whatever is still down the shaft burn, charred black with wide molten seams and curls of ash rising (fire); above it, the glacier wind across the shaft mouth has rimed his shoulders, arms and head white, the brows and beard hung with icicles (frost). The crack rings his chest at the level of the rim, glowing and steaming. As he hauls himself higher the line stays at the rim, so each part of him that comes up cracks as it freezes.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather miner's hood and short cape over the shoulders, frozen stiff; a wool tunic charred away below the rim. Secondary details: the hoist chain wrapped twice round his shoulders where he climbed it, its ore bucket hanging off one end; the headframe's two rimed timber legs either side, its pulley wheel cropped at the top; the pick, its haft charred at the head end and frozen at the grip; a ring of iron wedges and feathers on a cord at the neck; a coil of rope across the shoulder; a horn whistle for signalling the shift; two small Rimeburnt Thralls clinging to his back, climbing out with him; splintered ladder rungs on the rim; tally notches cut into the rim timber; a lamp on a hook at his chest, burnt black; obsidian rubble along the rim.
- **Head:** a broad, flat face, the mouth open in a roar of steam, frost in the brows and beard, the hood pushed back.
- **Signature shape:** the head and arms hooked over the shaft rim, the pick driven into the ice.
- **Colour:** ice white above, charcoal and molten orange below, black obsidian on the rim.
- **Motion:** idle is hauling himself an inch higher while the crack line steams. Attack is a downward pick blow from over the rim onto the front rank, with heat rolling up out of the shaft behind it.

### Elites

#### Jötun Thane

- **Lore line:** "A giant of the old ice. The fire in the deep has driven him mad with heat."
- **Was:** a frost giant of the old glacier.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.5, hunched under the frame top, fists clenched.
- **Body:** a giant of blue-white skin and icy beard, **melting**: water streams from him, his skin blistered and steaming where the heat touches it, eyes wild.
- **Dress and gear:** a mammoth-hide kilt, a glacier-ice club.
- **Head:** a huge bearded head, mouth roaring.
- **Signature shape:** the hunched giant with the ice club.
- **Colour:** blue-white, steam, molten orange in the blisters.
- **Motion:** idle is steaming and shuddering. Attack is a club smash.

#### Twinflame Seeress

- **Lore line:** "One face burns. The other has frozen solid. Both speak at once."
- **Was:** the hold's seeress.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, a staff in each hand.
- **Body and dress:** a robe of furs and rune-stitched wool, half frosted and half charred; **two faces on one head**, one burning and one frozen, looking left and right.
- **Head:** the two faces share one crown of braids.
- **Signature shape:** the two-faced head and two staves.
- **Colour:** frost white, flame orange, black.
- **Motion:** idle is both mouths murmuring. Attack is twin bolts of fire and frost.

#### Berserker

- **Lore line:** "He walked into the fire on purpose. He walked out again, and kept fighting."
- **Was:** one of the jarl's berserkers, sworn to fight in a bearskin and no armour.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, wide-legged and leaning forward, chest out, a bearded axe in each hand held low and out from the hips; the bear's head worn as a hood, pulled far forward. Weight forward on bent knees.
- **Body:** a heavy, barrel-chested warrior, bare to the waist under the pelt. His split runs diagonally, from the near shoulder across the chest to the far hip. Above and behind the line, the near shoulder and arm are iced to the elbow and the bearskin over his back is frozen stiff and hung with icicles (frost); below and in front of it, the belly, far side and far arm are charred black and split with glowing seams, ash curling off them (fire). The crack itself is jagged and glows at its deepest.
- **Dress and gear:** the bearskin, its forepaws knotted at his throat; wool breeches cross-gartered to the knee; fur-wrapped boots. Secondary details: two bearded axes, the near one frozen to his hand, the far one with a charred haft; iron arm rings on the frozen arm; knotwork tattoos smeared and blistered on the burnt arm; a seax on the belt in a scorched sheath; a necklace of bear teeth; a broken arrow shaft in the near thigh; leather wrist wraps; blood running from cuts on the frozen side; the pelt's hind claws hanging at his calves; frost crusted on the knotted paws.
- **Head:** under the bear's upper skull and snout, a bearded face with teeth bared, the beard frozen into a stiff fork, one eye rimed shut.
- **Signature shape:** the bear-head hood and the two axes held low.
- **Colour:** frost white pelt, charcoal, molten orange seams; red only in the blood at the cuts.
- **Motion:** idle is a heaving breath that flakes ash and frost. Attack is a spinning double-axe sweep across the front ranks.

#### Musk Ox

- **Lore line:** "The hold yoked them to the ore sledges. The fire came up under the herd."
- **Was:** a bull musk ox of the glacier herds, yoked by the hold to haul ore sledges from the shaft.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.8 at the hump and 2.4 long, head low and the horn boss forward, braced on four short, thick legs. The weight sits in the shoulders and hump.
- **Body:** a massive humped ox under a skirt of guard hair that reaches the ground. Its split runs lengthwise along the flank: above the line the back and hump are frozen, the long hair stiffened into a curtain of icicled tags and rime (frost); below it the hair is burned away, and the belly, legs and hooves are charred black, the hide split with molten seams where it stood over a vent (fire). The crack runs the length of the body where the burnt hair ends, and steam rolls out from under the frozen curtain.
- **Dress and gear:** what the hold put on it. Secondary details: a heavy wooden yoke across the neck with a broken trace chain hanging from it; a rope halter; an iron nose ring; the hold's brand burned into the hump; ice balls clotted in the coat; a length of ore-sledge trace dragging behind; split, charred hooves; a chipped horn tip; old gore scars on the shoulder; frozen breath hanging from the muzzle in icicles; a scorched patch along the jaw.
- **Head:** a low, broad head under the heavy horn boss, the horns sweeping down past the eyes and hooking up at the tips; small eyes deep in hair; steam from the nostrils.
- **Signature shape:** the low humped bulk with its skirt of hair and its hooked horns.
- **Colour:** frost white and grey coat, charcoal legs, molten orange seams.
- **Motion:** idle is a head shake that rattles the ice in its coat. Attack is a horn-boss charge that knocks a hero back a rank.

### Cast

#### Rimeburnt Thrall

- **Lore line:** "Miners who dug too deep and met the fire coming up. The ice kept what the fire left."
- **Was:** a glacier miner.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, pick in hand.
- **Body and dress:** burned black on the front where the fire met them, rimed white on the back; a miner's leather hood, a pick.
- **Signature shape:** the pick and the half-burned, half-frozen body.
- **Colour:** charcoal, white rime, orange seams.
- **Motion:** idle is a creak and hiss. Attack is a pick swing.

#### Magma Hound

- **Lore line:** "Its skin is cooling rock. Its insides are not."
- **Was:** a hold's war-dog.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.2 long, low and lean.
- **Body:** a wolfhound of cooling black basalt, molten orange showing in the cracks and at the mouth; frost forming on its back as it cools.
- **Signature shape:** the lean hound shape glowing at the jaws.
- **Colour:** black, molten orange, rime.
- **Motion:** idle is panting heat. Attack is a burning bite.

#### Obsidian Shieldmaiden

- **Lore line:** "Her armour is the volcano's glass. It cuts her as often as it cuts you."
- **Was:** a shieldmaiden of the hold.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, shield forward, spear back.
- **Body and dress:** **armour of chipped black obsidian plates** over mail, edges glassy and sharp, thin cuts on her arms where the armour bites; a round obsidian-faced shield; a spear.
- **Head:** braided hair, a nasal helm of obsidian.
- **Signature shape:** the glassy round shield.
- **Colour:** glossy black, frost white, blood red at the cuts.
- **Motion:** idle is a steady guard. Attack is a spear thrust.

#### Steam Wraith

- **Lore line:** "What rises when fire meets ice. It remembers being a man."
- **Was:** a man caught where fire met ice.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.1, floating, dissolving below the waist.
- **Body:** a figure of rising steam with a man's face and reaching hands; condensation drips from it.
- **Signature shape:** the rising column with reaching hands.
- **Colour:** white steam, a faint orange core.
- **Motion:** idle is billowing. Attack is a scalding embrace.

#### Fissure Worm

- **Lore line:** "It swims through glacier like water. The ice closes behind it."
- **Was:** a burrowing worm of the deep ice.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6, reared from a crack in the ice.
- **Body:** a pale segmented worm, its head a round, lamprey-like disc lined with rings of inward-hooked teeth and crusted with frost, its body glowing orange where it melts its way through.
- **Signature shape:** the reared worm and the toothed ring mouth.
- **Colour:** pale white, orange glow.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sway. Attack is a plunge and bite.

#### Fire-Setter

- **Lore line:** "The miners split rock with fire, then cold water. The mountain learned the trick from them."
- **Was:** a miner who cracked the rock face by fire-setting: stacking a fire against the stone, then dousing it with cold water.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, a long iron fork raised high in the near hand with a smouldering bundle of faggots on its tines, a wooden bucket swinging back in the far hand. Weight on the back foot, about to drive the fire forward.
- **Body:** lean and wiry. His split is in one limb: the fork arm is charred from the fingers to the shoulder blade, the skin split into glowing seams and flaking ash (fire), and a crack rings the shoulder like the edge of a pauldron. The rest of him is rimed white from quench water that splashed back on him, the jerkin frozen stiff and icicles hanging from the bucket hand and the knees (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a leather miner's hood; a leather jerkin frozen into creases; wool leggings with puttee wraps; iron-shod boots. Secondary details: the bucket of iron-hooped staves, its water frozen in a slop over the rim; the bundle of birch faggots, charred and ember-cored and shedding ash, with no flame tongues; a sooty rag over the mouth; a tinder pouch at the belt; an iron wedge and a wooden mallet; a chunk of split rock in a net bag; a burn hole through the hood on the fork side.
- **Head:** a soot-black face under the hood, the rag over the mouth, eyes squinting against smoke.
- **Signature shape:** the raised fork with its bundle, and the swinging bucket.
- **Colour:** charcoal and molten orange on the arm, frost white, black.
- **Motion:** idle is the bundle shedding embers while the bucket creaks. Attack is the burning bundle driven into a hero, then the bucket thrown after it, so the target cracks.

#### The Skald

- **Lore line:** "He kept the hold's saga in his head. He is adding the last verse."
- **Was:** the jarl's skald, poet of the hall and keeper of its saga.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, upright with the chest out and the head thrown back mid-verse; a lyre held against the hip in the far arm, the near hand lifted from the strings. Weight back on the heels.
- **Body:** a thick-necked man in his fifties. His split runs across the face at the mouth: from the upper lip up, his eyes, brow and hair are frozen white, the eyes frosted over and the hair stiff (frost); his jaw, throat and chest are charred and split with glowing seams, as if the words burn on the way out (fire). The crack runs from ear to ear through the open mouth, and steam leaks between the lips.
- **Dress and gear:** the finest cloak in the cast, heavy wool given by the jarl, pinned at the shoulder with a large iron ring brooch, its hem scorched; a tunic with a tablet-woven border. Secondary details: the lyre, six strings, its wooden body frosted at the top and charred at the base, one string snapped and curled; iron arm rings, the jarl's gifts; a drinking horn on a strap; a belt knife; a pouch; the braided beard burned to stubs at the jaw; frost on the cloak's shoulders; leather shoes with ankle thongs.
- **Head:** a frozen brow and frozen hair over an open singing mouth that glows inside, the beard burned away.
- **Signature shape:** the lyre at the hip and the head thrown back singing.
- **Colour:** frost white, charcoal, molten orange in the throat.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow strum and a murmur. Attack is a verse that causes stress.

#### Mead-Bearer

- **Lore line:** "She filled the horns at the jarl's table. The mead boils in the horn and freezes on the lip."
- **Was:** a serving woman of the jarl's hall, who carried the mead.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, stepping forward with a long drinking horn held out level in both hands, offering it. Weight on the front foot, the back heel raised.
- **Body:** a sturdy woman in her thirties. Her split is at the knees: she was standing in the hall when the floor cracked open, and her feet and shins went into the crack. From the knees down she is charred black with molten seams (fire); from the knees up she is frozen, rime over the dress and icicles at the elbows and hem (frost). The crack rings both knees, glowing.
- **Dress and gear:** an apron dress over a linen underdress, the apron held by two oval brooches at the shoulders with a string of black and white glass beads swagged between them; the hem burnt to a ragged edge at the knees. Secondary details: the horn, an ox horn with an iron rim and tip, its mead bubbling at the bottom and frozen at the lip; a clay pitcher hung at the hip on a cord; a knife and a needle case hanging from one brooch; a headscarf; an old burn scar on one wrist; a ring of storeroom keys at the belt.
- **Head:** a broad, tired face, frost on the lashes, cracked lips, the headscarf knotted at the nape.
- **Signature shape:** the long curved horn held out.
- **Colour:** frost white, black and white beads, molten orange at the knees.
- **Motion:** idle is holding the horn steady while steam rises from it. Her action is to give an ally the horn, which heals it and strengthens its next blow.

#### Ore-Hauler

- **Lore line:** "Thralls hauled the ore up from the deep shaft. The last load came up burning."
- **Was:** a thrall who dragged ore sledges from the shaft head.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, bent nearly double into a leather harness, chest down near the knees and one hand on the ground, a low iron-shod sledge heaped with ore dragging behind him. Weight thrown forward into the harness.
- **Body:** a huge, broad thrall. His split follows the harness: the broad strap that runs over his near shoulder and across his chest to the far hip carried the heat of the load, and along its line the skin is burnt in a stripe a hand wide, black and split with orange seams (fire). On either side of the stripe he is frozen, rime thick on his back and arms (frost); the crack runs along both edges of the stripe.
- **Dress and gear:** a wool breechcloth and leg wraps; bare feet black with frost. Secondary details: an iron thrall collar with a broken chain link; the leather harness with iron rings, its traces chained to the sledge; the sledge's oak runners shod with iron; the ore heaped on it in black lumps that glow at the core under a crust of frost; a frayed rope lashing the load; old whip scars across the back; a tally stick tied to the collar; one lump of ore fallen behind and smoking.
- **Head:** a shaven head, a heavy brow, the jaw clenched, steam from the nose.
- **Signature shape:** the bent body in harness with the heaped sledge behind.
- **Colour:** frost white, a charred black stripe with orange seams, black ore.
- **Motion:** idle is straining while the sledge creaks forward an inch. Attack is heaving the sledge round in a swing that spills burning ore over the front rank.

#### Snow Hare

- **Lore line:** "It came down off the glacier for the moss the vents had thawed. It was there when the ice broke."
- **Was:** a mountain hare of the glacier, white in winter, that grazed the thawed moss round the shaft-head vents and was caught by the hold's snares.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.4, sitting bolt upright on its haunches, ears straight up, forepaws held against the chest, the head turned to the company, listening. Weight on the long hind feet.
- **Body:** a big, long-legged hare in its winter white. Its split is a crazing: it bolted through the steam when a vent broke under it, and heat and cold struck it together, so its whole body is covered in a fine network of cracks like crackled glaze. Every small plate between them is either winter fur rimed stiff and white (frost) or singed to the skin, charred and glowing (fire), so it reads as a mosaic of white and orange. The cracks open widest at the joints and along the tall ears.
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: a wire snare still drawn round its neck, the broken end and its wooden peg trailing; black ear tips, one ear notched; frost on the whiskers and singed stubs on the other side of the muzzle; the broad hind feet furred and balled with ice; a tuft of moss in the mouth; steam from the nostrils; winter fur moulting in rimed clumps; a cracked claw.
- **Head:** a long, narrow head with big, wide-set eyes and twitching nostrils, the ears standing straight up.
- **Signature shape:** the upright, long-eared hare sitting on its haunches.
- **Colour:** frost white and molten orange mosaic, black ear tips.
- **Motion:** idle is listening, the ears turning, the nose twitching. Attack is a drumming of the hind feet on the ice: a crack runs out under a hero and burning steam bursts up through it.

#### Pit Adder

- **Lore line:** "The jarl threw his enemies to the adders. When the hold split, the pit split with it, and the adders came up warm."
- **Was:** the largest adder of the jarl's snake pit, where the hold put its enemies to death; the adders wintered there in a knot against the warm rock.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.45 reared: a thick adder in three tight coils on the ice, the front third raised in an S with the head drawn back to strike. Weight in the coiled body.
- **Body:** a heavy, short-tailed adder with a black zigzag down its back. Its split spirals with the coils: wherever the coils lay pressed against the warm rock of the pit, the scales are charred black and split along the zigzag into glowing orange seams (fire); every outward face of the coils, and the raised neck, is rimed white, the scales frozen up into stiff, faceted plates (frost). The crack follows the spiral, so the coiled body reads as a twist of white and burning black.
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: a jarl's iron arm ring that the snake has grown through behind the head; a half-shed skin, frozen stiff, hanging from the lowest coil; a chipped fang; frost on the tongue's fork; steam rising from the charred seams; a broken scale plate on the raised neck; a finger bone from the pit caught in the coils; ash flaking from the tail tip.
- **Head:** a broad, flat, heavy-browed head drawn back, the mouth half open on the fangs, the forked tongue out.
- **Signature shape:** the tight coils with the head raised and drawn back in an S.
- **Colour:** frost white, charcoal, molten orange seams along the zigzag.
- **Motion:** idle is the tongue flicking and the raised neck swaying. Attack is a strike: the bite burns and then freezes, and the hero cracks (Bleed).

#### Glacier Wolverine

- **Lore line:** "It went down the shaft for the dead and found them cooked. It keeps coming back."
- **Was:** a wolverine of the glacier, a scavenger.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5 tall and 1.0 long; low and humped, legs splayed, the head down and swinging, teeth bared. Weight in the heavy forequarters.
- **Body:** a thick-set, short-legged animal built like a small bear. Its split runs front to back: the head and forelegs, which it put into a vent to feed, are charred black with glowing seams, the fur singed to the skin (fire); from the shoulders back it is rimed white, the long shaggy fur frozen into clumps (frost). The crack rings the shoulders like a collar.
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: big hooked claws, one broken; a torn ear; the pale side stripe frozen into icicled tags; a bushy tail stiff with ice; a strip of miner's leather in the jaws; ash flaking from the muzzle; steam from the mouth.
- **Head:** a broad, low head with small ears, the jaws open on heavy teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the low, humped scavenger with its burnt head swung low.
- **Colour:** frost white, charcoal, molten orange seams.
- **Motion:** idle is a restless pacing swing of the head. Attack is a lunging bite that tears (Bleed).
