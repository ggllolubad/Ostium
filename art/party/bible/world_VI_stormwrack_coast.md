## VI. The Stormwrack Coast (Lightning + Water, Electrocute)

A wreckers' coast that lived by luring ships onto the rocks. The storm never ends, and the false lights became true lightning. **Lightning** leaves branching burns and fused metal; **water** soaks, bloats and barnacles. The cast are wreckers, drowned sailors and sea creatures. Wreckage from the Drowned Kingdom washes up here. **Palette:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow.

### Rulers

#### The Thunder Whale

- **Lore line:** "The storm on the coast is its song."
- **Was:** an old bull whale the wreckers harpooned and failed to kill.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 5, breaching from the surf, only the head and front third in frame.
- **Body:** a scarred whale of slate grey, its hide hung with dozens of rusted harpoons and their ropes; **the harpoons act as lightning rods**: each iron head is fused into the hide, branching burns spread from every one across the skin, and the old ropes have fused stiff. Barnacles crust the jaw. **The Tooth** is lodged in its blowhole.
- **Head:** a huge jaw half open, an old eye with a white cataract.
- **Signature shape:** the breaching head bristling with harpoons.
- **Colour:** slate grey, barnacle white, lamp-yellow scorch.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow rise and fall in the surf. Attack is a song: lightning arcs from harpoon to harpoon and strikes every soaked hero.

#### The Last Prize

- **Lore line:** "The richest ship the coast ever took. It has been going down for a hundred years and has not finished."
- **Was:** a great merchant carrack lured onto the rocks by the false lights, with her crew.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a broken-backed ship heeled over on the rocks broadside to the company, bows to the left. The mast stumps and upper deck are cut off by the frame top and the stern by the right edge; surf breaks white along the waterline. It never moves off the rocks; it lists and groans.
- **Body:** lightning dominates: struck again and again through her masts and ironwork, **every bolt and fitting is fused into a blackened weld**, with branching Lichtenberg scorch trees running across the planking from each one; the standing rigging has frayed and fused into stiff black rods. Water has swollen the hull and split it along the seams, the planks bulging, barnacles thick on the lower strakes and kelp hanging from the gunports. The drowned crew still stand their stations: gunners bloated at the ports with their hands fused to their guns, and three sailors at the rail welded to its iron fittings through their pikes and hooks.
- **Dress and gear:** four open gunports, the cannon muzzles fused to the port rims; the rail sailors' boarding pikes and hooks; a cracked ship's bell hanging scorched at the bow; a torn sail draped over the side like a curtain; the ship's name board half fallen; a stove-in hold spilling bales of cloth, spice casks and a split coin chest; an anchor hanging from the bow by a fused chain; the wreckers' own grapnel lines still hooked into her rail; a lit lantern at the stern; her boat hanging smashed against the side by one rope; a pump handle frozen mid-stroke; a gull perched on a fused mast stump.
- **Head:** none. Her figurehead is her face: a carved oak woman split down the middle by a strike, a Lichtenberg tree burned across the face.
- **Signature shape:** the heeled hull with its fused gunports and broken mast stumps.
- **Colour:** slate grey timber, white surf, lamp yellow at the stern lantern.
- **Motion:** idle is the hull groaning and listing with each wave, surf breaking over the rail. Attack is a broadside: the fused guns fire with a crack of lightning down the gun deck, and the drowned at the rail drag a hero aboard.

#### The Wind-Seller

- **Lore line:** "She sold sailors their winds tied up in knotted cord. On this coast she only ever sold the one kind."
- **Was:** the coast's wise-woman, who sold sailors winds tied in knotted cords and whistled up storms for the wreckers.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a giant old woman standing on a sea-stack in the surf, the foot of the stack in the waves at the bottom of the frame and the top of her head cut off by the frame top. She leans forward over the edge, one foot on a lower ledge, and a long knotted cord runs from her hands up out of frame into the storm.
- **Body:** huge and gaunt. Lightning dominates: struck so often that **Lichtenberg ferns cover her from scalp to ankle in a lace of white scars**, her long grey hair stands straight up into the wind, stiff and crackling, and her fingers are fused to the knotted cord at the knuckles. Water has soaked her to the skin, pale and sea-wrinkled, with barnacles crusting her bare feet and shins.
- **Dress and gear:** **the knotted cord**, tarred hemp with a knot every arm's length, three of them untied and frayed; layered grey wool shawls, soaked and hanging heavy and plumb, pinned with bone pins, the hems frayed and fused stiff; a sailor's oilskin coat taken in payment, far too big; a string of payment coins and ships' buttons round her neck; a whistle carved from a bird bone; a sack of knotted cords at the hip; a dead gull hanging from her belt; a carved wooden ship charm; ropes of kelp round her ankles; a driftwood staff leaning against the stack; a broken ship's lantern at her feet.
- **Head:** a long, wind-hollowed face, mouth pursed to whistle, eyes narrowed into the gale, the grey hair standing up in a stiff fan.
- **Signature shape:** the bent old woman on the sea-stack with the knotted cord rising into the sky.
- **Colour:** slate grey shawls and rock, white surf, lamp yellow lightning.
- **Motion:** idle is her fingers worrying at the next knot and a low whistle. Attack is a knot untied: a gust and a bolt strike a rank, hardest on the soaked.

### Elites

#### The Lighthouse Keeper

- **Lore line:** "He keeps the light burning in the wrong place."
- **Was:** the wreckers' lamp-keeper.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, tall and thin, a great lantern held aloft.
- **Body and dress:** an oilskin coat soaked dark, a long thin body; **a lighthouse lens-lantern held on a pole**, its light a crackling lightning flame; a coil of rope and a wrecker's hook at the belt.
- **Head:** a gaunt face, hollow cheeks, eyes squinting from the glare.
- **Signature shape:** the raised lantern on its pole.
- **Colour:** dark oilskin, lamp yellow, slate.
- **Motion:** idle is swinging the lantern slowly as a signal. Attack is a flash of lightning from the lens.

#### Harpy Matron

- **Lore line:** "The mother of the storm harpies, crowned with a drowned queen's circlet."
- **Was:** the first storm harpy, or the sea-cliff's oldest bird.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, perched, wings half spread.
- **Body:** a large harpy with a woman's torso and face and an albatross's wings and taloned legs, feathers slate grey and charged; **she wears a tarnished gold circlet of the Drowned Kingdom**, the only gold on the coast.
- **Head:** a wild, wind-torn face, long grey hair.
- **Signature shape:** the spread wings and the circlet.
- **Colour:** slate feathers, drowned gold, white surf.
- **Motion:** idle is a screech and a wing shake. Attack is a diving talon strike.

#### The Gibbeted Wrecker

- **Lore line:** "Hanged in irons on the headland as a warning to the coast. The irons are the highest metal for miles."
- **Was:** a wrecker caught and hanged in an iron gibbet cage on the cliff top as a warning.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3 to the top of the gibbet arm: a wooden gibbet post planted in rock with an iron cage hanging from its arm. The wrecker hangs inside at head height, gripping the bars, legs dangling through the bottom hoop. The cage swings; the post leans into the wind.
- **Body:** a gaunt, wasted man. Lightning dominates: struck through the cage so often that **the bars are fused to his bones** at the wrists, the ribs and the jaw; his skin is scorched black in branching Lichtenberg trees and his hair stands in a stiff, singed brush. Water has swollen and whitened what flesh is left, wrinkled and pale from years of rain and spray.
- **Dress and gear:** **the gibbet cage**, iron hoops and straps shaped to a man, its padlocked hasp fused shut; the gibbet chain, links welded rigid; a tarred oak post studded with iron spikes against climbers; a wooden plaque nailed to the post with his crime lettered in rubric red; his wrecker's storm lantern still hanging from his belt; a ragged wool jumper; one sea boot; the frayed end of a noose round the neck; a dead gull caught in the bars; gull droppings crusting the cage top; a rusted spike driven through the cage's foot.
- **Head:** a skin-tight face, the jaw fused open against a bar, the eyes gone white.
- **Signature shape:** the post and arm with the man-shaped cage hanging from it.
- **Colour:** slate grey iron, lamp yellow lightning, a line of rubric red on the plaque.
- **Motion:** idle is the cage creaking round in the wind, the legs swinging. Attack is a strike on the gibbet that jumps from the cage to the heroes while his legs kick.

#### The Strand-Wife

- **Lore line:** "After every wreck she walked the tideline and took what the drowned no longer needed. Their rings will not come off."
- **Was:** a wrecker's widow who stripped the drowned on the beach after each wreck: rings, buttons, boots and coats.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, a tall, gaunt woman walking forward and leaning against a drag: she hauls a drowned sailor behind her by a boat hook through his collar. The other arm is held out, fingers spread and heavy. Weight forward.
- **Body:** gaunt and long-armed. Lightning dominates: **her right hand is a fused knot of rings**, dozens of the drowned's rings welded by a strike into one scorched, knuckled mass of metal, with branching scars running up the arm from it; her hair stands in a stiff grey fan. Water has soaked her, pale and wrinkled, the layers sagging, barnacles at her ankles.
- **Dress and gear:** layers of the dead's clothes, all soaked and hanging heavy: a sailor's striped shirt, a merchant's coat, an officer's waistcoat over both; a necklace of ship's buttons; an officer's hat jammed over her headscarf; a boat hook; a sack of salvage on her back with a boot sticking out; a short knife for fingers; a pair of boots tied by the laces round her neck; sea boots too big for her; **the corpse she drags**, a bloated sailor stripped to his shirt, hooked by the collar.
- **Head:** a thin, hard, wind-burned face, lips moving as she counts.
- **Signature shape:** the gaunt figure hauling the dragged corpse on the boat hook.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow glints in the fused rings.
- **Motion:** idle is going through the drowned man's pockets. Attack is a blow of the ringed fist that discharges into a hero, and she takes something from them.

### Cast

#### Wrecker

- **Lore line:** "It lights a lamp on the cliff and waits for the ships to come in."
- **Was:** a coast villager who lived by wrecking.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, crouched, lamp in one hand and a cudgel in the other.
- **Body and dress:** a soaked wool jumper, a knitted cap, sea boots; a storm lantern and a cudgel studded with ship's nails; Lichtenberg scars up one arm.
- **Head:** a weathered, bearded face, eyes watchful.
- **Signature shape:** the lantern held out and the cudgel.
- **Colour:** grey wool, lamp yellow.
- **Motion:** idle is waving the lantern. Attack is a cudgel blow.

#### Shock Eel

- **Lore line:** "It lives in the shallows where the wrecks are. It is why there are no survivors."
- **Was:** an eel of the shallows.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6 long, reared out of the water.
- **Body:** a thick, slate-grey eel, its flanks lined with pale electric organs that glow before a discharge.
- **Signature shape:** the reared S-curve.
- **Colour:** slate, lamp-yellow glow.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow coil. Attack is a discharge.

#### Storm Harpy

- **Lore line:** "It rides the storm down onto the wrecks."
- **Was:** a sea-bird of the cliffs.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, mid-flight.
- **Body:** a smaller harpy, ragged grey feathers, a woman's face with a beak-like nose, talons; static lifts its feathers.
- **Signature shape:** the wings raised in a dive.
- **Colour:** slate, white.
- **Motion:** idle is hovering. Attack is a raking swoop.

#### Tide Crab

- **Lore line:** "It picks the wrecks clean. You are a wreck."
- **Was:** a shore crab.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, wide.
- **Body:** a slate-grey crab with a shell made of a ship's lantern housing or a dented helmet, claws hung with wreck debris and rope.
- **Signature shape:** the scavenged-shell crab.
- **Colour:** slate, rust.
- **Motion:** idle is picking at debris. Attack is a claw pinch.

#### Drowned Bosun

- **Lore line:** "The wreckers' last catch. He signed on."
- **Was:** a ship's bosun drowned on the rocks, now crewing for the wreckers.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, a bosun's call raised to the lips.
- **Body and dress:** a drowned sailor in a bosun's coat with tarnished buttons, bloated and barnacled; a boarding axe; **the silver bosun's whistle on a chain**.
- **Head:** a bloated bearded face, a cocked hat with a wilted feather.
- **Signature shape:** the whistle raised and the boarding axe.
- **Colour:** navy coat gone grey, barnacle white.
- **Motion:** idle is a silent whistle. Attack is an axe blow; the whistle rallies the crew.

#### Lantern Cow

- **Lore line:** "They tied a lamp to her horns and walked her slowly along the cliff. Ships took her for a harbour."
- **Was:** a hobbled cow the wreckers walked along the cliff top at night with a lantern on her horns, to look like a ship's light riding at anchor.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3 at the shoulder, a heavy cow plodding with a slow nodding walk, head low, forelegs short-stepping in a hobble; the lantern on her horns swings.
- **Body:** a gaunt coast cow, ribs showing. Lightning dominates: the iron lantern drew strike after strike, so **the horns are scorched black and split**, Lichtenberg trees branch from their bases down the face, neck and shoulders, and the hair along the spine stands in a stiff ridge. Water has soaked the hide until it sags, swollen the udder and wrinkled the legs pale where the surf reaches; barnacles crust the hooves.
- **Dress and gear:** a storm lantern lashed between the horns with tarred rope, its glass cracked and its iron frame fused to the horns; a leather hobble chained between the forelegs; a rope halter with a trailing lead; a bell on a strap; a soaked sackcloth blanket over the back to hide her shape; a brand on the hip; a torn ear tag; kelp tangled in the tail.
- **Head:** long and heavy, eyes rolled white with fear, nostrils wet, the lantern burning above.
- **Signature shape:** the lantern blazing between the horns of a low-headed cow.
- **Colour:** slate grey hide, lamp yellow lantern, white surf.
- **Motion:** idle is the slow nod, the lantern swinging like a riding light. Attack is a lumbering butt, with a strike running down from the lantern through her into the front rank.

#### Ship-Breaker

- **Lore line:** "He broke every wreck down to timber and nails. He does not stop at the wreck."
- **Was:** a ship-breaker who stripped the wrecks on the rocks for timber, copper and iron.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5, a broad, heavy man walking with a ship's oak knee, a curved timber brace, over his right shoulder like a club, and an iron crowbar in his left hand. Weight heavy on the front foot.
- **Body:** huge and slab-muscled. Lightning dominates: struck while prying copper sheathing off a hull, he has **strips of copper and iron bolts fused into his forearms and shoulders**, the metal blackened, Lichtenberg ferns spreading across the chest from the welds, his beard singed stiff. Water has bloated his belly and hands, pale and wrinkled, with barnacles in the crooks of the elbows.
- **Dress and gear:** **the oak knee**, its iron bolts still through it; an iron crowbar; a leather apron with pockets full of salvaged nails; a waxed-canvas smock; a coil of rope over the other shoulder; a sack of torn copper sheathing on the back; a bow saw tied across the sack; a belt of salvage hooks; sea boots.
- **Head:** a big head under a wool cap, a broken nose, small eyes, the stiff singed beard.
- **Signature shape:** the curved oak knee over the shoulder.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow at the welds.
- **Motion:** idle is shaking water out of his beard. Attack is an overhead blow with the oak knee.

#### Storm Petrels

- **Lore line:** "Sailors call them the storm's chickens and pray not to see them. On this coast, nobody has to wait."
- **Was:** storm petrels, small sooty seabirds that ride in ahead of every gale.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a flock about 0.6 across, low over the water, their feet pattering on the surf as if walking on it.
- **Body:** dozens of small dark birds. Lightning has made **every feather stand on end**, so each bird is a ruffled, spiky ball, the wingtips scorched and frayed and a fern of burns across each white rump band. Water has soaked and swollen them, sea-bedraggled.
- **Dress and gear:** one bird trailing a broken wing; a snarl of fishing line caught round two of them; a scrap of torn sail tangled in the flock; flecks of foam; a bird carrying a ship's button in its beak; a dead one floating below.
- **Head:** small, hooked, tube-nosed bills, all open.
- **Signature shape:** the low, pattering flock with dangling feet.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, ink black.
- **Motion:** idle is a fluttering hover, feet pattering. Attack is the flock pouring over a hero, crackling.

#### Rock Octopus

- **Lore line:** "It walled its den with whatever the wrecks gave up. The lightning found the copper."
- **Was:** an octopus of the tide pools under the wreckers' cliff, which walled its den with bright salvage from the wrecks: nails, buttons, coins and fittings.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5, risen up off a wet rock on the curled tips of its arms, the swollen mantle held high, two front arms lifted and stiff, reaching toward the company. Weight spread through the curled arm tips.
- **Body:** a warty, soft-bodied octopus. Lightning dominates: the strike hit the pool where it hid among its hoard, so **the copper and brass it gathered are fused into the suckers of its two raised arms**, which have set rigid, studded with scorched metal and branching like antlers at the welds. Pale Lichtenberg trees burn across the mantle and down every arm, and the small papillae of its skin stand up in stiff points. Water has swollen it past its size: the mantle hangs heavy and sagging, the skin pale and wrinkled where the surf leaves it, barnacles crusting the bases of the arms.
- **Dress and gear:** none; the hoard does the dressing. Secondary details: copper sheathing nails fused along the raised arms; a ship's brass button set in one sucker; a bent copper coin; a brass hinge; a ring of tarred line wound round one arm; one arm bitten off short and regrowing as a stump; kelp trailing from the mantle; empty crab shells, its meals, heaped round the rock; a broken cork float caught in the arms.
- **Head:** the bulbous mantle rising behind two raised, bulging eyes with bar-shaped pupils, the hooked beak hidden below.
- **Signature shape:** the tall swollen mantle on a splay of curled arms, with the two rigid, metal-studded arms raised.
- **Colour:** slate grey flesh, white surf barnacles, lamp yellow scorch at the welds.
- **Motion:** idle is the arm tips curling and uncurling while the mantle swells and sags. Attack is a raised arm whipped round a hero; the strike runs down it and the hero is held in place.

#### Drowned Drummer

- **Lore line:** "He beat to quarters as she struck. He is beating still, and the crew still come."
- **Was:** the marine drummer of a warship wrecked on the coast.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, marching in place, a big rope-tensioned drum slung at his left hip, both sticks raised. Weight on one leg, mid-step.
- **Body:** a young marine. Lightning dominates: struck through the drum's metal hoop, **the hoop is fused to his hip**, burns ferning down the thigh and up the ribs, the sticks scorched into his fists, his hair standing under the helmet. Water has bloated him pale, with barnacles across the shoulders.
- **Dress and gear:** **the drum**, a wooden shell with a split head and its tension ropes fused stiff; a drum sling across the chest; a soaked padded jacket; a dented steel morion helmet with a comb; a short sword at the hip; spare sticks in the belt; canvas trousers; stockings fallen round the ankles; shoes with pewter buckles.
- **Head:** a bloated young face, eyes rolled up, mouth set, the morion's comb rising above.
- **Signature shape:** the big drum at the hip and the two raised sticks.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow scorch.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow, silent beat. Attack is a roll on the split drum that causes stress, with a crack of lightning on the last beat.

#### Cragsman

- **Lore line:** "He went over the cliff on a rope for the harpies' eggs. Neither he nor the rope came back up."
- **Was:** a cragsman who climbed down the sea cliffs on a rope to rob seabird nests for eggs and feathers.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, hanging from a rope that comes down from out of frame, feet braced against the air as if against a cliff, body angled out; one hand on the rope, the other reaching out with a fowling pole.
- **Body:** wiry and long-armed. Lightning dominates: the wet rope brought a strike down into him, so **the rope is fused into his hand and burned into his waist** where it is tied, with Lichtenberg trees branching from the waist across the belly and legs, and his hair standing in a soaked crest. Water has soaked him pale and wrinkled and swollen the fingers.
- **Dress and gear:** a hemp rope tied round the waist in a bowline over a padded sling; a fowling pole with a horsehair noose at the tip; an egg basket at the hip, the eggs cracked; a sack of feathers; a ripped wool jumper; a knife; bare, calloused feet; a harpy's grey feather in his cap.
- **Head:** a narrow face, teeth bared, a knitted cap with the feather.
- **Signature shape:** the figure hanging on a rope from above with the long pole out.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow scorch.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow swing on the rope, kicking off from nothing. Attack is a swing across the ranks on the rope, the pole lashing.

#### Drowned Merchant

- **Lore line:** "He would not let go of the strongbox when she struck. The lightning saw to it that he never will."
- **Was:** a merchant passenger on a wrecked ship, drowned clutching his strongbox.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, round and stooped, both arms wrapped round an iron-bound strongbox hugged to his belly, shuffling in small steps and leaning back against its weight.
- **Body:** lightning dominates: **the box's iron straps are fused into his forearms**, with burns branching from the welds across the chest and neck and the last of his hair standing in a soaked tuft. Water has bloated his face and belly hugely, pale and wrinkled, splitting the fine clothes at the seams, with barnacles on the shoulders and knuckles.
- **Dress and gear:** **an iron-bound oak strongbox** with a big padlock; a velvet gown trimmed with fur, soaked and sagging; a doublet split down the front; a chain of keys; silver rings sunk into swollen fingers; a merchant's seal on a cord; a purse; one shoe lost; ledger pages stuck to the box.
- **Head:** a bloated, jowled face, mouth pursed, small sunken eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the round figure hugging the strongbox.
- **Colour:** slate grey, white surf, lamp yellow scorch at the welds.
- **Motion:** idle is clutching the box tighter. Attack is a ram with the box that discharges into a hero.
