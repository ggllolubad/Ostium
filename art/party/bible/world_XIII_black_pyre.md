# Act V: The Hollow Heaven

## XIII. The Black Pyre (Fire + Void, Hollow Flame)

A cathedral of the inquisition where saints were burned. The fire took on void: it gives no light and it does not go out. **Fire** chars bodies to ash and cinder; **void** makes the flame black with violet edges and leaves holes where flesh has been unmade. This world mirrors the abbey: clergy, penitents and inquisitors. Eyes may appear (void world). **Palette:** black flame with violet edges, ash grey, a single gold halo.

### Rulers

#### The Saint Unending

- **Lore line:** "She was burned for heresy. She has been burning for nine hundred years, and she has not stopped praying."
- **Was:** a woman burned for one sermon: that heaven was a garden, and a woman kept it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, bound to a stake atop a pyre of black wood, hands still pressed together in prayer.
- **Body:** a slender charred figure, skin cracked to the bone in places, **wreathed in black flame with violet edges that casts no light**. Chains bind her to the stake. Her body never finishes burning.
- **Dress and gear:** the remains of a white penitent's shift, edges glowing violet. **The only gold in the world: a halo behind her head.** **The Tooth** hangs from the chain at her throat.
- **Head:** a calm face, eyes closed, lips moving in prayer; hair rising as smoke.
- **Signature shape:** the figure bound to the stake with a halo.
- **Colour:** ash grey, black flame, violet edge, gold halo.
- **Motion:** idle is the flames rising and her lips moving. Attack is the black fire spreading to a hero; it cannot be put out.

#### The Iron Bull

- **Lore line:** "The heretics were put inside to be heard. They are still being heard."
- **Was:** the inquisition's iron bull, a hollow engine of execution heated from beneath, and the heretics who died in it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 at the shoulder, a hollow iron bull standing four-square on its brick hearth, head lowered and turned toward the party. The hump runs out of the frame top and the hindquarters out of the frame right. Its weight sits on four iron legs, planted.
- **Body:** riveted plates of wrought iron, blackened and flaking; the heat has split the seams open in long cracks whose edges glow violet, with black flame showing inside. Ash curls upward off its back in slow flakes. The void has bitten **clean round holes** through both flanks, and in the dark behind them the faces of the condemned press against the iron from inside, eyes open.
- **Dress and gear:** a hatch in the flank with an iron bar and a padlock melted shut; the nostrils fitted with iron pipes that carried the screams out as bellowing; an iron ring through the nose with a length of chain fastened to the floor; a hearth of firebricks under the belly where black flame burns and gives no light; two bellows nozzles let into the hearth; iron horns, one snapped off short; a band of judgement round the neck lettered in red rubric, half burned away; a ladder leaning against the flank up to the hatch; rivets popped from the seams and lying in the ash; heaps of ash and charred bone around the hooves; a long poker leaning on the hearth; scorched iron rings on the floor where it was once chained.
- **Head:** a crude cast bull's head with a hinged jaw hanging open, empty eye sockets with black flame behind them, the nose ring swinging.
- **Signature shape:** the hollow iron bull standing on its hearth.
- **Colour:** blackened iron, ash grey, violet seam edges, red rubric on the neck band.
- **Motion:** idle is smoke curling from the nostril pipes and the iron ticking as it heats. Attack is a bellow: the pipes blast black fire, carrying every voice inside at once, and the heroes take Hollow Flame and stress together.

#### The Confessor

- **Lore line:** "Confess, and be forgiven. Then burn, to be certain."
- **Was:** the cathedral's confessor, who heard every heretic's last confession, and the great confessional he sat in.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a confessional the size of a chapel, black oak carved like a small cathedral in three bays: the confessor's curtained seat in the middle and a penitent's kneeler and grille on each side. Its pinnacles rise out of the frame top. The booth is rooted to the floor; the confessor inside is a seated giant who fills it like a man in a coffin.
- **Body:** behind a curtain burned to rags, the confessor sits huge and charred, black flame burning inside the booth and coming out through every hole in the carving. One charred hand rests out through the curtain on the armrest. The oak is split along the grain into violet-edged seams; the carved tracery chars and curls upward into ash. The void has opened clean round holes in the woodwork, and eyes look out of some of them. **Two penitents kneel at the side grilles**, charred to their kneelers, still whispering.
- **Dress and gear:** a violet stole, the confessor's colour, its fringe burned to stubs; a charred rope girdle; a rosary of burned beads hanging from the resting hand; pierced oak grilles with the penitents' fingers hooked through them; kneelers worn hollow; a list of sins nailed to the door in red rubric; a hanging lamp burning black flame; iron door hinges warped by heat; a poor-box slot in the base; a slate of penances chalked in a column; a bell-pull hanging from the canopy; heaps of ash on the steps; the penitents' caps and rosaries.
- **Head:** the confessor's head bowed in the shadow of the booth under a charred cowl, the lips burned away from the teeth, the jaw still moving in absolution, the eyes closed.
- **Signature shape:** the tall three-bayed booth with a charred hand resting out through the curtain.
- **Colour:** black oak, ash grey, violet stole and flame edges, red rubric.
- **Motion:** idle is the curtain stirring, the jaw murmuring and the penitents' heads nodding. Attack is the hand reaching out to draw a hero to the grille; the hero confesses, loses a buff, and catches Hollow Flame.

### Elites

#### The Unburnt Inquisitor

- **Lore line:** "He burned a thousand saints and never caught alight himself. He wonders why."
- **Was:** the cathedral's grand inquisitor.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, upright, a lit torch raised and a book held to the chest.
- **Body and dress:** heavy red-black inquisitor's robes untouched by fire, a tall mitre, a torch of black flame, a book of judgements, a chain of keys. Perfectly clean in a world of ash.
- **Head:** a severe, untouched face, eyes cold.
- **Signature shape:** the tall mitre and the raised torch.
- **Colour:** deep red-black robes, black flame, ash.
- **Motion:** idle is reading the book. Attack is a sentence read aloud; fire lands on a hero.

#### Pyre Seraph

- **Lore line:** "An angel sent to put out the pyre. It went in to look."
- **Was:** an angel.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.4, hovering, wings spread.
- **Body:** a tall angel whose six wings are burned to black skeletons edged in violet flame; the body is grey ash held together by will; a broken halo, its gold burned black (the Saint's halo stays the only gold here).
- **Head:** a beautiful face, half burned away to a void.
- **Signature shape:** the six burned wings.
- **Colour:** ash grey, violet flame, a broken gold halo.
- **Motion:** idle is slow wing beats shedding ash. Attack is a dive of black fire.

#### The Questioner

- **Lore line:** "He asks each question once. He has a great many irons in the fire."
- **Was:** the inquisition's questioner, who put heretics to the question in the undercroft.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, broad and heavy, standing square beside a brazier on an iron tripod, long tongs raised in one hand gripping a black-burning iron, the other forearm resting on the brazier's rim. Weight on the back leg, belly forward.
- **Body:** a huge, thick-necked man with bare arms. The fire has worked on him slowly: the skin of his forearms has split along the muscle into seams with violet edges, charred black at the lips of each split, and ash curls off them. His far hand is gone at the wrist, the stump a clean void rim; it rests on the brazier and does not burn.
- **Dress and gear:** a long leather apron, scorched stiff; a sackcloth shirt with the sleeves torn off; a leather skullcap; heavy boots wrapped in leather; **the brazier**, an iron bowl on a three-legged stand, with half a dozen irons standing in its black coals, their handles wrapped in rag; the tongs; a belt hung with pincers, a thumbscrew, a knotted cord and a small hammer; a leather bucket of water that has never been used; a scorched hood hanging down his back; a key on a thong round his neck; old burn scars over both shins.
- **Head:** bald and heavy, a burn-shiny jaw, no eyebrows, small patient eyes, a mouth set in professional boredom.
- **Signature shape:** the tripod brazier of irons and the raised tongs.
- **Colour:** scorched leather, ash grey, black flame with violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is turning an iron in the coals and lifting it to inspect it. Attack is pressing a black-burning iron to a hero; the Hollow Flame will not go out.

#### The Bellows Sister

- **Lore line:** "Nine hundred years at the bellows. The fire has not once burned low."
- **Was:** the lay sister who kept the pyre's fire fed.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, big-boned and stooped forward, working a pair of bellows as tall as she is, braced against her hip with a hand on each handle, the nozzle aimed at the party. Weight low in the hips, feet wide.
- **Body:** a broad woman with forearms like hams. Fire has crazed the skin of her face and hands into a net of violet-edged seams; her hands are charred black to the wrists where they grip the handles; ash lifts from her shoulders with every pump. A clean round hole passes through her side, and the bellows' draught blows through it.
- **Dress and gear:** a lay sister's grey habit kilted up through the belt, sleeves pinned back to the shoulder; a leather apron scorched through; a wimple charred down to its band, the veil long burned off; iron pattens; **the bellows**, oak boards with leather sides patched many times, nail-studded, with an iron nozzle; a poker and fire-iron at her belt; a bundle of tinder tied at her hip; a sack of charcoal slung on her back; a rosary of charred beads wound round one wrist; a burn scar across one cheek older than the rest.
- **Head:** a broad, square face, eyebrows burned off, lips pursed as if to blow, eyes narrowed against the heat.
- **Signature shape:** the huge bellows held at the hip.
- **Colour:** grey habit, scorched leather, black flame with violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow pump that puffs ash from the nozzle. Attack is a hard pump: black fire roars from the nozzle across the front rank, and every Hollow Flame on the field burns hotter.

### Cast

#### Ash Penitent

- **Lore line:** "It whips itself with the chain between swings at you. It does not notice the difference."
- **Was:** a flagellant.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, hunched, chain whip raised.
- **Body and dress:** a bare back flayed and charred, a hood of sackcloth, **a chain flagellum**, ash caked on the skin.
- **Signature shape:** the hunched hooded figure with the chain.
- **Colour:** ash grey, raw red, iron.
- **Motion:** idle is whipping itself. Attack is a chain lash.

#### Cinder Hound

- **Lore line:** "It runs on ash and smells the dying."
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.1 long, nose down.
- **Body:** a lean hound of grey cinders held together by black flame, embers in its eyes, its paws leaving ashprints.
- **Signature shape:** the lean nose-down hound.
- **Colour:** ash grey, violet embers.
- **Motion:** idle is sniffing. Attack is a burning bite on the weakest hero.

#### Censer Deacon

- **Lore line:** "Its censer burns something that was once a saint."
- **Was:** a deacon of the cathedral.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, swinging a censer.
- **Body and dress:** a charred dalmatic, **a heavy iron censer on a chain trailing black smoke**; through its grille, a small skull burns.
- **Signature shape:** the swinging censer.
- **Colour:** ash, black smoke, violet.
- **Motion:** idle is the censer swinging. Attack is a cloud of black incense.

#### Candle Wraith

- **Lore line:** "A skull in a black flame. Blow it out and it lights again."
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, floating.
- **Body:** a skull wreathed in black flame, wax dripping from the jaw like a candle, a small iron candle-ring crown.
- **Signature shape:** the burning skull.
- **Colour:** bone, black flame, violet.
- **Motion:** idle is flickering. Attack is a flare of black fire.

#### Pyre Knight

- **Lore line:** "He lit the pyre. He stands guard so it is never put out."
- **Was:** a knight of the inquisition.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, tower shield planted, mace ready.
- **Body and dress:** plate armour blackened by flame, a surcoat burned to tatters, **a torch fixed to the top of his shield** burning black, a heavy mace.
- **Head:** a great helm with a burned cross-shaped visor.
- **Signature shape:** the shield with its torch.
- **Colour:** black steel, ash, violet flame.
- **Motion:** idle is a stolid guard. Attack is a mace blow that sets Hollow Flame.

#### Recanter

- **Lore line:** "He recanted, and was spared the fire. He must wear the coat until he dies. He has not died."
- **Was:** a heretic who recanted and was sentenced to wear the penitent's coat and carry the candle.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0 (1.3 to the tip of the hat), shuffling, head bowed under a tall cone hat, a lit candle held before him in both hands. Weight on the back foot, as if afraid to arrive.
- **Body:** thin and barefoot, the skin charred in patches where the candle has dripped on him, split at the knuckles into violet-edged seams. Where the black wax lands it leaves clean round holes in the flesh, rimmed like a punch.
- **Dress and gear:** **a tall cone hat** of pasteboard painted with flames; a sackcloth tabard painted with a red saltire, the colour of doom, and black flames pointing down, the mark of the spared; the tabard's hem charred; a rope noose round the neck, its tail hanging; a fat black candle burning black; a placard of his sentence hung on a cord; a wooden rosary; knees scabbed from kneeling.
- **Head:** shaven, gaunt, eyes downcast, lips moving in the recantation.
- **Signature shape:** the tall cone hat over the bowed head and the candle.
- **Colour:** ash-grey sackcloth, red saltire, black flame with violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is a shuffle forward and back, the candle dripping. Attack is tipping the candle; black wax runs onto a hero and lights.

#### Belfry Bats

- **Lore line:** "The bell tower burned. The bats did not leave."
- **Was:** bats of the cathedral belfry.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a swirl about 0.6 across.
- **Body:** a dozen bats whose wings have burned to the finger bones, the membranes charred into lace with violet edges; bodies cinder grey, ash flaking off them as they fly; clean round holes punched through several wings; one bat with no head, the neck a clean rim, still flying.
- **Dress and gear:** fur smoking at the edges; a strand of bell rope tangled round one bat; a scrap of burned hymn page carried in a claw; one bat clinging to another's back; a fleck of black flame riding on a wing.
- **Head:** small pug-nosed faces, ears scorched to stubs, mouths open in a shriek.
- **Signature shape:** the swirl of lace-winged bats.
- **Colour:** ash grey, black, violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is a swirling loop that sheds ash. Attack is the swirl diving through the back rank; the bites spread Hollow Flame.

#### The Familiar

- **Lore line:** "The court found it guilty of being her familiar. It was burned beside her. It walked out of the ash first."
- **Was:** a black nanny goat, the pet of a woman condemned for heresy, tried by the inquisition as her familiar and burned on the same pyre.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6 reared, a small goat up on its hind legs with the forelegs folded and the head tucked down, horns forward, about to butt; a burnt tether rope trails from its neck to a stub of charred stake. Weight on the hind hooves.
- **Body:** a lean she-goat. Fire has burned the black hair off in patches and split the hide beneath into violet-edged seams along the ribs and haunches, charred at the lips of each split; the beard smoulders and ash curls up from it. The void has taken a clean round hole through the near ear and another through the flank, and the dark shows through them.
- **Dress and gear:** what the court put on it. Secondary details: a small placard of its sentence hung on a cord round the neck, lettered in red rubric and charred at the corners; the tether rope burned through, the stake stub dragging at its end; a leather collar with a little bell whose clapper has burned away; pyre straw caught on the horns; split, blackened hooves; the inquisition's black-flame brand on the haunch; an udder shrunk and scorched; horns ringed and flaking.
- **Head:** a narrow head with back-swept horns, the beard smouldering, the slit-pupilled eyes gone ash pale.
- **Signature shape:** the small reared goat with its horns lowered and the tether trailing.
- **Colour:** charred black, ash grey, violet seam edges; a line of red rubric on the placard.
- **Motion:** idle is a stamp and a bleat that shakes ash from the beard. Attack is a rearing butt that knocks a hero back a rank and sets Hollow Flame.

#### Faggot-Bearer

- **Lore line:** "Every pyre needs wood. He has carried enough for nine hundred years of them."
- **Was:** a lay brother who carried firewood to the pyres.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5, an immense lay brother bent under a towering stack of bundled sticks on a back-frame, both hands on the shoulder straps, legs braced. Weight forward.
- **Body:** enormous and thick-limbed. His back and shoulders are charred where the burning load rests on them, the skin split into violet-edged seams across the shoulder blades; ash curls off him constantly. A clean round hole passes through his near calf, and he walks on it anyway.
- **Dress and gear:** a short lay habit of sackcloth, kilted up; a rope belt; a leather pad over the shoulders; **the back-frame**, stacked with tied bundles of black wood higher than his head, the topmost bundles burning black; a hatchet in the belt; a coil of willow binding; wooden clogs; sacking wrapped round the legs; a cord with a knot tied for every pyre, long enough to drag.
- **Head:** a small head pushed forward under the load, a tonsure burned to stubble, a dull, patient face.
- **Signature shape:** the towering stack of bundles on the bent back.
- **Colour:** sackcloth grey, black wood, violet flame edges.
- **Motion:** idle is hitching the load higher, ash falling. Attack is tipping the burning top bundles onto a hero.

#### The Scold

- **Lore line:** "He cried out in the nave that the saint was innocent. They gave him a mask with an iron tongue. He is still trying to say it."
- **Was:** a lay brother who spoke against the burning in the nave, sentenced to wear the shame mask and the neck-fiddle and to walk the cathedral before every pyre.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, shuffling forward stooped from the upper back, the head under a long-snouted iron shame mask, the neck and both wrists locked in a heavy wooden fiddle board held out level at chin height, the hands sticking up through its holes. Weight on the front foot, as if still being led.
- **Body:** thin and bare-armed. Fire has charred his hands and forearms where they stick out of the board, the skin split into violet-edged seams, ash curling off the knuckles; the mask's iron tongue-plate has burned into his mouth, and black flame leaks between the lip-bars whenever he tries to speak. The void has taken a clean round hole through his near shoulder, and the dark shows through it.
- **Dress and gear:** **the shame mask**, sheet iron hammered into a mocking face with a long hooked snout, tin ears and spectacles beaten into the brow, hinged at the jaw and padlocked at the nape; **the neck-fiddle**, a board of black oak with three holes for neck and wrists, iron-banded and padlocked at one end. Secondary details: a little bell hanging from the snout's tip; a placard of his offence nailed to the board in red rubric, charred at the corners; a sackcloth shift scorched at the hem; a rope girdle; a wooden rosary tangled round one wrist hole; bare feet blistered from the nave floor; the tonsure scorched under the mask's strap; ash caked in the board's holes.
- **Head:** the iron face, snout and tin ears over a slot mouth, black flame leaking from it; behind the eye holes, his own eyes, red-rimmed and wide.
- **Signature shape:** the long-snouted, long-eared iron mask above the board held out at chin height.
- **Colour:** black iron, black oak, ash grey, violet edges; red rubric on the placard.
- **Motion:** idle is a muffled straining at the gag while the bell on the snout tinkles. Attack is a choked cry through the mask: black flame bursts from the mouth slot over a hero (Hollow Flame, stress).

#### Ash Pardoner

- **Lore line:** "A pinch of saint's ash, sealed in lead, for every sin. He has sold her many times over."
- **Was:** a pardoner who sold the burned saints' ashes to pilgrims on the cathedral steps.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, stooped and wheedling, a tall pole in one hand hung with lead vials and sealed pardons, a tray at his belly, the other hand held out palm up. Weight on the front foot, leaning in to sell.
- **Body:** a sly, thin man. The ash has eaten into his fingers: the held-out hand is split and smouldering black at the tips with violet edges. One cheek has a clean round hole through it, showing the teeth.
- **Dress and gear:** a hooded coat of patched grey wool, the hems charred; **a pole with a crosspiece** hung with clinking lead ampullae and pardons sealed in grey wax; a neck tray of little lead vials and a horn scoop; a fat purse; a forged bull on a cord; a fake finger-bone relic in a little box; a pilgrim's broad hat hanging at his back.
- **Head:** a narrow, ratty face, a wide wheedling smile, a broken front tooth.
- **Signature shape:** the pole hung with lead vials and scrolls.
- **Colour:** grey wool, dull lead, black flame with violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is shaking the pole so the vials clink, the hand held out. Attack is a pinch of saint's ash flung in a hero's face, where it burns black.

#### Charred Destrier

- **Lore line:** "It carried the knights to the pyre. It still knows the way."
- **Was:** a warhorse of the inquisition's knights.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.7 at the head, a great warhorse standing riderless, head high and turned toward the party, one forehoof pawing. Weight back on the haunches.
- **Body:** a heavy destrier, its hide burned away in patches to charred black muscle, split along the neck and flanks into violet-edged seams; the mane burned short and smoking, ash curling up from it. A clean round hole passes through the base of the neck.
- **Dress and gear:** a caparison of ash-grey cloth burned to tatters, embroidered with the inquisition's black flame; a chanfron of blackened steel with a spike; a high-cantled war saddle; **the rider's legs still in the stirrups**, cut off clean at the hip by the void; reins trailing; an iron bit; a crupper strap; an empty scabbard hanging from the saddle; iron shoes.
- **Head:** a long head inside the chanfron, nostrils flared and smoking black, violet embers in the eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the tall horse with the empty saddle and the rider's legs.
- **Colour:** charred black, ash-grey caparison, violet edges.
- **Motion:** idle is a stamp and a head toss that sheds ash. Attack is rearing and striking down with both forehooves.
