## The Vessel: the secret seventh calling

Source: the Claude Doc "Ostium: The Lore", section "The Vessel: the secret seventh calling", and LORE_CHANGES_2026-11-23.md item 3. Hero tier. Rendered in Living Ink, rigged from the start.

The Vessel is the Prismatic Elementalist who took the Mother of Courses' place in the hidden ending, come back to the gate. It holds all six courses in one body without letting them fuse, as she once did. It is the only hero who carries courses as a resource instead of suffering them. It is the same person under the same name; the Hosteller writes it in the guest book again. It unlocks by winning the Vessel ending, after which the Prismatic leaves the roster for good and the Vessel waits at the gate on the next visit to the Guesthouse.

### The Vessel

- **Lore line:** none in the game data yet. (Fiction from the lore doc: "Their skin carries the six inks in bands from the fingertips up, and their eyes hold no colour. They sleep standing. Courses that would burn, freeze or rot anyone else settle on them and wait.")
- **Was:** the Prismatic Elementalist, a scholar of the Six Courses, after holding all six courses in one body.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, **6.4 heads**, faces right like every hero. **Calm and upright: the only hero who is not hunched.** Feet together, shoulders level and down, arms loose at the sides with the palms half open and turned slightly forward. Weight even on both feet; the centre of mass sits straight over the heels. Nothing in the pose braces against anything.
- **Body:** a thin scholar's body, unhurt. **The six inks run in bands up the skin from the fingertips**, in order from the fingertips upward: vermilion, pale blue, gold, deep blue, sick green, violet-black. The bands are as clean-edged as a scribe's rule lines, wrap the fingers, hands and forearms, and climb the arms, throat and jaw where the skin shows. The ink is in the skin, not on it: no flaking, no smear. **Course stacks sit visibly on the skin and do not hurt it**: a stack is drawn as that course's own mark resting on the surface (a bead of fire on a vermilion band, a skin of rime on a pale blue band, a branching spark on a gold band, a bead of water on a deep blue band, a ripe swelling on a sick green band, a clean-rimmed hole of void on a violet-black band), each one sitting still and contained, with no wound, scar, burn or blister around it. The skin under the marks is whole.
- **Dress and gear:** **plain undyed robes worn thin**: an undyed wool robe over an undyed linen undertunic, the wool worn so thin at the shoulders, elbows and knees that the weave and the inked skin show through. No course sigils, no embroidery, no halo pattern (the Prismatic's sigils are gone). A plain rope belt. Bare feet. Per the company rule, the lead ostium badge on a cord at the throat and a strip of red rubric cloth tied at one wrist. Secondary details (at least 12): darned patches at both elbows; a frayed, uneven hem, longer on one side; the undertunic's cuff showing below one sleeve only; the weave worn to threads over the shoulder blades; the rope belt knotted off-centre with one long tail; the badge cord knotted where it once broke; the rubric strip faded and fraying; a scribe's callus on the second finger of the right hand; a pale scar ring on one finger where a ring was once worn; old ink under the fingernails; dust from the road rising on the robe's hem; a single loose thread at the collar.
- **Head:** close-cropped hair, a smooth, calm face, the jaw unclenched. **The eyes have no colour**: irises and pupils are drawn as the white of the eye, rimmed with a fine ink line only, so the gaze still reads as level and forward. The ink bands reach up the throat to the jaw and stop there; the face itself is bare.
- **Signature shape:** the straight, upright figure with open hands, read against a company of hunched heroes.
- **Colour:** undyed wool and linen, the six course inks in bands (vermilion, pale blue, gold, deep blue, sick green, violet-black), colourless eyes.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow, even breath; on the in-breath the course stacks on the skin brighten slightly, then settle. Attack is an open hand lifted toward the foe and the course leaving the skin and crossing to them; the stance does not change.
- **Paths (chosen at level 4):** Hearth, Mouth and Whole. Path visual variants are not designed yet (open question).

### Review notes

- Eyes: the colourless eyes are a lore trait, not an eye motif; the Vessel carries no extra or misplaced eyes.
- Gold: the gold band is a course ink (lightning), not holy gold leaf.

### Director note 2026-11-23 (Cole: "more holy and significant, not like a lowly peasant with some special magic")

This note overrides the entry above where they conflict. The lore fixes stand: undyed cloth, the six inks in the order above, colourless eyes, upright, 6.4 heads, faces right, the company badge and rubric strip.

- **Read:** a living icon, not a poor pilgrim. The figure is the most significant in the company at a glance.
- **Robe:** still undyed, but a vestment. It has a full-length robe, long wide sleeves, a cowl or hood lying back over the shoulders, and a long scapular down the front. It falls in heavy, sculptural, vertical folds, like a carved saint. "Worn thin" shows only as a faint translucency and softness at the hem and sleeve edges.
  - Replaces: the darned elbows, frayed hem, threadbare holes, patches and road dust.
  - The rope belt becomes a plain cord, knotted once, with long even tails.
- **Inks as illumination:** translucent deep-pigment dyes written into the skin like an illuminated manuscript. Each rule line is edged with a hairline of gold leaf; faint script runs within the bands at 1x. The course stacks sit on their bands like seals.
  - Gold is permitted: the Vessel is holy and took the Mother's place.
- **Nimbus:** behind the head, a faint, broken six-part ink ring, one arc per course ink, gold-edged, on its own bone so the engine can pulse it. It echoes the Shattered God's six-rayed nimbus. No glow, halo light or rays.
  - Supersedes the "no halo pattern" line above.
- **Pose:** hieratic stillness, almost frontal at three-quarter, weight even. The head is level with the chin a touch raised, the gaze calm and forward. Both hands are open at waist height with the palms turned out (orans), so the inked palms face the viewer. The silhouette is a tall, still, near-symmetric column with the long sleeves hanging.
- **Face:** serene, ageless and unweathered, with clean planes and a calm mouth. The head is cropped close like a tonsure. A faint warm-cool light rises into the face from the inks below and in front.
- **Feet:** bare, clean and planted.
- **Rest, garth and portrait:** all three get the same treatment.
  - The rest pose stays asleep and standing.
  - The garth pose has one hand on the breast.
  - In the portrait the nimbus frames the head and one raised inked palm faces the viewer.
