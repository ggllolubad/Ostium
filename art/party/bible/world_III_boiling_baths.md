## III. The Boiling Baths (Fire + Water, Scald)

A temple of healing waters where the nobility came to be cleansed. The water never stopped heating, and cleansing turned into cooking. **Fire** cooks bodies from outside in: skin boiled pink and white, peeling in sheets, blistered. **Water** keeps everything wet, swollen and steaming. The cast are bath attendants, guards, bathers and what lived in the pools. **Palette:** pink-white marble, steam white, boiled red.

### Rulers

#### The Simmering Matron

- **Lore line:** "The matron of the baths. She has been simmering for a hundred years and is nearly ready."
- **Was:** the temple's high priestess of the waters, who never left the great pool.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, seated in the great pool to the chest, enthroned in steam, arms resting on the marble rim.
- **Body:** vast and soft, skin boiled pink-white and translucent like poached meat, sloughing in sheets into the water. Her hands are enormous and wrinkled from a century of soaking.
- **Dress and gear:** a priestess's linen veil plastered to her head, gold temple jewellery sunk into the swollen flesh of her arms. **The Tooth** hangs from her neck on a chain as a pendant.
- **Head:** a placid, heavy-lidded face, lips cracked, wet hair under the veil; she looks contented.
- **Signature shape:** the huge seated figure in a rising column of steam.
- **Colour:** boiled pink-white, marble, a little gold.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow bob in the pool, steam pouring off her. Attack is a wave of scalding water swept from the pool.

#### The Best Families

- **Lore line:** "The seventh pool was kept for the best families. They have climbed out to complain about the water."
- **Was:** the noble families who held the private seventh pool, cooked together where they sat when the water rose to the boil.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a heap of fused bathers spilling over the marble lip of their pool onto the floor tiles, the mass hauling itself forward on many arms. The back of the heap and the pool are cut off by the frame's right edge and top. Centre of mass low and forward; it drags.
- **Body:** twenty or more nobles cooked into one soft mass. Fire dominates: the skin is boiled pink-white and shining, peeling in long sheets, blistered at every fold, and where bodies touch the flesh has run together like meat poached in one pot. Water keeps the whole heap swollen, wrinkled and steaming. Arms reach out on every side, several still holding something; faces turn toward each other mid-conversation. Highest on the heap is the eldest, a bald patriarch, his head and shoulders free of the rest.
- **Dress and gear:** wet linen bath sheets plastered over shoulders, embroidered with the families' badges; bone hairpins in collapsed coiffures; pearl collars sunk into boiled necks; silver signet rings on swollen fingers; a wine cup held up by one hand; a cracked bronze strigil in another; a lady's feather fan wilted to a bare stalk; an empty lapdog's collar hanging from a wrist; a soaked cushion; a cracked oil flask; a pair of wooden bath clogs embedded in the base; a towel boy's arm caught in the mass, still holding out a towel; the pool token, a bronze disc stamped VII, on a cord round the patriarch's neck; a stretch of the marble pool rim fused into the underside.
- **Head:** no single head. The patriarch on top wears a wreath of bay leaves cooked grey and slipping over one ear, his mouth open in complaint; the faces around him are flushed, placid and dozing.
- **Signature shape:** the sprawling mound bristling with reaching arms, the patriarch's head on top.
- **Colour:** boiled pink-white flesh, steam white linen, boiled red at the blisters.
- **Motion:** idle is the heap shifting and murmuring, arms patting each other, steam rising. Attack is the heap surging forward: a dozen arms take hold of a hero and pull them into the steam.

#### The Great Copper

- **Lore line:** "The boiler that heats every pool in the temple. Its stokers are still on shift."
- **Was:** the temple's great copper boiler over the furnace, and the stokers who fed it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a riveted copper boiler three storeys high squatting on a brick firebox and leaning forward over the company like a fat body; its dome and chimney are cut off by the frame top and its pipes run out of frame to the pools. It never moves; its whole weight sits on the brick.
- **Body:** fire is the dominant mark: the copper plates are heat-blackened at the base and cooked a boiled red-brown above, blistered where the metal has bubbled, and split along two seams that glow from the fire inside. **The stokers are cooked onto the firebox**: four men pressed half into the hot brick face around the fire door, their skin boiled pink-white and baked onto the brick like bread to an oven wall, their arms still working long iron rakes in and out of the fire. Water swells the copper between the rivet rows into a bulging belly that weeps hot water from every seam, steam pouring from the cracks, and lime scale crusts bone white around every leak.
- **Dress and gear:** an iron fire door hanging open on one hinge, its latch broken; two iron fire-rakes and a coal shovel held by the fused stokers; a pressure valve on top with a weighted lever, the weight a lump of marble; a big bronze stopcock wheel on the side; lead pipes to the pools, bandaged with rags where they leak; a carved stone tablet set in the brick with the stokers' rota; a stoker's leather apron hanging from a rivet; a burst sack of coal heaped at the base; a cracked glass water gauge; an iron ladder bolted up the side, an attendant cooked onto its rungs with a bucket; a chalk tally on the brick; a dead Kettle Crab cooked on the warm brick ledge.
- **Head:** none. The fire door is its mouth; the stokers' faces around it are pink and shining, eyes squeezed shut against the heat.
- **Signature shape:** the bulging boiler over the open fire door.
- **Colour:** boiled red copper, steam white, pink-white stoker flesh.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow swelling and settling of the plates and a hiss from the valve. Attack is the valve lifting: scalding steam rolls across the ranks, and the stokers rake burning coal out of the door at the front rank.

### Elites

#### The Bath-Mistress

- **Lore line:** "She keeps the water hot and the guests clean."
- **Was:** the matron's steward, mistress of the attendants.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, upright and severe, a ladle held like a mace.
- **Body and dress:** a stiff steaming linen gown, sleeves rolled, forearms boiled red to the elbow; a ring of bath-house keys; a long-handled copper ladle and a scrubbing brush on a chain.
- **Head:** hair scraped back under a cap, a hard face, permanently flushed.
- **Signature shape:** the long copper ladle.
- **Colour:** white linen, boiled red, copper.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping the ladle in her palm. Attack is a ladleful of boiling water.

#### Cauldron Bearer

- **Lore line:** "It carries the boiling water from the furnace to the pools. It carries other things, too."
- **Was:** a furnace porter.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.2, bent under a yoke, legs braced.
- **Body:** a giant porter with skin boiled red and shiny, scars from a century of splashes; **a wooden yoke across the shoulders with a copper cauldron hanging at each end**, both steaming, one with a limb hanging over the rim.
- **Head:** a bald, sweating head, eyes squeezed half shut from steam.
- **Signature shape:** the yoke and the two hanging cauldrons.
- **Colour:** boiled red skin, copper, steam white.
- **Motion:** idle is the cauldrons swaying. Attack is swinging a cauldron and emptying it.

#### The Strigil-Master

- **Lore line:** "He scraped the nobility clean with a bronze blade. He has not stopped at the dirt."
- **Was:** the temple's chief masseur, who oiled the bathers and scraped them clean with a strigil.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.1, a heavy-shouldered man leaning forward over an unseen massage slab, both great hands forward, the strigil raised in the right. Knees bent, weight on the front foot, like a man about to lean into his work.
- **Body:** a huge bald man, chest and arms boiled pink-white and peeling. Fire has pushed his hands hard: they are cooked and swollen to twice their size, and **the skin of both hands has come away whole and hangs from his wrists like a pair of empty gloves**, leaving raw, boiled red hands beneath. Water keeps the folds of his belly swollen, wrinkled and steaming; oil and sweat run down him.
- **Dress and gear:** a linen loincloth under a wide leather belt and a short leather apron dark with oil; **a long curved bronze strigil**, an edgeless scraping blade shaped like a sickle, with curls of scraped skin on its edge; a second, broken strigil tucked in the belt; a bronze oil flask on a cord; a sponge in a net at the hip; leather wristbands sunk into the swollen wrists; a marble temple token on a thong; a towel over one shoulder; burst blisters down the shins; wooden bath clogs, one strap broken; a bronze ring in one ear.
- **Head:** a shaved head with a sweat rag tied round it, a heavy, patient face, eyes half shut in the steam, a split lower lip.
- **Signature shape:** the raised curved strigil and the empty skin-gloves hanging at the wrists.
- **Colour:** boiled red hands, pink-white skin, steam white linen.
- **Motion:** idle is drawing the strigil slowly down his own forearm, shedding a curl of skin. Attack is a long raking scrape that peels away a hero's protection.

#### The Invalid Countess

- **Lore line:** "She was carried here to be cured. Her bearers were told not to put her down."
- **Was:** a sick noblewoman carried to the temple in a litter, with the two bearers who carried her.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0 tall and 2.5 long, filling two ranks: a curtained litter on two long poles, one bearer at the front and one at the back, both bent at the knee under the load. The countess sits upright inside, leaning out over the front pole.
- **Body:** water has swollen the countess into a soft, wrinkled bulk that fills the litter, and fire has boiled her pink-white, peeling at the face and forearms in sheets. **The bearers' hands are cooked onto the poles**: palm skin wrapped round the wood like bark. They are lean, boiled red, with steam rising off their shoulders.
- **Dress and gear:** the litter is an oak box with carved panels and wet linen curtains tied back with cord, stained with tide lines of scale; two long poles with leather shoulder straps biting into the bearers; a cushion boiled shapeless; her bed jacket of soaked velvet over a nightgown; a pearl collar sunk into her neck; a medicine bottle on a cord; a tasselled hand-bell for summoning the bearers; a clay flask of temple water; a lap rug; an empty lapdog's basket hanging from the front pole; the bearers in livery tabards with the family badge, short breeches and bare scalded feet; one bearer's head bandaged.
- **Head:** the countess has a long, noble face under a lace nightcap soaked flat, heavy lids and a pursed, turned-down mouth. The bearers keep their heads bowed, bald patches boiled shiny.
- **Signature shape:** the curtained box on two poles between two bent bearers.
- **Colour:** boiled red bearers, pink-white countess, steam white curtains.
- **Motion:** idle is the countess ringing her hand-bell while the bearers shift under the weight. Attack is the bearers ramming the front pole into a hero; the countess throws a bottle of boiling water.

### Cast

#### Scalded Attendant

- **Lore line:** "It still brings towels. Its skin comes away on them."
- **Was:** a bath attendant.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, bowing slightly, offering a folded towel.
- **Body and dress:** a thin linen tunic, skin boiled white and peeling in long strips; **the towel in its hands is stained with skin**.
- **Head:** a polite servant's face, half the skin gone from one cheek.
- **Signature shape:** the bowing figure holding out the towel.
- **Colour:** linen white, raw pink.
- **Motion:** idle is a polite bow. Attack is a towel snapped like a whip, wet and scalding.

#### Kettle Crab

- **Lore line:** "Cooked alive in the pools and somehow still walking."
- **Was:** a crab from the temple's ornamental pools.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5 tall, wide.
- **Body:** a crab cooked bright red, shell cracked, steam venting from the cracks like a kettle spout.
- **Signature shape:** the low red shell with steam plumes.
- **Colour:** boiled red, steam white.
- **Motion:** idle is steam puffing. Attack is a claw pinch.

#### Boiled Brute

- **Lore line:** "A bath guard who stayed in the water too long. He is very soft now, and very strong."
- **Was:** a temple bath guard.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6, soft and sagging but huge.
- **Body and dress:** a body boiled until the flesh is soft and sagging like wet bread, folds hanging over a guard's leather belt; a guard's bronze collar sunk into the neck; a heavy club of temple marble.
- **Head:** a slack, melting face, small eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the sagging mass and the marble club.
- **Colour:** boiled pink-white, bronze.
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy wobble. Attack is a slow, enormous club swing.

#### Steam Nymph

- **Lore line:** "It drifts over the pools and sings to the drowned."
- **Was:** a bather, or the bathers' memory of one.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, floating above the floor, feet trailing into vapour.
- **Body:** a woman's shape made of steam, thicker at the face and hands, dissolving below the waist; a bathing veil the only solid thing.
- **Head:** a calm singing face, eyes closed.
- **Signature shape:** the floating form that fades out below the hips.
- **Colour:** steam white, a faint pink glow at the core.
- **Motion:** idle is drifting. Attack is a song and a scalding breath.

#### Bath Leech

- **Lore line:** "Grown fat on the bathers."
- **Was:** a leech of the pools.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.7 long, coiled.
- **Body:** a pale pink leech the size of a dog, swollen with blood, a ring-toothed mouth at one end; it rears up to strike.
- **Signature shape:** the reared, swollen body.
- **Colour:** fleshy pink, dark mouth.
- **Motion:** idle is a pulse along the body. Attack is a lunge and latch.

#### Hypocaust Stoker

- **Lore line:** "He kept the fires under the floors. He has not come up for air in a hundred years."
- **Was:** a stoker of the hypocaust, the low brick crawlspace under the temple floors where the hot air ran.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, in a permanent deep crouch from working under the floors, knees bent, back flat, a long iron fire-hook held out ahead in both hands. Weight low.
- **Body:** short and wiry. Fire has cooked **his back** under a century of hot floor tiles into a stiff, boiled pink-white shell, split in deep cracks along the spine and shoulder blades and peeling at the edges. Water from the dripping tiles has swollen and wrinkled his arms and legs.
- **Dress and gear:** a long iron fire-hook with a crook at the tip; a scorched leather cap with a neck flap; a loincloth under a short leather apron; rag wraps round the knees, worn through from crawling; an iron ash pan dragged on a short chain; a water skin; a wet rag tied over his nose and mouth; a broken floor tile stuck to his back.
- **Head:** a squinting face greyed with ash, the lashes and brows burnt away, the rag over his mouth.
- **Signature shape:** the low, flat-backed crouch behind the long hook.
- **Colour:** boiled red cracks, pink-white skin, ink black soot.
- **Motion:** idle is raking at nothing along the floor. Attack is a hooked pull that drags a hero forward and burns.

#### Votive Pilgrim

- **Lore line:** "She hung a clay leg at the spring and asked to walk again. She is walking."
- **Was:** a lame pilgrim who came to the healing waters with a votive offering.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, leaning hard on a crutch under the right arm, the left leg held off the ground, a cord of votive limbs swinging from the crutch.
- **Body:** a thin, poor woman. **Her lame leg was dipped in the waters until it cooked**: fire has boiled it pink-white and peeling in sheets from hip to foot, and water has swollen it to twice the width of the other leg, soft, wrinkled and steaming.
- **Dress and gear:** a wooden crutch padded with rag at the armpit; **a cord of clay and wax votives** hanging from it: a little leg, a hand, a foot, a swaddled baby; a pilgrim's cloak of undyed wool; a lead badge of the temple pinned at the shoulder; a clay ampulla of temple water on a cord at her neck; a wooden begging bowl at the belt; a rosary of knotted cord; a rag shoe on one foot only; an old bandage unwound and trailing from the swollen leg.
- **Head:** a gaunt face, wet hair under a headscarf, the look turned upward and hopeful.
- **Signature shape:** the crutch with its hanging cord of votive limbs.
- **Colour:** pink-white, steam white, bone clay.
- **Motion:** idle is a hop and a hopeful touch of the ampulla. Attack is a swing of the crutch, votives rattling; her other action splashes temple water on an ally.

#### Physic of the Waters

- **Lore line:** "He prescribed the waters for every complaint. He takes them himself now, hourly."
- **Was:** the temple's physician, who examined the nobility and prescribed their soaks.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, upright and pompous, one arm raised high holding a glass flask to the light, the other hand behind his back. Weight back on his heels.
- **Body:** a portly man. Fire has boiled his face and hands bright pink-white and set his scalp peeling in sheets; water has swollen his lips and eyelids and left his fingers soft and wrinkled like a bather's after an hour. Steam rises from his collar.
- **Dress and gear:** a short physician's gown, open, over a buttoned doublet and wet hose; **a glass urine flask** held up, its contents boiling; the flask's wicker carrying basket at his belt; a wax tablet and stylus; a leather case of lancets; a sand-glass on a cord for timing soaks; spectacles fogged white; a bronze physician's ring; a sponge; a list of prescriptions pinned to the gown, the ink running.
- **Head:** a fleshy, satisfied face, fogged spectacles on the nose, a velvet cap stuck flat to the head.
- **Signature shape:** the flask raised high in one hand.
- **Colour:** pink-white, steam white, boiled red.
- **Motion:** idle is squinting at the flask and nodding. Attack is flinging the boiling flask at a hero.

#### Lustral Priest

- **Lore line:** "He blesses every bather with the holy water. It is still holy, and it is boiling."
- **Was:** a priest of the temple who sprinkled bathers with consecrated water as they entered the pools.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, striding, the right arm flung out to the side mid-sprinkle, a bucket hanging from the left hand. Weight on the front foot.
- **Body:** a lean, bare-chested priest. Fire has blistered **the sprinkling arm and the chest**, where the water splashes back, until the blisters burst and peeled in sheets, the arm cooked red to the shoulder; water has swollen his hands and feet. Steam lifts off his shoulders.
- **Dress and gear:** a wet linen kilt to the knee, belted with cord; a linen stole embroidered with waves in gold thread (holy); **an aspergillum**, a gilded bronze rod with a pierced ball head (holy); a bronze bucket of steaming holy water; prayer beads wound round the left wrist; a small marble prayer tablet hanging at the belt; leather sandals with the soles curling; a temple seal ring; a burn bandage round the right forearm.
- **Head:** a shaved head, a sharp ascetic face, eyes squeezed shut in devotion, mouth open in a chant.
- **Signature shape:** the flung arm with the aspergillum, and the hanging bucket.
- **Colour:** pink-white, boiled red, a little gold on the aspergillum and stole.
- **Motion:** idle is dipping the aspergillum and shaking it out. Attack is a sweeping sprinkle that scalds a whole rank.

#### Lapdog

- **Lore line:** "It came in with a countess. It bites everyone but her."
- **Was:** a noblewoman's lapdog that followed her into the pools.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.35, a little dog braced stiff-legged, head lowered, barking.
- **Body:** fire has boiled it hairless: the skin pink-white, wrinkled and flaking, blistered along the back, with the last tufts of fur clumped wet on the ears and tail. Water has swollen it round and soft, the belly sagging nearly to the floor on short legs, steam rising off its back.
- **Dress and gear:** a velvet collar soaked dark with a small silver bell; a limp silk bow on the topknot; a tiny embroidered coat buttoned round the swollen belly and split at the seams; an engraved name disc; overgrown claws; a broken lead trailing; a missing front tooth.
- **Head:** a squashed face, small wet eyes deep in wrinkles, lips peeled back from tiny teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the round little body in its split coat, braced to bark.
- **Colour:** pink-white, boiled red blisters, steam white.
- **Motion:** idle is a shiver and a growl. Attack is a snapping bite at the ankles.

#### Hypocaust Roaches

- **Lore line:** "They lived in the warm under the floors. It is very warm now."
- **Was:** the cockroaches that bred in the warm brick of the hypocaust under the pools.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a heap about 0.4 high, pouring up out of a drain grate lifted on its edge in the marble floor.
- **Body:** hundreds of cockroaches cooked boiled red like shrimp, the shells cracked and split, legs curled, still moving. Water has swollen their bodies soft and glossy, and steam rises off the heap.
- **Dress and gear:** a bronze drain grate cast with a lion's mask, tipped up on the tide of them; broken marble tiles around the hole; a lost hairpin and a lost ring carried on the heap; a sodden sponge; a scrap of towel; a lost sandal; dead roaches upturned round the edge.
- **Head:** none.
- **Signature shape:** the tipped grate on a pouring heap.
- **Colour:** boiled red, pink-white marble, steam white.
- **Motion:** idle is the heap seething and the grate rattling. Attack is the heap flooding over a hero's feet and up the legs.

#### Pump Mule

- **Lore line:** "It turned the wheel that lifted the water. Nobody told it to stop."
- **Was:** the mule that walked in circles turning the wheel that raised spring water to the cisterns.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4 at the head, a mule leaning into its collar, head down, dragging; weight thrown forward.
- **Body:** fire has boiled its hide hairless and pink-white, peeling in sheets along the flanks where the traces rub, the mane cooked into a stiff ridge. Water swells the legs and belly; the hooves are soft and split. Ribs show under the soft skin, and steam rises off the back.
- **Dress and gear:** a padded leather collar boiled hard and cracked; a bridle with blinkers; rope and leather traces running back to **a broken length of the wheel's oak sweep-beam**, still hooked behind it; a bronze bell on the collar; a holed sackcloth blanket on the back; a temple brand on the hip; one hind shoe missing; a broken chain hobble on one fetlock.
- **Head:** a long bony head held low, ears flat, lips peeled back from long teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the leaning mule dragging the broken sweep-beam.
- **Colour:** pink-white hide, boiled red sores, steam white.
- **Motion:** idle is plodding on the spot, the beam scraping. Attack is a kick with both hind legs, then a swing of the beam.
