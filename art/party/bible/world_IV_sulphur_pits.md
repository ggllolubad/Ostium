# Act II: The Fires Beneath

## IV. The Sulphur Pits (Fire + Toxin, Combust)

Brimstone mines that supplied the world's powder. The air itself became fuel and every poison is a fuse. **Fire** chars and cracks skin with glowing seams; **toxin** swells it with gas sacs, yellow crust and ripening blisters. The cast are miners, their overseers and priests, and the vermin of the galleries. **Palette:** sulphur yellow, ochre smoke, lamp orange.

### Rulers

#### The Burning Lung

- **Lore line:** "The mine's first breath, grown into a toad the size of a chapel. Every breath it takes is fire."
- **Was:** the first pocket of gas the miners broke into; it took the shape of the toad that lived in it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, squatting in the main gallery, filling it wall to wall.
- **Body:** a colossal toad whose body is mostly lung: a vast translucent throat sac glowing orange from inside, ribs visible through it. The skin is crusted with yellow sulphur crystals and split by glowing seams. Pit props and broken mine-cart rails are stuck in its hide. **The Tooth** is a long shard of pale stone set in its brow, jutting from a collar of split hide.
- **Head:** a wide mouth that leaks flame at the corners, small bulging eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the huge glowing throat sac.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow crust, lamp orange glow, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is the sac slowly inflating and deflating. Attack is a breath of burning gas across the ranks.

#### The Owner

- **Lore line:** "He came down the shaft once, to see the gas for himself. He has swollen too fat to go back up."
- **Was:** the mine-owner, husband of the Miasma Matriarch, who rode down the shaft in the winding cage to inspect the workings.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, hanging head-down through the shaft mouth in the gallery roof. His hips and legs are wedged up in the shaft, out of frame; his torso and arms hang down into the gallery, the head lowest, the hands reaching to the floor. All his weight hangs from the wedged hips, and the shaft timbers creak with it.
- **Body:** a big man gone bigger. Fire dominates: **his skin is charred black and split all over in a net of glowing seams**, like a log in a grate, the cracks widest across the chest and back where his lamp oil soaked him; every split edge curls and flakes ash that drifts upward, toward his hidden legs. Toxin swelled him until he stuck: belly and chest ballooned with gas under the charred crust, sulphur blooming in the creases, ripe blisters along the arms that hiss when they burst. The hands are huge and swollen, the nails black.
- **Dress and gear:** a fine wool coat with a fur collar, charred to rags and hanging down round his face; a scorched linen ruff; a heavy silver chain of office swinging below his chin; a silver signet ring sunk in a swollen finger; a pocket ledger dangling on a chain; a purse split open, coins dropping; a gentleman's brass-bound lamp still clutched in one fist, lit; the bent iron bars of the broken winding cage round his waist, its chain running up out of frame; splintered shaft timbers and pit props jammed around him; a scorched deed tucked in his belt; miners' tally tokens spilling from a pocket; his broad hat lying on the gallery floor beneath his head.
- **Head:** upside down and lowest of all: a heavy jowled face, the beard singed to stubble, eyes squeezed shut against the heat, thin hair hanging and smouldering.
- **Signature shape:** the huge inverted torso hanging through the roof, arms dangling to the floor.
- **Colour:** lamp orange seams, sulphur yellow crust, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow turn and sway on the wedged hips, ash drifting upward from the seams. Attack is a two-handed grab from above that lifts a hero against the burning chest; on a heavy turn he drops the lamp into the gas.

#### The Powder Mill

- **Lore line:** "It ground the brimstone for the world's powder. The horse has never been unhitched."
- **Was:** the pithead powder mill, a great stone edge-runner wheel that crushed sulphur in a trough, and the blind mill horse that turned it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a millstone standing on its edge in a stone trough, its top and wooden frame cropped by the frame top. The horse is harnessed to the axle-beam in front, leaning into the collar, so the whole machine reads horse first as it advances left. Its weight is the stone's: everything in front of it is ground.
- **Body:** fire dominates both. **The stone wheel** is charred black and split with glowing seams that open and close as it turns, the grooves of its dressing caked with sulphur crust. **The horse** is a big blind draught horse whose hide is charred and cracked in glowing seams along every harness line, the mane burned to a stub; toxin has swollen its belly and neck with gas sacs under the crust and ringed its nostrils with yellow. The harness has burned into the hide at the hocks and the sulphur has grown over both, so horse and axle-beam are cemented into one.
- **Dress and gear:** an iron-banded timber axle-beam through the stone's centre, one band split; a burst horse collar of leather and straw; useless blinkers; harness chains; a bronze bell on the hame; the miller's leather apron hanging from the beam; the miller's boot sticking out from under the stone's rim; split powder sacks spilling yellow along the trough; a wooden scoop; a horn-screened lantern hung on the beam against sparks; the stone's iron tyre throwing sparks; a tally board of sacks nailed to the frame; lumps of brimstone in the trough; a broken wooden rake across the trough.
- **Head:** the horse's long head, eyes milk white, lips drawn back from long teeth, foam gone yellow.
- **Signature shape:** the upright millstone with the horse leaning in front of it.
- **Colour:** lamp orange seams, sulphur yellow, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is the horse treading in place while the stone grinds a quarter turn and sparks. Attack is the mill rolling forward: the stone crushes the front rank and the powder in the trough flashes.

### Elites

#### Powder Ogre

- **Lore line:** "It eats blasting powder. It burps fire."
- **Was:** a powder-store keeper who ate the stock.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3, pot-bellied, a powder keg under one arm.
- **Body and dress:** a huge sooty brute, belly scarred and bulging, scorched leather breeches, **a split powder keg under one arm**, black powder smeared around the mouth. A lit fuse cord tied around one wrist.
- **Head:** singed bald head, burnt eyebrows, a stupid satisfied grin.
- **Signature shape:** the keg under the arm and the bulging belly.
- **Colour:** soot black, ochre, a spark of lamp orange.
- **Motion:** idle is eating powder from the keg. Attack is a fiery belch.

#### Miasma Matriarch

- **Lore line:** "A head swollen with gas, drifting through the galleries."
- **Was:** the mine-owner's wife, who came down to see the gas.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, floating, a body hanging limp below a vast head.
- **Body:** **a woman's head swollen like a balloon** to three times a body's height, the skin stretched yellow and translucent with gas, veins dark; her small body and once-fine dress hang beneath it, feet off the ground. Smaller gas-head young drift in her wake.
- **Head:** the features stretched wide and thin, eyes like slits, mouth a small leaking hole.
- **Signature shape:** the floating balloon head over a dangling body.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow skin, ochre gas, faded dress.
- **Motion:** idle is drifting and bobbing. Attack is a slow exhalation of choking gas.

#### The Mounted Overseer

- **Lore line:** "He rode the galleries on a pit pony, counting heads. Neither of them has lost count."
- **Was:** the mine's overseer, who rode a pit pony through the main galleries to drive the shifts.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.1 to the tip of his raised whip, a stocky man on a small pit pony; the pony braced with its head low, the overseer leaning forward in the saddle, whip arm raised. Weight in the pony's forequarters.
- **Body:** fire dominates both. The overseer's face and forearms are charred and split in glowing seams, the edges flaking; toxin has swollen one side of his neck with a gas blister the size of a fist and crusted his beard yellow. The pony is shaggy and blind, its coat singed away in patches over cracked, glowing skin; toxin has bloated its belly so that it rides low, the girth sunk deep. Short thick legs, cracked hooves.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather jerkin over a wool coat over a linen shirt; a felt hat with its brim burned through on one side; a long leather whip; a tally board on a cord, its pegs half pushed in; a ring of iron keys to the powder store; a short cudgel hung from the saddle; a lantern on the saddle bow; scorched saddle bags; boots with iron spurs; a bandage on the whip hand; the pony's bridle with blinkers and a bronze plate on the brow; a scorched saddle cloth; a pick strapped behind the saddle.
- **Head:** a square, cracked face, a broken nose, a singed beard crusted yellow, eyes narrowed.
- **Signature shape:** the rider on the small, low pony with the whip raised.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow crust, lamp orange seams, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is the pony stamping while the overseer taps the tally board. Attack is a lash of the whip that marks a hero, with the pony barging after it.

#### The Shrine-Bearer

- **Lore line:** "He carried the little chapel down every shaft to bless the powder. He was carrying it when the powder answered."
- **Was:** the mine priest who carried the miners' portable shrine, a small wooden tower-chapel lit with lamps, through the galleries on feast days.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3 to the top of the shrine: a priest of human height bent forward under a wooden shrine-tower strapped to his back and rising high above his head, a processional staff in one hand. Knees bent, weight forward.
- **Body:** a broad, strong priest. Fire dominates: the shrine's votive lamps have burned onto his back for years, and **his back, neck and shoulders are charred and split with glowing seams**, the char running down the arms in streaks. Toxin has grown a sulphur crust over the shrine's base and his shoulders together, knitting them into one, and ripened the skin under the straps into blisters.
- **Dress and gear:** **the shrine**: an oak tower-chapel with three arched windows and a pitched roof, a small gilded holy figure inside (holy), its lamps burning; leather carrying straps padded with sacking; a surplice scorched to the waist over a miner's leather jerkin and wool breeches; a processional staff topped with a small gilt cross-lamp (holy); miners' tally tokens hung on the shrine as offerings; wax candles stuck along its eaves, melted into beards; a small bell on the roof ridge; scorched prayer slips nailed to its sides; a wooden rosary; leather knee pads for kneeling; hobnailed boots.
- **Head:** a tonsured, soot-streaked head, a broad, stubborn face, lips moving in prayer, eyes streaming from the smoke.
- **Signature shape:** the little tower-chapel rising over the bent figure.
- **Colour:** lamp orange, sulphur yellow, a touch of gold on the shrine's figure.
- **Motion:** idle is a kneeling dip and a mumbled blessing while the shrine's lamps flicker. Attack is a blessing of the gas: the lamps flare and burning gas rolls out of the shrine's windows over the front rank.

### Cast

#### Gas-Lung Miner

- **Lore line:** "Its lungs are full of yellow gas. Keep it away from the lamp."
- **Was:** a pit miner.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, stooped from years in low tunnels.
- **Body and dress:** a miner's leather cap and lamp, a pick, ragged shirt; **the chest is swollen like a bellows**, ribs bowed outward, yellow gas leaking from cracks in the skin.
- **Head:** a sooty face, wet cough, eyes yellowed.
- **Signature shape:** the barrel chest and the pick.
- **Colour:** soot, sulphur yellow, lamp orange.
- **Motion:** idle is wheezing. Attack is a pick swing; it bursts into flame if Burned.

#### Brimstone Toad

- **Lore line:** "Its skin sweats brimstone. Do not lick it."
- **Was:** a cave toad.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.55 tall, squat.
- **Body:** a lumpy toad crusted with yellow brimstone crystals, sweating yellow beads.
- **Signature shape:** the crystal-crusted squat body.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow, ochre.
- **Motion:** idle is a throat pulse. Attack is a spit of burning slime.

#### Fuse Imp

- **Lore line:** "It carries a lit torch into a gas mine. On purpose."
- **Was:** a spark that learned to walk.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5, skittering, a torch held high.
- **Body:** a wiry little soot-black imp with ember-cracked skin, long fingers, a tail tipped with a burning fuse; **it carries a lit torch bigger than itself**.
- **Head:** a grinning face, ears like flames.
- **Signature shape:** the tiny figure under a big torch.
- **Colour:** soot black, lamp orange.
- **Motion:** idle is a giggling hop. Attack is throwing the torch.

#### Pit Crawler

- **Lore line:** "A centipede as long as a mine cart, and much hungrier."
- **Was:** a cave centipede.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.8 long, low, front third reared.
- **Body:** a centipede the length of a mine cart, armour plates stained yellow, legs like pick-points, mandibles dripping.
- **Signature shape:** the reared segmented front.
- **Colour:** ochre plates, black legs.
- **Motion:** idle is a ripple of legs. Attack is a mandible strike.

#### Canary Priest

- **Lore line:** "They sing to the gas the way miners once listened to canaries. The gas listens."
- **Was:** the mine's chaplain.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, holding up a birdcage like a censer.
- **Body and dress:** a soot-stained surplice over a miner's jerkin; **a brass birdcage on a chain with a dead yellow canary inside**; yellow feathers sprouting from the cuffs and neck.
- **Head:** a gaunt face, mouth open in song.
- **Signature shape:** the raised birdcage.
- **Colour:** grey surplice, canary yellow, brass.
- **Motion:** idle is swinging the cage. Attack is a hymn that ignites the gas.

#### Gas-Burner

- **Lore line:** "He went in first each morning with a flame on a pole to burn off the gas. Somebody had to."
- **Was:** the firedamp man, who went into new workings ahead of the shift in a soaked hood, carrying a lit candle on a long pole to burn off the gas.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0 standing, but down on one knee with the back bent low, the long pole raised two-handed toward the roof ahead. Weight on the back knee.
- **Body:** lean and hard. Fire dominates: **the hands, the pole arm and the back of the neck** are charred and split in glowing seams. Toxin has ripened blisters along his back under the hood and left a yellow crust at its hem.
- **Dress and gear:** a hooded smock of thick sacking soaked in water, steaming, its top scorched and its hem charred and curled; a wet cloth over the face with eye slits cut in it; a long ash pole with an iron candle socket at the tip and a burning tallow candle; leather knee pads; a leather water bottle for re-soaking the hood; a tinderbox on the rope belt; bare, scorched feet.
- **Head:** hidden under the soaked hood, slits cut for the eyes; a charred chin shows below the cloth.
- **Signature shape:** the kneeling hooded figure with the long flame pole raised ahead.
- **Colour:** ochre smoke, lamp orange flame, sulphur yellow crust.
- **Motion:** idle is shuffling forward a hand's length and raising the pole to test the roof. Attack is lifting the flame into the gas: a flash of burning gas over the front rank.

#### Pit Boy

- **Lore line:** "He carried ore up the stairs on his back from the age of seven. He is still seven."
- **Was:** a carrying boy who hauled sacks of sulphur ore from the face up the mine stairs to the surface.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, bent nearly double under a sack of ore bigger than his torso, one hand on a knee, the other gripping the sack's neck over his shoulder. Legs bowed.
- **Body:** a thin child. Fire dominates: **his shoulders and the back of his neck** under the sack are charred and split in glowing seams where the hot ore rode. Toxin has swollen his knees with blisters and crusted his mouth and nostrils yellow.
- **Dress and gear:** a heavy sack of brimstone lumps, crystals poking through its holes; a loincloth and a ragged shirt cut down from a man's; a leather shoulder pad scorched through; a cord round the waist with a wooden count token; a rag tied round one knee; bare feet with cracked soles.
- **Head:** a gaunt, old-young face, cropped hair, eyes squinting.
- **Signature shape:** the small bent figure under the huge sack.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow, lamp orange, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is a panting rest, hitching the sack higher. Attack is tipping the sack and hurling a burning lump of ore.

#### Timberman

- **Lore line:** "He set the props that held the roof up. He still carries one everywhere, in case."
- **Was:** the mine timberman, who cut and set the pit props and roof beams.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5, a huge, broad-backed man carrying a long pit prop across his left shoulder, the beam twice his height, a timber maul in his right hand. Legs wide, body leaning against the weight.
- **Body:** very big and thick-limbed. Fire dominates: charred along the shoulder where the beam caught alight, the skin split and glowing at the neck and down the arm. Toxin has swollen the knuckles and elbows with blisters and crusted the creases of the neck yellow.
- **Dress and gear:** **the prop**, a rough-hewn pine log charred at one end with an iron cap plate nailed on; a short-handled timber maul; a leather shoulder pad; a wool shirt with the sleeves torn off under a leather jerkin; wool breeches tied below the knee; a belt of iron wedges and spikes; an adze at the hip; a lamp hooked on the belt; a notched measuring stick; a nail pouch.
- **Head:** a big square head under a leather cap, a cropped beard singed short, heavy brows.
- **Signature shape:** the long beam across the shoulder.
- **Colour:** ochre smoke, lamp orange seams, sulphur yellow.
- **Motion:** idle is shifting the beam to the other side of the shoulder. Attack is a sweeping swing of the beam across two ranks.

#### Gallery Rats

- **Lore line:** "They ate the miners' bread, then the miners' candles. What they ate burns."
- **Was:** the rats of the galleries, which lived on the miners' food and tallow candles.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a heap about 0.45 high, swarming over an overturned miner's food tin.
- **Body:** dozens of rats. Fire dominates: the fur is singed away in patches, the skin beneath charred and split in glowing seams, the tails burned to stubs. Toxin has swollen their bellies tight with gas, a few burst open, the snouts crusted yellow.
- **Dress and gear:** an iron food tin upturned; a crust of bread; gnawed tallow candle stubs; a miner's leather cap; a length of wick; a dropped lamp, still alight.
- **Head:** none to speak of: a heap of snouts and teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the heap of rats on the upturned tin.
- **Colour:** lamp orange seams, ochre smoke, sulphur yellow.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant seethe. Attack is the swarm pouring over a hero; a swollen rat bursts into flame.

#### Weighman

- **Lore line:** "He weighed every load that came up and found it short. He has never once been wrong."
- **Was:** the pithead weighman, the owner's clerk who weighed the ore and docked the miners' pay.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, upright and fussy, holding out a long iron steelyard by its hook at arm's length, a ledger under the other arm. Weight back.
- **Body:** a narrow, stooped clerk. Fire dominates: **the arm that holds the steelyard** is charred and split with glowing seams where the iron went hot in the gas flash, the ink-stained fingers cracked. Toxin has puffed his face and blistered his lips, and left a yellow crust at the cuffs and collar.
- **Dress and gear:** **a long iron steelyard** with a hook, a pan on chains and a pear-shaped sliding counterweight; a leather-bound ledger, scorched; a quill behind the ear; an ink horn and pen case at the belt; a fitted wool doublet over a linen shirt, with linen sleeve guards; spectacles on a cord; a ring of keys; a lump of chalk; a small purse of fines.
- **Head:** a thin face, a pursed mouth, spectacles low on the nose, thin hair combed over.
- **Signature shape:** the long steelyard held out with its hanging pan.
- **Colour:** ochre smoke, lamp orange seams, sulphur yellow crust.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping the counterweight along the beam. Attack is a swing of the steelyard's weight on its chain.

#### Lamp-Room Girl

- **Lore line:** "She hands every miner his lamp at the start of the shift. Everyone takes one."
- **Was:** a girl of the lamp room, who filled, trimmed and handed out the miners' oil lamps.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.9, a slight girl carrying a wooden yoke across her shoulders hung with a dozen lit oil lamps, one more lamp held out in her free hand. Weight on the back foot under the clinking load.
- **Body:** thin and young. Fire dominates: **her hands and the side of her face nearest the lamps** are charred and split with glowing seams, the hair burned short on that side. Toxin has raised blisters along her collarbone where the oil spilled and crusted her knuckles yellow.
- **Dress and gear:** a wooden yoke with iron hooks; a dozen small open iron oil lamps, all lit; a leather apron soaked in lamp oil, lamp numbers chalked on it; an oil can with a long spout at the belt; a wick-trimmer on a cord; a wool dress kilted up; a headscarf scorched along one edge; clogs.
- **Head:** a small, serious face, one cheek charred and split, the scarf burnt back from that side.
- **Signature shape:** the yoke of hanging lamps across the shoulders.
- **Colour:** lamp orange, ochre smoke, sulphur yellow.
- **Motion:** idle is the lamps swinging and clinking. Attack is a lit lamp thrown, bursting in flame.

#### Tub Hurrier

- **Lore line:** "Chained to the tub at the waist, she dragged it from the face to the shaft. She drags it full or empty."
- **Was:** a hurrier, a woman who dragged ore tubs along the low galleries by a chain from a leather girdle.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, bent almost flat, leaning into the chain with her hands on her knees, the ore tub behind her. Weight driven forward.
- **Body:** broad-shouldered and strong-legged. Fire dominates: her back and shoulders are charred and split in glowing seams from the sparks of the burning ore behind her. Toxin has ripened **the chafe line of the girdle** round her waist into blisters and yellow crust, and swollen her knees.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather girdle round the waist with an iron chain running between the legs to the tub; **a wooden ore tub on small iron wheels**, heaped with smouldering sulphur ore; trousers of sacking; a ragged linen shirt; leather pads on the hands and knees; a headscarf; a candle stub stuck to the tub's rim.
- **Head:** a set, strained face, jaw clenched, eyes on the floor.
- **Signature shape:** the bent body straining forward with the heaped tub behind.
- **Colour:** sulphur yellow ore, lamp orange, ochre smoke.
- **Motion:** idle is heaving the tub one step and resting. Attack is dragging the burning tub through a rank.
