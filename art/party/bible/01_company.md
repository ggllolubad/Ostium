# The company and the abbey

## The six callings

Heroes are hired people, not chosen ones. Each wears the working clothes of a real trade, patched by the abbey: every hero carries a lead ostium badge on a cord and a strip of red rubric cloth tied somewhere visible, the company's only uniform. Heroes face right.

### Bulwark: the Iron Vow

- **Was:** a garrison soldier from a tower that fell when something came through.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.1, the broadest silhouette and the heaviest armour in the company. Feet wide, knees bent, weight forward into the shield. Centre of mass low, behind the shield.
- **Body:** thick-necked and heavy, a fighter in his forties. Scarred knuckles; a nose broken more than once.
- **Dress and gear:** a mismatched harness of dented steel plate over a padded wool gambeson that shows at the joints. Mail skirt to mid-thigh. Steel gauntlets; iron sabatons caked in mud. Weapon: a short iron flanged mace on a leather wrist loop. **Shield: a tower shield made from an old church door**, oak planks bound with three iron strap hinges, a ring handle still on the face, the keyhole plate on the rim, studded with nail heads. It covers him from chin to shin.
- **Head:** a battered flat-topped great helm, riveted, with a narrow eye slit, breathing holes and a sword gash across the front; one eye catches the light through the slit. A mail aventail beneath. The face is never seen: he is the company's tank, not light infantry (Cole, 2026-11-22).
- **Signature shape:** the rectangular door-shield with its hinge straps.
- **Colour:** cold steel blue-grey, dark oak, a red rubric strip tied to the shield's ring handle.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow breath behind the shield, mace resting on its rim. Attack is a short step and an overhead mace blow over the shield's top edge.
- **Paths:**
  - **Last Bastion:** more damage than anyone. Plate patched with lead, a cracked shield held together with rope, a bandage under the helm. Looks like he should be dead.
  - **Ironwarden:** plate rimed white on the windward side, a fur mantle, frost in the beard. The door-shield is iron-bound pine from a northern chapel.
  - **Thornguard:** penitent armour with iron barbs welded outward along the arms and shield face, a hair shirt visible at the neck, a flagellant's cord at the belt.

### Duelist: the Quick Blade

- **Was:** a fencing master, bravo, headsman or barber-surgeon. The single cut is their trade.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, lean and long-limbed. Side-on fencing stance, rear arm back, weight on the back foot, ready to lunge.
- **Body:** wiry, long forearms, a duelling scar across one cheek.
- **Dress and gear:** a fitted black leather doublet with slashed sleeves showing grey linen, a **red sash** at the waist, high boots, one fingerless glove. Weapon: a long, narrow sword (a rapier with a swept iron hilt). Faint Lichtenberg scorch marks on the blade and the sword hand from the lightning-touched schools.
- **Head:** bare-headed, hair tied back, narrow face, a thin moustache or none; eyes always on the target.
- **Signature shape:** the long blade held out in line, with the sash trailing.
- **Colour:** black leather, oxblood-red sash, white-gold sparks on the steel.
- **Motion:** idle is a small bounce with the blade tip circling. Attack is a full lunge, the body nearly horizontal.
- **Paths:**
  - **Bladedancer:** lighter clothes, open shirt, two blades; more motion in the cloth.
  - **Executioner:** a headsman's black hood with eye holes and a broad two-handed heading sword with a square tip; a leather apron.
  - **Bloodletter:** a barber-surgeon's striped red-and-white sleeve, a lancet case on the belt, a bloodied bowl hanging from a cord, a short curved blade.

### Elementalist: the Conjoiner

- **Was:** a scholar of the Six Courses, argued over by the whole abbey.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0. Upright, one hand raised, palm open, as if lecturing.
- **Body:** thin, ink-stained, sleep-deprived. **Hands stained to the wrist in six ink colours**, one finger per course, as if dipped in a scribe's pots.
- **Dress and gear:** a heavy undyed wool scholar's robe, its hem and cuffs inked with the six course sigils in a ring. A leather satchel of scrolls. A copper astrolabe-like ring of six sigils hangs at the belt.
- **Head:** a close scholar's coif, gaunt cheeks, short beard or shaved, bright fevered eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the raised stained hand and the satchel of scrolls.
- **Colour:** undyed wool, the six course inks on the hands (vermilion, pale blue, gold, deep blue, sick green, violet-black).
- **Motion:** idle is turning a page in the air with the stained fingers. Attack is a casting flick, the sigil ring spinning.
- **Paths:**
  - **Accumulator:** carries a heavy brass vessel on the back that stores Focus; a bent posture under its weight.
  - **Catalyst:** ink-drawn ward sigils on the forearms and face; quicker, nervous.
  - **Prismatic:** all six sigils embroidered in a halo pattern on the hood; one eye clouded white from what it has seen.

### Alchemist: the Brewer

- **Was:** an apothecary, distiller or plague doctor.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95. Slightly hunched, a flask always in one hand.
- **Body:** stocky, burn-scarred forearms, a steady hand.
- **Dress and gear:** a long waxed-leather apron over a linen shirt, **a bandolier of glass flasks** across the chest (different shapes, corked and waxed), burn-scarred leather gloves to the elbow, a small copper still-coil on the back.
- **Head:** spectacles pushed up on a cap, a cloth tied over the mouth when brewing, practical face.
- **Signature shape:** the bandolier of flasks and the raised flask.
- **Colour:** brown leather, amber and green glass, copper.
- **Motion:** idle is swirling a flask to check it. Attack is an overarm throw.
- **Paths:**
  - **Transmuter:** a crucible on a chain, an alembic in hand, more copper and brass.
  - **Plaguewright:** **the beaked plague-doctor mask** with glass eyes, a black waxed coat, a pointer cane.
  - **Apothecary:** a herb bag and bundles of dried plants at the belt, bandage rolls, a gentler stance.

### Oracle: the Anchorite

- **Was:** a sibyl or anchorite who walled themself up beside a void door and listened for years.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95. Very still, head tilted as if listening; feet bare.
- **Body:** pale and thin from years without sun, long fingers, a light tremor.
- **Dress and gear:** a hooded robe in deep violet-black, **star charts sewn into the lining** so constellations show when it opens, an **hourglass on a chain** around one wrist, a wax tablet at the belt.
- **Head:** **eyes covered by a linen blindfold** or sewn-shut lids; long uncut hair. The face is calm.
- **Signature shape:** the blindfold and the swinging hourglass.
- **Colour:** violet-black robe, parchment star charts, pale skin. As the only hero from a void tradition, the Oracle may carry a single painted eye on the blindfold.
- **Motion:** idle is turning the hourglass over. Attack is a pointing hand; the robe opens and the stars show.
- **Paths:**
  - **Chronomancer:** a brass clock face hung on the chest, a broken pendulum as a staff.
  - **Augur:** a pouch of knucklebones, bird bones and feathers woven into the hair.
  - **Fatebinder:** a quill and an ink horn of red rubric; red script written on the robe and arms.

### Hexweaver: the Cunning Folk

- **Was:** a hedge witch, poppet-maker or corpse-candle burner.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95. Crouched low, knees bent, often working something with both hands.
- **Body:** wiry, old before their time, dirt under the nails, knotted fingers.
- **Dress and gear:** layered rags in green-black and bone, **knotted cord** tied around the arms and waist, **straw and cloth poppets** hanging from the belt, **black tallow candles** stuck to a shoulder plate of wax, a pouch of graveyard dirt.
- **Head:** a tangled head of hair under a ragged hood, a bone pin through it, sharp watchful eyes, a crooked smile.
- **Signature shape:** the poppets hanging at the belt and the candles burning on the shoulder.
- **Colour:** green-black, bone white, candle-flame yellow (the only warm light they carry).
- **Motion:** idle is twisting a cord between the fingers. Attack is driving a pin into a poppet.
- **Paths:**
  - **Pactbound:** a bandaged forearm that is always freshly cut, a bone knife, blood on the poppets.
  - **Doomsayer:** a handbell and a long staff topped with a small iron bell.
  - **Mirrorhex:** a cracked hand mirror on a cord, mirror shards stitched into the hood.

## The abbey keepers

The keepers are the only warm-lit people in the game. All wear the Ostiarii habit: an undyed grey-brown wool habit, a black scapular, a rope belt with **an iron key ring** (the order's sign: they keep doors) and a lead ostium badge. Each one also carries a mark from the world they survived. Keeper names are lore proposals.

| Keeper | Office | Build and face | What sets them apart | Mark of their world |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prior Aldric** | Chapter House | Tall, lean, sixties, close-cropped grey hair, a calm face that gives nothing away | A heavy ring of keys, a ledger of the dead under one arm, gloved hands | None visible. His secret is that he hides it |
| **The Abbot** | Vigil at the sealed door | Very old, kneeling, seen mostly from behind | A hood pulled low, candle wax pooled on the floor around his knees, one ear turned to the door | His ear is bandaged |
| **Brother Lucan, the Armarius** | Scriptorium | Thin, stooped, middle-aged, ink-black fingertips, reading lenses | A quill behind the ear, a gold-leaf book knife, a lectern | None; he has never gone through |
| **Sister Wenna, the Infirmarer** | Infirmarium | Broad, forties, strong forearms, sleeves rolled, a tired kind face | A vinegar-soaked cloth at her neck, a bone saw and herb bundles at her belt | Frost-scarred, grey-green fingertips (Hollow Barrow) |
| **Mother Hild, the Smith** | Undercroft | Short, powerful, fifties, a leather apron over the habit | Hammer and tongs; the forge behind her flickers with static | Burn-scarred forearms; her hair stands slightly with static (Storm Foundry) |
| **Brother Fenn, the Almoner** | Almonry | Round, soft hands, cheerful mouth, calculating eyes | A counting board, a fat purse, a ledger with red entries | None; he did not go through either |
| **Sister Ide, the Sacristan** | Sacristy | Slight, thirties, very upright | A cloth over her eyes, fingertips always touching a relic, frost on her breath indoors | Milky white eyes under the cloth (White Silence) |

| **The Hosteller** | Guesthouse | Plain, ageless, a face no one can describe afterwards | The guest book, a lantern | None. Same face every run |

## The shop keepers in full

Each keeper has their own temperament, voice and way of wearing the habit, so no two read alike in silhouette or manner (Cole, 2026-11-22). The shared parts stay: the grey-brown habit, the iron key ring and the lead ostium badge. Everything else is theirs. They are drawn waist-up or full-length at their counter, facing left toward the door the player came in by.

### Mother Hild, the Smith (Undercroft)

- **Temperament:** blunt, few words, gruff warmth. Treats every blade like a patient. Hums hymns off-key over the anvil. Does not trust the Prior and does not hide it.
- **Voice:** "Put it on the anvil. Not like that." / "It's not broken. You're holding it wrong."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.9, short and very broad. Planted feet, hammer resting on her shoulder, the other fist on her hip.
- **Body:** fifties, heavy shoulders, thick forearms covered in **branching Lichtenberg scars** from the Storm Foundry coal, burn-shiny knuckles.
- **Dress and gear:** the habit's skirts kilted up through the belt over leather breeches, sleeves cut off at the elbow, a scorched leather apron to the shins, iron-shod clogs. Tongs and a rune chisel at the belt; a second chisel behind her ear.
- **Head:** cropped grey hair that **stands up in a static halo**; smoked-glass goggles pushed up on the brow; a square, soot-lined face.
- **Signature shape:** the hammer on the shoulder and the static-frizzed hair.
- **Colour:** soot black, scorched leather, white-blue static sparks at the hair and anvil.
- **Motion:** idle is a shoulder roll and a tap of the hammer on the anvil that throws a spark.

### Sister Wenna, the Infirmarer (Infirmarium)

- **Temperament:** brisk, practical, gallows humour. Tender with the dying, short with the merely wounded. Carries the Barrow company she lost and never mentions them.
- **Voice:** "Sit. Bleed quietly." / "I've had worse in my bed than you. Most of them died."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, broad and square. Leans on her table with both hands, weight forward.
- **Body:** forties, strong forearms, **grey-green frost-scarred fingertips** from the Hollow Barrow.
- **Dress and gear:** habit sleeves rolled and pinned above the elbow; a linen apron stained rust-brown; a vinegar-soaked cloth knotted at the throat; herb bundles, a bone saw and a roll of bandage at the belt; the **waxed leather plague mask of the Barrow** hanging at her hip.
- **Head:** wimple tied back hard; a tired, kind, sunken-eyed face.
- **Signature shape:** rolled sleeves, the saw, the hanging mask.
- **Colour:** undyed linen, rust-brown stains, vinegar-yellow cloth, sage-green herbs.
- **Motion:** idle is wiping her hands on the apron, then lifting the vinegar cloth to her nose.

### Brother Fenn, the Almoner (Almonry)

- **Temperament:** jovial and oily. Quotes scripture on generosity while he counts. Charges for alms and keeps very good books. Likes the company: they spend.
- **Voice:** "Alms are free, friend. The handling is extra." / "Bless you. Sign here."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, round. Leans over the counter, hands spread on the ledger.
- **Body:** soft hands, a ruddy round face, a double chin, an ink-stained thumb.
- **Dress and gear:** a habit of **better wool than anyone else's**, dyed a shade darker, with fur-trimmed cuffs (his vanity); a fat purse and a counting board of beads at the belt; spectacles on a cord; a seal ring.
- **Head:** a glossy tonsure, cheerful mouth, small calculating eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the round body over the open ledger and the purse.
- **Colour:** rich dark brown wool, fur cuffs, red ink in the ledger.
- **Motion:** idle is rubbing his hands, then flicking a bead along the counting board.

### Godric, the Cellarer (Cellarium)

- **Temperament:** anxious and kind, a hoarder by terror. Counts under his breath, recounts, never says yes to more without a sigh. The Icebound Fleet taught him what running out means.
- **Voice:** "Twelve candles. I counted. I'll count again when you're back." / "Bread keeps. People don't."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, heavy-set and stooped. Hugs a bundle of candles to his chest.
- **Body:** sixties, big belly, **two fingers missing** on the left hand from frostbite.
- **Dress and gear:** a lay brother's short habit over **layer on layer of wool** (he has never been warm since), a stained leather apron, mittens with the two empty fingers sewn shut, a huge ring of store keys, candles tied in bundles at the belt, a tally stick behind the ear.
- **Head:** a knitted wool cap pulled to the brows, red nose, white stubble, worried eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the bulky layered body hugging candles.
- **Colour:** many browns and greys of wool, candle-wax cream, frost-pink fingers.
- **Motion:** idle is counting the candles with a fingertip, then blowing on his hand.

### Sister Ide, the Sacristan (Sacristy)

- **Temperament:** serene, soft-spoken, a little eerie. Talks about relics as if they were people. Never startled.
- **Voice:** "This one is still cold. It has not forgiven you." / "The Teeth are warm today."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, slight and very upright. One bare hand always resting on a relic or the case.
- **Body:** thirties, thin, pale, **frost on her lashes and her breath** indoors.
- **Dress and gear:** the only **bleached, near-white habit** in the abbey; one white glove worn, the other hand bare with blue-cold fingertips; a small sanctus bell and the sacristy keys at the belt; a candle snuffer.
- **Head:** a linen band bound over the eyes; a fine white veil; milky eyes glimpsed at the band's edge.
- **Signature shape:** the tall pale figure with the bound eyes and the bare hand out.
- **Colour:** bone white, frost blue, a single warm glint from the Teeth behind her.
- **Motion:** idle is a head tilt, listening, while the bare fingertips trace the glass.

### Brother Lucan, the Armarius (Scriptorium)

- **Temperament:** dry, precise, quietly sardonic. His wit lives in the margins. Writes every death in a beautiful hand and without comment.
- **Voice:** "I have written you down. Try to stay legible." / "There is a snail in the margin. It is winning."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, tall and stooped over the desk.
- **Body:** thin, long neck, **ink-black fingertips**.
- **Dress and gear:** linen oversleeves tied over the habit to the elbow; an ink horn, a pen-knife, pumice and a wax tablet at the belt; a quill behind the ear.
- **Head:** reading lenses on the nose, a thin face, a neat grey tonsure, raised eyebrows.
- **Signature shape:** the stoop over the slanted desk with a quill raised.
- **Colour:** grey habit, cream oversleeves, black ink, a fleck of red rubric and a glint of gold leaf.
- **Motion:** idle is trimming the quill with the knife and blowing on the page.

### Prior Aldric (Chapter House)

- **Temperament:** calm, gentle, persuasive, and never quite truthful. Remembers every name of the dead. The company likes him; that is the danger.
- **Voice:** "Every door you open, I write your name beside it." / "A vow is only a promise you can't take back."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, tall and lean. Perfectly still, hands folded over the ledger of the dead.
- **Body:** sixties, long elegant hands always gloved.
- **Dress and gear:** an **immaculate habit and black scapular**, the only clean one in the abbey; the **master ring of keys** on a chain; a plain iron pectoral cross; a single red cord of office at the waist.
- **Head:** close-cropped silver hair, a calm unreadable face, pale eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the tall still figure with the ledger and the heavy key ring.
- **Colour:** grey, black and a single red cord; very clean against the grime around him.
- **Motion:** idle is turning one key slowly on the ring.

### The Hosteller (Guesthouse)

- **Temperament:** blandly friendly and faintly wrong. Remembers every guest, is remembered by no one. The same face every run.
- **Voice:** "Welcome back. Or welcome. One of those." / "Your bed's made. It always is."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, medium in every way. Stands with the guest book under one arm and a lit lantern in the other hand.
- **Body:** ageless and hard to place; tidy hands.
- **Dress and gear:** not a monk: a servant's plain grey tunic and a clean apron, the order's key ring and badge; the guest book; the lantern, always lit.
- **Head:** a smooth, pleasant, **forgettable face** drawn with the least detail of anyone in the game: small eyes, a small smile, hair neatly combed. The plainness is the joke.
- **Signature shape:** the lantern held out and the book under the arm.
- **Colour:** plain grey and cream; the lantern's warm light is the only accent.
- **Motion:** idle is polishing a tankard, smiling at nothing.

In the Guesthouse, the roster's heroes rest in bunks along the wall. Each one is clickable and opens the company screen on that hero (Cole, 2026-11-22).
