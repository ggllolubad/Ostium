# Rival rulers: the two added rulers in Worlds II to XV (28 entries)

Source: bible/world_*.md, 2026-11-23. Each world's original ruler (listed first in its bible file) holds the Tooth; these two are the rival claimants.


## II. The Rotmire (Toxin + Water, Toxic Pool)

#### The Stilt Hall
- **Lore line:** "When the water turned, the village climbed into the moot-hall to wait. In the morning the hall had walked off."
- **Was:** the stilt villages' moot-hall, a long thatched house on oak piles where the reeve held court and the eel tithe was stored, and the families who sheltered in it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a long house standing knee-deep in black water on eight of its oak piles as legs, leaning toward the company. The thatch ridge is cut off by the frame top and the rear gable by the right edge. Its weight sits on the front piles, which are bowed and splayed under it.
- **Body:** toxin has ripened the piles into legs: the oak has swollen soft at two points on each into knee-like burls ringed with shelf fungus, mould fur creeps up from the waterline, and white mycelium threads the timber like veins. The wattle-and-daub walls bulge between the frame posts like a belly between ribs, blistered with bile-green pustules where the daub has split. Water has preserved everything below the thatch: the piles are tanned black and slick, and **the household is in the walls**, tanned bog-brown and unrotted, pressed half out of the daub at the two windows and the open door, still holding what they carried in (a lantern, a swaddled child, a ladle).
- **Signature shape:** the long thatched house walking on bowed piles.

#### The Old Heron
- **Lore line:** "It fished the fen before there were stilts. It has come to prefer the fishermen."
- **Was:** the great grey heron of the fen, older than the villages, which fed in the eel shallows under the stilt houses.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, standing in the shallows on one leg with the other drawn up, body hunched, neck folded back into an S. The head and the top of the neck are above the frame; in the attack the beak comes down into frame like a thrown spear. Centre of mass over the standing foot.
- **Body:** a heron's body the size of a cart, grey plumage clumped into wet, directional locks, with bile-green mould grown into the mats. Toxin has pushed one part hard: **the crop and gullet have swollen into a hanging sack** below the folded neck, wider than the body, the skin stretched thin and blistered, the outlines of swallowed things pressing through it (an eel trap, a boot, a hand). Water has preserved the legs: long and scaled, tanned dark brown like bog leather, with thick knuckled joints and long toes splayed on the mud. The long breast plumes hang lank and have rotted into holes at the tips.
- **Signature shape:** the folded S-neck over the hanging, swollen crop, on one stilt leg.


## III. The Boiling Baths (Fire + Water, Scald)

#### The Best Families
- **Lore line:** "The seventh pool was kept for the best families. They have climbed out to complain about the water."
- **Was:** the noble families who held the private seventh pool, cooked together where they sat when the water rose to the boil.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a heap of fused bathers spilling over the marble lip of their pool onto the floor tiles, the mass hauling itself forward on many arms. The back of the heap and the pool are cut off by the frame's right edge and top. Centre of mass low and forward; it drags.
- **Body:** twenty or more nobles cooked into one soft mass. Fire dominates: the skin is boiled pink-white and shining, peeling in long sheets, blistered at every fold, and where bodies touch the flesh has run together like meat poached in one pot. Water keeps the whole heap swollen, wrinkled and steaming. Arms reach out on every side, several still holding something; faces turn toward each other mid-conversation. Highest on the heap is the eldest, a bald patriarch, his head and shoulders free of the rest.
- **Signature shape:** the sprawling mound bristling with reaching arms, the patriarch's head on top.

#### The Great Copper
- **Lore line:** "The boiler that heats every pool in the temple. Its stokers are still on shift."
- **Was:** the temple's great copper boiler over the furnace, and the stokers who fed it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a riveted copper boiler three storeys high squatting on a brick firebox and leaning forward over the company like a fat body; its dome and chimney are cut off by the frame top and its pipes run out of frame to the pools. It never moves; its whole weight sits on the brick.
- **Body:** fire is the dominant mark: the copper plates are heat-blackened at the base and cooked a boiled red-brown above, blistered where the metal has bubbled, and split along two seams that glow from the fire inside. **The stokers are cooked onto the firebox**: four men pressed half into the hot brick face around the fire door, their skin boiled pink-white and baked onto the brick like bread to an oven wall, their arms still working long iron rakes in and out of the fire. Water swells the copper between the rivet rows into a bulging belly that weeps hot water from every seam, steam pouring from the cracks, and lime scale crusts bone white around every leak.
- **Signature shape:** the bulging boiler over the open fire door.


## Act II: The Fires Beneath

#### The Owner
- **Lore line:** "He came down the shaft once, to see the gas for himself. He has swollen too fat to go back up."
- **Was:** the mine-owner, husband of the Miasma Matriarch, who rode down the shaft in the winding cage to inspect the workings.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, hanging head-down through the shaft mouth in the gallery roof. His hips and legs are wedged up in the shaft, out of frame; his torso and arms hang down into the gallery, the head lowest, the hands reaching to the floor. All his weight hangs from the wedged hips, and the shaft timbers creak with it.
- **Body:** a big man gone bigger. Fire dominates: **his skin is charred black and split all over in a net of glowing seams**, like a log in a grate, the cracks widest across the chest and back where his lamp oil soaked him; every split edge curls and flakes ash that drifts upward, toward his hidden legs. Toxin swelled him until he stuck: belly and chest ballooned with gas under the charred crust, sulphur blooming in the creases, ripe blisters along the arms that hiss when they burst. The hands are huge and swollen, the nails black.
- **Signature shape:** the huge inverted torso hanging through the roof, arms dangling to the floor.

#### The Powder Mill
- **Lore line:** "It ground the brimstone for the world's powder. The horse has never been unhitched."
- **Was:** the pithead powder mill, a great stone edge-runner wheel that crushed sulphur in a trough, and the blind mill horse that turned it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a millstone standing on its edge in a stone trough, its top and wooden frame cropped by the frame top. The horse is harnessed to the axle-beam in front, leaning into the collar, so the whole machine reads horse first as it advances left. Its weight is the stone's: everything in front of it is ground.
- **Body:** fire dominates both. **The stone wheel** is charred black and split with glowing seams that open and close as it turns, the grooves of its dressing caked with sulphur crust. **The horse** is a big blind draught horse whose hide is charred and cracked in glowing seams along every harness line, the mane burned to a stub; toxin has swollen its belly and neck with gas sacs under the crust and ringed its nostrils with yellow. The harness has burned into the hide at the hocks and the sulphur has grown over both, so horse and axle-beam are cemented into one.
- **Signature shape:** the upright millstone with the horse leaning in front of it.


## V. The Storm Foundry (Fire + Lightning, Plasma)

#### The Crucible
- **Lore line:** "It was tipped to pour the great bell when the storm struck. It has been pouring ever since, and the bell is not finished."
- **Was:** the foundry's great crucible, hung from the casting crane over the bell pit, and the three pourers who tipped it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a huge iron-bound crucible hanging tilted from a crane's chains, lip down toward the company. The crane arm and chains run up out of frame and the pot fills the top half of the frame; a heavy rope of molten bronze falls from its lip into the casting pit below. It swings slowly; the weight is all in the pour.
- **Body:** a fireclay pot the size of a house, banded with iron. Fire dominates: the clay glows white-hot at the base and is cracked into a net of glowing seams, blackened and flaking higher up, and bronze slops over the lip. Lightning has **fused the iron bands and crane chains into one welded mass**, the links set rigid so the pot can never be righted, with branching Lichtenberg scorch trees climbing the clay from every band. **The three pourers hang from the long iron tipping bar**, charred black, their gauntleted hands welded to the iron, Lichtenberg ferns up their arms, hair standing stiff.
- **Signature shape:** the tilted pot on its rigid chains with the pour falling from the lip.

#### The Storm-Catcher
- **Lore line:** "He built the spire that caught the storm for the forges. He is still holding on to it."
- **Was:** the guild's engineer, who raised the iron spire and copper cables that drew the storm down into the forges.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a giant man braced at the foot of an iron spire, both arms raised in a V, each fist gripping a copper cable that runs up out of frame with the spire; legs wide, feet fused into the spire's iron footing. Cropped at the crown of the head; he leans back against the pull of the cables.
- **Body:** huge and gaunt. Fire dominates: **he is blackened all over and cracked like a hearth brick**, every crack glowing white-gold where the current runs hot inside, the glow brightest at the wrists and the chest. Lightning has ferned his skin with branching Lichtenberg scars from fingertips to feet, fused the copper cables into his palms so that they run under the skin of the forearms like veins of metal, and stood his hair and beard straight up, singed to wire.
- **Signature shape:** the arms raised in a V holding the two cables.


## VI. The Stormwrack Coast (Lightning + Water, Electrocute)

#### The Last Prize
- **Lore line:** "The richest ship the coast ever took. It has been going down for a hundred years and has not finished."
- **Was:** a great merchant carrack lured onto the rocks by the false lights, with her crew.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a broken-backed ship heeled over on the rocks broadside to the company, bows to the left. The mast stumps and upper deck are cut off by the frame top and the stern by the right edge; surf breaks white along the waterline. It never moves off the rocks; it lists and groans.
- **Body:** lightning dominates: struck again and again through her masts and ironwork, **every bolt and fitting is fused into a blackened weld**, with branching Lichtenberg scorch trees running across the planking from each one; the standing rigging has frayed and fused into stiff black rods. Water has swollen the hull and split it along the seams, the planks bulging, barnacles thick on the lower strakes and kelp hanging from the gunports. The drowned crew still stand their stations: gunners bloated at the ports with their hands fused to their guns, and three sailors at the rail welded to its iron fittings through their pikes and hooks.
- **Signature shape:** the heeled hull with its fused gunports and broken mast stumps.

#### The Wind-Seller
- **Lore line:** "She sold sailors their winds tied up in knotted cord. On this coast she only ever sold the one kind."
- **Was:** the coast's wise-woman, who sold sailors winds tied in knotted cords and whistled up storms for the wreckers.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a giant old woman standing on a sea-stack in the surf, the foot of the stack in the waves at the bottom of the frame and the top of her head cut off by the frame top. She leans forward over the edge, one foot on a lower ledge, and a long knotted cord runs from her hands up out of frame into the storm.
- **Body:** huge and gaunt. Lightning dominates: struck so often that **Lichtenberg ferns cover her from scalp to ankle in a lace of white scars**, her long grey hair stands straight up into the wind, stiff and crackling, and her fingers are fused to the knotted cord at the knuckles. Water has soaked her to the skin, pale and sea-wrinkled, with barnacles crusting her bare feet and shins.
- **Signature shape:** the bent old woman on the sea-stack with the knotted cord rising into the sky.


## Act III: The Long Cold

#### The Broadside
- **Lore line:** "She fired the first broadside of the battle. The ice has kept her loaded ever since."
- **Was:** the fleet's great three-decked ship of the line, and the gun crews of her lower deck, who were at their guns when the sea froze.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, the frozen hull side-on to the company and heeled toward it at fifteen degrees, a wall of black planking that runs off the frame at the top and at both sides. Its weight sits in the ice at the waterline, where the sea has gripped it.
- **Body:** the hull is the body. Black tarred oak planking, swollen by the sea frozen in its seams, so the strakes bulge between the frames like a drowned belly and have split along the grain (water). Rime crusts the whole windward face in a white rind, thickest at the top, and icicles longer than a man hang plumb from every port sill, channel and wale (frost). Two tiers of square gunports; at each open port a drowned gunner leans out to the waist, pale, swollen and wrinkled, frozen to the sill with his hands still on the gun or the rammer. Below the ice line the planking is crusted with barnacles and frozen kelp streamers. One gun near the centre has burst along its barrel where the ice inside it expanded, peeled open like a split reed.
- **Signature shape:** the leaning wall of hull with its two rows of square ports and muzzles.

#### The Shrouds
- **Lore line:** "The topmen were aloft when the sea froze. No one has called them down."
- **Was:** the topmen of the fleet's tallest mainmast, and the standing rigging they climbed.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, hanging. A black mainmast rises from the ice at the right of the frame and out of its top; from the unseen masthead a broad fan of shrouds and ratlines slants down to the ice at the left, a net three ranks wide with a dozen frozen men caught in it. Its weight hangs from above; the lowest topmen trail their feet across the ice.
- **Body:** the ropes are frozen as stiff as iron bar and furred white on the windward side, icicles hanging from every ratline like the teeth of a comb (frost). The topmen hang at every height, stopped at their work: one reaching for the next ratline, one hanging by an arm, one folded over the lower yard, one upside down with a foot caught. All of them drowned first: the storm's spray rose up the rigging and froze on them, so their faces and hands are swollen, wrinkled and white under a glaze and their bellies distended (water). Rime has bonded their hands and knees to the ropes so that limb and line run together without a seam.
- **Signature shape:** the slanting web of rigging hung with frozen men.


## VIII. The Sundered Rime (Fire + Frost, Thermal Shock)

#### The Sleeper Under the Hold
- **Lore line:** "The hold was built on the glacier, and the glacier on her. The miners found her ribs first."
- **Was:** a giantess of the old ice, the eldest of the jötnar, asleep under the glacier long before the hold was built. The miners' deep galleries were cut into her.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3 high and 6 long, lying on her back in the glacier floor, seen from the side. The hill of her chest and shoulder fills the frame and runs off both sides; her head is thrown back at the left edge, cropped by the frame; one arm lifts from the ice and the hand hangs open over the company. Her weight is the ground itself.
- **Body:** blue-white skin like old glacier ice, faceted in broad planes, rimed thick and hung with downward icicles along the lifted arm, the jaw and the underside of the breast (frost). Her split runs from outside to inside: the miners cut timbered adits into her chest and shoulder, and the fire coming up from below has filled those galleries, so every tunnel mouth glows molten orange from within, its edges charred and ash curling up out of it (fire). Cracks run outward from each adit across the ice skin like crazed glaze, glowing at their roots and white at their tips; steam rises wherever the fire reaches the surface.
- **Signature shape:** the hill of her chest pierced by glowing timbered tunnel mouths, with one hand hanging over it.

#### The Firstbreaker
- **Lore line:** "He swung the pick that let the fire in. He has been climbing out ever since."
- **Was:** the hold's foreman of miners, who was at the face of the deepest shaft when it broke into fire.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, climbing out of the deep shaft: only his head, shoulders, arms and chest show above the timbered rim. Both forearms are hooked over the rim and the near hand grips a pick driven into the ice for purchase. He leans out over the company, his head near the frame top, cropped at both sides by the headframe timbers. His weight hangs on his forearms.
- **Body:** a broad, heavy-shouldered miner grown vast. His split is horizontal and lies exactly at the shaft rim: below it his chest, belly and whatever is still down the shaft burn, charred black with wide molten seams and curls of ash rising (fire); above it, the glacier wind across the shaft mouth has rimed his shoulders, arms and head white, the brows and beard hung with icicles (frost). The crack rings his chest at the level of the rim, glowing and steaming. As he hauls himself higher the line stays at the rim, so each part of him that comes up cracks as it freezes.
- **Signature shape:** the head and arms hooked over the shaft rim, the pick driven into the ice.


## IX. The Hollow Barrow (Frost + Toxin, Cryo-rot)

#### The Rat King
- **Lore line:** "The barrow rats froze together at the tails. There is one of them now, and it is always hungry."
- **Was:** the plague rats of the barrow, thousands of them, which fed on the buried kingdom.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a heaving mound of rats three men high and wider than it is tall, cropped by the frame at the top and the right. Its weight sags and spreads across the floor; it moves by rolling forward as the rats at its front claw at the ground.
- **Body:** thousands of ordinary grey rats, knotted by their tails into one core. The tails, frozen and plaited together, form a pale, scaly trunk like a root ball through the middle of the mound, and the rats hang off it heads outward, all mouths. The outer rats are rimed grey-green, many frozen stiff and dead in place, and icicles hang from the mound's lower edge (frost). The living rats carry plague sores and buboes the size of eggs, their fur rotting off in patches; the dead ones wear grey-green mould fur, and small fruiting bodies sprout from the oldest (toxin).
- **Signature shape:** the low, wide mound bristling with heads, tails and bones.

#### The Dead-Cart
- **Lore line:** "It went round the barrow calling for the dead. It no longer waits for them to finish dying."
- **Was:** the plague cart that collected the barrow's dead, with its carter and the horse that pulled it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 to the top of its load, moving right to left: a heavy two-wheeled cart drawn by a gaunt draught horse, the carter walking at the horse's head with a long hook, and the heap of the dead on the cart rising out of the frame top. The weight sits on the horse's braced forelegs and the cart's wheels.
- **Body:** the heap is the creature: forty or fifty plague dead in grave linen, frozen together into one mass, rimed on the windward side with icicles hanging from the cart bed (frost), and felted through with grey-green mould that grows between the bodies like moss in a dry-stone wall, fruiting bodies clustered in the folds of the linen (toxin). Arms hang over the cart's sides and some of them reach; heads loll. The horse is starved to the bones, its hide grey and frozen, mould in the mane and tail, buboes swelling its throat so the head hangs low.
- **Signature shape:** the horse and cart under a towering heap of the dead.


## Act IV: The Stilling

#### The Procession
- **Lore line:** "The pilgrims were carrying their saint to the shrine when the storm broke. The hive carries all of them now."
- **Was:** a pilgrim procession on the plain road, and the painted wooden saint they carried on a float.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 to the saint's head, moving left at a slow sway: twelve pilgrims shoulder to shoulder under the carrying poles of a wooden float, the saint's statue standing on it above them, cropped by the frame top. The weight is in the bearers' bent knees.
- **Body:** lightning struck the saint's iron halo, the tallest thing on the plain, and ran down through the statue and the poles into every bearer: one branching fern-burn crosses all their backs and arms, carrying on from body to body as a single pattern, and the poles are scorched and fused into their shoulders, so the bearers have grown together into one mass of bowed backs and legs (lightning). The hive then filled the hollow statue with grey paper comb, which has spilled down over the float's edge onto the bearers; their skin is swollen and shifts with larvae, their muscles are locked stiff with venom and their heads loll (toxin).
- **Signature shape:** the tall saint on its float above a mass of bowed bearers.

#### The Cicada Under the Road
- **Lore line:** "It went underground the year the lightning came. It has come up to sing."
- **Was:** a cicada nymph that burrowed under the plain road the year of the first storm and fed on roots in the dark for seventeen years.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, heaving up through the broken paving of the road. The front half of the nymph is reared out of the ground, its digging forelegs hooked over the cobbles; its back has split open along its length, and the pale adult is pushing up out of the old shell with its wings still crumpled, cropped by the frame top. The weight hangs on the hooked forelegs.
- **Body:** the nymph's shell is dull black and earth-caked, with broad, spade-toothed forelegs. The split in its back follows the lightning scar where a storm struck the road above it: the edges of the split are scorched, the scar branches across the shell in Lichtenberg ferns, and the shell's seams are fused shut everywhere except along the strike (lightning). The adult rising out of it is wrong: puffy and pale, banded venom green, pustules along the abdomen, the crumpled wings thick-veined and wet, the whole body swollen with venom (toxin).
- **Signature shape:** the reared shell splitting open along its back, with the pale body rising out of it.


## XI. The Stilled Spire (Frost + Lightning, Lockdown)

#### The Stilled College
- **Lore line:** "They voted to stop the hour. The motion carried."
- **Was:** the observatory's college of astronomers, sitting in full chapter in the lecture hall when the hour stopped.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a frozen surge of some thirty bodies heaped from the lecture-hall floor up and out of the frame top, the whole mass leaning forward over the party like a wave about to break. Its weight sits in the broad base of kneeling and fallen scholars; the crest is cropped by the frame.
- **Body:** the astronomers froze in one movement, half of them rising from the benches to point at the sky, and the lightning that came down the spire ran through every hand that held brass and fused each man to the next. **A forest of arms points up out of frame**, every forefinger aimed at the same star, each arm crusted with rime on the windward side, icicles hanging straight down from elbows, sleeves and fingertips. Faces in the mass are frozen open-mouthed mid-argument, beards stiff, lips cracked and blue. Aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch across bare forearms and necks and run from one body into the next wherever they touch. The bodies at the base are pressed flat and faceted with ice like broken flagstones.
- **Signature shape:** the leaning wave of bodies and the forest of pointing arms.

#### The Great Refractor
- **Lore line:** "Pointed at the heavens for three hundred years. Someone has turned it round."
- **Was:** the observatory's great telescope, and the night assistant who was at its eyepiece when the hour stopped.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, a brass telescope tube as long as a nave, swung down on its iron mount until it lies level across the frame, aimed at the party. The pier and the rear of the tube rise out of the frame top right; the lens end hangs low over the front rank. Its weight sits in the iron pier and the lead counterweight.
- **Body:** the tube is riveted brass in tapering sections, rimed thick on the windward side, with icicles hanging along its underside in a fringe. Lightning came down the spire into the mount: the gears are fused into one lump of scorched metal, and aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch along the tube from the mount to the lens. The great objective lens is frosted milky and cracked once across. At the lower rear, **the assistant is frozen on the observing ladder**, his brow fused to the eyepiece cup, one hand still on the slow-motion handle.
- **Signature shape:** the long tube laid level across the frame on its pier.


## XII. The White Silence (Frost + Void, Absolute Zero)

#### The Unspoken Bear
- **Lore line:** "The hunters never said her name aloud. Now nothing can be said aloud."
- **Was:** the old white she-bear the hunters honoured with offerings and never hunted.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, reared on her hind legs out of a snowdrift, forepaws raised wide, mouth open in a roar. The head is cut off at the brow and the raised paws by the frame top. Her weight sinks into the haunches and the drift.
- **Body:** a vast white she-bear, her fur in long clumped strands frozen stiff and hanging plumb, every clump tipped with icicles; rime so thick on the windward side that she reads as a drift that stood up. The void has taken pieces of her: **clean round holes** through the belly, through one forepaw and through the near shoulder, the white tundra showing through them, the rims sharp as a punch. She casts no shadow. Inside the open mouth there is no tongue and no throat, only black.
- **Signature shape:** the reared bear with forepaws spread and the black open mouth.

#### The Hearth Grandmother
- **Lore line:** "She sews new faces for the ones who lost theirs. None of them fit."
- **Was:** the clan's eldest woman, keeper of the winter fire and sewer of every coat in the camp.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, seated cross-legged before a dead hearth, bent over her sewing, huge and round-backed under heaped hides. Her humped back and hood rise out of the frame top; her bent head is in frame. Her weight settles low and wide.
- **Body:** an enormous old woman with knotted, frost-split hands, her skin white with rime; the windward side of her back heaped with drifted snow; icicles hanging from her chin, her nose and the edges of the hides. The void has taken her ears, leaving clean round holes; she casts no shadow. **Her hearth fire is gone**: where it burned there is a round black hole in the ground, ringed by the hearth stones.
- **Signature shape:** the vast bent figure over the half-sewn mask, the long needle raised.


## Act V: The Hollow Heaven

#### The Iron Bull
- **Lore line:** "The heretics were put inside to be heard. They are still being heard."
- **Was:** the inquisition's iron bull, a hollow engine of execution heated from beneath, and the heretics who died in it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 at the shoulder, a hollow iron bull standing four-square on its brick hearth, head lowered and turned toward the party. The hump runs out of the frame top and the hindquarters out of the frame right. Its weight sits on four iron legs, planted.
- **Body:** riveted plates of wrought iron, blackened and flaking; the heat has split the seams open in long cracks whose edges glow violet, with black flame showing inside. Ash curls upward off its back in slow flakes. The void has bitten **clean round holes** through both flanks, and in the dark behind them the faces of the condemned press against the iron from inside, eyes open.
- **Signature shape:** the hollow iron bull standing on its hearth.

#### The Confessor
- **Lore line:** "Confess, and be forgiven. Then burn, to be certain."
- **Was:** the cathedral's confessor, who heard every heretic's last confession, and the great confessional he sat in.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a confessional the size of a chapel, black oak carved like a small cathedral in three bays: the confessor's curtained seat in the middle and a penitent's kneeler and grille on each side. Its pinnacles rise out of the frame top. The booth is rooted to the floor; the confessor inside is a seated giant who fills it like a man in a coffin.
- **Body:** behind a curtain burned to rags, the confessor sits huge and charred, black flame burning inside the booth and coming out through every hole in the carving. One charred hand rests out through the curtain on the armrest. The oak is split along the grain into violet-edged seams; the carved tracery chars and curls upward into ash. The void has opened clean round holes in the woodwork, and eyes look out of some of them. **Two penitents kneel at the side grilles**, charred to their kneelers, still whispering.
- **Signature shape:** the tall three-bayed booth with a charred hand resting out through the curtain.


## XIV. The Blighted Garden (Toxin + Void, Blight)

#### The Lady of the Bower
- **Lore line:** "She lay down in her bower to wait for spring. It came, and it has not stopped coming."
- **Was:** the fey lady whose garden it was, who lay down to sleep in her rose bower when the rot began.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, half-reclined across the back ranks on a bed of rotting blooms, propped on one elbow, her upper body three humans high even lying down. The bower's arch and her far shoulder run out of the frame top; her train spreads out of the frame right. Her weight goes down through the propping elbow into the bed.
- **Body:** a vast, slender, long-limbed woman, her skin pale and blotched with mould bloom, swelling into soft pustules along the collarbone and the propping arm. Pale-yellow bracket fungus fruits from her hip and thigh. **Below the waist she has become the bed**: gown, flowers and flesh are one mat of bloom and rot. Eyes have opened in the blooms all over the bed and the bower, and a clean round hole passes through the palm of her raised hand.
- **Signature shape:** the reclining, propped figure under the arch of the bower.

#### The Green Lion
- **Lore line:** "Clipped into a lion for her pleasure. The rot gave it a mind. It has kept the shape, and grown the teeth."
- **Was:** the great topiary at the garden gate, a yew hedge clipped into a lion rampant.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, a hedge in the shape of a rearing lion, up on its hind legs, forepaws raised to strike, rising out of the stone planter it has split. The head and the raised near paw are cut off by the frame top. Its weight goes down into the root-ball.
- **Body:** dense yew, once clipped smooth, now shaggy and rotting: whole sides gone dead and black, furred with grey-lilac mould, bracket fungus shelved along the flank. The mane bursts with pale-yellow puffballs that smoke spore. The void has clipped clean round holes through the body, and eyes of every size have opened in the foliage. The claws are thorny dead branches; the teeth in the open mouth are splintered stakes.
- **Signature shape:** the rampant lion of hedge with its clipped ball tail.


## XV. The Torn Sky (Lightning + Void, Discharge)

#### The Wheel of Thrones
- **Lore line:** "It carried the god's seat. It is still turning, looking for the weight."
- **Was:** one of the Thrones, the living wheels that bore the god's seat through heaven.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, two great wheels set one inside the other at right angles, turning slowly in the air above a broken floor. The upper rims run out of the frame top and the near rim is cut by the frame edge. It hovers, but the lowest point of the outer rim grinds along the floor and carries its weight there.
- **Body:** rims as broad as a road, gold leaf cracked over black, **studded all round with eyes** of different sizes that blink out of time. The gold is crazed into a net of cracks with black void in every one. Violet Lichtenberg burns branch across rims and spokes; the hub is a lump of metal fused by the strike that welded the axle solid. Several spokes have snapped, and one whole sector of the outer rim is missing with clean edges, darkness behind it.
- **Signature shape:** the two crossed wheels with eyes along the rims.

#### The Fourfold Beast
- **Lore line:** "It had a face for each wind, to watch the throne from every side. The war took three of them."
- **Was:** one of the living creatures that guarded the god's throne, with the faces of a man, a lion, an ox and an eagle.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, couched like a sphinx on a fallen slab of heaven, forequarters raised, great wings spread up and out of the frame top, the head turned to the party. Its weight goes down through the lion's forepaws.
- **Body:** a lion's body, eagle's wings and one broad neck carrying four faces, all in gold leaf cracked over black. Violet branching burns run along the flanks; the wing feathers are fused by the strike into stiff, scorched vanes, and **the wings are full of eyes**. Only the lion's face remains whole: the man's face is cracked away to black void, the ox's is a clean-rimmed hole, and of the eagle's head only the beak hangs on by a shard.
- **Signature shape:** the four-faced head on the couched, winged lion.
