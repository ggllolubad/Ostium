## IX. The Hollow Barrow (Frost + Toxin, Cryo-rot)

A plague-struck kingdom that buried itself alive to stop the plague. The cold preserved everything, the plague included. **Frost** keeps bodies grey and stiff, rimed in the hollows; **toxin** blooms on them anyway: grey-green mould, plague sores, fungal fur. The cast are the buried household, the barrow's guards, its sick and its scavengers. **Palette:** grey-green frost, bone, mould.

### Rulers

#### The Barrow King

- **Lore line:** "He sealed his kingdom in the barrow to stop the plague. The plague was already inside."
- **Was:** the king who ordered the burial.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, seated on a stone grave-throne, a sword across the knees.
- **Body:** a mummified king, skin grey and tight, frosted white in the hollows, **plague buboes swollen at the neck and armpits** under the frost, grey-green mould growing in the beard. A burial mask of beaten silver has slipped aside.
- **Dress and gear:** a mail shirt and a mouldering burial mantle; grave goods around him; a long sword. **The Tooth** is the seal on his signet ring, the ring the Riven Jarl wore a copy of.
- **Head:** a tarnished iron crown, a sunken face, eyes frozen open.
- **Signature shape:** the seated king with sword across knees.
- **Colour:** grey, frost white, mould green, tarnished silver.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow breath of frozen spores. Attack is a sword sweep that spreads rot.

#### The Rat King

- **Lore line:** "The barrow rats froze together at the tails. There is one of them now, and it is always hungry."
- **Was:** the plague rats of the barrow, thousands of them, which fed on the buried kingdom.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a heaving mound of rats three men high and wider than it is tall, cropped by the frame at the top and the right. Its weight sags and spreads across the floor; it moves by rolling forward as the rats at its front claw at the ground.
- **Body:** thousands of ordinary grey rats, knotted by their tails into one core. The tails, frozen and plaited together, form a pale, scaly trunk like a root ball through the middle of the mound, and the rats hang off it heads outward, all mouths. The outer rats are rimed grey-green, many frozen stiff and dead in place, and icicles hang from the mound's lower edge (frost). The living rats carry plague sores and buboes the size of eggs, their fur rotting off in patches; the dead ones wear grey-green mould fur, and small fruiting bodies sprout from the oldest (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** the grave goods it has dragged into itself. Secondary details: a tarnished silver goblet; a bent burial mask of beaten silver; a scatter of coins frozen into the fur; a bone comb; a child's shoe; a ring-mail sleeve; gnawed long bones jutting out like spines; strips of grave linen woven in as nesting; the knot of tails showing at the core as a lumped fist; dead rats picked to skeletons on the underside; plague fleas as a dark dust in the fur of the front rats; a lead coffin plate with its name gnawed off.
- **Head:** none of its own. At the front, a cluster of the largest rats with bone-coloured incisors bared.
- **Signature shape:** the low, wide mound bristling with heads, tails and bones.
- **Colour:** grey fur, mould green, bone.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant seethe, as if the mound breathes. Attack is the mound surging forward to bury the front rank, spreading Toxin.

#### The Dead-Cart

- **Lore line:** "It went round the barrow calling for the dead. It no longer waits for them to finish dying."
- **Was:** the plague cart that collected the barrow's dead, with its carter and the horse that pulled it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5 to the top of its load, moving right to left: a heavy two-wheeled cart drawn by a gaunt draught horse, the carter walking at the horse's head with a long hook, and the heap of the dead on the cart rising out of the frame top. The weight sits on the horse's braced forelegs and the cart's wheels.
- **Body:** the heap is the creature: forty or fifty plague dead in grave linen, frozen together into one mass, rimed on the windward side with icicles hanging from the cart bed (frost), and felted through with grey-green mould that grows between the bodies like moss in a dry-stone wall, fruiting bodies clustered in the folds of the linen (toxin). Arms hang over the cart's sides and some of them reach; heads loll. The horse is starved to the bones, its hide grey and frozen, mould in the mane and tail, buboes swelling its throat so the head hangs low.
- **Dress and gear:** the carter wears a long waxed leather coat stiff with frost, leather gauntlets and a vinegar-soaked rag tied over his mouth, and carries a long hooked pole. Secondary details: spoked wheels with iron tyres, one buckled; the horse's collar, hames and blinkers; a slit sack of lime spilling white powder down the cart's side; chalked tallies of the dead on the tail-board; a shovel lashed to the side; a rope thrown over the heap and frozen into it; a wooden rattle hung on the shaft; an unlit lantern on a pole; a small, child-sized bundle on the top of the heap; the tail-chain dragging; frozen ruts behind the wheels.
- **Head:** the horse's long, bowed head in its blinkers, the bit fixed in frozen lips; beside it, the carter's grey face over the rag, mould in his beard.
- **Signature shape:** the horse and cart under a towering heap of the dead.
- **Colour:** grey-green frost, bone linen, mould green.
- **Motion:** idle is the horse stamping while the heap shifts and its hanging arms sway. Attack is the cart rolling forward a rank as the arms of the heap seize a hero and drag them up into it.

### Elites

#### Barrow Thane

- **Lore line:** "A lord buried with his household. He still keeps them in line."
- **Was:** a lord of the king's court.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, a commanding stance, a flail in hand.
- **Body and dress:** a draugr lord in rusted lamellar, a fur mantle stiff with frost, **a flail of chains and frozen weights**, mould on the face.
- **Head:** a spangenhelm with a mail face-curtain.
- **Signature shape:** the raised flail.
- **Colour:** rust, grey-green, frost.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow scan of his household. Attack is a flail blow.

#### Rot Witch

- **Lore line:** "She keeps the barrow's dead from rotting, and their plague from dying."
- **Was:** the court's embalmer.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.8, bent over a bowl.
- **Body and dress:** a gaunt woman in grey wrappings, hands green to the wrist, a bowl of embalming salts, bundles of herbs, a bone needle and thread.
- **Head:** a hood of grave linen, sharp eyes, mould growing at the hairline.
- **Signature shape:** the bent figure over a steaming bowl of cold salts.
- **Colour:** grey linen, mould green.
- **Motion:** idle is stirring the bowl. Attack is a hurled handful of plague salts.

#### Barrow Chaplain

- **Lore line:** "He gave the last rites to everyone in the barrow. He is giving them again."
- **Was:** the royal household's chaplain, who said the rites in the barrow's chapel and over its dead.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, walking slowly in procession, a tall processional cross on a pole held upright in both hands and rising well above his head. Weight leaning into the pole at each step.
- **Body:** a heavy old priest, frozen grey and rimed in the hollows (frost). The toxin is pushed hard in one place: a plague bubo has swollen the side of his neck to the size of a loaf, so his head is pushed over and tilts away from it; mould grows at his lips and in his stubble (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a cope, the great semicircular cape of a priest in procession, frozen stiff into a wide, rigid bell around him; an alb beneath it, its hem frozen in folds and stained with mould to the knee. Secondary details: the cope's embroidered band furred with mould; its clasp at the chest in tarnished silver; a stole hanging below the cope, one end torn; the processional cross with a small crucifix figure, its gilt worn dull; a holy-water bucket hooked on the near wrist, its water frozen solid round the sprinkler; a rosary of bone beads; a book of rites chained to the girdle, its pages frozen together; a reliquary pendant; candle stubs pushed into the girdle; frost hanging in drips from the cross's arms.
- **Head:** a tonsured head pushed over by the bubo, the mouth slack and murmuring, the eyes frosted, mould in the stubble.
- **Signature shape:** the tall cross above the wide, stiff bell of the cope and the tilted head.
- **Colour:** bone alb, grey-green mould, dull gilt on the cross (holy).
- **Motion:** idle is a slow processional step, the lips moving. His action is to strike the frozen holy water across the field: his allies gain Block and the heroes take Toxin.

#### Pest-House Watchman

- **Lore line:** "He nailed the sick into their houses. Then someone nailed him into his watch-box."
- **Was:** the watchman of the barrow's pest-house, who kept the sick shut in.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.1, big and square, a tall plank watch-box strapped to his back like a shell, its roof boards jutting over his head; an iron door-bar held two-handed as a club, ready to swing. Feet planted wide, weight low.
- **Body:** a big man, frozen grey (frost). Nail holes go through both palms, where his hands were nailed to the sides of the box before he tore free; grey-green mould blooms out of each wound and along the joints of his fingers, and buboes in his armpits push the seams of his jack open (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a padded leather jack, studded and split at the seams; an iron kettle hat under the box's roof. Secondary details: the watch-box of oak planks with its front torn away, the bent nails still pointing inward round the opening; the plague cross daubed in red on the box's roof board, a plea for mercy lettered beneath it; the iron door-bar with a hasp and staple still on one end; a ring of pest-house keys at the belt; a leather apron of nails; a claw hammer through the belt; a padlock hanging from a chain at the hip; a vinegar rag tied over nose and mouth; a lantern hook on the box's corner; mould growing in the box's inner corners; frozen breath on the rag.
- **Head:** a broad, frozen face under the kettle hat and the box roof, the rag over the mouth, the eyes sunk and frosted.
- **Signature shape:** the man in an upright box worn like a shell, with the door-bar raised.
- **Colour:** grey-green, bone, the red plague cross (doom).
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy shift that knocks the box against his shoulders. Attack is a door-bar blow that pins a hero in place (Stun).

#### Mould Wight

#### Gravedigger

- **Lore line:** "He dug graves in the barrow until there was no floor left. Then he started on the walls."
- **Was:** the household's gravedigger, who buried the first plague dead inside the barrow.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, both hands on a long-handled spade, one foot up on the blade's shoulder to drive it down, his weight leaning hard over the handle.
- **Body:** a stringy old labourer with long arms and a stoop, the skin grey and rimed in the hollows (frost); small pale toadstools and mould sprout from his shoulders and back where grave earth has stuck to him (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a ragged wool tunic hitched up through the belt; earth-caked leggings tied at the knee with twine. Secondary details: the wooden spade shod with iron at the blade, a notch cut in its handle for every grave; a mattock slung on the back; a coil of lowering rope over the shoulder; a sack of lime at the hip; a knotted rag on his head; frozen clods of earth caked on his boots and shins; a broken finger set crooked.
- **Head:** a long grey face, a toothless mouth, frost in the stubble.
- **Signature shape:** the long spade with his foot on the blade.
- **Colour:** grey-green, bone, black earth.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow scoop of frozen earth thrown over the shoulder. Attack is a chop with the spade's edge.

#### The Swollen

- **Lore line:** "The plague sores kept growing in the cold. They are waiting for the thaw."
- **Was:** a laundress of the royal household.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, lurching sideways, the near arm held out stiff from the body by a bubo in the armpit the size of a man's head; the far hand drags a wooden laundry beetle. Weight on the far leg.
- **Body:** a stout woman. The toxin has pushed one gland past all reason: the armpit bubo is huge and taut, its skin grey-green and veined black (toxin), rimed white where the frost lies on it and frozen hard as a bladder of ice (frost). Smaller buboes swell her neck and groin; mould grows at her lips.
- **Dress and gear:** a linen shift with the sleeves rolled, the near sleeve torn open by the swelling; a sacking apron; wooden pattens. Secondary details: the beetle, a flat wooden bat worn smooth; a wet sheet frozen stiff over the far shoulder; a headcloth; lye scars across the knuckles; a peg bag at the apron; frozen suds crusted on the apron.
- **Head:** a round, sagging face under the headcloth, the neck swollen on one side, the eyes frosted.
- **Signature shape:** the arm held out stiff by the great swelling under it.
- **Colour:** grey-green skin, bone linen, black veining.
- **Motion:** idle is a lurching sway while the bubo creaks. Attack is a beetle swing; when she dies, the bubo bursts and spreads Toxin.

#### Court Fool

- **Lore line:** "He kept the court laughing while the doors were sealed. It is the only joke he remembers."
- **Was:** the king's fool.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.9 standing (0.55 seated), sitting slumped on the steps of the king's dais where he sat down after the last joke: knees drawn up and splayed, forearms resting on them, the marotte lying across his lap, the hooded head sunk forward between the shoulders. Weight down through the hips into the stone step.
- **Body:** small and wiry, frozen where he sat to wait: frost has settled on every upturned surface, the shoulders, knees and the crown of the hood, and icicles hang from the elbows and the hood's points (frost); grey-green mould fur in the armpits and at the groin, and sores at the corners of the grin (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** a motley tunic of chequered halves, stiff with frost; parti-coloured hose, one leg torn; pointed shoes. Secondary details: a hood with three limp points hanging forward over his face, each ending in a little bell packed with ice; the marotte, a stick topped with a carved head of the king in his crown, lying face-up in his lap; a string of tarnished bells at the wrist; a slapstick of two laths in the belt; a frayed ruff; a patch on one elbow; face paint cracked by the frost; the dais steps under him, rimed grey-green, a dropped silver cup frozen to the step beside his foot.
- **Head:** a painted face cracked like old plaster, a fixed grin, mouldy teeth, looking up from under the hanging hood points.
- **Signature shape:** the small slumped seated figure with the three-pointed hood hanging forward and the marotte across the knees.
- **Colour:** bone and grey-green motley, mould green, frost white.
- **Motion:** idle is the head lifting slowly and the marotte giving one half-hearted jingle before he slumps again. Attack is a joke that causes stress.

#### The King's Hawk

- **Lore line:** "It was buried hooded on its block at the king's right hand, so he would not ride out alone. It is still waiting for the hood to come off."
- **Was:** the Barrow King's falcon, set on its perch among the grave goods and sealed into the barrow alive with the household.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.5 to the top of the hood, a hooded falcon standing upright on a turned wooden block perch, the wings half opened and drooping over the block's edge, the blind head turned toward the company. Weight on the clenched feet.
- **Body:** a big falcon, once white barred with grey. Frost has stiffened it: rime crusts the windward wing, the feathers are frozen into hard, overlapping plates, and icicles hang from the tail and the jesses (frost). The toxin has gone to the feet and the crop: the feet are swollen into hard, sore-crusted pads, so the talons sink into the perch's leather cushion, the crop bulges under the throat like a bubo, and the feathers moult out in tufts furred with grey-green mould (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** the falconer's furniture. Secondary details: **a hood of tooled leather** with a small plume on top, its braces tied; leather jesses on both legs with tarnished silver rings engraved with the king's name; a leash tied to the iron ring of the block; a pair of hawk bells on the legs, packed with ice; the block of turned oak on an iron spike driven into the barrow floor; a dead mouse left on the block as an offering, rotted to fur; mould furring the hood's plume; a broken flight feather; droppings frozen white down the block's side.
- **Head:** the hood over the eyes, the hooked beak open below it, frost on the cere, the head turning in small, sharp jerks.
- **Signature shape:** the hooded falcon on its block, with the plume rising from the hood.
- **Colour:** grey-green mould, frost white feathers, tarnished silver.
- **Motion:** idle is the hooded head jerking toward every sound while the bells clink dully. Attack is a bating lunge to the end of the leash, the swollen talons raking a hero (Toxin).

#### Sexton Beetles

- **Lore line:** "They bury what they eat. In the barrow they never run short of either."
- **Was:** the burying beetles of the barrow earth, which bury small dead things to feed their young.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a heap 0.5 high: a dozen beetles, each the size of a fist, working over a mound of grave earth from which a frozen hand reaches up.
- **Body:** glossy black beetles with two bone-white bands across the wing cases and clubbed antennae. Frost has slowed them: rime on their backs, one frozen on its back with its legs in the air (frost). Mould fur sprouts from the joints of the older beetles, and a pale fungal stalk grows out of a dead one (toxin).
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: the hand, grey and frozen, wearing a ring; mites riding on the beetles' backs; frozen clods; scraps of grave linen; a beetle dragging a finger bone; a scatter of pale eggs in the loosened earth.
- **Head:** none; the beetles' antennae all point toward the company.
- **Signature shape:** the frozen hand rising from the beetle heap.
- **Colour:** black, bone bands, mould green.
- **Motion:** idle is digging, earth flicking. Attack is the swarm pouring over a hero's feet and dragging at them (Toxin, Slow).

#### Barrow Mason

- **Dress and gear:** a leather apron white with frozen lime mortar; a sleeveless tunic; leg wraps. Secondary details: the hod, a wooden V-trough on a pole, loaded with three squared stones; a trowel through the belt; iron chisels in a leather roll; a plumb bob on a cord; a set square tucked into the apron; his mason's mark cut on a lead tag at the neck; a cloth cap; a split thumbnail.
- **Head:** a square, heavy head, a broken nose, stone dust frozen in the beard, the eyes frosted.
- **Signature shape:** the loaded hod over the shoulder, and the mallet.
- **Colour:** grey-green, bone-white lime, grey stone.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping a stone with the mallet. Attack is a mallet blow; his action walls a hero in (Stun).
