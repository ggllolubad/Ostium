## XIV. The Blighted Garden (Toxin + Void, Blight)

A fey garden, once the most beautiful place in the world. It rotted, and the rot grew minds. **Toxin** covers bodies in fungus, bloom and spore; **void** gives the growth eyes and leaves holes where things used to be. Everything here is overgrown: armour, deer, gardeners. Eyes in the leaves are expected (void world). **Palette:** sick pastel blooms (pink, lilac, pale yellow) over black loam, with eyes.

### Rulers

#### The Antlered Mother

- **Lore line:** "The garden's heart: a great stag grown through with fungus and eyes. She is the garden now."
- **Was:** the white stag of the fey garden.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, standing among her roots, head raised, antlers filling the top of the frame.
- **Body:** a colossal white hind (antlered, though a doe), her hide split open along the flanks where pastel bracket fungus and blooms grow out; **dozens of eyes open in the fungus**, blinking out of time; roots spread from her hooves into the ground and up toward the sky. **The Tooth** is lodged in the fork of her antlers.
- **Head:** a long, gentle deer face with eyes too many and too human.
- **Signature shape:** the vast branching antlers hung with growth.
- **Colour:** bone white, pastel blooms, black loam.
- **Motion:** idle is the eyes blinking in waves. Attack is a spore exhalation that stops healing.

#### The Lady of the Bower

- **Lore line:** "She lay down in her bower to wait for spring. It came, and it has not stopped coming."
- **Was:** the fey lady whose garden it was, who lay down to sleep in her rose bower when the rot began.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, half-reclined across the back ranks on a bed of rotting blooms, propped on one elbow, her upper body three humans high even lying down. The bower's arch and her far shoulder run out of the frame top; her train spreads out of the frame right. Her weight goes down through the propping elbow into the bed.
- **Body:** a vast, slender, long-limbed woman, her skin pale and blotched with mould bloom, swelling into soft pustules along the collarbone and the propping arm. Pale-yellow bracket fungus fruits from her hip and thigh. **Below the waist she has become the bed**: gown, flowers and flesh are one mat of bloom and rot. Eyes have opened in the blooms all over the bed and the bower, and a clean round hole passes through the palm of her raised hand.
- **Dress and gear:** the bower, an arch of woven willow grown over with rotting pink and lilac roses, the canes split and furred with mould; her gown of lilac velvet rotted into the moss; a girdle of linked pale stones; a circlet of white flowers browned at every edge; a hand mirror in the hanging hand, fogged with mould; rings sunk into swollen fingers; a lute dropped beside her and grown through with fungus; a spilled cup; her hair spread over the cushions and grown with moss; cushions split and bursting with spore; a book of hours swollen to twice its size; fallen petals heaped in drifts at the bed's foot.
- **Head:** a long, lovely face with one cheek swollen under a soft bloom of mould, heavy-lidded eyes half closed, a dreamy smile; hair a black mass knotted with flowers.
- **Signature shape:** the reclining, propped figure under the arch of the bower.
- **Colour:** lilac and pink blooms, pale-yellow fungus, black loam.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow sigh that lifts a cloud of pollen from the bed; the eyes in the blooms open after it. Attack is lifting the hand and blowing across the palm; a drift of spore settles over the party and puts the back rank to sleep.

#### The Green Lion

- **Lore line:** "Clipped into a lion for her pleasure. The rot gave it a mind. It has kept the shape, and grown the teeth."
- **Was:** the great topiary at the garden gate, a yew hedge clipped into a lion rampant.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, a hedge in the shape of a rearing lion, up on its hind legs, forepaws raised to strike, rising out of the stone planter it has split. The head and the raised near paw are cut off by the frame top. Its weight goes down into the root-ball.
- **Body:** dense yew, once clipped smooth, now shaggy and rotting: whole sides gone dead and black, furred with grey-lilac mould, bracket fungus shelved along the flank. The mane bursts with pale-yellow puffballs that smoke spore. The void has clipped clean round holes through the body, and eyes of every size have opened in the foliage. The claws are thorny dead branches; the teeth in the open mouth are splintered stakes.
- **Dress and gear:** the gardeners' work still on it: the iron training frame it was clipped over, showing through the dead patches; a ladder still leaning against one leg; a pair of shears stuck in the flank; a gardener's rope harness snagged on the shoulder; the clipped ball at the tip of the tail; the stone planter split open, roots spilling out over the rim; a lead plant label hanging from a wire; a gardener's body tangled in the near paw's branches; a stone ball from the gatepost lying cracked at its feet; dead clippings heaped around the planter; a bird's nest of rotted twigs in the mane.
- **Head:** the clipped lion head, mostly cropped: in frame, only the lower jaw, a gaping square mouth lined with stake teeth, moss hanging from it.
- **Signature shape:** the rampant lion of hedge with its clipped ball tail.
- **Colour:** near-black dead yew, grey-lilac mould, pale-yellow puffballs.
- **Motion:** idle is the whole hedge creaking and swaying, the eyes blinking in waves. Attack is a downward swipe of the near paw, thorn claws first.

### Elites

#### The Gardener

- **Lore line:** "He keeps the garden. He prunes what grows wrong. You are growing wrong."
- **Was:** the head gardener.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, holding long shears open.
- **Body and dress:** a gardener's smock and apron overgrown with moss, a wide straw hat with flowers growing from it, **enormous hedge shears**; fungus grows from his back like a cloak.
- **Head:** a kind, empty face with flowers in the eye sockets.
- **Signature shape:** the open shears and the straw hat.
- **Colour:** moss green, straw, pastel flowers.
- **Motion:** idle is snipping the air. Attack is a shear cut that removes a buff.

#### Blight Dryad

- **Lore line:** "She was the spirit of an oak. The oak is dead. She is not."
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.2, tall and thin, branching arms.
- **Body:** a woman's shape of dead grey oak wood, cracked and hollow, bracket fungus on her shoulders, **a void hole where her heart-wood was**, leaves of rot.
- **Head:** a carved-bark face with sap tears.
- **Signature shape:** the branching arms of a dead tree.
- **Colour:** dead grey wood, pastel fungus.
- **Motion:** idle is creaking sway. Attack is roots bursting from the ground.

#### The Lady's Fool

- **Lore line:** "She kept him to make her laugh. The rot thinks he is very funny."
- **Was:** the lady's jester.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.8, lanky and capering on one leg, the other knee kicked high, one arm flung up with his bauble, the other hand on his hip. Weight on the ball of the planted foot.
- **Body:** long-limbed and loose-jointed. The mould has bloomed in patches that follow the diamonds of his motley, as if the cloth had soaked through. The toxin has pushed one thing hard: **his throat has swollen into a soft pink fruiting gall** that rings his neck like a ruff and lifts his chin. Eyes have opened in the moss patches on his chest and sleeve.
- **Dress and gear:** motley in pink and lilac diamonds, half rotted to holes and moss; **a three-pointed fool's cap whose bells have become pale-yellow puffballs**, puffing spore when he shakes his head; a bauble, a carved fool's head on a stick, its little face grown over with fungus; a slapstick at the belt; bells on a garter below one knee, swollen shut; pointed shoes with curled toes, one sole gone; tights torn at one knee with mould showing through; a wilted flower behind one ear; a ruff collar half swallowed by the gall; a purse of dice; a lady's ribbon knotted round one wrist.
- **Head:** a long, rubbery face, the grin pulled too wide by the swelling, painted eyebrows, one cheek blooming lilac.
- **Signature shape:** the three-pointed cap with puffball bells over the high-kicking caper.
- **Colour:** pink and lilac motley, pale-yellow puffballs, black loam.
- **Motion:** idle is a little jig, shaking the cap so spore puffs from each point. Attack is a cartwheel to a hero and a slap of the bauble; the spores make the hero's next action strike an ally.

#### The Truffle Boar

- **Lore line:** "It was kept to find the truffles. It found all of them. It ate all of them."
- **Was:** the garden's truffle boar, a fey hog larger than any mortal one, kept to root out truffles under the oaks.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9 at the shoulder, massive in the forequarters, head down, snout ploughing a furrow through the loam, hindquarters lower. Weight forward on the front legs.
- **Body:** a vast bristled boar. **The bristle ridge along its spine has become a crest of fruiting bodies**, a dense ridge of pale-yellow and lilac mushrooms from skull to tail; the flanks swell with soft pink galls the shape of truffles; the hide peels away in patches to mould. Eyes have opened along the base of the fungal crest.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather collar with an iron ring and a broken lead rope trailing; an iron bell on the collar packed with moss; a gardener's notched tag in one ear; a broken spear shaft in the shoulder from an old hunt; a muzzle strap torn off and hanging from one tusk; long yellowed tusks crusted with loam; crushed truffles in the jaws; loam caked to the knees; a torn ear; a scar across the snout.
- **Head:** a long snout with a wet, cracked disc, small sunken eyes, torn ears, the mouth frothing spore.
- **Signature shape:** the low, heavy boar with the mushroom crest along its back.
- **Colour:** black loam, pale-yellow and lilac fungus, pink galls.
- **Motion:** idle is rooting, the snout ploughing and the crest quivering. Attack is a tusking charge into the front rank that leaves spore in the wound.

### Cast

#### Fungal Husk

- **Lore line:** "A gardener the mushrooms grew into."
- **Was:** an under-gardener.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, stiff, head bent sideways.
- **Body and dress:** a gardener's clothes split by mushrooms growing from the shoulders, back and head; a trowel in hand.
- **Signature shape:** the figure with a mushroom crown.
- **Colour:** brown cloth, pastel caps.
- **Motion:** idle is puffing spores. Attack is a spore burst.

#### Spore Deer

- **Lore line:** "The fungus grows from its antlers. It is not the deer making the decisions now."
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3, head low.
- **Body:** a deer with fungus branching from its antlers like coral, eyes clouded, moving jerkily.
- **Signature shape:** the fungal antlers.
- **Colour:** fawn, pastel fungus.
- **Motion:** idle is a twitch. Attack is an antler charge.

#### Thorn Knight

- **Lore line:** "Sworn to guard the garden. The garden grew through his armour."
- **Was:** a knight of the garden's guard.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, sword drawn.
- **Body and dress:** plate armour **with briars growing through the gaps and out of the visor**, thorns along the arms, a rose blooming from the helm.
- **Signature shape:** the knight bristling with briars.
- **Colour:** dark steel, briar green, pastel rose.
- **Motion:** idle is thorns creaking. Attack is a sword cut; thorns punish attackers.

#### Moth Witch

- **Lore line:** "The eyes on her wings are real. They blink."
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, wings spread wide.
- **Body:** a pale woman with great moth wings whose eye-spots are **real eyes that blink**; furred antennae; dust falling from the wings.
- **Signature shape:** the spread wings with eyes.
- **Colour:** dusty lilac, pale yellow, black eyes.
- **Motion:** idle is wings slowly opening and closing. Attack is a wing beat of dust that curses.

#### Root Horror

- **Lore line:** "The garden's roots grew a mouth."
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6, a mass rising from the ground.
- **Body:** a knot of black roots rising from the soil, opening into a ring-shaped mouth of thorn teeth; eyes in the bark around it.
- **Signature shape:** the root mass with a gaping mouth.
- **Colour:** black, pastel buds.
- **Motion:** idle is roots writhing. Attack is a root grab and bite.

#### Scarecrow

- **Lore line:** "It was put up to frighten the birds. The rot put something in the straw."
- **Was:** the scarecrow of the garden's kitchen plot.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.1, hung on its pole with the arms spread along the crossbar, the pole's foot torn out of the ground and dragging. It leans forward on the pole, which takes its weight like a third leg.
- **Body:** a sacking body stuffed with straw, the straw rotted black and grown through with pale fungus; mould blooms along the seams and spore drifts out of them; pustules of fungus bulge the sacking. Eyes have opened in the straw at the cuffs, and the void has punched a clean round hole through the chest.
- **Dress and gear:** an old gardener's coat with half the buttons gone and a patch on one elbow; rope bindings at the wrists, tying the arms to the crossbar; a battered felt hat with a crow's feather; a dead crow tied to one arm by the feet; a tin cup hanging from a nail that clinks; a rotting turnip in one pocket; rag strips fluttering from the crossbar.
- **Head:** a stuffed sack with a stitched face, the mouth sewn in black thread, mould growing out of the eye holes.
- **Signature shape:** the cross of the arms spread along the crossbar.
- **Colour:** grey sacking, black straw, pale-yellow and lilac fungus.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow swing on the pole, straw dropping. Attack is lurching forward on the pole and clubbing with the crossbar arm.

#### Blight Snails

- **Lore line:** "Slow. Very slow. They have all the time the garden has."
- **Was:** the garden's snails.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a cluster about 0.5 high, crawling over and out of a toppled garden urn.
- **Body:** a dozen fat snails, each as big as a fist, their shells blotched pink and lilac with mould and swelling with galls, their bodies grey and pustular. Their eye stalks end in real eyes, too many on some snails. A couple of shells have clean round holes in them with nothing inside.
- **Dress and gear:** the stone urn, tipped over and cracked; a lead garden label; dead flower stems in the urn's mouth; trails drying to a dull crust; one snail riding on another; a broken shell; a leaf eaten to lace.
- **Head:** soft grey heads, each with its crowd of eye stalks.
- **Signature shape:** the toppled urn crawling with snails.
- **Colour:** grey bodies, pink and lilac shells, black loam.
- **Motion:** idle is the eye stalks swaying. Attack is the cluster sliding over a hero; where it touches, the skin rots (Blight).

#### Flower-Strewer

- **Lore line:** "She strewed petals for the lady to walk on. The lady has not walked in a long time."
- **Was:** a little girl of the household who strewed petals in front of the lady.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, stepping forward, one hand flinging petals, a basket on the other arm. Weight on the front foot, mid-skip.
- **Body:** a little girl whose skin is mottled with mould bloom, with a soft swelling of fungus at the side of her neck. Her hair has grown into fine fungal threads; the flinging arm is grown with buds along the forearm. Eyes have opened among the petals in her basket.
- **Dress and gear:** a pink linen pinafore over a lilac dress, both rotting at the hem; a willow basket heaped with petals that have become flakes of spore; a wreath of browned flowers; a ribbon trailing from the wreath; bare feet black with loam; a petal stuck to one cheek.
- **Head:** a small, solemn, heart-shaped face, one cheek swollen, eyes downcast.
- **Signature shape:** the small girl with the basket and the flung arm.
- **Colour:** pink pinafore, lilac dress, pale-yellow spore.
- **Motion:** idle is skipping in place, scattering a few petals. Attack is a flung handful of petals that settle on a hero and bloom.

#### Mole-Catcher

- **Lore line:** "He was paid a penny a mole. The moles have stopped dying."
- **Was:** the garden's molecatcher.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, crouched low over a molehill, a long narrow mole spade raised in both hands to stab down. Weight in the bent knees.
- **Body:** wiry and weathered; a hump of soft lilac fungus has grown on his back; his hands are swollen with pustules; a clean round hole passes through his left palm. **The string of moles at his belt has grown together** into one furred, fungal mass that twitches.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather jerkin over a smock; leather knee pads; the long, narrow-bladed mole spade; the string of moles, his tally; iron mole traps clipped to the belt; a felt hat with a moleskin band; a bag of bait worms; gaiters caked in loam to the knee.
- **Head:** a squint, a long nose, a stubbled jaw, earth in the wrinkles.
- **Signature shape:** the crouch with the long, narrow spade raised and the moles hanging.
- **Colour:** black loam leather, grey moles, lilac fungus.
- **Motion:** idle is listening to the ground, head cocked. Attack is a sudden two-handed spade stab; the ground gives way under a hero.

#### Pond Swan

- **Lore line:** "The swans were the lady's. They still bite anyone who is not."
- **Was:** one of the lady's swans from the lily pond.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.3 with the neck raised, standing on the bank, wings half raised and arched in threat, neck drawn back in an S. Weight on the broad, splayed feet.
- **Body:** a big white swan, feathers clumped and peeling with grey mould; the breast swollen with a soft pink gall, and in the gall a few real eyes that blink; brackets of pale-yellow fungus shelved along the arched wings; webbed feet blotched and peeling; a clean round hole through one wing.
- **Dress and gear:** a lily stem trailing from one foot; an iron ownership ring on the leg; rotted pond weed hanging from the neck; a broken flight feather sticking out; black loam on the feet; a notch out of the beak.
- **Head:** a long beak with a black knob, open in a hiss, the tongue grey.
- **Signature shape:** the arched S-neck between half-raised wings.
- **Colour:** bone white, grey mould, pink gall, pale-yellow fungus.
- **Motion:** idle is a hiss and a slow ruffle. Attack is a lunge and a beating of wings that knocks a hero back a rank.

#### Orchard Picker

- **Lore line:** "The fruit is ripe. It has been ripe for a very long time."
- **Was:** a picker in the lady's orchard.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, a sturdy woman walking with a tall three-legged orchard ladder over one shoulder and a picking basket on the other hip. Weight on the back foot under the ladder.
- **Body:** solid and square; her hands swollen with fruit-like galls, the fingers soft and peeling; a hanging cluster of rotten fruit has grown out of her shoulder under the ladder. Eyes have opened in the fruit.
- **Dress and gear:** **the ladder**, three-legged and tapering to a point above her head; a hooked picking pole tucked through its rungs; an apron with a big front pocket; a kilted skirt; a kerchief; sleeves rolled; wooden pattens; a willow basket of swollen fruit splitting with mould; a knife on a cord.
- **Head:** a broad, tired face under the kerchief, one cheek swollen with a pink gall.
- **Signature shape:** the tall, tapering ladder over the shoulder.
- **Colour:** lilac kerchief, pink fruit, pale-yellow mould, black loam.
- **Motion:** idle is shifting the ladder; a fruit drops and bursts. Attack is throwing a rotten fruit that bursts in spore.

#### Beekeeper

- **Lore line:** "The bees are gone. The skep is still full."
- **Was:** the garden's beekeeper.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, stout, standing side-on with a straw skep held under one arm and a smoking torch in the other hand, head pushed forward. Weight settled on both feet.
- **Body:** heavy-set; the skin pocked with swollen sting pustules; the skep has grown into his arm, its straw rooted into the sleeve. It is full of spore instead of bees, and an eye looks out of its entrance hole.
- **Dress and gear:** **a woven willow face cage** over a cloth hood, shaped like a basket; a long linen smock; leather gloves to the elbow; the straw skep; a torch of smouldering rag on a stick, whose smoke is spore; a honey knife; a wax-sealed pot at the belt; a feather brush; gaiters to the knee.
- **Head:** the basket-woven face cage, the face only dimly seen behind the weave, moss growing through it.
- **Signature shape:** the round wicker head and the skep under the arm.
- **Colour:** undyed linen, pale-yellow straw, lilac mould.
- **Motion:** idle is patting the skep, which puffs. Attack is thumping the skep; a cloud of spore pours out over a hero.
