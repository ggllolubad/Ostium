# Act III: The Long Cold

## VII. The Icebound Fleet (Frost + Water, Freeze)

A war fleet that sailed north to kill a leviathan. The sea froze mid-battle and the crews are still at their posts. **Frost** coats every windward surface in rime, hangs icicles from chins and elbows and stops bodies mid-gesture; **water** leaves them drowned underneath, pale and swollen inside the ice. The cast are sailors, whalers and officers of the fleet, and the animals of the ice. **Palette:** white sea, black hulls, frozen rigging (grey-blue), with no warm colour.

### Rulers

#### The Leviathan in the Ice

- **Lore line:** "The fleet sailed to kill it. It froze the sea around them all."
- **Was:** the leviathan itself.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 6, mostly beneath the ice: a vast head and one eye visible through a broken sheet of sea-ice.
- **Body:** a serpent-whale of black hide and white scars, frozen into the sea with the ships around it. Broken masts, harpoons and boarding nets are embedded in its back. **The Tooth** is one of its teeth, the only one that is pale stone.
- **Head:** a long jaw lined with ice-white teeth, one huge eye looking up through the ice.
- **Signature shape:** the jaw and eye rising through cracked ice.
- **Colour:** black hide, white ice, grey-blue.
- **Motion:** idle is the eye following the company; cracks spread. Attack is a jaw breaking up through the ice and freezing a hero.

#### The Broadside

- **Lore line:** "She fired the first broadside of the battle. The ice has kept her loaded ever since."
- **Was:** the fleet's great three-decked ship of the line, and the gun crews of her lower deck, who were at their guns when the sea froze.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, the frozen hull side-on to the company and heeled toward it at fifteen degrees, a wall of black planking that runs off the frame at the top and at both sides. Its weight sits in the ice at the waterline, where the sea has gripped it.
- **Body:** the hull is the body. Black tarred oak planking, swollen by the sea frozen in its seams, so the strakes bulge between the frames like a drowned belly and have split along the grain (water). Rime crusts the whole windward face in a white rind, thickest at the top, and icicles longer than a man hang plumb from every port sill, channel and wale (frost). Two tiers of square gunports; at each open port a drowned gunner leans out to the waist, pale, swollen and wrinkled, frozen to the sill with his hands still on the gun or the rammer. Below the ice line the planking is crusted with barnacles and frozen kelp streamers. One gun near the centre has burst along its barrel where the ice inside it expanded, peeled open like a split reed.
- **Dress and gear:** the gunners wear striped shirts and tarred canvas trousers, with kerchiefs knotted over their ears against the noise; rammers, sponges and handspikes are frozen in their grip. The guns are black iron with rimed muzzles, their wooden tompions hanging on cords. Secondary details: port lids hanging open on one hinge; the channels with deadeyes, lanyards and chain-plates, the shrouds rising out of frame; a boarding net frozen over the side in a stiff sheet; a broken harpoon from the leviathan buried in the wale; three shot-holes plugged with wooden bungs; a rope entry ladder of wooden battens with a boarder frozen halfway up it; a gun captain's linstock, its match long dead; a powder horn on one gunner's shoulder; a sheet of ice bridging two ports like a membrane; a fender of old cable hanging on a rope; the ship's name carved on a board, half under rime.
- **Head:** none. The gun captain at the centre port serves: an older, grey-whiskered face with a clay pipe frozen in the teeth, looking down along his gun.
- **Signature shape:** the leaning wall of hull with its two rows of square ports and muzzles.
- **Colour:** black hull, ice white, grey-blue rime and rigging.
- **Motion:** idle is the ice creaking along the waterline while the gunners breathe frost and a port lid swings. Attack is a ripple of cracks down the hull as the ice inside each barrel expands and drives the shot out, port by port, with a burst of frost from every muzzle and no flame.

#### The Shrouds

- **Lore line:** "The topmen were aloft when the sea froze. No one has called them down."
- **Was:** the topmen of the fleet's tallest mainmast, and the standing rigging they climbed.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, hanging. A black mainmast rises from the ice at the right of the frame and out of its top; from the unseen masthead a broad fan of shrouds and ratlines slants down to the ice at the left, a net three ranks wide with a dozen frozen men caught in it. Its weight hangs from above; the lowest topmen trail their feet across the ice.
- **Body:** the ropes are frozen as stiff as iron bar and furred white on the windward side, icicles hanging from every ratline like the teeth of a comb (frost). The topmen hang at every height, stopped at their work: one reaching for the next ratline, one hanging by an arm, one folded over the lower yard, one upside down with a foot caught. All of them drowned first: the storm's spray rose up the rigging and froze on them, so their faces and hands are swollen, wrinkled and white under a glaze and their bellies distended (water). Rime has bonded their hands and knees to the ropes so that limb and line run together without a seam.
- **Dress and gear:** tarred jackets, checked shirts and canvas trousers cut off at the shin; bare feet blackened by frost. Secondary details: a lower yard crossing near the frame top with its footropes and stirrups, two men laid along it; a topsail furled and frozen into a hard, folded slab, its gaskets still tied; deadeyes and lanyards where the shrouds meet the channel; wooden blocks and sheaves frozen mid-run; a marlinspike swinging on a lanyard from one man's wrist; a sheath knife on a cord; a canvas tar bucket hung from a ratline, a frozen spill down the ropes below it; a lookout's spyglass jammed in the rigging; a pigtail queue of tarred hair stiff as a stick; a parted line hanging loose and frozen into a curl; a knitted cap snagged on a ratline; an empty bosun's chair dangling on its line.
- **Head:** none of its own. The topmen's faces all turn down toward the company, mouths open as if calling a sighting.
- **Signature shape:** the slanting web of rigging hung with frozen men.
- **Colour:** grey-blue frozen rope, ice white, black tar and mast.
- **Motion:** idle is the whole web creaking and swaying while icicles drop and shatter. Attack is a topman letting go and dropping onto a hero, marlinspike first, before the web hauls him back up on his lanyard.

### Elites

#### The Ice-Wed Captain

- **Lore line:** "Married to the sea, and the sea froze at the wedding."
- **Was:** the fleet's admiral.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, at a frozen ship's wheel, sword drawn.
- **Body and dress:** a long naval coat stiff with ice, epaulettes crusted white, a drawn sabre with an icicle blade; **a bridal veil of frozen sea-spray** hangs from his tricorn, and a wedding ring of ice on his hand.
- **Head:** a proud drowned face under the ice, beard frozen solid.
- **Signature shape:** the tricorn with its frozen veil.
- **Colour:** dark navy gone grey, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is a stiff salute. Attack is a sabre cut that freezes.

#### Frost Selkie

- **Lore line:** "She lost her skin to a sailor. She took his in return."
- **Was:** a seal-woman of the northern sea.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, half-crouched on the ice.
- **Body:** a pale, thin woman with grey seal-like eyes and webbed hands, **wearing a sailor's flayed skin as a cloak**, tattoos still visible on it. Frost on her shoulders.
- **Head:** long black wet hair, a wide dark-eyed face.
- **Signature shape:** the crouching figure wrapped in a human-skin cloak.
- **Colour:** pale grey, black hair, the cloak's pallid skin.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow seal-like sway. Attack is a lunge and bite.

#### The White Bear

- **Lore line:** "It crossed the ice to eat the frozen crews. It fell through where the ice was thin, and climbed out anyway."
- **Was:** a great white bear of the sea-ice, drawn to the fleet by the smell of the dead.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9 at the shoulder and 3 long, on all fours and coming on over the ice, the long neck stretched forward and the head hung below the shoulders, swinging from side to side; the near forepaw is lifted mid-stride. Weight in the massive shoulders and the broad forepaws.
- **Body:** a huge old male, drowned under the ice and frozen when it climbed back out. The fur hangs wet and plumb, clumped into heavy ropes that froze where they hung, so the belly, forelegs and throat are a fringe of icicled tags (water, then frost). The belly is swollen and sagging with the sea it swallowed; the lips, gums and nose are blue-black, the tongue swollen and grey. Rime crusts the windward side thickly, and the bear froze as it walked, so the lifted forepaw is locked a little too high and too long in the stride. Bald patches on the flanks show pale, wrinkled skin.
- **Dress and gear:** none of its own; it wears the fleet's attempts to kill it. Secondary details: a whaler's harpoon broken off in its near shoulder, a long tail of frozen line trailing from it to the ground; a torn boarding net snagged over the far shoulder and forearm, its knots iced; a cutlass slash healed into a white seam across the muzzle; a musket ball lodged as a lump under the chest skin; one claw broken to the quick; ice balls frozen into the fur of the feet; a sailor's jacket sleeve frozen in its jaws, hanging; old rake scars from other bears along the flank; slabs of sea-ice welded to the hind feet; frozen drool hanging from the lower lip in a stiff cord.
- **Head:** a long, narrow bear's head carried low and forward, small ears flattened under ice, the lips drawn back from bone-coloured teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the long, low bear on all fours with its head hung below the shoulders.
- **Colour:** grey-white fur, blue-black lips and nose, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is the low head swinging from side to side, water dripping from the fur and freezing as it falls. Attack is a lunge and a downward blow of one forepaw that drives a hero down into the ice.

#### The Ship's Surgeon

- **Lore line:** "He took the frozen fingers to save the hand, and the hand to save the arm. He has not finished saving anyone."
- **Was:** the flagship's surgeon, who worked the cockpit on the orlop deck through the long freeze, taking off frostbitten limbs.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, tall and gaunt, stooped at the upper back from years under low deck beams. The near arm is raised to shoulder height with the saw; the far hand is forward and open, as if holding a patient still. Weight on the forward foot.
- **Body:** long-armed and rawboned, the skin grey-white and loose; drowned, so the hands are swollen and wrinkled and the nails blue-black (water). Frost stopped the saw arm mid-stroke: it is locked at the shoulder, rimed thick on its windward side, icicles hanging from the elbow and the saw frame (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a short wool coat with the sleeves cut off at the elbow over a canvas smock, the shirt sleeves rolled; a heavy leather apron from chest to shin, stiff as a board with blood frozen black and cracking where it folds. A steel bow-framed amputation saw. Secondary details: a leather instrument roll buckled across the chest like a bandolier, holding knives, bone nippers and a trephine; a screw tourniquet hanging from a strap at the hip; a leather bite-stick on a cord round the neck, chewed through; a string of frozen fingers threaded on tarred twine at the belt like a rosary; a pewter bleeding bowl hooked to the belt; a corked bottle of spirit; a bandage roll tucked in the apron string; a stub of tallow candle stuck to the apron bib; sawdust frozen to the shoes; a knife nick across the far thumb.
- **Head:** bald and long-jawed, spectacles with one lens frosted over, frost in the eyebrows, the face calm and absorbed.
- **Signature shape:** the bow saw raised beside the stooped head, over the board-stiff apron.
- **Colour:** grey-white skin, blood frozen black, steel grey-blue.
- **Motion:** idle is wiping the saw slowly on the apron. Attack is one long sawing stroke across a hero's limb that leaves it frozen.

### Cast

#### Frozen Whaler

- **Lore line:** "It still hunts whales. Anything large and slow will do."
- **Was:** a fleet harpooner.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, harpoon cocked to throw.
- **Body and dress:** a whaler's thick wool coat and fur hat crusted with rime; a barbed harpoon on a line; one arm frozen permanently in the throwing pose.
- **Head:** a bearded face, icicles hanging from the beard.
- **Signature shape:** the raised harpoon.
- **Colour:** grey wool, ice white, iron.
- **Motion:** idle is a creaking stance. Attack is a harpoon throw that drags.

#### Rime Gulls

- **Lore line:** "They followed the fleet out and froze with it. They thawed first."
- **Was:** gulls that followed the fleet.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a flock about 0.6 across.
- **Body:** three or four gulls with frost-stiffened, ragged feathers, ice on their wingtips, sharp beaks.
- **Signature shape:** the cluster of beating wings.
- **Colour:** white, grey, black wingtips.
- **Motion:** idle is wheeling. Attack is a pecking dive.

#### Ice Eel

- **Lore line:** "It hunts through holes in the ice."
- **Was:** an eel of the frozen sea.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5, rising from a hole in the ice.
- **Body:** a pale, almost translucent eel with frost along its back and needle teeth; the rest of it below the ice.
- **Signature shape:** the eel rising from the ice hole.
- **Colour:** translucent white-blue.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow rise and dip. Attack is a strike from the hole.

#### Drowned Deckhand

- **Lore line:** "Still ready to board. Still singing."
- **Was:** a sailor of the boarding crews.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, cutlass raised, mouth open in a shanty.
- **Body and dress:** a sailor's jacket and striped trousers stiff with ice, a cutlass, a boarding pistol in the belt; drowned and pale under the frost.
- **Head:** an open, singing mouth with frozen breath, a knitted cap.
- **Signature shape:** the raised cutlass and open mouth.
- **Colour:** dark jacket, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is swaying to a song. Attack is a cutlass cut.

#### Walrus Brute

- **Lore line:** "It hauled out on the ice beside the ships and never left."
- **Was:** a bull walrus.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.6, hauled up on its flippers.
- **Body:** a huge scarred bull walrus, rime on its back, one tusk broken and replaced with a sailor's cutlass jammed in the gum.
- **Signature shape:** the bulk with its tusks.
- **Colour:** grey-brown hide, ivory, ice.
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy snort. Attack is a crushing tusk lunge.

#### Powder Monkey

- **Lore line:** "He was sent below for powder as the sea froze. He is still bringing it."
- **Was:** a ship's boy of the gun deck, who ran cartridges from the magazine to the guns.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6, caught mid-run and leaning hard forward, the cartridge case hugged to his chest in both arms, the rear foot off the ground. Weight over the front foot.
- **Body:** a thin boy of ten, drowned: face and hands puffy and wrinkled white, lips blue-black (water). Frost stopped him running: his hair is frozen streaming back, and icicles hang from his elbows, his chin and the hem of his jacket (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a sailor's jacket far too big for him, the sleeves rolled four times; canvas trousers cut off at the knee; bare feet black with frostbite. The case is a lidded cylinder of wooden staves bound with iron hoops, a rope handle on the lid. Secondary details: a knitted cap pulled to the eyebrows; a kerchief knotted at the throat; a rope belt with a gunner's priming wire pushed through it; a patch on one knee; a tin whistle on a string; frozen spray streaked down his back; a bruise on the cheek under the frost.
- **Head:** hollow cheeks, eyes wide, the mouth open on a frozen pant.
- **Signature shape:** the small running figure hugging a cylinder.
- **Colour:** dark jacket, grey-white skin, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is running in place with stiff, cracking strides. His action is to run a cartridge to an ally, which loads its next attack.

#### Sea Lice

- **Lore line:** "Sailors are sewn into their hammocks for burial. The lice do not mind the stitching."
- **Was:** the pale scavenging sea lice of the northern sea floor, which strip a body in a night.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, a heap 0.5 high and 1.0 long, low to the ice.
- **Body:** a sailor's burial hammock, sewn shut from head to foot with sailmaker's twine, swollen with the body and the water inside it (water) and frozen hard (frost), split open along its seam. From the split pours a seething mound of pale sea lice, each the size of a thumb, curled and many-legged, translucent grey with a dark gut line. On the windward side the outermost lice froze where they crawled, into a white crust.
- **Dress and gear:** secondary details: two round shot sewn into the foot of the hammock to sink it; the rows of stitches, the last one through the dead man's nose where the canvas puckers; a bare ankle bone showing through the split; a canvas name tag stitched on, unreadable; a strand of frozen kelp; frozen drips hanging from the seam; lice half stuck in the frozen crust, still moving.
- **Head:** none.
- **Signature shape:** the long sewn bundle split open, lice spilling from it.
- **Colour:** grey canvas, translucent grey-white lice, black gut lines.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant seething crawl. Attack is the heap surging over a hero's legs and stripping them (Bleed).

#### Capstan Gang

- **Lore line:** "They were weighing anchor when the sea froze. They have not stopped pushing."
- **Was:** a capstan crew of the flagship, heaving round to raise the anchor, with the fiddler who kept their time.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.5 tall at the fiddler's head, 1.8 wide. A capstan drum torn up from the deck, four sailors leaning into its bars with legs driving, bodies nearly level with the ground; a fiddler sits on the drum head. The weight is low and spread through eight braced legs.
- **Body:** four drowned sailors, swollen and sagging (water), frozen to the bars at the hands and chests so they cannot let go, rime thickest on their backs and icicles hanging from their bellies and elbows (frost). The drum is a waisted oak capstan gone grey, an iron pawl ring at its foot and a jagged collar of torn deck planking still fixed round its base. The anchor cable wraps the drum three times and runs out to the right, frozen solid as a tree trunk; it drags behind as the gang walks the drum forward.
- **Dress and gear:** striped and checked shirts, canvas trousers, bare feet. Secondary details: knitted caps; tarred pigtail queues; a kerchief tied over one man's head; one capstan bar snapped short, its man still leaning on the stub; the iron pawls; the cable's frozen, splayed fibres; the fiddler's fiddle gone grey with rime, the bow frozen mid-stroke; a tar bucket frozen to the drum; a short nipper rope hanging from the cable.
- **Head:** the fiddler's: a thin old face, head cocked to the instrument, eyes shut.
- **Signature shape:** the drum with radiating bars and leaning men, a fiddler on top.
- **Colour:** black and grey-blue, ice white, oak gone grey.
- **Motion:** idle is the men straining, ice cracking at their feet, the fiddler's bow moving a few frozen inches. Attack is the gang heaving round, so the drum swings and its bars sweep the front ranks.

#### The Sailmaker

- **Lore line:** "He sews the dead into their hammocks. The last stitch goes through the nose, to be sure."
- **Was:** the fleet's sailmaker, whose second trade was sewing up the dead for burial.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, an old man bent under a sewn hammock carried over the near shoulder, walking slowly; the free hand holds a long sail needle out like a dagger. Weight on the bent forward leg.
- **Body:** thick wrists, a callused palm, a broad old back. Drowned, so the face is swollen and the lips blue-black (water); the beard is frozen into one plate with icicles at its point, and rime coats his windward side (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a canvas smock and tarred trousers under a canvas apron with pockets. The bundle is a body sewn in its hammock, frozen as stiff as a log, two round shot at its foot. Secondary details: a sailmaker's palm strapped round the needle hand, its iron thimble plate worn bright; a ball of tarred twine on a lanyard; a wooden fid pushed through the belt; a lump of beeswax on a cord; a horn needle case at the apron; a bench hook hanging from the apron string; a knitted cap; a twine end pinched between his lips.
- **Head:** a broad, frozen old face, squinting, the twine end in his teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the long sewn bundle across the shoulder.
- **Colour:** grey canvas, ice white, black tar.
- **Motion:** idle is shifting the bundle's weight while the needle hand twitches. Attack is a needle stab that stitches a hero in place (Stun).

#### Fleet Marine

- **Lore line:** "He was posted to keep order on deck. Order has been kept."
- **Was:** a marine, one of the fleet's sea-soldiers.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, at the charge, musket and bayonet levelled at waist height with the body angled behind it, feet wide, rear knee bent. Weight driving forward off the back foot.
- **Body:** a sturdy, square soldier, drowned and swollen at the face and hands (water); frost locked him in the thrust, rimed down his windward side, an icicle hanging from the bayonet point (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a short wool jacket with turned-back facings and pewter buttons; white cross-belts gone grey and stiff; buttoned gaiters to the knee. A long musket of dark wood and iron, its barrel plugged with ice. Secondary details: a black leather cartridge box on the hip; a wooden canteen on a cord; an empty bayonet scabbard; a leather stock at the throat; a round hat with the brim turned up on one side and a rimed cockade; a missing button; frozen spray across the belts; a torn gaiter.
- **Head:** square-jawed and clean-shaven, the hair clubbed in a frozen queue.
- **Signature shape:** the long levelled musket and bayonet.
- **Colour:** dark jacket, grey cross-belts, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is a rigid stance with small, creaking adjustments. Attack is a bayonet lunge.

#### The Galley Cook

- **Lore line:** "The stores ran out in the second month. The pot did not."
- **Was:** the flagship's cook, an old one-legged sailor kept on for the galley.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, broad and heavy, leaning on an oak peg leg, a great iron kettle hooked over the far forearm and a cleaver in the near hand. Weight on the good leg, the peg planted wide for balance.
- **Body:** a fat old sailor gone to drowned bloat, belly and jowls sagging over the apron, the skin white and wrinkled (water); the near leg gone below the knee. The broth in the kettle has frozen solid with the ladle standing in it, and things are frozen in the broth: a knuckle bone, a twist of what might be a sleeve (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** a stained canvas smock and a leather apron stiff with grease and frost, a neckcloth, sleeves rolled. Secondary details: the peg leg strapped on with leather and finished with an iron ring at the tip; a ring of bread-room keys at the belt; a meat fork pushed through the apron string; a salt horn on a cord; a knitted cap; an old burn scar across the near forearm; frozen sweat beads on the brow.
- **Head:** jowly, the nose broken, small wet eyes under frosted brows.
- **Signature shape:** the peg leg and the kettle on the arm.
- **Colour:** grey-white skin, black iron, ice white.
- **Motion:** idle is stirring the frozen pot with the cleaver's tip. Attack is a cleaver chop.

#### Ice-Edge Orca

- **Lore line:** "It learned to slide onto the ice for seals. Sailors stand closer to the edge."
- **Was:** an orca of the northern sea, drowned under the closing ice like everything else there that breathed air.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4 tall at the fin and 2.0 long, slid up on its belly onto the ice edge, the front half on the floe and the tail still in the black water, head lifted and jaws open. Weight on the belly and the braced flippers.
- **Body:** black hide with white patches, drowned and swollen, the belly sagging in pale, wrinkled folds and the skin peeling in grey sheets along the flank (water). The tall dorsal fin, curled over at the tip, is rimed on its windward face and hung with icicles along its trailing edge; the blowhole is sealed with a plug of ice (frost).
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: rake scars across the flank; a notch bitten out of the fin; a length of boarding net snagged on a tooth; barnacles crusted at the corner of the jaw; an ice crust on the flippers; frozen spray down the back; a broken harpoon head lodged behind the flipper.
- **Head:** a blunt black head with its white side patch, the jaw open on rows of conical teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the tall curled dorsal fin over open jaws at the ice edge.
- **Colour:** black hide, white patches, grey-blue water.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow rock on the ice edge. Attack is a surging slide forward and a bite that drags a hero toward the water.
