## V. The Storm Foundry (Fire + Lightning, Plasma)

A smiths' guild-city that forged bells, armour and automata. The forge fire caught a storm and kept it; the foundry sits inside a thunderhead and the smiths never stop. **Fire** blackens and glows; **lightning** scars metal and flesh with branching burns and fuses bronze. The cast are smiths, slaves, apprentices and their machines. **Palette:** bronze, white-gold burns and sparks, soot.

### Rulers

#### The Anvil Titan

- **Lore line:** "The foundry's first work. Its makers built it to strike the anvil forever. It found something softer."
- **Was:** the foundry's first automaton, a bronze giant built to work the great anvil.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, standing at the great anvil, hammer raised, cropped at the shoulders.
- **Body:** a riveted bronze colossus, plates scorched black, seams glowing white-gold where the storm runs inside. **A lightning rod spike rises from its back**, its tip fused into a blackened knob, and branching white-gold burns run from its foot down the back plates and along the hammer arm, where the hand is fused to the haft. Furnace grilles in the chest show a fire.
- **Dress and gear:** a hammer the size of a cart. **The Tooth** is riveted into its chest plate as a maker's seal.
- **Head:** a faceless bronze helm with a single slit glowing white.
- **Signature shape:** the raised hammer and the back spike.
- **Colour:** bronze, soot black, white-gold seams and burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow, rhythmic tap on the anvil. Attack is the full hammer blow, with a strike arcing down from the spike through the hammer into the hero.

#### The Crucible

- **Lore line:** "It was tipped to pour the great bell when the storm struck. It has been pouring ever since, and the bell is not finished."
- **Was:** the foundry's great crucible, hung from the casting crane over the bell pit, and the three pourers who tipped it.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a huge iron-bound crucible hanging tilted from a crane's chains, lip down toward the company. The crane arm and chains run up out of frame and the pot fills the top half of the frame; a heavy rope of molten bronze falls from its lip into the casting pit below. It swings slowly; the weight is all in the pour.
- **Body:** a fireclay pot the size of a house, banded with iron. Fire dominates: the clay glows white-hot at the base and is cracked into a net of glowing seams, blackened and flaking higher up, and bronze slops over the lip. Lightning has **fused the iron bands and crane chains into one welded mass**, the links set rigid so the pot can never be righted, with branching Lichtenberg scorch trees climbing the clay from every band. **The three pourers hang from the long iron tipping bar**, charred black, their gauntleted hands welded to the iron, Lichtenberg ferns up their arms, hair standing stiff.
- **Dress and gear:** the pourers' leather aprons burned to rags; their leather gauntlets fused to the bar; one pourer's wooden clog fallen into the pit; an iron skimming rod hooked over the lip; the guild's mark stamped on the top band; a crane hook and pulley block above; a chalk tally of pours on a band; a leather water bucket on a chain hanging from the bar, steaming; a hammer wedged under a band; frozen slag drips down the side like candle wax; below, the bell pit's clay runner cup, and the crown of the half-cast bell showing in the pit; a broken wooden ladder leaning against the pit's edge.
- **Head:** none. The lip is its mouth.
- **Signature shape:** the tilted pot on its rigid chains with the pour falling from the lip.
- **Colour:** bronze pour, white-gold sparks on the fused chains, soot.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow swing on the chains, the pour stretching and the pourers' legs dangling. Attack is the crucible tipping further: a wave of molten bronze over the front ranks, sparks cracking down the stream.

#### The Storm-Catcher

- **Lore line:** "He built the spire that caught the storm for the forges. He is still holding on to it."
- **Was:** the guild's engineer, who raised the iron spire and copper cables that drew the storm down into the forges.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3.5, a giant man braced at the foot of an iron spire, both arms raised in a V, each fist gripping a copper cable that runs up out of frame with the spire; legs wide, feet fused into the spire's iron footing. Cropped at the crown of the head; he leans back against the pull of the cables.
- **Body:** huge and gaunt. Fire dominates: **he is blackened all over and cracked like a hearth brick**, every crack glowing white-gold where the current runs hot inside, the glow brightest at the wrists and the chest. Lightning has ferned his skin with branching Lichtenberg scars from fingertips to feet, fused the copper cables into his palms so that they run under the skin of the forearms like veins of metal, and stood his hair and beard straight up, singed to wire.
- **Dress and gear:** a guild master's leather coat charred away below the waist; a heavy bronze guild chain fused into the chest; a leather tool belt with dividers, a plumb bob and a folding rule; a leather tube of scorched drawings; iron cable clamps bolted round both forearms; glazed clay insulators on the cables above his fists; copper wire wound round one upper arm; a burnt notebook on a cord; a ring of keys; a guild seal ring; one boot burst open by the fused foot; the spire's riveted iron footing plate; a lightning-split weathervane fallen at his feet.
- **Head:** tilted back, jaw locked open, eyes burned blank, the hair a crown of standing wire.
- **Signature shape:** the arms raised in a V holding the two cables.
- **Colour:** soot black, white-gold glow and sparks, bronze chain and clamps.
- **Motion:** idle is a constant tremor, sparks running down the cables into his fists. Attack is a heave on the cables: the storm strikes down from above the frame onto the heroes he has marked.

### Elites

#### The Sparkblind Smith

- **Lore line:** "The master smith watched the fire until the sparks took his eyes. He works by the ring of the iron now."
- **Was:** the guild's master smith, blinded by sparks at his own anvil.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.3, broad, a hammer and tongs.
- **Body and dress:** a giant in a scorched leather apron, burn-scarred arms like pillars, a forge-glove on one hand holding glowing tongs. Lichtenberg scars spread across the shoulders. Fire has peppered the forearms and chest with old spark pits.
- **Head:** a heavy face behind **a riveted iron visor welded shut over the eyes**: lapped plates, a row of rivets along each join and beaded weld seams, cut with **one narrow horizontal slit that glows white-gold** with the forge heat inside. Nothing shows through the slit; there is no eye in this design. **Below the visor's lower edge the cheeks are scarred**: pitted spark burns and shiny weld-splash scars running down to a heavy jaw, the beard singed short.
- **Signature shape:** the blank riveted visor with its glowing slit, and the tongs.
- **Colour:** leather brown, soot, white-gold on the tongs and in the visor slit.
- **Motion:** idle is turning a hot bar in the tongs, the head cocked to listen to it. Attack is a hammer blow that brands.

#### Talos Sentinel

- **Lore line:** "A guardian of hollow bronze, filled with molten ichor. One vein in the heel holds it all."
- **Was:** the foundry gate guardian.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.4, a statue-like stance, spear and round shield.
- **Body:** a hollow bronze warrior in classical form, molten ichor glowing through cracks; **a single bronze nail plugs a vein at the heel**, a visible weak point.
- **Head:** a crested bronze helm with an expressionless face.
- **Signature shape:** the classical crested silhouette with spear.
- **Colour:** bronze, molten orange.
- **Motion:** idle is ichor dripping from the heel. Attack is a spear thrust.

#### The Wire-Drawer

- **Lore line:** "He drew bronze into wire for the automata. The storm runs down every inch he ever made."
- **Was:** a guild wire-drawer, who pulled hot metal through a draw-plate into wire for cables, chain and automata.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.1, a big man leaning back hard as if hauling on a line, heavy draw-tongs held in both fists close to his chest, one foot braced forward. Heavy coils of wire hang from his shoulders.
- **Body:** broad and long-armed. Fire dominates: forearms and chest blackened and split with glowing seams, the hands burned smooth and shining. Lightning has **fused wire into him**: loops of bronze wire are welded into the skin of his shoulders and back in bands, with branching scars spreading from every weld, and his hair stands in stiff, singed tufts.
- **Dress and gear:** **great coils of drawn bronze wire** hung over both shoulders like a mantle, the ends trailing to the floor, one end fused into his back; heavy draw-tongs with a hooked jaw; a steel draw-plate full of graded holes hanging on his chest by a chain; a leather apron scorched through; leather gauntlets, one burst at the knuckles; a belt of wire spools; a tallow pot for greasing the wire; leather leggings; iron-shod clogs; a turn of wire wrapped round one knee as a brace; a journeyman's bronze guild badge.
- **Head:** a bald, soot-black head, ears singed, jaw clenched, eyes squinting behind smoked-glass goggles on a strap.
- **Signature shape:** the heavy wire coils over the shoulders and the draw-plate on the chest.
- **Colour:** bronze wire, soot, white-gold sparks.
- **Motion:** idle is the coils ticking as they cool, sparks creeping round them. Attack is a length of live wire whipped round a hero and hauled tight.

#### The Dray Horse

- **Lore line:** "It hauled the bells out of the pits on a sledge. The storm shod it, and it will not be unshod."
- **Was:** a foundry dray horse that hauled castings and ore on sledges through the guild-city.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0 at the head, a huge draught horse rearing half up on its hind legs, forelegs raised, harness straps flying. Weight on the hind quarters.
- **Body:** a heavy draught horse with feathered legs. Fire dominates: the coat is burned off in streaks, the hide beneath blackened and split in glowing seams along the shoulders and flanks, the feathered fetlocks singed into black curls. Lightning entered through **its iron shoes, which have fused up into the hooves**: Lichtenberg trees climb all four legs from the hooves, and the mane and tail stand out stiff and crackling. The bronze harness fittings are welded to the hide.
- **Dress and gear:** a heavy collar of leather and straw, burst at one side; bronze hame knobs fused into the neck; broken trace chains hanging; a bridle with blinkers, one torn away; a bronze medallion on the brow; the broken shaft of a sledge dragging from the harness; a scorched wool saddle cloth; a nosebag hanging empty; a bell on the collar; the guild brand on the hip; a split hoof bound with wire.
- **Head:** a long, heavy head, nostrils flared and sooted, lips drawn back, one eye wild and the other closed by a burn.
- **Signature shape:** the rearing horse with its stiff, standing mane.
- **Colour:** soot black hide, white-gold sparks at the shoes, bronze fittings.
- **Motion:** idle is stamping and snorting, sparks flying from the shoes. Attack is a rear and a crash of both forehooves that throws sparks across the rank.

#### Salamander

- **Lore line:** "The smiths swore it lived in the fire. Now it does."
- **Was:** a black-and-yellow fire salamander that wintered in the damp of the foundry's log and charcoal stacks and ran out of the wood when it was thrown on the forge; the smiths took it for the fire's own creature and left it scraps on the hearthstone.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.3 tall and 0.6 long, low on four splayed legs on a hearthstone, the broad head raised toward the company and the thick tail curled round behind. Weight in the splayed forelegs.
- **Body:** a thick-bodied salamander, glossy black with yellow blotches. Fire dominates: the skin has dried and split along the back into a net of seams that glow white-gold from inside, so every yellow blotch burns like a coal; the toes are charred and flaking, and ash curls off the ridge of the spine. Lightning struck it where it lay on the anvil's foot: a fern of pale branching scars runs from the forefeet up across the near flank, the tail tip is fused into a bead of bronze it drags behind it, and iron filings from the anvil floor cling to its back and stand up in short, stiff bristles.
- **Dress and gear:** none. Secondary details: the bronze bead on the tail; the standing filings; old skin half shed and curling off one hind leg; a drop of solder fused to one forefoot; a missing toe; a split across the throat; a scorched notch in the tail's upper edge; a bent guild nail it lies curled round; a crust of soot on the belly.
- **Head:** a broad, flat head with small black eyes, the swollen glands behind them cracked and glowing, the mouth a wide, closed line.
- **Signature shape:** the low, splayed body with its glowing blotches and the bronze bead dragging from the tail.
- **Colour:** soot black, white-gold blotches and seams, bronze bead.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow blink while a lick of sparks runs over the cracked back. Attack is a darting bite; the blotches flare and the bitten hero burns.

#### Mould-Sitter

- **Lore line:** "He sat for the guild's statue of a working man. The guild was short of time, and cast from life."
- **Was:** a labourer paid to sit as the model for a bronze statue, still inside the clay mould when the storm struck the pour.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, stiff in the statue's pose: chest out, fists on hips, chin raised. He walks in stiff jerks, dragging the casting base on his feet.
- **Body:** **he is inside a cracked clay mould.** Fire baked the clay black and poured bronze in round him; where the shell has fallen away, bronze skin shows over the flesh, glowing in the cracks. Lightning fused shell, bronze and man together, with branching scorch trees across the clay and sparks snapping between the iron bands.
- **Dress and gear:** the two-part clay mould, bound with iron bands and twisted wire round the chest and thighs; **the clay pour cup**, a funnel still standing on the crown of his head with a frozen bronze drip down one side; iron vent rods jutting from both shoulders like quills; a strip of his own work shirt showing at a crack; wax runners hanging from the elbows; bare bronze feet fused to the casting base; the guild maker's stamp on the base.
- **Head:** the mould's face half fallen away, showing a bronze face, mouth open, eyes shut; the funnel rising from the top of the head.
- **Signature shape:** the funnel on the head and the vent rods jutting from the shoulders.
- **Colour:** soot black clay, bronze, white-gold sparks.
- **Motion:** idle is a stiff sway, clay flakes falling. Attack is a heavy, stiff-armed blow, molten bronze dripping from the funnel.

#### Coke-Heaver

- **Was:** a furnace labourer who carried coke from the yards to the forges in a basket on his back.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, a huge stooped labourer under a tall wicker coke basket, a broad iron shovel in both hands held like a spade-axe. Leaning forward, weight on the front foot.
- **Body:** massive in the back and shoulders. Fire dominates: **the coke in the basket smoulders and has burned through the wicker into his back**, which is blackened and split in glowing seams. Lightning, striking his iron shovel, has stood his hair and beard upright and branched scars up his neck and across his face.
- **Dress and gear:** a tall wicker basket heaped with glowing coke, the wicker charred; shoulder straps of rope over leather pads; a broad iron coke shovel, its blade scorched; a sackcloth smock with burned holes; leather leggings bound with cord; wooden clogs; a water skin; a rag hanging loose below his mouth.
- **Head:** a broad, flat, black-smudged face, eyes squeezed shut, hair and beard upright.
- **Signature shape:** the tall smouldering basket on the stooped back, with the shovel held forward.
- **Colour:** soot, white-gold coke glow, bronze.
- **Motion:** idle is a shrug to settle the basket. Attack is a shovel swing that throws burning coke across a rank.

#### Nail-Wife

- **Lore line:** "She made nails by the thousand at her little hearth. When the storm came through, they all came back to her."
- **Was:** a nail-maker, one of the women who forged nails at small hearths in the back streets of the guild-city.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, a short woman leaning to her right, the heavy, bristling left arm and side held out and low, a nail hammer in the right hand. Weight on the clean side.
- **Body:** fire is the larger mark: her whole body is blackened and the skin cracked with glowing seams from ankle to jaw. Lightning struck her hearth and **welded hundreds of iron nails onto her left arm and side**, points out, bristling like a hedgehog's back, each nail scorched black, with branching scars spreading from every weld; her hair is frizzed upright.
- **Dress and gear:** a leather apron with a pocket of nails; a nail-header, an iron block with a hole, on a cord at the belt; a small hammer; a wool kirtle kilted up; a shawl charred at the edges, pinned with a nail; a bucket of nails at her feet; clogs; a rag over her nose.
- **Head:** a lined face, a row of nails clamped in her mouth, hair standing under a scorched cap.
- **Signature shape:** the bristling, nail-studded arm and side.
- **Colour:** soot, grey iron, white-gold sparks.
- **Motion:** idle is hammering a nail head on the header. Attack is a swing of the bristling arm; nails break off and stay in the hero.

#### Guild Warden

- **Lore line:** "He kept the guild's true measure and fined anyone short of it. He has fined the storm twice."
- **Was:** a warden of the smiths' guild, who inspected work, checked weights and measures and fined bad craft.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, very upright and stiff, the guild's bronze standard-yard held upright before him in both hands like a sceptre, chin up. Weight even on both feet; he does not bend.
- **Body:** a lean, elderly master. Fire has blackened his face and hands and split them in glowing seams. Lightning has **fused his chain of office into his chest**: the bronze plates are sunk into the skin, Lichtenberg ferns spreading from each link across the chest and up the throat; his white hair stands straight up.
- **Dress and gear:** a short guild livery gown, belted, with slashed sleeves charred at the edges, over a doublet and hose; the bronze standard-yard rod stamped with marks; the chain of office, bronze plates each cast with a guild mark; a pouch of nested bronze weights; a seal matrix on a cord; a ledger of fines chained to the belt; a touchstone in a pouch; spectacles on a cord; a flat cap fused down to the scalp.
- **Head:** an old, narrow face, thin lips, white hair standing up around the cap.
- **Signature shape:** the long rod held upright before the stiff figure.
- **Colour:** bronze, soot, white-gold sparks.
- **Motion:** idle is tapping the rod on the floor and peering. Attack is a rap of the rod that brands a guild mark into a hero.

#### Automaton Tender

- **Body:** sturdy. Fire has blackened her forearms and split the backs of her hands in glowing seams. Lightning has branched scars from the key hand across the shoulder and **fused the oil can into her other hand** by its handle.
- **Dress and gear:** **the crank key**, a long iron bar with a square socket at one end and a T-handle at the other; a long-spouted bronze oil can; a leather apron black with oil; a wool kirtle with the sleeves rolled; a tool roll of spanners at the belt; rags stuffed in the apron; a ring of small winding keys on a chain; a scorched headscarf; wooden clogs; a burn bandage round one wrist.

- **Signature shape:** the long crank key over the shoulder.
- **Colour:** soot, bronze, white-gold sparks.
- **Motion:** idle is oiling the key's socket. Attack is a swing of the key; her other action winds up a machine ally.
