## XI. The Stilled Spire (Frost + Lightning, Lockdown)

An observatory whose astronomers tried to stop time. They mostly succeeded: the clocks still tick inside the ice. **Frost** freezes bodies mid-motion; **lightning** fuses brass and leaves aurora-coloured burns. The cast are astronomers, guards, pilgrims who came to watch, and the observatory's machines. **Palette:** brass, pale blue, aurora green.

### Rulers

#### The Last Hour

- **Lore line:** "The great clock at the top of the spire. When it strikes twelve, the world stops. It is one minute to."
- **Was:** the observatory's great astronomical clock.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4, a clock face filling the frame, its mechanism exposed.
- **Body:** a vast brass clock face with zodiac and star rings, frosted over; **the minute hand stands one minute before twelve**; gears, chains and pendulum exposed, the brass fused into lumps at the pivots and branched with aurora-green burns. A frozen astronomer is caught in the gears. **The Tooth** is the clock's central pivot.
- **Head:** the clock face itself, with a sun-and-moon dial like a face.
- **Signature shape:** the round clock face with hands almost at twelve.
- **Colour:** brass, pale blue frost, aurora green.
- **Motion:** idle is the second hand ticking and stopping. Attack is a chime that pushes heroes back in the turn order.

#### The Stilled College

- **Lore line:** "They voted to stop the hour. The motion carried."
- **Was:** the observatory's college of astronomers, sitting in full chapter in the lecture hall when the hour stopped.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 3, a frozen surge of some thirty bodies heaped from the lecture-hall floor up and out of the frame top, the whole mass leaning forward over the party like a wave about to break. Its weight sits in the broad base of kneeling and fallen scholars; the crest is cropped by the frame.
- **Body:** the astronomers froze in one movement, half of them rising from the benches to point at the sky, and the lightning that came down the spire ran through every hand that held brass and fused each man to the next. **A forest of arms points up out of frame**, every forefinger aimed at the same star, each arm crusted with rime on the windward side, icicles hanging straight down from elbows, sleeves and fingertips. Faces in the mass are frozen open-mouthed mid-argument, beards stiff, lips cracked and blue. Aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch across bare forearms and necks and run from one body into the next wherever they touch. The bodies at the base are pressed flat and faceted with ice like broken flagstones.
- **Dress and gear:** black scholars' gowns with pale blue hoods, frozen into hard folds that hang plumb; square caps frozen to heads at odd angles; brass astrolabes, quadrants and dividers fused into the hands; a cracked brass lectern carried up on the crest, its chained book frozen open; a chalk slate hanging on a cord with a half-finished sum; spectacles frosted white on three faces; ink horns at belts, the ink frozen into black icicles; the college mace, its brass head scorched, held up by the dean; an hourglass on a chain with the sand stopped mid-fall; a torn star chart wound around four arms; oak splinters of the benches frozen into the base; a fallen candle-stand jutting from the heap; one bare foot with a missing shoe.
- **Head:** no single head. The dean's face sits at the centre of the crest, just under the frame line: an old, furious face with a forked beard frozen stiff, mouth open on the word "aye".
- **Signature shape:** the leaning wave of bodies and the forest of pointing arms.
- **Colour:** black gowns with pale blue hoods, brass instruments, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a creak through the mass as one arm tries to lower and freezes back into place. Attack is every arm swinging down at once to point at one hero; a branching burn flowers across that hero and holds them where they stand.

#### The Great Refractor

- **Lore line:** "Pointed at the heavens for three hundred years. Someone has turned it round."
- **Was:** the observatory's great telescope, and the night assistant who was at its eyepiece when the hour stopped.
- **Scale and stance:** Ruler, 4.5, a brass telescope tube as long as a nave, swung down on its iron mount until it lies level across the frame, aimed at the party. The pier and the rear of the tube rise out of the frame top right; the lens end hangs low over the front rank. Its weight sits in the iron pier and the lead counterweight.
- **Body:** the tube is riveted brass in tapering sections, rimed thick on the windward side, with icicles hanging along its underside in a fringe. Lightning came down the spire into the mount: the gears are fused into one lump of scorched metal, and aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch along the tube from the mount to the lens. The great objective lens is frosted milky and cracked once across. At the lower rear, **the assistant is frozen on the observing ladder**, his brow fused to the eyepiece cup, one hand still on the slow-motion handle.
- **Dress and gear:** a riveted iron pier with a cast maker's plate; a lead counterweight on an iron arm; brass setting circles engraved with degrees; a finder scope strapped alongside the main tube; a dew cap of black-painted iron at the lens end, hung with icicles; hand-wheels with oak grips worn bright; the clockwork drive in a glass-fronted case at the pier's foot, still ticking; the oak observing ladder, its rungs worn hollow; the dome-shutter chains hanging slack across the tube; the assistant's quilted coat and fingerless mittens, his scarf frozen sideways in a wind that stopped; his log board hanging on a cord with the last entry unfinished; a dark lantern at his belt with its shutter closed.
- **Head:** none; the hooded lens end is its business end. The assistant's head is young, his hair standing up from the strike and frozen like that, his face hidden against the eyepiece.
- **Signature shape:** the long tube laid level across the frame on its pier.
- **Colour:** brass, pale blue frost, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is the tube creeping a fraction to follow the party as the drive ticks, then the clunk of a gear. Attack is a sighting: the tube settles on one hero, the drive clicks to a stop, the lens frosts over, and that hero cannot act until the tube moves on (Lockdown).

### Elites

#### The Horologist

- **Lore line:** "The last astronomer. He keeps the hours from moving."
- **Was:** the observatory's master.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9, holding up a pocket watch.
- **Body and dress:** a scholar's robe crusted with frost, a brass armillary sphere on his back, **a pocket watch held up on its chain**; his body is half frozen and half fused with brass gears at the joints.
- **Head:** a long beard frozen stiff, a brass lens over one eye.
- **Signature shape:** the armillary on the back and the raised watch.
- **Colour:** dark robe, brass, frost blue.
- **Motion:** idle is winding the watch. Attack is stopping a hero with a click of the watch.

#### Rime Colossus

- **Lore line:** "An ice statue of the founder. Someone wound it up."
- **Was:** a statue of the observatory's founder.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.5, a statue's formal pose, now moving.
- **Body:** a robed figure carved in blue ice, **a brass wind-up key turning in its back**, clockwork visible through the clear ice of its chest.
- **Head:** a stern, carved face with blank eyes.
- **Signature shape:** the statue with the wind-up key.
- **Colour:** clear blue ice, brass.
- **Motion:** idle is ticking. Attack is a slow, crushing fist.

#### The Gin Ox

- **Lore line:** "It walked the dome round for forty years. The dome has stopped. It has not."
- **Was:** the draught ox that turned the observatory dome, harnessed to the sweep of the great gin in the undercroft.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 1.9 at the hump, long and heavy, head low, leaning into its yoke with one foreleg lifted mid-stride. Its centre of mass sits forward in the shoulders, pressed against the yoke.
- **Body:** a big draught ox, the hide stiff with frost: the windward flank crusted in rime, the hair frozen in faceted planes over the ribs, icicles hanging from the dewlap, the belly and the muzzle. The lightning came down the gin's iron shaft into the yoke: aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch from the neck over both shoulders, and the hair along the spine stands straight up in a ridge and has frozen that way. One horn is scorched black to the base.
- **Dress and gear:** an oak neck-yoke with an iron bow, still bolted to **a broken length of the gin's sweep beam**, three paces of oak jutting out sideways from its shoulders and dragging its splintered end; brass caps on the horn tips, one missing; a leather head collar studded with brass rosettes; a sacking blanket over the loins, frozen stiff; brass tally plates nailed along the beam, one for every thousand turns; iron shoes worn bright; a tug-chain trailing from the yoke; a halter rope gnawed through; a cow bell at the throat with its clapper frozen to the side; an old whip scar across the rump; ice balled between the cleats of the hooves.
- **Head:** a broad, heavy face, the muzzle hung with icicles of frozen breath, the eyes frosted white, the nostrils flared.
- **Signature shape:** the low ox with the broken sweep beam jutting from its yoke.
- **Colour:** grey hide, pale blue frost, brass fittings, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a slow trudge in place, always turning a little to the left, as if the circle were still there. Attack is a head-down shove that swings the beam through the front rank.

#### The Rodwarden

- **Lore line:** "He kept the spire's rods so the lightning went into the ground. Then the ground froze."
- **Was:** the keeper of the lightning rods, who climbed the spire's roofs in storms to mend them.
- **Scale and stance:** Elite, 2.0, tall and rawboned, bent forward under a back-frame of rods, a grounding pole planted ahead of him like a crutch. His weight is split between the rear foot and the pole.
- **Body:** long and gaunt, frozen at the instant the last strike went through him: the near arm locked straight on the pole, the body rimed so thick on the windward side that the crust reads like bark, icicles hanging from the elbow, the beard and the pole hand. Under the rime, every inch of bare skin is covered in **aurora-green Lichtenberg scars**, old faded ones with new raw ones over them. The pole hand is fused to the brass grip; two fingers of the other hand are cauterised stumps.
- **Dress and gear:** **an oak back-frame carrying a dozen spare iron lightning rods**, fanned upward above his head, each with a scorched brass point; a coil of braided iron cable over one shoulder, its end trailing; a long iron grounding pole with a forked foot; a waxed canvas storm coat; a leather climbing harness with an iron hook at the chest; wool mittens with the palms burned through; roofer's boots with iron cleats; a hammer and a bag of iron staples at the belt; an iron chain wrapped round one ankle and dragging; a sheet of lead flashing tucked into the harness; a leather strap clenched between his teeth.
- **Head:** a long, hollow face, the scalp scarred bare in patches; what hair is left stands straight up and is frozen in spikes, each hung with a small icicle.
- **Signature shape:** the fan of rods rising above his head.
- **Colour:** brass rod tips, pale blue frost, aurora-green scars.
- **Motion:** idle is a twitch through the whole body every few breaths, as if the last strike were still passing. Attack is planting the pole: the strike comes down the rods, and burns flower across the floor into the front rank.

### Cast

#### Stopped Pilgrim

- **Lore line:** "It came to see the stars stop. It has not blinked since."
- **Was:** a pilgrim who crossed the hive plain to watch.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, frozen mid-step, head tilted up.
- **Body and dress:** a pilgrim's cloak, staff and badge, frosted over; eyes wide open and frosted.
- **Signature shape:** the pilgrim mid-step gazing up.
- **Colour:** grey-brown cloak, frost blue.
- **Motion:** idle is not moving at all, then a sudden jerk. Attack is a staff blow.

#### Clock Sentry

- **Lore line:** "Tick, then tock. Always in that order. Plan for it."
- **Was:** a mechanical gate guard.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, stiff, halberd upright.
- **Body:** a brass automaton with a clock face for a chest, its two hands showing which action comes next.
- **Signature shape:** the clock-chested figure.
- **Colour:** brass, frost blue.
- **Motion:** idle is a tick, a tock. Attack is a precisely timed halberd blow.

#### Gear Spider

- **Lore line:** "It repairs the orrery. It considers you a fault."
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.6 wide.
- **Body:** a brass spider of gears and springs, tool-tipped legs; on the front of its body, set off-centre, a mechanism-front: a stopped escapement wheel and pallet under a cracked crystal cover.
- **Signature shape:** the brass spider.
- **Colour:** brass, copper.
- **Motion:** idle is tinkering. Attack is a needle-leg strike.

#### Aurora Moth

- **Lore line:** "Drawn to the frozen stars. Its wings carry the northern lights."
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.7 wingspan, fluttering.
- **Body:** a large white moth whose wings shimmer with aurora green and violet bands.
- **Signature shape:** the spread wings.
- **Colour:** white, aurora green, violet.
- **Motion:** idle is a flutter. Attack is a dazzling wing beat.

#### Frozen Halberdier

- **Lore line:** "A guard frozen mid-patrol. It finishes the patrol now."
- **Was:** an observatory guard.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, marching, halberd on shoulder.
- **Body and dress:** a guard in a quilted coat and kettle hat, frozen white, icicles from the halberd blade.
- **Signature shape:** the marching halberdier.
- **Colour:** frost white, steel.
- **Motion:** idle is marching in place. Attack is a halberd hook.

#### Ephemeris Clerk

- **Lore line:** "It worked out where every star would be for the next hundred years. It is still right."
- **Was:** one of the observatory's computers: a clerk who did the long sums for the star tables.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 0.95, narrow-shouldered, standing with a writing desk strapped to its chest, head bent over it, the writing hand frozen mid-figure. Weight on the heels, back rounded.
- **Body:** thin and round-shouldered from desk work, frozen mid-sum: the writing hand rigid around a quill, a black icicle of frozen ink hanging from the nib. Rime crusts the windward cheek and shoulder. Aurora-green burns run from the desk's brass fittings into the fingers, which are fused to the quill.
- **Dress and gear:** **a slanted writing desk on shoulder straps**, with **a fan of paper tables frozen stiff and standing out from it like a wing**; an ink horn on a cord, frozen solid; a counting board hung at the belt; a short black gown with ink-stained cuffs; pale blue sleeve garters; fingerless mittens; a pen-knife; spare quills stuck in the cap band; a lead weight holding the pages down; felt slippers wrapped in rags.
- **Head:** a long-nosed, tired face, spectacles frosted white, a felt cap, lips moving with a number.
- **Signature shape:** the chest desk with its frozen fan of pages.
- **Colour:** black gown, pale blue frost, brass fittings, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is the quill scratching once and freezing again. Attack is writing a hero into the table; that hero's next turn comes later.

#### The College Tortoise

- **Lore line:** "The college kept it to show each new scholar how long a life can last. It is still making the point."
- **Was:** the college's tortoise, two hundred years old, which wandered the observatory's quadrangle and had the year of every new master painted on its shell.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.3, a high-domed tortoise frozen mid-step, one forefoot raised and the long neck stretched out ahead, as if it had been walking toward the party for a century. Weight on the three planted feet.
- **Body:** a heavy land tortoise, its scutes ridged with growth rings. Frost caught it mid-stride: rime crusts the windward side of the shell thick enough to facet it, icicles hang from the shell's rim and the raised foot, and the eyes are frosted white (frost). Lightning came down through the brass plate the college riveted to its shell: the plate is fused into the scutes, aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch from it across the dome, and the seams between the scutes are scorched black (lightning).
- **Dress and gear:** none of its own; the college's marks. Secondary details: the brass plate with the college arms, fused; the masters' years painted in a column down the shell, the oldest flaked away; a hole drilled through the rear rim with a frozen length of tether cord; a lettuce leaf frozen in the beak; a chip out of the front rim; scaly forelegs with blunt claws, ice balled between them; a chalked wager number on the shell, half rubbed out; a scrap of star chart frozen to the underside.
- **Head:** a small, beaked, ancient head on a long wrinkled neck, the mouth a little open, frost on the eyelids.
- **Signature shape:** the high dome on its raised forefoot, with the neck stretched out ahead.
- **Colour:** brass plate, pale blue frost, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is nothing for a long time, then the raised foot coming down one step and another rising. Attack is a slow, crushing bite; the bitten hero is slowed (Slow).

#### The Patroness

- **Lore line:** "She paid for the spire so she could watch the stars stop. She is getting her money's worth."
- **Was:** the noblewoman who paid for the observatory and came with her household for its last night.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.0, upright and imperious, a brass spyglass extended and levelled at the party in one hand, the other fist on her hip. Weight on the back foot, hidden under the gown.
- **Body:** middle-aged, heavy-jawed, frozen at the moment she turned to look. Rime crusts the windward side of her face and throat, thick in the folds of the neck. Aurora-green burns branch from the spyglass's brass into her gloved hand and up the sleeve; a ring has fused through the glove.
- **Dress and gear:** a houppelande of pale blue velvet gone grey, its deep hanging sleeves frozen stiff and pointing straight down, icicles at their dagged edges; grey fur trim matted with frost; a girdle of brass plaques; **a horned headdress**, two padded horns wrapped in brass net; a pomander on a chain; a brass chatelaine of keys; a fat purse; rings on every gloved finger; the train of the gown frozen in a sweep behind her, a crust of frost along its hem.
- **Head:** the horned headdress over a plucked high brow, a pursed mouth, one cheek pressed to the spyglass as she squints along it.
- **Signature shape:** the horned headdress and the levelled spyglass.
- **Colour:** pale blue velvet, brass, frost white, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is lowering the spyglass, sniffing, and raising it again. Attack is fixing a hero through the spyglass; that hero is marked, and every foe acts on it first.

#### The Patroness's Monkey

- **Lore line:** "It came up the spire on a brass chain for the last night. It stole a star-glass on the way. It is still running with it."
- **Was:** the Patroness's pet monkey, brought on a chain with her household for the observatory's last night, which slipped its collar and stole a brass astrolabe from a scholar's desk.
- **Scale and stance:** Small, 0.4, crouched low on its hind legs with the stolen astrolabe hugged to its chest in both hands, the long tail curled up and over in a hook behind it, the head turned back over the shoulder toward the company. Weight on the hind feet, ready to bolt.
- **Body:** a small, thin-limbed monkey. Frost stopped it mid-flight: rime crusts the windward flank and the tail, icicles hang from the elbows and the chin, and the eyes are frosted white (frost). Lightning came down through the brass in its arms: **the astrolabe is fused into its hands and chest**, aurora-green Lichtenberg burns branch from it across the belly and up the arms, and every hair of its coat stands straight out from the strike and has frozen that way in a stiff halo (lightning).
- **Dress and gear:** what the household put on it. Secondary details: a velvet collar in the Patroness's pale blue, gone grey, with a broken length of fine brass chain hanging from it; a tiny brass bell on the collar, its clapper frozen; a little embroidered jacket with one sleeve torn off; the astrolabe's pierced brass star-plate and pointer, fused; a stolen sugared almond frozen in its cheek; a ring caught on one toe; a bald patch on the tail where it was gripped; frost in the whiskers.
- **Head:** a small, wrinkled, old-looking face turned back over the shoulder, lips drawn back from small teeth, the ears frozen stiff.
- **Signature shape:** the crouched monkey with the hooked tail and the brass disc hugged to its chest.
- **Colour:** grey fur, pale blue collar and frost, brass astrolabe, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is nothing, then a sudden jerk as it jumps to a new crouch and freezes again. Attack is a leap onto a hero's back and a bite; it steals the hero's next buff.

#### Speculum Bearer

- **Lore line:** "The great mirror was too heavy for two men. They gave it to one."
- **Was:** a glass-porter who carried mirrors and lenses up the spire stair.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.4, a huge man bent nearly double under a round metal mirror strapped to his back, knees bent, both hands gripping the straps at his chest. Weight forward and low.
- **Body:** an enormous back and neck, forearms like posts, bare calves. Frost crusts his windward side; icicles hang from his chin, his elbows and the lower rim of the mirror. Lightning struck the mirror on the stair: **the metal has fused to his back** at its centre, the skin growing into it, with aurora-green burns branching out from the join across his shoulders.
- **Dress and gear:** **the speculum, a disc of pale brass-grey mirror metal wider than he is tall**, polished face outwards, frost-blind and cracked once; an oak carrying frame with padded straps; a porter's shoulder pad of stuffed leather; a quilted jerkin; rope lashings; rags wrapped round the shins; a polishing cloth and a pumice bag hanging from the frame; one boot replaced by sacking.
- **Head:** a small head on a thick neck, pushed forward under the load, a felt cap, a broken nose, frost in the stubble, the jaw set hard.
- **Signature shape:** the bent figure under the great disc.
- **Colour:** pale brass speculum, pale blue frost, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a heavy shift of the load and a frozen breath. Attack is turning and swinging the mirror into a hero edge-first.

#### Clock Jack

- **Lore line:** "It struck the hours. There have been none to strike. It has been saving up."
- **Was:** the carved jack that struck the bell of the spire's lower clock on every hour.
- **Scale and stance:** Human, 1.05, stiff, standing on its iron bracket-plinth, a long-handled hammer raised over its shoulder, a small bell hanging on a bracket in front of it. Weight square, as carved.
- **Body:** a carved oak man-at-arms, jointed at shoulder and elbow with iron pins. The wood has split along the grain where ice got in, and frost has faceted the windward side; icicles hang from the hammer arm. Lightning fused the shoulder pins into one scorched lump, and burns branch across the paint. It moves only on its pins, in jerks.
- **Dress and gear:** a painted surcoat of pale blue and brass checks, flaking to bare oak; a carved breastplate with painted rivets; a wooden kettle hat painted brass; **the hammer**, an iron head on an oak handle; **the bell**, on a wrought bracket bolted to the plinth; the drive rod rising from the plinth into its back; a counterweight on a chain; the observatory's arms carved on its chest; old nail holes and a bird's nest wedged behind one knee.
- **Head:** a carved face with a painted moustache and round cheeks, the paint flaked, the mouth a fixed grim line.
- **Signature shape:** the stiff carved figure with the hammer raised over its bell.
- **Colour:** flaking pale blue and brass paint, dark oak, frost.
- **Motion:** idle is nothing, then a single jerk of the hammer that stops an inch short of the bell. Attack is the strike on the bell; the nearest hero is stunned by the note.

#### Gate Mastiff

- **Lore line:** "Chained at the gate to bark at strangers. It has not finished barking."
- **Was:** the observatory's gate dog.
- **Scale and stance:** Large, 1.2 long, heavy-chested, braced at the end of its chain, forelegs planted, head thrust forward mid-bark. Weight back on the haunches against the chain.
- **Body:** a broad mastiff with heavy jowls and cropped ears, frozen mid-bark, the jaws wide, its breath hanging from the lips in a jagged beard of icicles. Rime is thick on the windward flank, the fur frozen into faceted plates. Lightning came down the chain: the fur along the spine stands in a frozen ridge, and burns branch from the collar across the chest.
- **Dress and gear:** a spiked iron collar fused to the chain; a heavy chain dragging the iron staple torn from the gatepost; a brass name plate on the collar; a gnawed bone frozen under one paw; a pilgrim's torn shoe caught in the chain; old bite scars on the muzzle; ice balled between the toes.
- **Head:** broad and wrinkled, lips drawn back, teeth bared, ice crusted on the teeth.
- **Signature shape:** the open-jawed dog straining at its chain.
- **Colour:** grey fur, pale blue frost, brass name plate, aurora-green burns.
- **Motion:** idle is a straining lean on the chain, then the jaws snapping without a sound. Attack is a lunge to the chain's length and a bite that holds a hero in place.
