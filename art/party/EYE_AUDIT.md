# Eye audit (lore changes 2026-11-23, item 10)

Rule: eye motifs (extra eyes, eyes on bodies or objects, eye-like markings, glowing eye dots, a single giant eye)
appear only in the void worlds: I, XII, XIII, XIV, XV. A creature's own natural eyes are not a motif.

Scope checked: every figure in worlds II to XI (world_II, II_b, III, III_b, IV, IV_b, V, V_b, VI, VI_b, VII, VII_b,
VIII, IX, X, XI), the eight keepers and the six heroes (dirAC, dirAC2). Method: each world's sheet read whole, every
candidate zoomed 2x to 4x on its own render over a mid-dark ground, then traced in the code; plus a text search of all
those files for eye, pupil, iris, lens, socket, ocelli and similar. V_b, VI_b and VII_b were checked but not edited
(in production).

## Final status

Every finding is fixed or closed by ruling. Only the Oracle's painted eye remains, and it is allowed by the bible.
No figure outside a void world now carries an eye motif.
