# Hero path variants, batch B: Alchemist, Oracle, Hexweaver (2026-11-24)

Source: `worlds/hero_paths_B.js` (seeds 7200-7208). Each builder copies its base from `dirAC2.js` (same body, face, scale,
stance family, lead ostium badge on a cord and red rubric strip) and branches in only what the bible's "Paths:" line lists.
All base bones are declared in every variant; new held objects get their own bones (listed below). Shared and frozen files
were not edited. `chainLinks`, `script`, `smallPoppet` and `feather` are local helpers in this file.

Outputs: `out/paths/<calling>_<path>.png/.svg/_sil.png`, pose tests `out/paths/<calling>_<path>_pose.png` (tiled in
`out/paths/paths_B_poses.png`), sheet `out/paths/paths_B_sheet.png` (each base beside its three paths at full size, plus
colour and silhouettes at battle size, 0.3x). Rigs: `out/rig/worlds/hero_paths_B_<key>/` (no MISSING JOINTS on any). render.js
also leaves its own copies at `out/worlds/hero_paths_B_*`.

## Weak spots still open

1. **Plaguewright** (after the second review): the hood still squares off a little behind the mask at full size, and the lens is small under the hat brim. Lowering the far arm moved the flask out of the raised, signature position, so his raised-flask read now rests on the other two paths and the base.
2. **Transmuter quicksilver**: the meniscus is a thin line and reads at full size only; at battle size the cucurbit reads as a pale glass bulb.
3. **Augur's locks** are partly hidden behind the near forearm (as the base's anatomy requires), so the woven bird bones are only clear at full size. The battle-size read rests on the feather plume.
4. **Fatebinder's script** reads as a red texture block at battle size, not as legible lines (which is intended), and at full size it is quite regular.
5. **Rig notes for animation:**
   - The chronomancer's staff and the doomsayer's staff are planted on the ground but parented to the hand. Any arm animation needs IK or a counter-rotation to keep the foot of the staff planted.
   - In the chronomancer and fatebinder, `hourglass` hangs from `forearm_n`, not `hand_n`, because it is on a wrist chain. The base's "turn the hourglass" idle needs retargeting.
   - The doomsayer's and mirrorhex's `pin` joint and the doomsayer's `poppet` joint are declared but carry no layers.
   - In the plaguewright the far arm draws before the head, the reverse of the base, and its rest pose is lowered, so the base throw animation needs new keys and z-order overrides.
   - Large `robe_front` rotations make the lining panel swing out like a blade. This is inherited from the base.
6. **Crowding:** the Pactbound and Doomsayer belts now hold herb bundle, skull, two poppets, pouch and badge. This is busy at full size but stays inside the silhouette.
