# World XV, the Torn Sky: finale-tier rebuild of all four rooms (2026-11-24)

Cole's verdict on the round-2 XV rooms: the blandest world in the game, though it is the second-to-last. This round
rebuilds all four rooms (same keys, same contract) as the game's showpiece: colossal scale, deep smooth skies, storm
walls, shafts of heavenly light, torn voids with something looking through, building-sized broken halos, the kneeling
host at kilometre scale, floating islands of heaven with waterfalls and chains, a sea of cloud far below, and debris
that drifts in sequence from the structure it broke from.

## Check after (against the plan)

Holds. At thumbnail the four separate instantly and still read as one world (gold leaf over black, violet burns, voids
with eyes, heaven's pale weathered stone): **ruins** = violet storm, a diagonal broken viaduct climbing into a warm rent,
mid key; **gate** = silver-white light key, the gate's verticals and the kneeling host against a glaring cloud sea, one
black tear; **choir** = warm dark interior in one-point perspective, tiers and the organ; **boss** = black void and a
white-gold column of light, radial glory, the highest contrast. Deviations: the boss room's "open-air apse" became a
ruined colonnade (its columns broken at different heights so the torn sky and the eye show above them); the choir's
"vault split over the seraph" became the wall torn open over its post (the vault is above the frame at this camera);
the gate's arch springs at 22 m (not higher) so the whole broken crown is in frame.

## Lore per room (what is in each)

- **sky_ruins, The Floating Stair.** The processional stair of heaven as a viaduct seen from the side, climbing left
  toward the light: six flights on rampant arches and piers; above the landing each flight has drifted further along the
  stair's own line and turned a little more (one motion, never scatter); the piers broke off below (jagged break faces,
  no floating drums); kneeling guardians of the host on each flight's head newel; gilt nosings worn to black; violet
  burns running flight to flight. A cathedral island far up in the rent of warm light (the stair's destination), a ruined
  island moored to the stair by great catenary chains, waterfalls off both lips; a building-sized halo hanging broken
  behind the upper flights, its broken arc drifting outward in order; a towering billowing storm wall on the right with
  rain and a tear in it, an eye looking in; the cloud sea below. The landing: wet flags with gilt processional bands,
  gold puddles, the fused strike point with burns, a stone bench and a lit iron votive lamp (scale). Mid: a fallen spear
  and its cracked halo, rubble. Fore: the landing's newel with a kneeling guardian of the host on it, backlit.
- **sky_gate, The Gate of Heaven.** The causeway from the fighters to the gate, its left edge open over the drop with a
  broken balustrade; the gate's two towers (110 m away, ~100 m tall): coursed weathered ashlar, pilaster buttresses, one
  colossal niche each with a standing angel of the host (the left one taken by the void, an eye in the absence), a gilt
  roundel of rays, small high lancets for scale, gilt only on the carved string-courses and mouldings; the arch broken at
  the crown, its voussoirs lifting away from the break in order; one gate leaf (a 20 m gilt grille) hanging from a fused
  hinge, the other flat on the causeway; the tear filling the gateway and running behind the towers, a vast eye in it
  turned down at the causeway (no stars: a clean absence). Along the rim the host kneels on columns rising from the
  clouds, facing the gate (one headless, one with a broken wing); a far island; a high cold sun. Hound prints as small
  clean voids in the paving; the strike scar; leaf blown from the gate. The porter's sentry booth with its door (scale);
  the porter's fallen key (mid). Fore: the balustrade's end pier with a gilt lamp bracket.
- **sky_choir, The Choir of Heaven.** A 46 m hall in one-point perspective. Each wall bay by bay in tiers: kneeling desks
  with books; stalls with blind tracery; projecting canopies with stone ogee hoods, gilt fillets, crockets and tapered
  pinnacles of varied height (some broken, the centre stall taller); a gilt diaper over the wall, a carved singer in
  some bays; the gallery's gilt parapet on a corbelled shelf; the arcade of carved angels singing from scrolls
  (some fallen, one headless); gilt string-courses; the triforium; the clerestory burning gold. Bay states vary (void-cut
  through the tiers with eyes, burned black, parapet lost, statue fallen). The left wall in its own shade, the right lit.
  The fused organ (three flats, melted runs, burns) under a burning rose; the eagle lectern with the hymnal open (rubric
  initial) in the lantern's gold shaft, on a gilt inlaid circle; black marble, polished, reflecting the windows; fallen
  hymnals; the wall torn open over the seraph's post, the storm and an eye through it. Fore: the near stall end with a
  small gilt kneeling angel; a sanctuary lamp on its chain, lit (scale).
- **sky_boss, The Seat of the Mother of Courses.** A column of white-gold light falling straight onto her seat (her
  hollow pieces back-lit in it; a soft fore-layer shaft and falling gold leaf over her); her glory: a cathedral-sized
  broken halo (its broken arc drifting outward in order) and tapered gilt rays, some snapped with the outer parts drifting
  out along their own line; the whole upper sky torn open over the summit, a vast eye looking down at her; the apse's
  colossal fluted columns curving round toward her, broken at different heights, the drums of three lifting apart along
  their axes in sequence, weathered; the host kneeling round the summit at kilometre scale, silhouetted against the void;
  the islands of heaven and the cloud sea far below; the storm wall behind her right. The floor: pale weathered marble,
  gilt rings round her dais, three curved dais steps, a blazing pool of light before her, the strike's scar, drifts of
  her gold leaf; the six courses running out from her dais as sinuous gilt-lipped channels in which only void now runs,
  thinning to nothing toward us, two broken and spilling into clean-rimmed pools; a prie-dieu and a candle-stand (scale).
  Mid: a fallen, split column drum. Fore: the nearest column, fused and burned. No war.

## Rules audit

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| No lightning drawn in the air | None. Burns are on surfaces only; light is shafts and glows. Cloud rims were smoothed after an early pass made them zigzag like bolts. |
| Eyes only in void worlds | XV is a void world: eyes in the tears (one per room plus the void-cut niche/bay). |
| Void = irregular clean-rimmed absence | `tornVoid` everywhere; no circles, no glow, no stars or sparkles inside (removed from the gate). The courses' void thins to nothing. |
| Gold only on the holy | Heaven is holy: gilt on halos, nosings, carved mouldings, organ, grilles, rays; modelled light-to-dark with lost leaf showing black, grime and burnish (`giltW`). The gate towers are stone, gilt only on carving. |
| Red only for blood, doom, rubric | Only the hymnal's rubric initial (choir). |
| Gradients only for skies, fog, light | Skies, cloud bodies, shafts, glows, the eye's surrounding darkness, water/mist; objects keep cel bands. |
| No war of gods | No armies, banners, battlefields or strewn weapons; one dropped spear and the porter's key; guardians died at their posts. |
| Deliberate drift | Every suspended thing is a sequence from the structure it broke from: stair flights, halo arcs, ray tips, arch voussoirs, column drums, island underside pieces. |
| Organic forms smooth | Clouds, islands, wings, robes, waterfalls, courses are smoothed curves; feathers are rounded scallops; only broken stone has angular fracture lines. |
| Weathered, never pristine | materials_v2 marks on all stone, stains and grime, lost leaf on gilt. |

## Weak spots (honest list, after the director pass)

- **Colossi:** now sculpted and readable, but still drawn from flat cel shapes: the arm and sleeve read a little like
  tubes, the face is small at kilometre scale, and the wings' fan is regular. The gate's right colossus is mostly behind
  the Rift Leviathan's render.
- **Eyes:** painterly now, but the iris colour (violet) is saturated against the muted tears; the ragged lips are flat
  dark shapes rather than torn material.
- **sky_boss:** the fore rubble covers the base cleanly but sits as a low band across her stage; the hand-edge cut is
  softened by light, not hidden; the six courses read as six at full size, less surely at half; the halo's right arc is
  still a segmented band.
- **sky_gate:** the fallen grille is readable but thin (the causeway is near eye level); the porter's lodge overlaps the
  Bulwark in the preview; tower tops remain cropped by the frame (intended).
- **sky_choir:** the far canopy rhythm is broken but still dense; the seraph's tear is mostly behind its own render.
- **sky_ruins:** unchanged apart from the eye and the guardian: the landing floor is plain; the halo reads as a ring of
  plates; the island tops still read a little like dishes.
- **Clouds:** the storm-wall billows are a little uniform; the cloud-sea rims evenly bright.
- Far SVGs: ruins 3.2 MB, gate 5.2 MB, choir 5.8 MB, boss 3.6 MB.
