## Abbey (hub) systems, built 2026-11-23

- Click-based garth hub; only HUD is the ostia dish. Title screen sits in the sky above the abbey and pans down 5.2 s into the garth (`scripts/hub.gd`, `show_title`, `_play`).
- Meta state in `Run.meta` (user://meta.json); abbey rules in `data/abbey.json`; logic in `scripts/abbey.gd` (class Abbey); tests in `tests/abbey_test.gd` and `tests/flow_test.gd` (both 0 fails).
- Roster: named heroes with a Reason (Debt, Penance, Vengeance, Faith, Hunger, Exile). Wounds persist; no permadeath. Fallen heroes come home Shattered for 2 descents, or Wenna mends them. Recruits at the gate (3, 24 to 46 ostia). Party 2 to 4, 6 beds (more via Chapter works).
- Currency: abbey shops spend ostia; in-run merchant, forge and camp spend gold. Gold brought home converts at 10:1.
- Shops (`scripts/shop_panel.gd`, one oak-and-iron frame, keeper's object close up; art from `grim/panels.js`):
  - Smithy (Mother Hild): temper (2 levels), cut sockets (max 2), set and strip runes, buy runes, equip and unequip.
  - Infirmarium (Sister Wenna): treat injuries, ease stress, lift Dread, mend Shattered.
  - Almonry (Brother Fenn): buy gear, flasks, widen belt (2 to 4), sell at 40% (runes return to the pouch; 40% of tempering spend added; road-bought gear capped at its gold value).
  - Cellarium (Godric): provisions food, light, medicine, tools for the next descent.
  - Sacristy: identify veiled relics, choose carried relics (slots grow with Teeth), buy relics.
  - Scriptorium (Brother Lucan): bestiary (depth 0 to 3, study for 10), worlds, truth pages, chronicle.
  - Chapter (Prior Aldric): unlock callings and hub works, swear vows (ascension).
  - Guesthouse and Company: recruit, dismiss, choose the company, go to the church and doors.
- Title sky: `grim/title_sky.py` paints the sky in numpy (metaball cumulus, per-billow rim lighting, stepped inked rims, textured moon, forge smoke) and blends its bottom into the lit garth using `grim/hub/garth_lit_ref.png`.

## Release fixes, phase 1 (2026-11-23)

- Saves: `scripts/save_io.gd` (class SaveIO) writes run, meta and settings crash-safe (tmp, read-back check, .bak kept until swap) and recovers from .bak/.tmp. `SaveIO.dir` moves all saves; tests use `user://test_saves`. `Run.SAVE_VERSION` = 1 with `_migrate_meta` / `_migrate_run`; a run from a newer build is refused.
- Fights: `Run.save_fight` stores the whole combat state (packed with var_to_bytes, base64) after every round and at commit with the committed Ordo; `main.resume_run_fight` restores it and replays a committed round. `run_ui.after_fight` clears the fight before paying spoils. Leave is disabled while a round resolves.
- Combat header shows real doom count and level in a descent. Practice and gauntlet are debug-build only. Resolve gain capped at 30 per fight (`Combat.RESOLVE_PER_FIGHT`). Legacy `Run.new_run` removed.
- Metadata: version 0.9.0; company and copyright "Grey Lantern Productions". `application/modify_resources` is still false, so the exe carries no icon or version info yet.
- Tests: `tests/phase1_test.gd` (54 checks) plus abbey, road, flow: 0 fails; run, card, sim, ui_play: no errors.
