# World II: The Rotmire (role map)

Gate 2, Deliverable B. Design brief only: no final numbers.

## 1. World

**The Rotmire.** Courses: Toxin and Water. Conjunction: **Toxic Pool** (Toxin meets Water): the rank itself poisons whoever stands in it for 3 rounds. The affliction is Fen Rot (gain 2 Toxin when drawn). This is the only Conjunction that poisons a place rather than a body, so fights here are about where the company stands. Foes pool the hero ranks, then pin heroes inside them (glaive, clap-net, knotted cord, the Stilt Hall's household), while a Swap costs 1 vigour and moves the other hero into the pool. The company can pool foe ranks too, and foes rarely move, but nearly everything here lives in poisoned water and resists Toxin. Toxin also lowers Armor, so heroes who stand their ground in a pool lose their plate.

## 3. Rulers

Each ruler's signature is Toxic Pool made large.

**The Mire Mother** (mire_boss)
- Phase 1, sunk to the chin: Poison Flood pools every hero rank at once; bog lullaby stress; the hags tend her.
- Phase 2 (about 55% HP), she rises from the fen: drowning weed drags the rearmost hero forward into the pooled front, Anchored.
- Phase 3 (about 25% HP), the mouth opens: she spits the hags' offerings into the company, adding Fen Rot to heroes' decks (new effect, uses the existing add-card effect).
- Commission: "The Tooth is lodged in her lower jaw as one of her teeth. The hags will not sell it. Take it from the jaw."

**The Stilt Hall** (mire_hut: the stilt village, where its empty piles stand)
- Phase 1, wading: stamps a pile on a rank each round (Toxic Pool there); the household reaches from the doorway (pull).
- Phase 2 (about half HP), the doors open: pulls a hero inside every other round; calls the tanned household out as Bog Bodies into the empty rank.
- Commission: "The moot-hall walked off in the night with the village inside, toward the Mother's mere and her Tooth. Break it on its piles before it gets there."

**The Old Heron** (mire_drown: the still mere and the eel shallows)
- Phase 1, still: does little, then spears whoever moved; the standing leg sinks an inch each round.
- Phase 2 (about half HP), feeding: lifts a hero toward the crop (held), swallows it next round for heavy damage and Toxin unless the crop is struck.
- Phase 3 (about 20% HP), the crop splits: swallowed things spill (Toxic Pool on the front two ranks) and it strikes twice a round.
- Commission: "It fished the fen before there were stilts and has come to prefer the fishermen. The villages want it dead before it wades into the Mother's mere."

Rooms: World II has one ruler room (`mire_boss`). The two rivals are placed in the regular rooms their lore names (the Stilt Hall's empty piles stand at `mire_hut`; the Heron fishes the still shallows of `mire_drown`). Those rooms have no ruler stage (the right 45% kept clear), so each needs a ruler variant, or the rivals fall back to `mire_boss`.

## 5. What this world tests

- **Position under a hazard.** Toxic Pools make Swap a real cost (1 vigour, and it moves an ally into the pool); pins (Eel-Catcher, Stilt-Fowler, Coven Mother) and the Heron's Still water punish both staying and moving. Companies that need fixed ranks (Bulwark in front, casters at the back) feel it most.
- **Cleansing and Ward.** Alchemist (Apothecary) and Oracle cleanses matter; the Coven Mother's curses ignore Ward, so a Ward-only plan is not enough.
- **Hexweaver.** The Sin-Eater eats Hex and Marks off foes; the Hexweaver's answer is to kill him and move his spilled sins back onto foes.
- **Toxin builds.** Almost every foe resists Toxin: Plaguewright Alchemists must bring a second plan. Fire is the common weakness here (peat, thatch, moss), which also lets the company Combust the Toxin it has stacked.
- **Armor.** Toxin lowers Armor; Bulwark Armor stacks (Ironwarden, Last Bastion) wear away in the pools.
- **Healing foes** (Troll, Leech Swarm, Leech-Barber, hags, Mother) test burst and kill order; the Reeve's tithe steals the company's own Block and healing.
- **The Vessel** stands in a Toxic Pool unharmed (body rule) and can hold the poisoned rank for the company.
