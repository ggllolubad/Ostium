# Elementalist: the Conjoiner (Gate 2 calling brief)

## 1. Fantasy

A scholar of the Six Courses, argued over by the whole abbey: half the chapter calls the work heresy, and the other half reads the notes. Years at the ink pots have stained the hands to the wrist, one finger for each course. Where every other body in the worlds below suffers a course, the Conjoiner writes it: one ink laid down and left to settle, a second struck across it, and the two meet in the foe as a Conjunction. The Accumulator bends under a brass vessel of held Focus and waits for one great working. The Catalyst, ward sigils inked on the arms, works fast and nervous, one spark after another. The Prismatic, one eye clouded white, tries to hold all six courses apart at once, as the Mother of Courses did.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home:** ranks 3 and 4. Most cards are usable from 2 to 4.
- **Hits:** any rank, 1 to 4. Area cards reach every foe. A few short, strong Volatile strikes work only from rank 2.
- Ward cards target any hero.

## 3. Core loop

Lay Stable stacks of one course on a foe, then strike it with a Volatile application of another: the two meet as a Conjunction, with power equal to the stacks applied plus those consumed (capped at 12). Focus adds to the next application, Attunement decides which course the attuned cards apply, and Ward keeps the foes' courses off the company. Resources: Stable and Volatile stacks, Focus, Attunement, Ward, Conjunctions.

**Starters (5 distinct):** a Stable attuned bolt, a Volatile attuned flick, a Focus card, a Ward card for any hero, and a Conjoin card (sets off a Stable stack already on the foe). Attuned starters mean no world can make the starting deck dead.

**Tweaks for approval:** Bolt, Attune and Channel become one kit. The starter deck drops from 8 distinct cards with fixed courses to 5 attuned ones. Focus can be given to another hero, for course applications only (a new company hook that stays clear of the Oracle's Prime).

## 4. Kit skill

**Turn the Ring** (1 vigour): Attune to a course you choose and gain 1 Focus.

Why it is not a card: the attuned course must be choosable every round, or a hand built on one course goes dead against a foe that resists it. It applies nothing itself, so it pays only when a course card follows. The engine already has the effect (attune_to); the UI needs a course picker.

**Path kits (level 4):**
- Accumulator, **Stopper the Jar:** a foe's stacks of your attuned course do not fall this round.
- Catalyst, **Conjoin:** turn 1 Stable stack on a foe Volatile, setting off its Conjunctions now.
- Prismatic, **Sixth Ink:** apply 1 Stable stack of a course the target lacks.

## 5. Archetypes

### Accumulator: The Great Working
- **Plan.** Setup: high Stable stacks of the attuned course on one foe, banked Focus, stacks kept from falling. Payoff: double a course, raise the Conjunction cap (path passive and path cards). Finisher: one Volatile strike of a second course for a Conjunction at the cap, or damage per stack.
- **Key commons:** *Lay the Ground* (3 Stable attuned). *Hold the Breath* (Retain; gain Focus). *Steep* (apply more of the largest course already on the target). *Seal the Jar* (the target's stacks do not fall next round). *Pour Out* (Volatile application of the next course that spends all Focus). *Weigh the Stacks* (damage per attuned stack on the target, not consumed).
- **Weak against:** foes with Ward or resistance to the attuned course; cleansers; groups of small foes; foes that die or are replaced before the working pays off.

### Catalyst: Chain Reaction
- **Plan.** Setup: two cheap courses laid on several foes; 0-cost Volatile sparks. Payoff: cards that grow with each Conjunction this round (Block, Ward, damage, draw). Finisher: an application that reaches every foe, setting off a Conjunction on each.
- **Key commons:** *Spark* (0: 1 Volatile attuned). *Twin Sigil* (1 Stable of one course, then 1 Volatile of another, on one foe: a Conjunction in one card). *Scatter Ink* (1 Stable attuned on every foe). *Warding Sigil* (a hero gains Ward, more per Conjunction this round). *Reaction Shield* (Block per Conjunction this round). *Feedback* (damage per Conjunction this round).
- **Weak against:** one big foe alone (a ruler gives one target per Conjunction); Ward on foes; Neurotoxin and Venom Tremor, which strip riders; False Light discards; lines that resist both courses in hand.

### Prismatic: All Six
- **Plan.** Setup: Stable stacks of many different courses, held apart (no Volatile until ready). Payoff: Spectrum, the count of distinct courses on a foe, feeds damage, Ward and Focus. Finisher: one Volatile strike on a foe with many courses sets off every pair at once.
- **Key commons:** *Sixth Ink* (1 Stable of a course the target lacks). *Turn the Page* (Attune to the next course; draw). *Count the Colours* (damage per Spectrum). *Prism Ward* (Ward per distinct course across all foes). *Hold Apart* (the target's courses cannot conjoin this round). *Spectrum Bolt* (hit, apply attuned, then Attune to the next course).
- **Weak against:** immunity (each immune course caps Spectrum); cleansers; stacks falling during a slow build; allies' Volatile hits setting the foe off early; fights that end in 3 rounds.

**Bridges:** Focus serves the Accumulator and the Catalyst. Spectrum cards read stacks laid by any archetype or any hero. Attuned commons work in all three.

## 6. Company hooks

The Elementalist is the company's Conjoiner: every other calling lays a course or wants one set off, so its best cards cash in stacks it did not lay.

## 7. Legendaries

- **Accumulator, The Brass Reservoir:** once a fight, one Conjunction has no cap; bank stacks and Focus for rounds, then end the largest foe in one stroke (a ruler drops a phase).
- **Accumulator, The Perpetual Working:** the Elementalist's Conjunctions leave the Stable stacks behind instead of consuming them; the same great Conjunction goes off every round.
- **Catalyst, Chain of Sigils:** every Conjunction the Elementalist sets off spreads 1 Volatile stack of each of its courses to the foes beside it; one spark sets off the whole line.
- **Catalyst, The Quickened Hand:** each Conjunction the Elementalist sets off makes the next course card this round cost 0; a good hand empties in one cascade.
- **Prismatic, The Sixfold Ring:** a foe holding all six courses, when set off, takes every pair's Conjunction at once; paint one foe with all six and end it.
- **Prismatic, What the Clouded Eye Saw:** while four or more distinct courses lie across the foes, no course on them falls and every Elementalist damage card deals extra per distinct course; keep the whole field painted and every strike lands hard.

## 8. Weakness

- Resistance and immunity: every foe resists the two courses of its world and is immune to what it is made of, so the Elementalist must attune away from the world's pair.
- Foes with Ward, and foes that cleanse their own stacks.
- Blows on the back rank and sprays that hit every hero: 30 HP and 1 Grit.
- Burst in round 1, before any stack is down.
- Neurotoxin and Venom Tremor, which strip the course rider off a damage card.
- Pulls that drag the Elementalist to rank 1, where most of its cards are locked.
- Fast fights where stacks never pay off.

Good test encounters: a Venom Spires group with a back-rank striker; a world's elite pair that resists both of its courses and carries Ward; a cleanser among three foes.

## 9. Keywords

- **Attuned** (existing engine concept, needs a hover entry): your current course; attuned cards apply it.
- **Conjoin** (new keyword for the existing react effect): turn a Stable stack on the target Volatile, so its Conjunctions happen now.
- **Spectrum** (new): the number of distinct courses on the target.
- Existing and used: Stable, Volatile, Conjunction, Focus, Ward, Resist, Weak, Immune, the six course statuses, Echo, Retain, Exhaust.

## 10. Risks

- **Conjunction spikes.** Combust (3x) and Absolute Zero can decide a fight in one card. The cap of 12 stays; only the Accumulator path raises it, and only The Brass Reservoir lifts it, once a fight; rulers drop a phase rather than die.
- **Catalyst's Conjoin kit as default.** It sets off only stacks already there, once a round; the simulator checks its use rate against the Slash lesson.
- **Friction with allies.** A Volatile hit from another hero sets off stacks early, which hurts the Prismatic most. *Hold Apart* answers it, and the planning preview shows every Conjunction before the Ordo runs.
- **Focus overlapping Prime.** Focus given to another hero boosts course applications only; Prime stays the Oracle's general boost.
- **Complexity.** Attune choice, two stack types and Spectrum ask a lot. Commons each do one thing; the counting cards sit at uncommon and above.
- **Fire bias.** 43 foes are weak to fire today. Attuned cards follow weaknesses, so the Stage 4 rebalance matters here more than anywhere.
- **Prismatic and Vessel overlap.** Both are about all six courses. The Prismatic paints foes; the Vessel holds courses in its own body. Their cards should never share a verb.
- **Assembly.** About 3 of an archetype's 6 commons show up per descent. Attuned commons count for every archetype, and each archetype's commons cover setup and payoff twice over.
