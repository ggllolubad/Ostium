# World I: The Drowned Kingdom (role map)

Gate 2, Deliverable B. Design brief only: no final numbers.

## 1. World

**The Drowned Kingdom.** Courses: Water and Void. Conjunction: **Drown** (Void meets Water). On a foe, Drown turns its next action into a basic blow; on a hero it deals 3 stress per power, at most 20 (Rules v2, rule 7). The affliction is Drowning (gain 2 Soak when drawn), so a hero who breaks here walks around pre-soaked. Fights here are a stress war fought through the Ordo: foes spread Soak widely and slowly (sweeps, tears, bursts), then a few voices (the Siren's lure, the King's decree, the Bell's knell) land Void to cash every Soak into Drown at once. The company can play the same trick back: Soak a ruler or elite, then Drown it on the turn its big intent is shown, and the blow comes out basic. Ward, Swap and kill order on the Void-bringers decide most fights.

## 3. Rulers

Each ruler's signature is Drown made large.

**The Drowned King** (throne)
- Phase 1, on the throne: Crown of Tides Soaks every hero; sceptre backhand to the front; calls his last loyal knight when it falls.
- Phase 2 (about 60% HP), he rises: Drown the Court lands Void on every hero, Drowning anyone still Soaked; his backhand now reaches rank 2.
- Phase 3 (about 30% HP), he gorges on his court: eats a living retinue member to heal and gain Might. Kill the court first or it feeds him.
- Commission: "The Tooth sits in the crown's front socket, where a jewel was. The sea grew the crown into his skull. Cut it out."

**The Bell Below** (belfry)
- Phase 1, tolling: a visible count; each toll is stress to all and a ring that shoves the front hero back; the reaching dead drag the rearmost forward between tolls.
- Phase 2 (about half HP), the bell cracks and the ringer bound to its clapper wakes: it tolls every round, and its Knell Drowns every hero who carries Soak.
- Commission: "The abbey church has no bell. It went through the first door, and the crypt hears it every night. Silence it."

**Mother of Brine** (brine_grotto)
- Phase 1, lullaby: bears a daughter (Brine Siren) into an empty rank; lullaby stress; embrace on the front hero.
- Phase 2 (about 55% HP), the song turns to a scream: daughters come every other round; she devours a hero at Death's Door.
- Phase 3 (about 25% HP), she devours her own: eats a daughter to heal. The company must choose: kill the daughters (grief stress) or leave them as her food.
- Commission: "Every siren in the kingdom is her daughter, and she is still delivering. End the line at the mother."

## 5. What this world tests

- **Stress resistance and relief.** Drown on heroes, tolls, songs and grief make this the stress world: companies without stress relief (Oracle, Hexweaver, the Rallying virtue) break here.
- **Back-rank safety.** Hooks, reels, the Grasping Deep and the Pearl Diver pull or dive at ranks 3-4: Bulwark's Guard and back-rank Block are tested; back-line Elementalists and Oracles whose cards lock to their home rank lose turns after being dragged.
- **Reading the Ordo.** Drowning a foe on its big intent rewards Elementalist setup and Oracle ordering; Ward (Elementalist, Alchemist, Oracle) answers the Void-bringers.
- **Multi-hit builds** (Bladedancer, swarm-style Thorns) are punished by the Bell's Struck bronze; Executioner-style heavy hits shine.
- **Sustain walls** (Nun, Choirboy, Knight) test burst and kill order; the Nun's Salt tears reward an Elementalist who Drowns what she heals.
- **The Vessel** can take Soak and Erode off allies onto itself and is never Drowned (body rule): this is a world where it carries the company.
