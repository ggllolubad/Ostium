# Duelist: the Quick Blade (Gate 2 calling brief)

## 1. Fantasy

A fencing master, a bravo, a headsman, a barber-surgeon: whatever they were before the doors opened, their trade was the single cut. The abbey hires them for the moment a foe's guard opens, and pays them by the kill. Some learned in the lightning-touched schools, and the scorch runs in branching lines up the blade and the sword hand. They stand side-on, weight on the back foot, eyes never off the target. The Bladedancer takes up a second blade and never stops moving; the Executioner pulls on the black hood and carries out the sentence; the Bloodletter opens the lancet case and lets the foe empty into the bowl.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home:** ranks 1 and 2. Cards are usable from 1 to 3; lunges from rank 3 advance the Duelist into reach.
- **Hits:** ranks 1 and 2; thrusts reach rank 3. The Executioner drags foes forward from ranks 3 and 4. Bleed and Mark cards reach further than blows.
- A Retreat card or two lets a wounded Duelist step back to rank 3 without the universal Swap.

## 3. Core loop

The Duelist chains: two of the Duelist's actions back to back in the Ordo give Momentum, and finishers spend it. Mark chooses the foe; Follow-up rewards hitting the foe the previous action hit, whoever made it, so the Duelist cuts right after the company's blows. Resources: Momentum, Mark, Follow-up, Bleed, Lightning (Static), Dodge, Riposte.

**Starters (5 distinct):** a 0-cost cut that gains Momentum, a lunge (advance, hit), a Mark-and-cut, a parry (Riposte and Dodge), and a finisher that spends Momentum and doubles against a Marked foe.

**Tweaks for approval:** Slash, Step and Feint are cut. Riposte becomes the Duelist's alone. Lightning narrows to a thread of about 5 cards (the lightning-touched schools) rather than a theme at every rarity; it is mostly a company hook.

## 4. Kit skill

**Find the Gap** (1 vigour): Mark a foe and gain 1 Momentum.

Why it is not a card: it lets the Duelist open a chain in any round, which is the calling's whole rhythm, so it must never be missing from a hand. It deals no damage, so it pays only when a finisher follows. Slash (damage and Bleed) was the most used action in the whole baseline; this kit cannot become the default because Momentum without a finisher is wasted.

**Path kits (level 4):**
- Bladedancer, **Quickstep:** Swap with an adjacent hero and gain 1 Momentum (it is the Duelist's Swap for the round).
- Executioner, **Drag to the Block:** pull a foe in rank 3 or 4 forward 1.
- Bloodletter, **Staunch:** a hero heals HP equal to the Bleed on a chosen foe, up to a cap.

## 5. Archetypes

### Bladedancer: The Measure
- **Plan.** Setup: 0-cost cuts and Momentum gainers played back to back; Dodge to survive rank 1. Payoff: cuts that add Momentum to their damage without spending it; Echo. Finisher: a flurry that strikes once per Momentum, or a sweep across ranks 1 and 2.
- **Key commons:** *Feather Cut* (0: small hit, gain Momentum). *Two-Step* (hit twice). *Light Feet* (Dodge, gain Momentum). *Turn of the Wrist* (hit, plus some per Momentum, not spent). *Sweep* (each foe in ranks 1 and 2 takes a hit). *Arc Cut* (hit, apply 1 Lightning; many hits build Static fast).
- **Weak against:** Armor (every small hit loses its Armor's worth); Thorns and Riposte foes; Lockdown, Stillness and Stun, which break the chain; False Light discards; Venom Tremor stripping a cut's rider.

### Executioner: The Sentence
- **Plan.** Setup: Mark for 2 rounds, drag foes into reach, soften with heavy strokes. Payoff: extra damage against Marked foes, and damage per HP the target is missing. Finisher: Execute a Marked foe at or below a threshold that Momentum raises.
- **Key commons:** *Pronounce Sentence* (0: Mark for 2 rounds). *Measure the Neck* (hit, more against a Marked foe). *Haul Forward* (pull 1, Mark). *Heading Stroke* (2 vigour, heavy hit). *The Crowd Watches* (hit; if it kills, every hero sheds stress). *Weigh the Wound* (hit, plus some per HP the target is missing).
- **Weak against:** rulers and high-HP elites (an Execute only drops a ruler to its next phase); summoners that refill the ranks the Executioner clears; foes that clear Mark; Block, which soaks the softening blows.

### Bloodletter: The Barber's Bowl
- **Plan.** Setup: Bleed on one foe, then across the line. Payoff: double Bleed, healing and damage per Bleed, Vulnerable at high Bleed. Finisher: consume all Bleed on a foe for damage and heal the most wounded hero.
- **Key commons:** *Lancet* (small hit, Bleed). *Open the Vein* (Bleed). *Letting Cut* (hit; Follow-up: more Bleed). *Bloodmist* (Bleed on every foe). *Cupping Glass* (a hero heals equal to the target's Bleed). *Deep Cut* (Bleed; the target becomes Vulnerable at high Bleed).
- **Weak against:** short fights (Bleed needs rounds); foe healers and cleansers; summons that replace a bleeding foe with a fresh one; Blight and Toxin on the company, which shut off the healing.

**Bridges:** Mark and Follow-up appear in all three. Bleed lowers a foe toward the Execute threshold at round end. One uncommon makes each Bladedancer hit add Bleed.

## 7. Legendaries

- **Bladedancer, The Endless Measure:** every cut after the Duelist's second in a round strikes again at half power; a long chain lands twice the hits.
- **Bladedancer, Two Blades, One Heart:** while holding 3 or more Momentum the Duelist Dodges the first blow each round and finishers hit every foe in ranks 1 and 2; the front holds untouched while the sweep clears the line.
- **Executioner, The Headsman's Round:** each Execute refunds its vigour and strikes the next Marked foe at once; kills chain through a whole group.
- **Executioner, The Long Sentence:** one foe stays Marked all fight, and every hit any hero lands on it adds to its Sentence; when the Sentence passes its HP it dies (a ruler drops a phase). The answer to rulers.
- **Bloodletter, Red Tide:** Bleed stops falling, and a bleeding foe that dies passes its Bleed to its neighbours; the line bleeds out together.
- **Bloodletter, The Bowl Runs Over:** all Bleed damage heals the most wounded hero; the company outlasts any long fight.

## 8. Weakness

- Armor and Block, which blunt many small hits.
- Thorns, Riposte and Dodge on foes.
- Rank disruption: pushed to rank 3 or 4, most Duelist cards are locked.
- Chain breakers: Stun, Lockdown, Stillness, Neurotoxin and Venom Tremor (Stilled Spire, Venom Spires).
- Sprays that hit every hero: the Duelist stands in front with 34 HP and 2 Grit.
- For the Executioner, a ruler alone in its room.

Good test encounters: an armoured front with a thorned foe; a Stilled Spire group that makes heroes act last; a ruler with no escorts.

## 9. Keywords

- **Finisher** (new label): spends all your Momentum for its effect.
- **Execute N** (new keyword for an existing effect): a foe at N HP or less dies; a ruler drops to its next phase instead, matching Absolute Zero in Rules v2. Doom (Hexweaver brief) lowers every Execute check; The Long Sentence counts hits landed instead, so the two capstones stay distinct.
- Existing statuses that need a hover entry: **Dodge**.
- Existing and used: Momentum, Mark, Follow-up, Bleed, Riposte, Vulnerable, Static, Echo, Retain.

## 10. Risks

- **Kit dominance.** Slash was the most used action; Find the Gap deals no damage.
- **Vigour hogging.** Chains reward one hero spending most of the pool. Momentum comes one per back-to-back action, 0-cost cuts are small, and the simulator watches each hero's share of vigour.
- **Multiplying hits.** Multi-hit times Might times Vulnerable grows fast. Multi-hit cards keep a small base, and Might from the Alchemist is temporary.
- **Execute on elites.** Thresholds stay flat numbers and Momentum raises them modestly, so elites must be worn down first.
- **Damage over time stacking.** Bleed with Burn, Toxin and Hex can trivialise a 12-round ruler fight. Bleed falls by 1 each round; Red Tide is the one exception and is a capstone.
- **Fragility.** A Duelist who dies in rank 1 every fight is unfun. Dodge and Riposte appear in commons, and a Retreat card steps back.
- **Assembly.** About 3 of an archetype's 6 commons show up in a descent; each archetype's commons cover setup and payoff twice over, and Mark and Follow-up commons count for all three.
