# Calling brief: the Oracle (the Anchorite)

Gate 2, Deliverable A. Draft for Cole's review. No final numbers.

## 1. Fantasy

A sibyl or anchorite who walled themself up beside a void door and listened for years. Pale from the dark, barefoot, very still, head tilted as if listening; a linen blindfold over the eyes, an hourglass on a chain at one wrist, star charts sewn into the lining of a violet-black robe. The Oracle does not fight the foe so much as arrive before it. The blow lands where the stars said it would; the company's next strike finds the opening already made. The only hero from a void tradition, it points, the robe opens, and the stars show.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home ranks:** 3 and 4. The Oracle is the most fragile hero.
- **Used from:** 2 to 4.
- **Hits:** void reaches every rank, so most foe cards hit ranks 1 to 4.
- **Allies:** Prime and Ward cards reach any hero.
- **Rank locks:** a few Chronomancer and Augur cards only work from rank 4, so the listener stays at the back.

## 3. Core loop

Each round the Oracle reads the foes' intents, Delays the one that matters and Primes the company's best action. Between those it lays Void and Ward. Resources:
- **Prime:** the next action from any hero gets stronger.
- **Delay:** a foe's action moves to the end of the foes' queue.
- **Void stacks**, and **Ward** against courses.
- **Path resources:** Echo (Chronomancer), Omen (Augur), and the count of Primes spent this round (Fatebinder).

## 4. Kit skill

- **Hasten** (1 vigour, once per round): Prime a small amount.
- **Why it is not a card:** Prime is the Oracle's promise to the company. The kit makes sure one Prime is there every round, whatever the draw. It is small, and it only pays if the player orders the Ordo well, so a good card should usually beat it.
- **Path kit skills at level 4 (optional):**
  - Chronomancer, **Turn the Glass**: your next card this round gains Echo.
  - Augur, **Read the Bones**: see one foe's intent for next round, and gain Omen.
  - Fatebinder, **Bind**: your next Prime also carries to the action after the one it strengthens, at half its size.

## 5. Archetypes

### Chronomancer: The Stopped Hour (control of the Ordo)

- **Plan.** Setup: Delay the intents that matter, and bank vigour and draw for next round (borrowed time). Payoff: cards that hit harder against a Delayed foe, Echo cards that replay, and Momentum handed to allies. Finisher: Stop Time Delays every foe, followed by a big Echoed strike.
- **Key commons:**
  - Stall (Delay and a small hit)
  - Hourglass (vigour next round)
  - Glimpse Ahead (draw next round)
  - Stutter (an Echoed Void hit)
  - Late Blow (extra damage to a foe Delayed this round)
  - Quicken (another hero gains Momentum)
- **Weak against:** swarms with many small intents and no single big one, Stillness and Lockdown on the Oracle (it acts last, after the damage it meant to stop), and ruler signature moves that cannot wait twice.

### Augur: Read the Bones (answering intents)

- **Plan.** Setup: gain Omen by answering what the foes show (a card played against a foe that intends to attack, a Ward that catches a course, a kill). Payoff: damage and Ward that grow with Omen or with the damage the foes intend this round, and turning an attack onto its neighbour. Finisher: Fulfil the Omen spends all Omen as one strike.
- **Key commons:**
  - Cast the Knucklebones (Void; Omen if the target intends an attack)
  - Forewarn (Block on the hero a foe means to hit)
  - Bird-Bone Ward (Ward on every hero targeted by a course)
  - Entrails (Omen on a kill)
  - Omen Bolt (damage that grows with Omen, not spent)
  - Ill Omen (Vulnerable on a foe that intends to attack)
- **Weak against:** foes that do not attack (supports, afflicters, summoners), stress that goes around Block and Ward, and an Oracle that gets hit before Omen builds.

### Fatebinder: The Red Thread (Prime chains)

- **Plan.** Setup: many cheap Primes, written in rubric, some Retained. Payoff: cards that count the Primes spent this round (damage, Block for the company), and Primes placed on each ally's biggest card. Finisher: Severance, an execute whose threshold grows with every Prime spent this round, or one huge Prime on an ally's capstone.
- **Key commons:**
  - Knot (a cheap Prime)
  - Bind Fate (a Prime that Retains)
  - Guiding Word (a Prime, and another hero gains Focus)
  - Red Letter (a hit that grows per Prime spent this round)
  - Woven Shield (Block for every hero, growing per Prime spent)
  - Pull the Thread (Pull a foe forward, apply Void)
- **Weak against:** anything that breaks the Ordo or the hand: False Light discards, Venom Tremor, Stillness and Lockdown reorder actions so a Prime lands on the wrong one, and the Patroness's Monkey (Stilled Spire) steals the next buff. A company with no big actions to Prime also wastes it.

## 6. Company hooks

Prime is the Oracle's gift: it makes anyone's best card better. Delay is the second, buying the company a round.

| Calling | Oracle gives | Oracle wants | Pairing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | Prime on Bash or a stun; Ward against course attacks on the front rank; Delay so the Taunt absorbs one big hit, not two | Guard and Taunt: the Oracle is the easiest hero to kill | Fatebinder Primes the Bulwark's Toll of the Tower |
| Duelist | Prime on an execute or a Momentum finisher; Momentum from the Chronomancer; Vulnerable | A kill to fire Fate's Edge, and Follow-up after the Oracle's Void hit on the same foe | Fatebinder Primes the Executioner's Heading Stroke; Chronomancer's Quicken feeds the Bladedancer |
| Elementalist | Prime on a Volatile application, so more stacks go in and the Conjunction is stronger; the void half of five Conjunctions (Hollow Flame, Absolute Zero, Discharge, Drown, Blight) | Frost on a Void-heavy foe for Absolute Zero | Augur's Void and the Elementalist's Frost: Absolute Zero |
| Alchemist | Prime on a thrown belt flask; Delay to buy the rounds a brew needs | Salts for the Oracle's stress; Water on foes for Drown | Prime a Combust trigger |
| Hexweaver | Prime on the Hexweaver's spend; Void on foes beside the Hexweaver's Toxin (Blight) | Hex on the foe the Oracle cannot Delay; Doom to read for an execute | Delay a foe while its Doom catches up with its HP |
| Vessel | Prime on a Give, so more stacks go across; Void for the Vessel to take, toward holding all six | The Vessel's Mantle to catch course attacks, so the Oracle's Ward can go elsewhere | Fatebinder Primes The Whole Conjunction |

## 7. Legendaries

- **Chronomancer: The Last Hour.** Your Echoes replay at full power, and each Echo can echo once more.
- **Chronomancer: The Turned Glass.** At the start of each round, the foe with the strongest intent is Delayed, and a foe left waiting takes void damage.
- **Augur: Cassandra.** At round end, every foe takes void damage equal to the damage it intended this round.
- **Augur: The Bone Pouch.** Omen never fades, and each Omen gives every hero Ward at round start.
- **Fatebinder: The Rubric.** Every Prime you place also strengthens the action after it.
- **Fatebinder: The Severed Thread.** The action that spends the round's third Prime resolves twice.

## 8. Weakness

- **Divers:** foes that reach rank 3 or 4 kill it fast.
- **Swarms:** many small hitters give Delay and the Augur nothing worth answering.
- **Ordo breakers:** Stillness, Lockdown, buff thieves and hand disruption.
- **Void-proof foes:** foes made of or living in void blunt its damage (Drowned Kingdom, White Silence, Black Pyre, Blighted Garden, Torn Sky).
- **Small companies:** a two-hero company gives Prime fewer worthwhile targets.

## 9. Keywords

- **Delay:** an existing effect that needs a hover definition. The foe's action moves to the end of the foes' queue; if it is already last, it waits until next round.
- **Omen:** an existing engine resource that needs a hover definition. The Augur's stored foresight, spent by Fulfil cards.

**Tweaks for approval:**
- **Doom moves to the Hexweaver.** The Oracle gives up Doom entirely; it moves to the Doomsayer (see the Hexweaver brief). The Augur's Omen and intent reading take its place, so the two void callings stop sharing a win condition.
- **Delay chain limit.** A foe can wait into the next round at most once in a row, so chained Delays cannot lock a ruler.
- **Prime on belt flasks.** A belt flask counts as an action for Prime.

## 10. Risks

- **The kit could dominate.**
  - The danger: Hasten could become the default play every round, as Slash did.
  - Guards: it is small, and Prime only pays when it is placed well. The sim tracks its use rate against Slash's baseline.
- **Prime stacking.**
  - The danger: the Fatebinder needs many Primes in one round, which could pile onto one action and one-shot rulers.
  - Guard: several Primes on one action add up but count toward the Conjunction cap and the ruler phase rule. Severance only executes rulers down to their next phase.
- **Delay lock.**
  - The danger: chained Delays could stop a foe from ever acting.
  - Guards: the chain limit above, and ruler signature moves marked as unable to wait.
- **A passenger feeling.**
  - The danger: an Oracle that only buffs feels like a passenger.
  - Guard: each archetype has its own finisher (Echoed strike, Fulfil, Severance), and every common either deals Void or protects.
- **Company size.**
  - The danger: Prime is worth more with more heroes.
  - Guard: the two- and four-hero clear rates are checked for the Oracle on its own.
