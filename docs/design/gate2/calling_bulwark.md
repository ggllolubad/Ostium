# Bulwark: the Iron Vow (Gate 2 calling brief)

## 1. Fantasy

He held a tower once, and something came through it anyway. The abbey gave him a door to carry instead: an old church door, oak bound with three iron hinges, the ring handle still on its face, a strip of rubric tied to the ring. He does not ask what is behind the company. He stands so that it stays behind him. The garrison taught him one trade: be where the blow is going, and still be standing after it lands. His paths are three ways of paying for that. The Last Bastion takes more than anyone and does not fall; the Ironwarden carries the cold of a northern chapel on his windward side; the Thornguard wears his penance outward, on barbs.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home:** rank 1. Most cards also work from rank 2; self-Block cards work from any rank.
- **Hits:** ranks 1 and 2, the mace coming over the shield's top edge. A few hooks reach ranks 3 and 4, only to drag a foe forward.
- **Protects:** Guard targets any other hero, usually the back ranks (3 and 4).

## 3. Core loop

Read the intents, then put the door where the blows are going: Block for himself, Guard on the hero a foe means to strike, or Taunt to pull every single-target blow onto him. Then make the blows cost the foe: Block and Armor turn into mace damage, Thorns hurt whoever strikes him, and Scars (HP lost this fight) power the Last Bastion. Resources: Block, Fortify, Armor, Guard, Taunt, Thorns, Frost, Scars, Grit.

**Starters (5 distinct):** a Block card, a plain mace blow, a Guard card, a Taunt with Block, and a heavy blow that adds part of his Block as damage. Together they teach block, cover, punish.

**Tweaks for approval:** Riposte leaves the Bulwark and becomes the Duelist's alone (the Bulwark answers blows with Thorns). Bleed leaves the Thornguard (Duelist only), except one bridge uncommon. Shield Up, Challenge and Bash are cut.

## 4. Kit skill

**Bar the Door** (1 vigour): Guard another hero this round, and gain a little Block for each foe that means to strike them.

Why it is not a card: the company plans every round around a Bulwark who can always cover one hero; as a card, a bad draw leaves the back rank bare in exactly the round the intents demand cover. It gives almost nothing when no blow is aimed at an ally, so it is not the default play (Shield Up, a flat Block kit, was the fourth most used action in the baseline).

**Path kits (level 4):**
- Last Bastion, **Come On, Then:** Taunt this round. No Block.
- Ironwarden, **Bank the Cold:** Fortify (your Block is kept into next round).
- Thornguard, **Scourge:** lose a little HP, gain Thorns.

## 5. Archetypes

### Last Bastion: The Wound Holds
- **Plan.** Setup: Taunt and Guard so the company's blows land on him; oaths that cost HP up front. Payoff: Block and damage that grow with Scars, and cards that turn stronger at Death's Door. Finisher: a heavy mace blow that deals damage per Scar.
- **Key commons:** *Draw Their Eyes* (Taunt, Block). *Old Wound* (Block, more per Scar). *Toll of the Tower* (mace blow, more per Scar). *Blood for Iron* (lose HP, gain Armor for the fight; Exhaust). *Still Standing* (Retain; Block, much more at Death's Door). *Grit Your Teeth* (shed stress, Block).
- **Weak against:** damage through Block and Armor (Discharge, Ash-Mark, Absolute Zero on a hero); Blight and Toxin, which stop the healing he needs between fights; multi-hit foes that strip all his Grit in one round; foes that never strike him.

### Ironwarden: Winter Wall
- **Plan.** Setup: Fortified Block, and Frost on every foe that means to strike him (read from the intents when played). Payoff: Block that carries from round to round, Weaken on the attackers, Block per Frost on the foes. Finisher: a shield blow that deals his Block and Shatters a Frozen foe.
- **Key commons:** *Rime Coat* (Block; each foe that means to strike you gains Frost). *Hold Through the Night* (Block, Fortify). *Hoarfrost Blow* (mace blow, Stable Frost). *Windward Shield* (another hero gains Block; the front foe gains Frost). *Cold Iron* (Block, plus some per Frost on all foes). *Break the Ice* (damage equal to half your Block; Shatter).
- **Weak against:** frost-resistant and frost-immune foes (Icebound Fleet, Sundered Rime, Hollow Barrow, Stilled Spire, White Silence); Erode, which cuts Block gained; Block strippers; heavy burst in round 1, before the wall has grown.

### Thornguard: Penance
- **Plan.** Setup: Thorns, Taunt, and self-harm that buys more Thorns. Payoff: swarms and multi-hit foes wound themselves on him; mace blows add his Thorns. Finisher: spend all his Thorns as one blow (one uncommon lays them as Bleed instead, the bridge to the Bloodletter).
- **Key commons:** *Barbed Plate* (Thorns, Block). *Hair Shirt* (lose HP, more Thorns; also adds Scars). *Come and Take It* (Taunt, Thorns). *Spiked Boss* (rank 1 blow, Thorns). *Thorn Hedge* (another hero gains Thorns). *Penitent's Blow* (mace blow plus your Thorns).
- **Weak against:** foes that do not strike: casters, stress dealers, course appliers, summoners. Single heavy hitters (Thorns pay once per hit). Stun on the Thornguard.

**Bridges:** Last Bastion and Thornguard both want to be struck, so Taunt commons serve both, and Hair Shirt feeds Scars. Last Bastion and Ironwarden both turn Block into damage. Uncommons sit on these seams.

## 7. Legendaries

- **Last Bastion, The Door Does Not Fall:** at Death's Door his last Grit is spent only once his Block is gone, and every hit he survives there adds to his next blow; he wins by living on the threshold and answering each blow.
- **Last Bastion, The Ledger of Wounds:** once a fight, a blow deals damage equal to his Scars; a long ruler fight ends in one stroke.
- **Ironwarden, Winter Without End:** his Block never clears and grows each round by the Frost on all foes; a wall that only rises, spent by Shatter blows.
- **Ironwarden, The Long Night:** every foe that strikes him gains Frost, and a foe that reaches enough Frost is Frozen for his next blow to Shatter; he wins by freezing the front line one by one.
- **Thornguard, Crown of Iron Thorns:** every foe that strikes any hero takes his Thorns; swarms and multi-hitters kill themselves on the company.
- **Thornguard, The Scourged Hand:** his Thorns no longer fall, and his mace blows deal his Thorns twice; penance becomes the weapon.

## 8. Weakness

- Blows aimed at every hero: Taunt and Guard only redirect single-target attacks.
- Pressure that is not a blow: stress, course applications, afflictions, summons.
- Damage through Block and Armor: Discharge, Ash-Mark, Absolute Zero on a hero.
- Block strippers and Erode (void worlds).
- Displacement: foes that push or pull him out of rank 1.
- Threats he cannot reach: casters and summoners in ranks 3 and 4 behind an armoured front.

Good test encounters: a summoner behind two armoured foes; a group whose attacks hit all heroes; a Black Pyre fight where Ash-Mark ignores his door.

## 9. Keywords

- **Scars** (new): HP this hero has lost this fight. Healing does not lower it. Capped at max HP.
- **Shatter** (new name for an existing rule): bonus damage to a Frozen foe from a physical hit, ending the Freeze.
- Existing statuses that need a hover entry: **Thorns**, **Fortify**, **Anchor**.
- Existing and used: Block, Armor, Guard, Taunt, Grit, Death's Door, Stun, Weaken, Retain, Exhaust.

## 10. Risks

- **Armor stacking.** Permanent Armor comes only from Exhausting oaths, rares and path cards; Rules v2's cap of 5 is the fallback.
- **Fortify growth.** Fortified Block plus Block-to-damage could climb without limit over a 12-round ruler fight. Fortify keeps Block for one round only, and finishers spend the Block they count. Winter Without End is the single exception and is a capstone.
- **Scars loops.** Heal-and-hurt (Apothecary healing, self-harm cards) inflates Scars. Scars are capped at max HP, and self-harm cards cost vigour.
- **Playing wounded between fights.** Payoffs read Scars, not missing HP, so starting a fight low gives nothing.
- **Passivity.** An all-Block Bulwark is slow and dull. Every archetype has a mace finisher among its commons, and most Block cards carry a rider that pays off.
- **Thorns against swarms.** Thorns pay once per hit and need Taunt to gather the hits; Stage 4 keeps multi-hit swarms to a few worlds.
- **Assembly.** About 10 offers show roughly 12 distinct commons a descent, so about 3 of an archetype's 6. Each archetype's commons cover setup and payoff twice over, so any 3 make it work, and the Taunt and Block commons count for two archetypes.
