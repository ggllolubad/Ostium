# Calling brief: the Alchemist (the Brewer)

Gate 2, Deliverable A. Draft for Cole's review. No final numbers.

## 1. Fantasy

An apothecary, distiller or plague doctor, hired for a steady hand. Stocky and slightly hunched, forearms burn-scarred under gloves to the elbow, spectacles pushed up on the cap and a cloth tied over the mouth when brewing. The bandolier of corked and waxed flasks across the chest is the company's medicine chest and its artillery at once, and the copper still-coil on the back is never quite cold. Where the Elementalist argues the Six Courses, the Alchemist bottles them: fire and water first, rot when the Plaguewright's beak goes on, bitter herbs and bandage rolls when the Apothecary kneels by the wounded. The idle is a swirled flask held to the light; the attack is an overarm throw.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home ranks:** 3 and 4. The Alchemist is fragile and works from the back.
- **Used from:** 2 to 4 for throws; belt and dosing cards from any rank.
- **Hits:** thrown cards reach ranks 1 to 4. Splash cards hit a target and the foes beside it, or ranks 1 and 2 only (a pot lobbed into the front line).

## 3. Core loop

Each round the Alchemist throws and brews. Throws lay Stable Fire and Water (Toxin on the Plaguewright) on foes for anyone's Volatile card to set off. Brews put Fresh flasks on the company's belt, which any hero can drink or throw. Resources: course stacks on foes, flasks on the belt, and Improved (+) flasks.

## 4. Kit skill

- **Brew** (1 vigour, once per round): put a Fresh basic flask on the belt: a fire pot, a brine jar or a plain draught. It is ready at the start of the next round.
- **Why it is not a card:** the belt belongs to the company, so its brewer must be able to feed it from any hand. Brew is worth less than a card because the flask still costs a hero's belt use, arrives a round late and spoils when the fight ends.
- **Path kit skills at level 4 (optional):**
  - Transmuter, **Transmute**: turn one stack of a course on a foe into another course.
  - Plaguewright, **Lance the Boil**: copy one Toxin stack from a foe onto each foe beside it.
  - Apothecary, **Refill**: a flask emptied this fight returns to the belt Fresh.

## 5. Archetypes

### Transmuter: Reagents (Conjunction engineering)

- **Plan.** Setup: lay Stable Fire and Water on one foe and splash its neighbours. Payoff: transmute those stacks into whatever completes a Conjunction or hits the foe's weakness, then set them off with a reagent. Finisher: dissolve every course on a target into one reaction, or Improve a belt flask into a much stronger version and throw it.
- **Key commons:**
  - Firepot (a hit with Fire)
  - Brine Jar (Water on the target and one neighbour)
  - Lye Splash (strip Armor)
  - Base to Gold (turn one course on a target into another)
  - Reagent (turn the target's largest course Volatile, setting it off)
  - Rectify (Improve a Fresh flask on the belt)
- **Weak against:** foes made of fire or water (the Boiling Baths), foes that cast Ward, and fights too short for setup.

### Plaguewright: Contagion (Toxin and Combust)

- **Plan.** Setup: put Toxin on a foe and its neighbours; Toxin also wears down Armor. Payoff: spread and ripen it (copy it to neighbours, double it). Finisher: Combust (fire with toxin, the highest multiplier) from a Volatile Fire, or damage per Toxin on the target.
- **Key commons:**
  - Plague Flask (Toxin, plus a little on the neighbours)
  - Miasma Jar (Toxin on ranks 1 and 2)
  - Sour Wick (a small Volatile Fire that sets off Combust)
  - Black Bile (damage per Toxin)
  - Rat Bag (Toxin; when the target dies, its Toxin passes to the next foe)
  - Fume Hood (Ward for the Alchemist)
- **Weak against:** worlds whose creatures are made of or live in toxin (Rotmire, Sulphur Pits, Hollow Barrow, Venom Spires, Blighted Garden), lone targets where spread is wasted, and foes that cleanse.

### Apothecary: Full Belt (sustain that arms the company)

- **Plan.** Setup: brew Fresh draughts, tinctures and salts. Payoff: Dosed cards give a bonus whenever any hero uses a flask (the drinker gains Block, the Alchemist draws), and Improved flasks hit harder. Finisher: Refill the best flask and dose the company for a decisive round; the archetype wins by keeping the killers standing and fed with Might and Momentum draughts.
- **Key commons:**
  - Field Dressing (heal)
  - Tincture (Block and stress relief)
  - Steady Hand (the next flask used this round counts as Improved)
  - Bandolier (brew two weak Fresh flasks)
  - Poultice (Regen)
  - Smelling Salts (brew a stress flask)
- **Weak against:** Blight and "cannot be healed" effects (the Blighted Garden, Barrow Sickness), burst that kills before the belt pays, and foes that smash or steal belt flasks.

## 6. Company hooks

The belt is the Alchemist's main gift: every flask is another hero's action.

## 7. Legendaries

- **Transmuter: The Great Work.** When you set off a Conjunction, the leftover stacks transmute and set off a second one on the same foe.
- **Transmuter: Solve et Coagula.** Strip every course from every foe and pour the total onto one foe as a single course of your choice.
- **Plaguewright: Black Death.** Toxin on foes never fades and spreads to each neighbour at round end.
- **Plaguewright: Firedamp.** A foe that dies with Toxin on it combusts, and its neighbours take its Toxin as damage.
- **Apothecary: The Physick Garden.** At the start of each round, brew an Improved Fresh flask of your choice.
- **Apothecary: Theriac.** Every flask a hero uses also Primes that hero's next action.

## 8. Weakness

- **Fire- and water-immune foes:** they blunt the base pool; Toxin-proof foes blunt the Plaguewright.
- **Ward:** foes that cast Ward cancel the stacks it lays.
- **Divers:** foes that reach rank 3 or 4 hit a hero with little HP.
- **Early pressure:** the opening rounds are weak while flasks brew and stacks build, so heavy early damage punishes it.
- **No healing:** no-heal effects shut off the Apothecary.
- **Flask theft:** encounter idea, a foe intent that steals or smashes one belt flask (fits wreckers and thieves).

## 9. Keywords

- **Belt:** the company's shared flasks. Any hero may use one flask a round as an action in the Ordo. Rules v2 does not yet define belt use; this brief assumes it costs no vigour, at most one per hero per round, and that the belt holds a fixed number of flasks.
- **Brew:** put a named flask on the belt; it is ready at the start of the next round.
- **Fresh:** a brewed or refilled flask; it spoils when the fight ends, so it never adds to the run's stock.
- **Improve:** a flask on the belt becomes its upgraded (+) version for this fight. This reuses the Upgrade keyword.
- **Refill:** a flask emptied this fight returns to the belt, Fresh.
- **Ailment:** any harmful status or course stack on a hero (Burn, Soak, Weaken, Bleed, Vulnerable and so on). It is separate from affliction cards. This term is shared with the Hexweaver and the Vessel, and Cleanse removes ailments.

**Tweaks for approval:**
- Flasks leave the Alchemist's hand and live on the company belt (Cole's decision, applied here).
- The kit shrinks from Toss, Brew and Tonic to Brew alone.
- Fire and Water stay the base courses, with Toxin on the Plaguewright path.

## 10. Risks

- **Free belt actions are card advantage.**
  - The danger: free belt uses could outvalue whole cards.
  - Guards: a fixed number of belt slots, one use per hero per round, Fresh flasks spoil, and a brew pays a card and vigour now for a flask next round. The sim measures value per vigour including the belt use.
- **Combust has the highest multiplier.**
  - The danger: the Plaguewright's finisher could be too strong.
  - Guards: the Conjunction cap holds it, so watch Firedamp and any cap raise (the Accumulator).
- **Apothecary stalling.**
  - The danger: endless sustain could stretch fights out.
  - Guards: steady stress pressure and longer fights tax stalling, and healing per vigour sits in the same band as Block.
- **Dependence on partners.**
  - The danger: if only other callings can set off its stacks, a weak company leaves it with no damage.
  - Guards: each archetype carries its own Volatile common (Reagent, Sour Wick), so it can finish alone.
- **Refill farming.**
  - The danger: refilled run flasks could be kept for later fights.
  - Guard: Refill always returns a flask as Fresh, so a run flask is still spent once.
