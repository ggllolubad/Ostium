# Calling brief: the Vessel (the secret seventh calling)

Gate 2, Deliverable A. Draft for Cole's review. No final numbers. The redesign plan has the Vessel designed last, so this brief is the frame for it, not a commitment that holds up the six callings.

## 1. Fantasy

The Prismatic who took the Mother of Courses' place in the hidden ending, come back to the gate under the same name. The six inks run in bands up the skin from the fingertips (vermilion, pale blue, gold, deep blue, sick green, violet-black), edged in gold like an illuminated page. The eyes hold no colour, and the Vessel sleeps standing. Courses that would burn, freeze or rot anyone else settle on it and wait, sitting on their bands like seals. It holds all six in one body without letting them fuse, as she once did. It is the only hero who is not hunched and the only one who carries courses as a resource instead of suffering them. When it lifts an open hand, the course leaves the skin and crosses to the foe; the stance does not change.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home ranks:** 2 and 3. From there the Vessel can reach the heroes on both sides and the foes ahead.
- **Take cards:** reach any hero, and foes in ranks 1 to 3.
- **Give cards:** hit ranks 1 to 3; a few Mouth cards reach rank 4.
- **Hearth cards:** favour the heroes adjacent to the Vessel.

## 3. Core loop

Each round the Vessel takes courses into its body and gives them back where they hurt most.
- **Taking:** from heroes, which cleanses them; from foes, which moves one foe's stacks toward another; and from belt flasks.
- **Giving:** onto a foe, Stable to build a Conjunction or Volatile to set it off.

Its resource is held stacks on its own skin. They never harm it and never react, and the Whole check pays for holding all six courses at once.

## 4. Kit skill

- **Draw Breath** (1 vigour, once per round): take one stack of one course from any hero, or from a foe in reach, onto yourself.
- **Why it is not a card:** the body must never stay empty, and the company should always have a way to lift a course off a hero. A single stack is worth less than any common, so the kit feeds the calling without replacing its cards.
- **Path kit skills at level 4 (optional):**
  - Hearth, **Shelter**: this round, the next course aimed at an adjacent hero lands on you instead.
  - Mouth, **Breathe Out**: give one held stack, Stable, to a foe.
  - Whole, **Weigh**: move one held stack from your largest course to your smallest.

### The three paths

The path names (Hearth, Mouth, Whole) come from the bible. Their mechanics are proposed here, since the bible gives none.

#### Hearth: The Warm Room (shelter, then weight) (proposed)

- **Plan.** Setup: take courses off heroes and catch those aimed at them (Shelter, Mantle). Payoff: held stacks turn into Block and Regen for the heroes beside the Vessel. Finisher: a strike equal to everything held, or Unclench to heal the company for the final rounds.
- **Key commons:**
  - Lift (take a course from an adjacent hero; they gain Block)
  - Stand Near (the next course aimed at an adjacent hero comes to you)
  - Banked Coals (Block equal to your total held stacks)
  - The Weight of It (damage equal to your total held stacks)
  - Hearthstone (Regen for adjacent heroes, more for each different course held)
- **Weak against:** foes that lay no courses (the body stays empty), physical burst on a scholar's body, and a body full to capacity, where overflow is lost.

#### Mouth: The Conduit (take from one, give to another) (proposed)

- **Plan.** Setup: swallow courses from foes and belt flasks until the body holds a mixed store. Payoff: give a Stable course to a foe that carries its partner, then Spit a Volatile one to set off the Conjunction. Finisher: Exhale empties the store onto one foe for a chain of Conjunctions.
- **Key commons:**
  - Spit
  - Swallow
  - Mouthful (take one stack of every course from a foe)
  - Retch (give your largest held course to a foe and its neighbour)
  - Taste (draw next round if the target carries a course you hold)
  - Open Hand
- **Weak against:** foes immune to what it holds (each world's creatures shrug off what they are made of), foes that cast Ward, and its slow opening rounds while the body is empty.

#### Whole: Holds All Six (breadth and balance) (proposed)

- **Plan.** Setup: gather one of each course from varied sources (Six Breaths, the company's leftovers, flasks). Payoff: cards that grow with the number of different courses held, and more when the stacks are even. Finisher: The Whole Conjunction, or The Weighing at all six.
- **Key commons:**
  - Ink Band (gain a stack of the course you hold least)
  - Rule Line (damage for each different course held)
  - Balance the Scale (move a stack from your largest course to your smallest; draw)
  - Nimbus (Ward and Block when all held courses are equal)
  - Illumination (Prime for each different course held)
  - Six Breaths
- **Weak against:** worlds that supply only their own two courses, long setup against early pressure, and anything that makes it give before it is whole.

## 6. Company hooks

The Vessel's gift is that it carries the company's courses, so they never burn, freeze or rot the others. Its gives also hand any partner the missing half of a Conjunction.

| Calling | Vessel gives | Vessel wants | Pairing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | It lifts the courses a front-rank tank collects, and Mantle catches course attacks on the back ranks, so the Bulwark only covers physical blows | Guard and Taunt: courses cannot hurt the Vessel, swords can | Ironwarden Frosts a foe, the Mouth Vessel gives Water: Freeze |
| Duelist | Water given to a foe the Duelist's lightning-touched blade will strike | Pull to bring rank 4 foes into reach | The Duelist's Lightning on a foe the Vessel Soaked: Electrocute |
| Elementalist | It holds whatever the Elementalist is not attuned to and gives it to complete a pair; it takes the Elementalist's own backlash | A Volatile to set off what the Vessel laid Stable. | Alchemist | It drinks course flasks from the belt, and by the body rule a flask becomes held stock; it takes the Fire that hot cures leave | Brewed course flasks; Transmuter conversions that hand it the course it lacks | Transmuter with Whole: the sixth course comes from the belt |
| Oracle | It supplies the partner course for the Oracle's Void (Water for Drown, Frost for Absolute Zero) | Prime on a Give or on The Whole Conjunction | Fatebinder Primes The Whole Conjunction |
| Hexweaver | It takes the course stacks, which leaves Pass It On for Bleed, Vulnerable and affliction cards; Toxin and Void given onto Hexed foes (Blight) | Hex on foes, because the Vessel's body is a thin scholar's against physical blows | Doomsayer Hexes the line while the Hearth Vessel holds every course |

## 7. Legendaries

- **Hearth: The Hearth Kept.** At round start, take every course from every hero, and each stack taken gives that hero Block.
- **Hearth: The Weight of the World.** Once a round, a hit from the Vessel also deals damage equal to everything it holds.
- **Mouth: The Unstopped Mouth.** Every course a foe lays on a hero is swallowed and spat back at that foe, Volatile.
- **Mouth: The Mother's Tongue.** Whenever you give a course to a foe, every other foe receives it too.
- **Whole: The Whole Conjunction** (lore card). Hold all six, then pour them into one foe as every Conjunction at once.
- **Whole: The Mother's Place.** While you hold all six, every Conjunction any hero sets off counts as full power.

## 8. Weakness

- **Physical foes:** they lay no courses, so there is nothing to take, and their blows land on a thin body.
- **Ward:** foes that cast it cancel what the Vessel gives.
- **Immunity:** foes made of the courses it holds ignore its gives.
- **Early pressure:** the empty opening rounds are its weakest.
- **Afflictions it cannot take:** False Light, The Split, Venom Tremor, Stillness, Ash-Mark and Seen are not course stacks, so the Vessel cannot lift them. The worlds behind them (VI, VIII, X, XI, XIII, XV) test it.

## 9. Keywords

- **Body rule:** from the redesign plan. Courses never harm the Vessel and never form a Conjunction on it.
- **Hold (held):** course stacks on the Vessel's skin.
  - They do not fall at round end. This is a rule change for approval, from the lore: "they settle and wait".
  - Each course holds up to a capacity. Six is the thematic starting value.
- **Take:** move stacks of a course from a unit onto the Vessel. Ward does not stop a Take.
- **Give:** move held stacks onto a foe, Stable unless the card says Volatile.
- **Whole:** the check that the Vessel holds at least one stack of all six courses.

**Open question for Cole:** does the Vessel start at the Prismatic's level and gear? It unlocks late, so its balance band depends on the answer.

## 10. Risks

- **Course worlds trivialised.**
  - The danger: the Vessel lifts course afflictions and enemy course attacks, so it could flatten Drowned Kingdom, Rotmire, Storm Foundry, Icebound Fleet, White Silence and Blighted Garden.
  - Guards: the capacity cap, Mantle as a one-round rare, and physical damage still landing. The sim reports the Vessel's clear rate per world.
- **The Whole Conjunction one-shots rulers.**
  - The danger: a full body poured into a ruler could end the fight.
  - Guards: each Conjunction stays under the cap, a ruler drops to its next phase rather than dying, and the card empties the body, so it needs a full refill.
- **Unlimited hoarding.**
  - The danger: held stacks that never fade could pile up without limit.
  - Guard: the capacity per course; overflow is lost.
- **Overlap with the Alchemist and the Hexweaver.**
  - The danger: all three cleanse heroes.
  - Guard: the Vessel keeps what it takes (a store), the Alchemist removes ailments, and the Hexweaver turns them into Hex and damage.
- **Rules load.**
  - The danger: the Take effect and the Whole check are new engine work.
  - Guard: Gate 1 already lists both, and they ship with the Vessel last.
