# Calling brief: the Hexweaver (the Cunning Folk)

Gate 2, Deliverable A. Draft for Cole's review. No final numbers.

## 1. Fantasy

A hedge witch, poppet-maker or corpse-candle burner: cunning folk, wiry and old before their time, crouched low with dirt under the nails and knotted cord tied round the arms and waist. Straw and cloth poppets hang from the belt; black tallow candles burn on a shoulder plate of wax, the only warm light they carry. The Hexweaver does not strike a foe so much as drive a pin into its likeness. What ails the company (the rot, the cold, the bleeding) can be bound into a poppet and handed on to something that deserves it.

## 2. Ranks

- **Home ranks:** 2 and 3. The Hexweaver crouches behind the front line but close enough to touch.
- **Used from:** 2 to 4.
- **Hits:** curses ignore rank, so Hex, Doom and Transfer cards hit ranks 1 to 4.
- **Rank locks:** Toxin touches and Hag's Finger (a Pull) hit ranks 1 to 3. Pactbound strikes favour rank 2.

## 3. Core loop

Each round the Hexweaver lays Hex, then chooses whether to keep it as protection or spend it on a payoff. Every 2 Hex cuts a foe's damage by 1, and Hex never fades. Around that it moves the company's ailments onto foes and lays Toxin and Void. Resources:
- **Hex:** kept for protection or spent for payoffs.
- **Toxin and Void stacks** on foes.
- **Ailments** moved off heroes.
- **Path resources:** its own HP (Pactbound), Doom (Doomsayer), and turned attacks with Thorns (Mirrorhex).

## 4. Kit skill

- **Pass It On** (1 vigour, once per round): move one ailment from any hero onto a foe. A course arrives as Stable stacks; a status arrives as itself.
- **Why it is not a card:** it is the company's promise that what afflicts a hero can be handed on, so it must be there when needed. It is reactive and worth nothing when no hero carries an ailment, so it is never the default best play.
- **Path kit skills at level 4 (optional):**
  - Pactbound, **Bloodletting**: lose a little HP; the next Hex you apply this round is doubled.
  - Doomsayer, **Ring the Bell**: every foe with Hex gains a little Doom.
  - Mirrorhex, **Glint**: the next ailment a foe lays on a hero this round lands on that foe instead.

## 5. Archetypes

### Pactbound: Blood Price (self-harm for power)

- **Plan.** Setup: pay HP for Hex, Prime and vigour, and take ailments from allies onto yourself on purpose. Payoff: cards that grow with your missing HP and with the ailments you carry. Finisher: dump every ailment you carry onto one foe as damage, then a Death's Door strike.
- **Key commons:**
  - Tithe of Blood (lose HP, gain Might)
  - Spite (damage per ailment you carry)
  - Drain (a hit that heals)
  - Blood Offering (lose HP, gain vigour next round)
  - Take the Rot (move an ally's ailment onto yourself, apply Hex)
  - Martyr's Pin (lose HP, apply heavy Hex)
- **Weak against:** no-heal effects (Blight, Barrow Sickness), damage that ignores Block (Ash-Mark), Vulnerable (Seen), and burst while at Death's Door.

### Doomsayer: The Knell (Doom and execution)

- **Plan.** Setup: Hex the whole line; Hex is protection while it waits. Payoff: turn Hex into Doom, which lowers every execute check against that foe, and pass Doom on when a doomed foe dies. Finisher: Toll executes a foe whose Doom has met its HP; Toll of Bells doubles Doom on every foe first.
- **Key commons:**
  - Handbell (Hex on every foe)
  - Bad Tidings (Doom)
  - Corpse Candle (Doom and Toxin)
  - Knell (every Hexed foe gains Doom)
  - Dirge (damage to every foe, more to the Hexed)
  - Hasten the End (damage from the target's Doom)
- **Weak against:** high-HP lone targets (a ruler only drops a phase), summoners refilling ranks with fresh foes that carry no Hex or Doom, and foes that cleanse themselves.

### Mirrorhex: The Cracked Glass (turn and reflect)

- **Plan.** Setup: Hex for protection, and Thorns and Ward on allies (Riposte stays the Duelist's). Payoff: turn a foe's attack onto its neighbour, reflect ailments back at the foes that lay them, and steal Block and Might. Finisher: Hall of Mirrors, where every foe's single-target attack strikes its neighbour.
- **Key commons:**
  - Mirror Shard (Thorns and Ward)
  - Silver Lie (take a foe's Block)
  - Cracked Glass (damage per Might on the target)
  - Mirror, Mirror (the target's attack hits the foe beside it)
  - Echoing Hex (Hex with Echo)
  - Borrowed Strength (strip a foe's Might; an ally gains it)
- **Weak against:** lone foes, who have no neighbour to turn onto (rulers alone, a last survivor). Attacks that hit every hero, and foes that afflict without attacking, also slip past it.

## 6. Company hooks

The Hexweaver gives the company two things: it hands the company's ailments to the foes, and its Hex blunts any foe the company cannot kill yet.

| Calling | Hexweaver gives | Hexweaver wants | Pairing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | Hex on the foe the Bulwark Taunts (Hex counts per hit, so it is best against multi-hitters); Bleed and Vulnerable lifted off the tank | Guard for a Pactbound low on HP | Mirrorhex reflection beside Thornguard's Thorns: foes punished twice |
| Duelist | Doom lowers the Executioner's execute threshold; Might, Thorns and Riposte stripped from a duelling foe; Vulnerable once a foe carries Hex | Kills on doomed foes to pass their Doom on; Follow-up on the foe the Hexweaver just spent Hex on | Doomsayer with Executioner: a Mark and a Toll on the same foe |

## 7. Legendaries

- **Pactbound: Signed in Blood.** HP you lose to your own cards is also dealt to a foe as true damage.
- **Pactbound: The Open Door.** At Death's Door your cards cost less and hit far harder, and your own cards cannot kill you.
- **Doomsayer: The Great Bell.** At round end every foe gains Doom equal to its Hex, and any foe whose Doom meets its HP dies (a ruler drops a phase).
- **Doomsayer: Death Knell.** When a foe dies, its Hex and Doom pass in full to every other foe.
- **Mirrorhex: Hall of Mirrors.** For this round and the next, every foe's single-target attack strikes its neighbour.
- **Mirrorhex: The Witch's Glass.** Every ailment a foe lays on a hero lands on that foe instead, doubled.

## 8. Weakness

- **Big single hits:** Hex cuts each hit by a flat amount, so it is strongest against many small hits and weakest against one huge blow.
- **Non-damage pressure:** foes that deal stress or apply statuses get little from Hex.
- **Summoners and cleansers:** they waste Hex and Doom.
- **Low burst:** it kills slowly before its payoffs are set.
- **Healing denial:** no-heal effects punish the Pactbound.
- **Lone foes:** they blank the Mirrorhex.
- **Course-proof foes:** foes made of or living in toxin or void blunt its courses.

## 9. Keywords

- **Ailment:** any harmful status or course stack on a hero. It is separate from affliction cards. This term is shared with the Alchemist and the Vessel.
- **Spend (Hex):** remove Hex from a foe to pay for a card's effect. Rules v2 already implies this; it needs a hover line.
- **Doom (redefined):** a mark that does not fade. Every execute check against the foe treats its HP as lower by its Doom. Toll executes when Doom meets HP. A ruler that would be executed drops to its next phase instead, as with Absolute Zero.
- **Unbind:** a new effect. Remove an affliction card from a hero's hand for this fight; the target foe suffers its when-drawn effect and gains Hex. It is used by an uncommon (working name Bind the Fear) and the Pactbound path.

**Tweaks for approval:**
- **Doom moves here from the Oracle.** It gains a passive meaning (lower execute checks), which hooks the Duelist's Executioner and Absolute Zero.
- **The kit becomes Pass It On.** It replaces Hex, Transfer and Unleash.
- **Hexweaver cards can reach affliction cards.** This is a new effect.

## 10. Risks

- **Hex lock.**
  - The danger: Hex never fades, so Hex on every foe could cut the company's damage taken to near zero.
  - Guards: Hex has to be spent to win, which keeps a real choice; elites and rulers get moves that shed Hex. If the sim still shows lockouts, the fallback is a floor: no hit drops below half its damage.
- **Doom and rulers.**
  - The danger: executions could end ruler fights at once.
  - Guard: executing a ruler drops it to its next phase, so Doomsayer and Executioner pairs speed up ruler fights but never skip them.
- **Affliction trivialised.**
  - The danger: Unbind could make the affliction system meaningless.
  - Guards: Unbind only reaches cards in hand, lasts one fight, and the Infirmarium is still the only permanent cure. The kit moves ailments, never affliction cards.
- **Heal and harm loops.**
  - The danger: Pactbound with Apothecary healing could loop for endless vigour.
  - Guard: vigour gains from self-harm are Exhaust or once per round.
- **Overlap with the Vessel.**
  - The danger: both callings lift ailments off heroes.
  - Guard: the Hexweaver turns what it moves into Hex and damage, while the Vessel stores courses. The briefs split statuses from courses so both stay worth taking together.
