# Gate 3 check: callings B (Alchemist, Oracle, Hexweaver, neutral)

Checked against `scripts/combat.gd` (`_fx1`, `q`, `_exec`, `_rite_fire`, `_apply_element`, `_delay`, `end_round`,
`resolve_player_action`, `commit_cards`), the three Gate 2 briefs, DECISIONS, GATE3_VOCAB and the engine update. A
scratch copy of `build_v3.py --budget` (output kept out of the repo) reports "validation problems: none". The budget
tool does not price Echo, Conjunctions set off by a card's own stacks, or Delay's real effect, so several cards below
pass the budget and are still outliers.

Two engine facts drive many findings:

- Heroes resolve every action, then Echoes, then the foes act in `enemy_queue` order. `DELAY` only moves a foe to the
  back of that queue. A foe loses an action only when it is already last (it is postponed, acts once over two rounds,
  and cannot wait twice in a row).
- `commit_cards` exhausts a card only if `kw` holds "Exhaust". A Rite without it goes to the discard, comes back, and
  each replay appends another copy of the rite to `s.rites`.

## alchemist

- [balance] al_sour_wick: 1 Toxin then 2 Volatile Fire sets off Combust on its own Toxin (power 3, 9 damage on a bare foe; 18 on a foe with 4+ Toxin, and it also sets off every other course there) for a 1-cost common. The budget tool prices it at 5.2 points because it does not value the Conjunction. Quicklime (starter) needs Water from another card first. Fix: drop the Toxin, so the card needs setup, or cut it to 1 Volatile Fire.
- [balance] al_catalytic_still + fl_steam: the Steam Flask sets off its own Scald (1 Water, then 2 Volatile Fire: power 3, 6 damage and Vulnerable). That Conjunction fires Catalytic Still, which brews the next Steam Flask. So a 1-cost rare gives one free flask action every round for the rest of the fight, two upgraded. Athanor, the other 1-cost Transmuter rite, gives 1 Fire and 1 Water a round. Fix: brew a Brine Jar or Fire Pot (neither reacts with itself), or make the Steam Flask all Stable.
- [balance] al_boiling_flood: 3 Water then 1 Volatile Fire on every foe is a power-3 Scald on each one: 6 damage and Vulnerable on every foe, plus 1 Water left over. That is about twice its 2-vigour budget, and the upgrade makes it cost 1. Fix: upgrade to 4 Water at cost 2, or make the base 2 Water and 1 Volatile Fire.
- [balance] al_prima_materia: on a bare foe, Apply-missing lays 1 of each course. Conjoin 2 then turns Fire Volatile (Fire comes first in ELEMENTS) and sets off five Conjunctions at power 3: Thermal Shock 3, Plasma 6 with splash, Scald 6 with Vulnerable, Combust 9, and Hollow Flame (3 Burn that never fades). That is about 24 damage and +5 `reactions` for 1 vigour, which Exhaust does not pay for, and it also feeds Event Horizon (+10 to every foe). Fix: cost 2, or apply only the missing course(s) and Conjoin 1.
- [balance] fl_rage + al_refill (kit), al_second_dose, al_restock: Might lasts the fight, and Refill returns the most recently emptied flask every round. The Apothecary kit plus one Bloodrage Draught gives the company +1 permanent Might per round for 1 vigour, and it compounds on multi-hit Duelist cards. Fix: make the draught `ST("empower", 3)` (one-shot), or exclude Might flasks from Refill.
- [balance] fl_acid: the Improve upgrade lowers Toxin from 4 to 2 and adds -1 Armor. Toxin already cuts Armor stack for stack (`armor - toxin` in `_hurt`), and Plaguewright payoffs count Toxin, so Improving an Acid Flask is a downgrade (Rectify, Quintessence and The Physick Garden can do it to the player). Fix: upgrade to 5 Toxin, and the target loses 1 Armor.
- [bug] al_black_death: stacks applied at round end are marked fresh and do not fall that round, so every Toxic foe gains a net 1 Toxin a round. The NEEDS line says the +1 "offsets the fade", which is wrong. SPREAD also runs foe by foe in rank order, so Toxin copied onto rank 2 is copied again onto rank 3 in the same pass: one Toxic foe poisons the whole line in one round end, which is more than "copies 1 Toxin onto each foe beside it". Fix: say "builds by 1 each round" and correct the NEEDS line; either snapshot which foes hold Toxin before spreading (engine) or describe the chain in the text.
- [bug] al_athanor, al_rat_bag, al_black_death, al_the_great_work (up): rite applications run as the Alchemist, so `_apply_element` spends all of the Alchemist's Focus (from a Clarity Draught or the Oracle's Guiding Word) on a one-stack tick. Athanor does it at round start, before any card. Fix (engine): rite effects should not spend the owner's Focus.
- [depth] al_fen_water: the Volatile Water reacts with the card's own 3 Toxin (Toxic Pool) and spends 2 of them. The net result is 1 Toxin and a rank hazard, which is weaker for the Plaguewright than Plague Flask, and the two halves of the card work against each other. Fix: make the Water Stable, or apply the Volatile Water first.
- [style] al_corpse_candles: nearly the same name as the Hexweaver's corpse_candle ("Corpse Candle"). Fix: rename one (for example "Marsh Lights").
- [style] al_brew flavour: "The still-coil on her back" genders the Alchemist; the bible does not. Fix: "on the back".

## oracle

- [bug] or_stop_time: "Delay every foe" processes the foes in queue order and only cycles the queue (A,B,C to B,C,A to C,A,B to A,B,C). No foe is ever last when it is Delayed, so none is postponed, and the rare is "deal 4 to each" for 2 vigour (about 45% of budget). The Chronomancer finisher in the brief does not exist. Fix (engine): a mass Delay should postpone every foe it reaches (once in a row), or rewrite the card so that every foe meaning to attack waits until next round.
- [bug] or_reverse_the_glass: the same queue cycle applies, so no attacking foe loses its action. Fix: as for Stop Time.
- [bug] or_the_turned_glass: the rite Delays the front foe, which is normally first in the queue, so it is only moved to the back. It loses an action only when it fights alone (then every other round, which halves a lone ruler's actions). Fix: Delay the last foe in the queue (a new `to=`), or postpone outright.
- [balance] or_stall (starter), or_stopped_clock, or_stopped_sand, or_broken_pendulum, or_forestalled: a single Delay is all or nothing. On the foe already last in the queue it costs that foe an action (worth a Stun, 9 points) for 1 vigour, every other round; the starter Stall does this to the rank-4 foe. On any other foe it only enables Late Blow. Fix: decide Delay's rule in the sim (postpone always, at a higher cost), and make the hover text say that only the last foe in the queue waits.
- [bug] or_the_last_hour, or_the_turned_glass, or_cassandra, or_the_bone_pouch, or_the_rubric: these Rites have no Exhaust (`kw=["Rite"]`), so they are replayed and their rites stack (The Rubric twice gives Prime 6 per card). Fix: `kw=["Rite", "Exhaust"]` and "Exhaust." in the text and upgrade text.
- [depth] Fatebinder (or_red_letter, or_tapestry, or_cut_the_cord, or_severance, or_woven_shield, or_woven_mantle, or_scarlet_line, or_red_harvest, or_deja_vu, or_bind, or_red_knot, or_gathering_thread, or_the_severed_thread): `prime_count` counts actions that spent Prime, not Prime cards. Since Prime now carries over, Prime cards played back to back merge into one (Knot, Knot, Bind Fate, then a hit counts 1), so the brief's "many cheap Primes" adds nothing. The Severed Thread's `prime_count>=3` needs about six alternating actions in one round. Any Block or healing card also spends Prime and counts (Knot then Drawn Veil counts 1), and a payoff reads the count before its own Prime is spent. Fix: count Prime placed this round (each PRIME effect) or Prime cards played, and state the rule in hover text.
- [balance] or_turn_the_glass (kit): Echo is not priced in budget.py. Deal 3 + 1 Void, then 2 + 1 Void comes to about 6.3 points against a kit budget of 4.2, which is a full common (Stutter). Rules v2 sets a kit at about 70% of a common. Fix: Deal 2 with no Void, or drop the Echo. Also price Echo in budget.py at half the card's effects.
- [balance] or_stutter: with Echo it is about 8.2 points for a 1-cost common (137%). Fix: Deal 3.
- [balance] or_knot vs or_hasten: Knot is 0-cost Prime 3, Knot+ is Prime 4. The Oracle's kit Hasten is Prime 4 for 1 vigour. An upgraded common is strictly better than the kit, and Knot is always correct before any hit. Fix: Knot Prime 2 (3 upgraded), or Hasten 5 (6 upgraded).
- [balance] or_borrowed_time: 0-cost, +1 vigour next round for 7 stress (4 upgraded), not Exhaust. It is the only repeatable free-vigour card in these four sets (Blood Offering, Pact of Bones and Fenn's Alms all Exhaust; the Hexweaver brief's guard is "Exhaust or once per round"). Fix: Exhaust.
- [balance] or_glimpse_ahead: the upgrade removes the stress, leaving a 0-cost "draw 1 next round" with no cost, which is always correct to play. Fix: upgrade to Retain, or to 1 stress.
- [bug] or_rewind: heals before it cleanses, and `_heal` subtracts the target's Toxin, so the Toxin it is about to remove still cuts the heal. The same order is in al_panacea and the neutral herb_bundle. Fix: CLEANSE first (for Panacea and Herb Bundle, whose heal counts ailments, say in the text that Toxin cuts healing).
- [depth] oracle.py (and hexweaver.py) have no ARCHETYPES dict, so `build_v3.py --install` gives the simulator empty key lists for all six of their paths. Assembly rates (GATE3_SPEC check 3) cannot be measured. Fix: add six C/U key cards per path in both files.
- [style] or_turn_the_glass: the same name as the Hexweaver's turn_the_glass. The calling also has Reverse the Glass and The Turned Glass. Fix: rename the kit (for example "Second Hand").
- [style] or_quicken / or_quickening: near-identical names in one calling. Fix: rename Quickening.

## hexweaver

- [bug] bind_the_fear, take_the_curse_in: UNBIND runs the affliction's `on_draw` with the Hexweaver as the actor. Every affliction's effects land on "self" (`to="self"`, SELF, SELFDMG), so the Hexweaver takes the 2 Soak, 2 HP, discard or Lockdown, and the foe gets only the Hex. The text says "The target suffers its when-drawn effect". Fix: in neutral.py, write afflictions with no `to=` (the effect then lands on the drawing hero when drawn and on the foe when unbound), and add a target HP-loss effect to replace SELFDMG (a NEEDS line).
- [bug] take_the_rot, bear_the_burden, sin_eater: TRANSFER onto yourself from "ally_most" picks the hero with the most ailments, which is often the Hexweaver, because carrying them is the Pactbound plan. Self to self, the engine adds each status and then zeroes the source, so Bleed, Vulnerable, Mark, Hex and Doom vanish, and courses are re-applied through your own Ward. The card quietly cleanses you and lowers Spite and Blood for Blood. Fix (engine): an "other hero with the most" source for these, or skip when source equals target.
- [bug] buried_bottle, death_watch, signed_in_blood, the_open_door, the_great_bell, death_knell, hall_of_mirrors, the_witchs_glass: these Rites have no Exhaust and stack on replay. Hall of Mirrors is the worst: every reshuffle turns every foe's attack for two more rounds. Death Watch (a path card, not a legendary) gives +3 Doom per copy per kill. Fix: `kw=["Rite", "Exhaust"]` and "Exhaust." in the text.
- [balance] Hex lock (evil_eye, martyrs_pin, twofold_curse, witching_hour, bloodletting): Hex never fades, and `_attack` subtracts Hex/2 with no floor; the brief's fallback (no hit below half) is not in the engine. Martyr's Pin then Twofold Curse gives (7+2)x2 = 18 Hex, -9 per hit, which permanently blanks a foe hitting for about 9 at Threat 0, for 2 vigour. The Bloodletting kit adds 5 Hex every round. Foes shed Hex only through CLEANSE, which strips course stacks first. Fix: the half-damage floor in `_attack`, or cap Hex's cut.
- [balance] bell_book_candle: 2 Doom per Hex with no cap. On the 18-Hex foe above it adds 40 Doom, which kills at round end any foe at 40 HP or less (a ruler drops a phase) for 2 vigour. The budget reads 0.87 because it assumes 4 Hex. Fix: 1 Doom per Hex (Nine Tailors already does that to every foe), or `max=`.
- [balance] bloodletting (kit): 5 Hex and shed 2 stress for 2 HP beats the starter Evil Eye (4 Hex) and equals Evil Eye+. A kit should be about 70% of a common. Fix: 3 Hex.
- [balance] echoing_hex: Echo is unpriced. 4 Hex + 1 Ward, then 2 Hex + 1 Ward, totals 6 Hex and 2 Ward for 1 vigour (about 170% of a common; Whispered Name gives 3 Hex). Fix: Apply 2 Hex, Echo.
- [balance] blood_price (starter): 0-cost Prime 5 (7 upgraded) for 2 HP. It out-Primes the Oracle's Knot (3/4) and kit Hasten (4/5 for 1 vigour), though Prime is the Oracle's gift. Fix: Prime 3 (4 upgraded).
- [balance] the_open_door: at Death's Door each card played gives 1 Grit, and each hit costs 1. Playing 3 or 4 cards a round banks more Grit than most foes can strip, so the Pactbound is close to unkillable while at Death's Door. Fix: `"per_round": 1` on the rite.
- [balance] tithe_of_blood: 0-cost, 3 HP for +1 Might for the fight, not Exhaust. It is replayed every reshuffle, and next to any heal (Drain, the Apothecary) it is always correct. Fix: Exhaust, or Empower in place of Might.
- [balance] deaths_ledger: Doom equal to missing HP kills, at round end, any non-ruler foe at half HP or less; in effect it doubles the damage dealt so far. It is acceptable as an Exhaust rare, but watch it in the sim with Toll of Bells (which raises the line to two thirds).
- [bug] signed_in_blood: at Death's Door each card's 1 HP costs a Grit, so once Grit runs out the rite kills its owner, which is the opposite of the path's Open Door promise. Fix: `if_="!dd"` on the SELFDMG, or a warning in the text.
- [depth] glass_splinters: it reads your own Thorns, but `arch` claims bulwark_thorns. The Bulwark's Thorns cards are SELF; only Hedge of Spikes (rare) gives the Hexweaver Thorns. Fix: tag it mirrorhex only, or read the target ally's Thorns.
- [depth] Pactbound carrying Toxin: each Toxin stack on the Hexweaver cuts every heal it gets (Drain, the Drink Deep lifesteal, the Apothecary) by 1, so Take the Rot works against Drain. If this tension is intended, say it on Drain; if not, have Take the Rot move statuses only.
- [style] "ailment" means a stack in `ailments`, CLEANSE and Spite, but a kind ("the largest ailment, all its stacks") in Pass It On. Fix: one definition in the hover text ("stack"), and Pass It On reads "the largest ailment (every stack of it)".
- [style] turn_the_glass clashes with the Oracle kit; blood_price clashes with bw_blood_price (Bulwark); corpse_candle clashes with al_corpse_candles. Rite text reads "Rite. Whenever" here but "Rite: whenever" in the other callings. Echoing Hex puts "Echo." last; every other Echo card leads with it.

## neutral

- [bug] fall_back: `if_="targeting_me"` reads the actor (a hero). `q()` returns targeting_me only for a foe (it reads the unit's intent), so the extra 3 Block never happens. Fix: `if_="targeted"` (or "incoming_me").
- [bug] into_the_doorway: `if_="m.targeting_me"` reads the ally, so it has the same problem and the extra 3 Block never happens. Fix: `if_="m.targeted"`.
- [bug] aff_* (all 15): the on_draw effects target "self", so Unbind applies them to the Hexweaver (see the Hexweaver section). Fix: no `to=` on A, ST, DISCARD_RANDOM and LOSE_ARMOR, and a target HP-loss effect for Fever and Ash-Mark.
- [depth] aff_rime (The Split): LOSE_ARMOR on a hero with 0 Armor does nothing, and five of the six callings start at 0 (only the Bulwark has 2). Outside armour gear, the Sundered Rime's affliction is blank. Fix: add "if you have no Armor, become Vulnerable this round" (two effects gated on `armor` and `!armor`).
- [bug] aff_coast (False Light): on_draw fires while the hand is being drawn. As the first card of a round it discards nothing (the hand is empty unless something was Retained), and later in the draw it can only hit cards drawn so far. Fix (engine): run on_draw effects after the draw finishes.
- [balance] touch_the_relic: hero Ward never fades, and Oracle or Hexweaver companies stack it (Starlit Ward 3, Bone Pouch up to 4 a round), so the 12 Block cap (14 upgraded) is reached routinely. That is about 13 points for 1 vigour, above the callings' own Block cards; the neutral pool should sit at or under budget. Fix: cap 8, or 1 Block per Ward.
- [balance] vinegar_cloth: 0-cost with no drawback, so it is always correct to play when drawn (it is weak). Fix: Exhaust, or a 1-cost version that sheds more.
- [style] follow_through: the same name as du_follow_through (Duelist). Fix: rename (for example "Into the Gap"). red_rubric sits close to the Oracle's The Rubric and Rubric Capital, and herb_bundle close to al_herb_bag; consider renaming.

The frost+void branch of `_reaction` tests `t.hp <= p * 2` with no Doom (EXEC does subtract it). The Oracle's Cold Star and every frost+void card ignore the Hexweaver's Doom. Fix: subtract `st.doom` in that check.
- [depth] "The Fire that hot cures leave" (Alchemist brief, Hexweaver hook; Hexweaver brief, Alchemist hook): no Alchemist card puts Fire on a hero, so Pass It On has nothing of the Alchemist's to move. ## Summary

Counts: 16 bug, 22 balance, 8 depth, 7 style (53 findings; several lines cover a group of cards).

The three most important problems:

1. **Rites without Exhaust.** All 5 Oracle rites and all 8 Hexweaver rites (including the path card Death Watch) are reshuffled and replayed, and each replay stacks another copy of the rite. Hall of Mirrors and The Rubric become permanent.
2. **Delay barely works under the heroes-then-foes Ordo.** Stop Time and Reverse the Glass postpone nobody (the queue just cycles), and The Turned Glass only reorders unless the foe is alone. A single Delay is a free Stun on the last foe in the queue and nothing on any other foe, so the Chronomancer's control plan rests on an all-or-nothing effect.
3. **Unbind hits the Hexweaver, not the foe.** Every affliction's effects target "self", so Bind the Fear and Take the Curse In give their when-drawn harm to the Hexweaver. Close behind: Fall Back and Into the Doorway test `targeting_me` on a hero, which is always 0; self-Transfer wipes the Pactbound's own statuses; Absolute Zero ignores Doom; and Hex has no damage floor (Hex lock for 2 vigour).
