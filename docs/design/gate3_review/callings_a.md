# Gate 3 check: Bulwark, Duelist, Elementalist

Checked against `scripts/combat.gd` (line numbers below), GATE3_VOCAB, GATE3_ENGINE_UPDATE, DECISIONS and the three
Gate 2 briefs. The build ran clean in a scratch copy ("validation problems: none"); budget flags for these three
modules are kits only.

## Bulwark (`tools/content/v3/bulwark.py`)

- [bug] bw_bramble_wall: the card Taunts first, but `attackers` reads each foe's original `intent_target`; Taunt only redirects when the blow resolves (combat.gd 655), so "2 Thorns for each foe that means to strike you" leaves out every foe the same card has just pulled onto him. The same gap hits Rime Coat, Spiked Boss, Deep Winter, Breath of the North and Shield Rim when they are played after a Taunt. Fix in the engine: when Taunt lands, retarget single-target attack intents to the taunter, so the readers and the intent arrows agree.
- [bug] attackers / targeting_me / targeted (combat.gd 739, 808, 933) count only intents aimed at him or at `all_heroes`. A `front2` blow that will hit the rank-1 Bulwark gives no Frost, Thorns or Block from any "means to strike you" card. Fix: count `front2`/`back2` intents when the hero stands in those ranks.
- [bug] Self-harm at Death's Door: SELFDMG goes through `_lose_hp`, which at Death's Door spends a Grit or kills (combat.gd 1458). The Last Bastion's Death's Door payoffs (Still Standing, Unbowed, Answer the Blow, Defiance, Still Here, The Door Does Not Fall) share an archetype with Blood for Iron, Blood Price, Hair Shirt, Flagellant's Cord and Bleed for Them. Text like "Lose 3 HP" hides that the card costs a Grit or kills him at 0 HP. Fix: in the engine, self-harm does nothing at Death's Door, or add "At Death's Door, lose 1 Grit instead" to the text.
- [bug] bw_defiance: STRESS(0, per="scars", k=-0.5) floors a negative number, so an odd Scar count sheds 1 extra (5 Scars sheds 3; the text says 2). Fix: round toward zero in the engine, or write "rounded up".
- [balance] Fortify stacks: `_add_status` adds n, and start_round keeps Block while fortify > 0 (combat.gd 190, 1573). Playing the kit Bank the Cold every round keeps Fortify at 1, so from level 4 the Ironwarden's Block never clears for 1 vigour a round. That is Winter Without End's capstone. Two Fortify cards in one round hold Block for 2 rounds. The brief's risk section says Fortify holds Block for one round only. Fix: engine sets fortify = max(fortify, n); Bank the Cold becomes "Fortify. Gain 3 Block" with the Block kept for one round only.
- [balance] bw_weight_of_the_door (also Over the Rim and Break the Ice) reads Block and does not spend it. With the Fortify issue above, Weight of the Door deals 6 plus a pile that only grows (30+ by round 4) on every play. The brief says "finishers spend the Block they count". Fix: add CONS(s="block", to="self"), as Rimeshield Blow and Shatterguard do.
- [balance] Armor has no cap (combat.gd 1014; the Rules v2 cap of 5 is not in). Base 2, plus Blood for Iron (a common, 2 per copy), plus Patched with Lead (1 to 2): three Blood for Iron give 8+ Armor, which zeroes most foe hits (about 9 at Threat 0) and every multi-hitter. Fix: cap at 5 in the engine, or Blood for Iron gives 1 Armor or moves to uncommon.
- [balance] bw_the_door_does_not_fall: every hit at Death's Door, even a fully blocked one, gives +4 Block (which refills the wall for the next hit) and +1 Might for the fight, with no per_round limit. Against a Taunted group that is 4 to 8 Might a round, compounding. Fix: per_round 2, or Might on the first hit each round only.
- [balance] bw_ring_the_helm: Tenacity clears at round end (combat.gd 1895), so Ring the Helm plus any Mark stuns the same elite every round for 2 vigour. Mark can come from the Duelist's kit every round or Pronounce Sentence at 0. Any pull also flags the foes it shuffles as moved (combat.gd 1591). Fix: BASHSTUN skips a foe that lost an action last round, or the card Exhausts.
- [balance] bw_hunker (0 cost: 2 Block and Anchor) and bw_breath_of_the_north (0 cost: 2 Frost per attacker, 6+ against three) have no real drawback, so playing them is always correct. Upgraded Draw Their Eyes is a free Taunt. Fix: Hunker costs 1 and gives 5 Block; Breath of the North costs 1 or gets max=4.
- [depth] Thornguard: Thorn Hedge (C), Hedge of Spikes (R) and Crown of Iron Thorns pay when an ally is struck, but the archetype's core (Come and Take It, Flagellant's Cord, Bramble Wall, Punishing Wall) Taunts every single-target blow off the allies. Ally Thorns then pay only against sprays, and they halve each round. Fix: give the ally cards a rider that does not need the ally struck, or a Guard version ("Guard them; they gain Thorns").
- [style] bw_grit_your_teeth, bw_bleed_for_them: the text says "Dosed:" but kw lacks "Dosed" (Duelist and Elementalist set it), so the hover text is missing. Add kw=["Dosed"].
- [style] The NEEDS line for bw_the_door_does_not_fall is stale: a hit fully absorbed by Block already costs no Grit (`_lose_hp` returns when d <= 0, combat.gd 1455). Delete it.
- [style] Names: bw_blood_price duplicates Hexweaver's "Blood Price"; bw_old_wound is one letter from Hexweaver's "Old Wounds"; bw_unbowed reuses the virtue Unbowed. Rename all three.
- [style] Ironwarden cards make the door "pine" (Bank the Cold, Windward Shield, Winter Vigil, Glacier Wall, Pine and Iron). The brief has one oak church door, and the path rigs share the same door part. Use "the oak door", or add the northern door to the brief.
- [style] bw_the_ledger_of_wounds flavour: "Prior Aldric keeps a ledger of the dead" contradicts data/abbey.json ("Prior Aldric's ledgers count Teeth, not dead"). Reword.
- [style] Padded triples: bw_deathless ("Lead in the plate, rope round the door, a bandage under the helm."), bw_frostbite_bash ("Frost in his beard, frost on the mace, frost in you now."), bw_draw_their_eyes ("Look at me. Not at them. At me.").

## Duelist (`tools/content/v3/duelist.py`)

- [bug] du_serrated_rhythm, du_the_endless_measure: `!t.played` is true for any hero who has not acted yet. Let the Fever Out (ally, physical) and the kit Quickstep (adjacent ally, physical) are physical cards, so the rites put Bleed on an ally or deal 3 to one; the Bleed lands right after Let the Fever Out cleansed. Fix: an engine `"target": "enemy"` filter on play rites (the NEEDS line asks for it). Until then, give those two cards a non-physical cat.
- [bug] du_the_long_sentence: Mark 20 falls by 1 each round and the rite needs t.mark>=10, so it stops 11 rounds after it is played, which falls inside a long ruler fight. The text says "for the rest of the fight". The card also uses Hexweaver Doom, though the brief keeps the two capstones distinct ("counts hits landed instead"). Fix: Mark 99 with if t.mark>=50; leave the Doom choice to Cole.
- [bug] A "deal N more" clause is a separate hit. Each D() runs its own `_attack` (combat.gd 966), so the extra damage pays Armor, foe Dodge, foe Thorns and Riposte again and adds Might again. Affected: Measure the Neck (up to 3 hits: a Marked, moved foe with 2 Armor takes 3, not 9), Follow Through, Slip and Cut, In the Wake, Heading Stroke, Shatter Point, The Square Tip. The same applies to the Bulwark's Answer the Blow, Break the Ice and Shatterguard. Fix: one hit using per= on a 0/1 quantity (D(3, per="same_target", k=4), D(3, per="dd", k=6)), or text that says "hit again".
- [balance] Momentum never falls (end_round does not touch it, combat.gd 1881). Turn of the Wrist (C: 1 + 2 per Momentum) and Quick Hands (U: twice 1 + Momentum) read it without spending it. A Bladedancer gains 4 to 5 a round (back-to-back plays, Light Feet, Catch the Rhythm, the Oracle's Quicken, the Alchemist's Haste Draught), so Turn of the Wrist hits 25 to 30 by round 3, every play. Final Judgment's Execute 5 + 4 per Momentum then reaches elite HP, against the brief's "Momentum raises them modestly". Fix: Momentum halves at round end (as Thorns do), or put max= on the readers (for example 9).
- [balance] Riposte falls only when it fires (combat.gd 1404), and the Bulwark's Taunt and Guard keep blows off the Duelist, so Parry, En Garde, Open Guard and Counter-Time stockpile it. Answering Steel (U: 3 per Riposte, not spent) passes 40 by round 4. Fix: Riposte halves at round end, or Answering Steel spends its Riposte (CONS, then per="consumed").
- [balance] du_weigh_the_wound (C: 3 + 1 per 4 missing HP) and du_second_stroke (U: 6 + 1 per 2 missing HP) scale with absolute missing HP. At Threat 0 a ruler has 330 to 480 HP; at half HP Weigh the Wound deals 44 to 63 and Second Stroke 88 to 126, which makes them the best ruler cards in the game. The brief says the Executioner is weak against rulers, and the budget assumed 14 missing HP. Fix: max=12 and max=20, or scale on t.hp_pct.
- [balance] du_haemorrhage (U, no Exhaust): +1 Bleed, then double, and it repeats. Two plays turn 10 Bleed into 46 (true damage at round end), which Red Hands, Bled Pale and Drain the Bowl then cash in. Fix: Exhaust (the Bulwark's Rose of Iron does) or a max.
- [balance] du_bowl_passed_round: the upgrade removes Exhaust from a card that heals every hero 1 per 2 Bleed on all foes. With 16 Bleed on the line that is 32 HP for 1 vigour, every play. Fix: keep Exhaust and raise the rate instead.
- [balance] du_drag_to_the_block: this kit is strictly worse than two commons, the Bulwark's Hook and Drag (pull 1, deal 4) and the Duelist's Haul Forward (pull 1, deal 2, Mark). Budget 2.0 of 4.2. Fix: the base kit pulls and Marks (today's upgrade); the upgrade Marks for 2 rounds.
- [depth] The Bladedancer's lightning thread is cat="lightning", so its blade damage is not physical. Arc Cut, Thunder Lunge, Forked Blade and Charged Point never Shatter a Frozen foe, skip physical resist and weakness, and do not trigger Serrated Rhythm or The Endless Measure (both filter on cat physical). The brief's Elementalist pairing ("the Bladedancer's first hit Shatters it") works only with plain cuts. Fix: cat="physical" with A(L, n, attr="stable"), since the course comes from the effect.
- [depth] Bloodletter healing goes to the most wounded hero (Staunch, Cupping Glass, Drain the Bowl, Barber's Trade, The Bowl Runs Over). A Last Bastion at 0 HP is always that hero, and any heal lifts him off Death's Door (combat.gd 1553), switching off his payoffs. The brief lists "Staunch heals the Last Bastion" as a pairing. Fix: target "ally" (the player chooses) on Staunch and Cupping Glass at least.
- [depth] EXEC ignores Prime (combat.gd 1213), so the brief's Oracle hook "Prime on an Execute or a finisher" does nothing for Final Judgment or Hanging Day; the Prime passes on to the next action. Fix: the engine adds Prime to the Execute threshold.
- [depth] du_open_guard: Taunt goes to the first Taunting hero in rank order (combat.gd 656), so Open Guard draws nothing in a round the rank-1 Bulwark also Taunts. Its Riposte 4 is still the card's value, but say so, or drop the Taunt for more Riposte.
- [style] du_follow_through duplicates the neutral card "Follow Through" (neutral.py follow_through). Rename one.
- [style] du_duellists_eye is named "The Duellist's Eye"; the calling is spelled "Duelist".
- [style] du_final_judgment flavour "No appeal. No reprieve. The bell has already rung for it." is a padded triple.

## Elementalist (`tools/content/v3/elementalist.py`)

- [bug] Prime is not spent by AT, AN, REACT or MISSING; only A() calls `_take_prime` (combat.gd 1027 to 1046), and VOCAB says course stacks spend half. The brief's Oracle hook (Prime on the Volatile strike, so the Conjunction grows) therefore works only on the fixed-course A() cards (Cold for the Void, Strip the Plate, Rime and Brine). On Spark, Pour Out, Twin Sigil, Heavy Pour and The Brass Reservoir the Prime passes to the next action. Fix in the engine: apply_attuned, apply_next and react call `_take_prime(s, ctx, true)`.
- [bug] HERO has no "attuned" key, so the Elementalist starts on water (data default, index 3). All 20 Drowned Kingdom creatures resist water: Sigil Flick's 1 Volatile halves to 0 ("Resisted"), and Ink Bolt lands 1. Strike Across and the Conjoin kit on a water pile remove a stack and apply nothing (REACT subtracts the stack before the resisted application, combat.gd 1045). The brief says no world can make the starting deck dead. Fix: set HERO["attuned"] (lightning, which the Drowned are weak to) or set it per world; REACT should not remove the stack when the result resists to 0.
- [bug] el_perpetual_working, el_stopper_the_jar, el_seal_the_jar: every application spends all of the hero's Focus (combat.gd 1602), so these "apply 1" and "apply 3" effects pour the Accumulator's banked Focus into a Stable top-up. The rite even does this in the middle of a round, on an ally's Conjunction. The text never says so. Fix: let rite applications skip Focus in the engine, or add "(uses your Focus)" to the text.
- [balance] Focus never falls. el_stored_breath (R, Retain, no Exhaust: +3, then double) compounds it (4, 14, 34), and el_weight_of_the_vessel (5 + 2 per Focus, not spent), el_under_the_weight and el_brass_gauge (Block equal to Focus every round) read it without spending. That is 73 damage for 1 vigour by the third Stored Breath, and the Alchemist's Clarity Draught adds 5 Focus. Fix: Stored Breath Exhausts or gets a max; Weight of the Vessel uses k=1 or spends half.
- [balance] el_pressure (path, no Exhaust: doubles the largest course) feeds uncapped readers: el_weight_and_measure (R: 4 per stack of the largest course), el_weigh_the_stacks (U: 2 per attuned stack, not spent) and plain Burn. 12 Fire doubled twice is 48 Burn a round, and Weight and Measure then deals 194. Fix: Pressure Exhausts (as Rose of Iron does); put max= on the readers.
- [balance] el_sulphur_match (C): any Toxin on the target makes it a Combust (3x power). With 4 Toxin (from Gall Ink or the Plaguewright) it deals 18 to 21 for 1 vigour, about three commons' worth. Fix: 1 Volatile Fire, or drop the D(3) rider.
- [balance] el_ring_turn (0 cost, path: Attune and 2 Focus) is strictly better than the base kit Turn the Ring (1 vigour: Attune and 1 Focus). Fix: Ring Turn costs 1 and does more, or the kit gets better.
- [depth] Catalyst and Prismatic have no common payoff. Every Conjunction counter (Warding Sigil, Reaction Shield, Feedback) and Spectrum reader (Count the Colours, Prism Ward) is uncommon or higher, so a draft of commons is all setup. The brief's assembly section ("commons cover setup and payoff twice over") then fails, though its complexity section puts counting cards at uncommon. Cole should pick one. Suggest moving Reaction Shield and Count the Colours to common.
- [depth] Hold Apart is cut (NEEDS), and nothing else protects a Prismatic pile from the company's Volatile hits: the Duelist's Ride the Lightning, the Volatile Water from the Bulwark's The Long Night, or the Elementalist's own Sigil Flick starters. This is the friction risk the brief names. Keep the NEEDS line high on the engine list.
- [style] el_conjoin is named for its own keyword, so the card reads "Conjoin: Conjoin 1." Turn the Ring (kit) and Ring Turn (path) have near-identical names and near-identical effects, and so do Stopper the Jar (kit) and Seal the Jar. Rename el_conjoin, el_ring_turn and el_stopper_the_jar.

## Company hooks across the starting company

Hooks that work in the engine: the Bulwark's Counterweight (Prime 3, pull) feeds a Duelist Follow-up on the same foe (both land). Hook and Drag, Draw the Tide and Haul Forward give `t.moved` to Measure the Neck, Ring the Helm and Cross Stroke. Rime Coat, Frost the Swing and Rime and Brine Freeze, and the Bulwark's mace or plain Duelist cuts Shatter. Break the Ice reads Conjunctions. Ground the Arc and Charged Point read the Duelist's Lightning. Shatter Point reads the Ironwarden's Frost. Iron Maiden reads the Bloodletter's Bleed, and Barbs Left In feeds it.
- [depth] Broken or inverted hooks (detailed above): the Bulwark's Taunt makes the Duelist's Riposte stockpile without limit. Bloodletter heals lift the Last Bastion off Death's Door. Prime skips Elementalist course cards and Executes. Ring the Helm plus the Duelist's Mark stuns an elite every round. The lightning cuts cannot Shatter the Elementalist's Freeze.
- [depth] Elementalist Volatile strikes take up to 2 per stack applied from the Ironwarden's Frost (combat.gd 1629). Right after the Elementalist cashes the Frost in, Cold Iron, Brittle, Glacier Wall and Shatterguard read near 0. Only Break the Ice pays the Bulwark for a Conjunction. A Bulwark card that pays per Conjunction (Block or Frost re-laid) would close the loop.

## Summary

Counts: bug 10, balance 16, depth 9, style 10 (45 findings).

Three most important:
1. Resources that never fall are read without being spent: Momentum (Turn of the Wrist, Quick Hands, Final Judgment), Riposte (Answering Steel) and Focus (Stored Breath with Weight of the Vessel). Uncapped doublers (Haemorrhage, Pressure) are the same problem. Each becomes a 30 to 100+ damage card by round 3 or 4. Fix in the engine (round-end decay) or with max= on the readers.
2. Fortify stacks, so the Ironwarden kit Bank the Cold keeps Block forever for 1 vigour a round, and Weight of the Door, Over the Rim and Break the Ice read that pile without spending it.
3. Weigh the Wound and Second Stroke scale on absolute missing HP and deal 44 to 126 to a ruler at half HP. The Executioner becomes the best ruler killer, against the brief.

Next tier: Prime ignored by attuned, next-course and Conjoin applications and by Execute (breaks the Oracle hooks); Taunt does not update the "means to strike you" readers; self-harm at Death's Door costs Grit or kills; the Elementalist starts on water against the all-water-resistant first world.
