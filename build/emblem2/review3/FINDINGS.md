## Across the library

1. **L8 misreads were not fixed:** bandage, suture, stinger, hood, shard, bellows, cord and hand. New misreads: brazier, coins, ring, bellrope, vessel, knuckles, mortar and door.
2. **Layouts over the cap:** ledge 39%, close 33%. Four identical pairs.
3. **Family hashing makes duplicates:** 7 objects on 15 of 46 cards, each pair the same variant.
4. **Objects picked by a note noun, not the card:** Handbell, Frost Phial, Gold Leaf, Ringing, Augury of Birds, Rat Bag, Hartshorn, Wheel Turns, Scourge.
5. **Still lifes, not acts:** 44 of 46 cards. Two hand cards, and they share the same hand.
6. **The hang layout is broken:** the chain ends in the air on 4 cards.
7. **Crops cut the signature:** anchor crown, handbell mouth, flask body, alembic receiver, rod base.
8. **Object FX bypass the ink rules:** sine-wave steam, cartoon flames, twig lightning, flat fog.
9. **Void holes:** identical floating dots on all 8 void cards.
10. **Grit gap:** coarse uniform crosshatch, soft cel blobs that ignore form, sponge noise, flat fills, no contact shadows.
11. **Colour:** rainbows, stray red, ochre that reads as gold, and one sepia ground for every physical card.

Fixes that lift the most cards:
- (a) hang, close and water layouts honour a declared hook and signature box;
- (b) all object FX go through card_kit ink FX;
- (c) per-card family variants, with a unique (object, variant, layout) per batch;
- (d) the card name's noun first, plus a forced-object table;
- (e) an act and hand option on object comps;
- (f) void holes seeded per card and drawn with D.hole on the object;
- (g) ledge contact shadow and snapping.
